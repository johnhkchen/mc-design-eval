# Golden Gate — Front Elevation (vision end-frame)

> **Subject is the GOLDEN GATE BRIDGE — a bridge, NOT a temple.** Realize the bridge massing below
> (twin towers + main cables + portal); ignore any generic "temple" phrasing in the surrounding
> instructions. This is a forward-looking concept ("where it's heading"), not a shipped build.

## 1. Lore & setting
The closing "ambition" frame for the evolution-showcase: the staged sculptor pointed at something
that is unmistakably *not* a temple — the Golden Gate Bridge, read head-on as a single grand face.
One iconic silhouette, built from cubic blocks, floating against pure black so it segments cleanly.
A statement of reach: the method generalizes past temples.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
A **head-on FRONT ELEVATION** of the bridge's twin **Art-Deco towers**, seen from the water so the
two towers stack into one symmetrical face. The logic is **vertical hierarchy + cable geometry**:
two tall stepped towers govern the frame; the **main suspension cables** sweep between and over them
as bold block catenaries; the **roadway deck** runs as a horizontal band low across the towers, with
the **portal opening** (the gap traffic passes through) punched between the tower legs. Strict
bilateral symmetry about the centerline. Subtraction gives relief: the portal and the tower-leg gaps
are recesses, not clutter.

## 3. Color palette (silhouette-bright, segments on black)
- **Dominant — International Orange (terracotta / red-orange block):** the towers, cables, and deck
  — the bridge's own signature color, carried across the whole outer silhouette so no edge vanishes
  into the black field.
- **Supporting — warm light stone / smooth quartz:** the stepped setback bands and tower-cap
  detailing, for contrast against the orange.
- **Accent — deep slate-grey:** reserved for **recessed interior** only (the portal void, the gaps
  between tower legs) — never on the outline.
- The outermost elements (tower crests, the high point of the cable sweep) stay bright orange/stone,
  never a dark or dim tone.

## 4. Motifs & ornament (bold block scale only)
- **Stepped Art-Deco setbacks** capping each tower — chunky receding blocks, no fine spire.
- **Cable catenary** rendered as a thick block arc from tower top to mid-deck and back up — a few
  whole blocks thick, bold, readable.
- **Vertical fluting** on the tower shafts as plain wide color-blocked bands (no thin lines).
- **Portal frame** — a bold stepped rectangular border around the central opening.
- No figures, no text, no filigree, no thin finials — block-scale geometry carries it.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Towers:** two identical towers, **tall** — height ≈ **3:1** over width; stepped tops.
- **Cables:** the main cable sweeps from each tower crest down to the deck midpoint and up to the
  opposite crest — the signature catenary, bold and continuous.
- **Deck:** a horizontal orange band crossing low (~lower third), the portal opening beneath/between
  the towers ≈ **1:2** wide-to-tall.
- **Massing:** the two towers + cable sweep fill most of the frame, centered, fully visible with a
  small margin on every side.
- **Symmetry:** strict bilateral about the central axis.
