## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-47** / Story **S-186**. The follow-on stub from E-46's DO-NOT-PROMOTE (T-185-01). Make the
style-distance term read the **picture**, not the **pack**, then re-run the E-46 gate.

**The defect (T-185-01 `FINDINGS.md` + `LOCALIZATION.md`):** on the S-183 decoupling corpus at VOTES=6 the
recalibrated term is **PACK-DRIVEN** — `packEffect 45 ≫ conceptImageEffect 8`; swapping the *picture* (right→
wrong, pack held right) costs only 8 points, swapping the *pack* (matched→foreign, picture held right) costs 45;
`ct-wrongpack` (faithful build, wrong pack) scores a flat 0; on the gatehouse the wrong-picture build *outscores*
the faithful one (CIE −12). Hard-middle term-vs-label agreement is 0.20 against **labelable** ground truth
(proxy self-consistency 0.9). The gate is sound; the term measures pack-material agreement, not picture
resemblance.

**The exact seam (`src/workshop/diagnose.mjs`):** `diagnoseRenderArgs` sets `style: program?.style ?? pack.style`
and `style_profile: styleProfileBlock({ pack })` — the entire "expected roof/walls/openings" half of the
DiagnoseBuild prompt is **pack-derived**; the concept image is passed only as ancillary grounding. `styleFidelity
Score` (`src/workshop/bakeoff-score.mjs`) then penalizes departures from that pack spec. E-45 fixed *how*
wrong-style is penalized; it did not change *what "expected" means*.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Conditioning the wrong-style judgement on the rendered-build-vs-concept-image match makes the term
**PICTURE-DRIVEN** on the same corpus, and the re-run gate licenses a (human-signed-off) promotion. **Fails if:**
the picture effect rises only by re-coupling to the pack (no real decoupling — the crux cells `*-wrongpack` /
`*-samepack-*` must move the *right* way, not just the headline effect); matched builds regress; or the re-gated
decomposition stays PACK-DRIVEN / INCONCLUSIVE / hard-middle agreement < 0.70.
