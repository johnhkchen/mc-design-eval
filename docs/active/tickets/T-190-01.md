## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-48** / Story **S-190**. The payoff. With the gradient wired (S-188) and the highest-leverage hands
built (S-189), run the **sustained picture-driven climb** on the gatehouse and answer: does the build
**measurably approach its picture, round over round, under its own steam**, in a way a **human agrees with on
the glance**? Where is the **ceiling**?

This is the M1 capstone evidence — one house that looks like its picture, reached by the loop, not by hand.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A ≥4–5-round run shows a **monotone-ish lift toward the concept** (picture-critique trend improves; beside-
concept renders visibly close the gap), a **human glance agrees** on a sampled before/after, and the lift is
**mostly autonomous**. **Fails if:** it plateaus early (remaining gap = hands not built → name for E-49); it
oscillates (accept-gate/restraint insufficient); the human glance **disagrees** with the critique's
"improvement" (gradient still isn't the glance → E-47 family); or the lift needs a human steering each round
(not autonomous → report how much human was required).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Sustained climb (≥4–5 rounds) run on the gatehouse; per-round picture-critique trend + beside-concept
      renders (first / best / last) in the work dir.
- [ ] A **human-glance check** on a before/after sample (the critique is the steering proxy; the glance is
      the judge).
- [ ] The **ceiling** named: where it plateaus, what it still can't fix, and **how much human intervention**
      the run actually required (the M1→M4 autonomy honesty).
- [ ] **Recorded honestly:** lift / plateau / oscillation / glance-disagreement — at full strength; the
      plateau's "what's missing" scopes E-49.
- [ ] `npm test` green; frozen instrument untouched.

## Notes

  result, not a failure.
