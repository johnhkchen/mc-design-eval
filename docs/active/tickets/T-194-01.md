## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-51** / Story **S-194**. Reviewer authorized **letting the loop carve openings** (2026-06-17). The
E-50 plateau's arched gate was unbuildable under the blanket no-air-op rule. Implement the charter narrowing +
the hand.

- **Narrow no-air-op → aperture-coherence gate.** Carving (removing material) allowed **only to form a
  *declared* opening** (door/gate/window in the recognition program); everything else stays no-air-op
  ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]).
- **Aperture-coherence gate:** a carved opening must be a coherent **dressed** aperture (continuous
  head/jambs/sill, framed, no ragged edges/stray voids); **closure holds on every surface except the declared
  aperture** ([[generate-first-wall-watertightness]] precedent).
- **The hand:** carve a declared opening to its intended width/shape + dress it (head/jambs/sill; S-179
  voussoir for arched heads).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The loop carves the gatehouse gate to a wide **arched dressed** aperture that reads on the glance, closure held
everywhere but the aperture. **Fails if:** carving yields ragged voids / reopens holes (the coherence gate
can't tell an opening from a hole, [[reference-is-spec-not-substrate]]) → **refute the relaxation, revert
openings to recess-only**, wide arches a named limit; or the carve breaks closure on a *non*-aperture surface
(the "declared opening only" guard leaked → re-scope).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No-air-op narrowed to an aperture-coherence gate: carve only declared openings; closure-except-aperture
      enforced; non-aperture surfaces still no-air-op (unit-tested).
- [ ] Carve+dress hand built (width/shape + head/jambs/sill + S-179 voussoir arch).
- [ ] **The wide arched gate reads** on the gatehouse beside concept; `closureOf` measured
      everywhere-but-aperture and not regressed.
- [ ] **Recorded honestly:** does the coherence gate keep carves clean? If not, **revert to recess-only** and
      name wide arches as the bounded limit (do not ship a leaky carve).
- [ ] `npm test` green; frozen instrument untouched.
