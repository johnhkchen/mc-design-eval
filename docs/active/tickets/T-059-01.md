## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-18**, the **thin-form fix**. Voxelizing a TRELLIS GLB is the decisive form win — except on
**thin** subjects. **Bow & arrow** voxelizes worst of the 7 (form IoU **0.473**): thin members (bowstave,
string, arrow shaft) are below a voxel thick in places, so at the global scale (`voxelizeGlb`, E-16) they
**drop out or stair-step** into disconnected fragments. This ticket builds a **thin-feature-aware** layer
over `voxelizeGlb`: detect thin members (local thickness below a threshold / the mesh medial axis) and
**guarantee they survive** — connectivity (a thin member is a connected chain, never severed) and finer
effective resolution where thin (or a per-subject scale bump so a thin member spans ≥1 voxel on its thin
axis). (Sword — the thinnest — gets *no* GLB at all and is the consolidation's form-routing finding, not
this ticket.)

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `voxelizeGlbThin(glb, opts) → occupancy`: detect thin members + preserve their connectivity /
      resolve at finer scale; shares the E-16 GLB mesh parser (no duplicate). Deterministic, **pure,
      GL-free**.
- [ ] Unit-tested on a **synthetic thin mesh** (a ~1-unit rod): it voxelizes to a **connected chain**, not
      disconnected fragments; a flat plate is unaffected.
- [ ] Applied to **bow-and-arrow** (+ koi fins): **form IoU before/after** recorded (target: bow-and-arrow
      rises above 0.473, or the limit is shown and explained), plus a **no-dropped-thin-components** check
      (connected-component count of thin members).
- [ ] `npm test` green.
