## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-52** / Story **S-203**. The defining feature. The E-49 capstone (T-201) logged `carve_arch` producing
a ragged notched carve (columns 3,4,8,9) that the aperture-coherence gate **correctly refuted**, falling back
to `frame_arch` which left a too-narrow framed slot ("passage too narrow for an arch head — needs a wider
opening (a rebuild)"). A 1-wide passage can't be *widened by carving*; build a hand that **rebuilds** the
declared gate aperture to its intended wide arched form.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The rebuild hand yields a **wide arched gate** passing the aperture-coherence gate (coherent head/jambs/sill +
voussoir from S-179), reading as an arch on the glance, closure held everywhere but the declared aperture (on
the S-202 plane metric). **Fails if:** the rebuild drops closure below the form-ready threshold → the climb
re-locks detail / oscillates (S-203 + S-202 are then coupled, co-design closure-except-aperture); the wide
opening breaks structural integrity (lintel/arch can't span → needs a load path the generator lacks, name it);
or the coherence gate refutes the rebuild too (carving declared wide openings is a deeper limit than E-51's
charter assumed — a real bounding result).
