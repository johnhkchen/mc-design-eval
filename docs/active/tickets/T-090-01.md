## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-25** / Story **S-090**. The E-24 zone-fill (T-085-01) establishes each structural zone's dominant
material on the **wall fields** — but measured on the committed cottage artifact
(`benchmarks/sculpture/spray-paint/cottage/artifact.json`, 2026-06-10), the *full exposed surface* tells a
different story:

- Roof projected straight down: **96.7% spruce** (640/662 columns). Roof band (y≥14) full exposed
  surface: **42.7% spruce_planks, 33.5% stone_bricks, 10.2% cobblestone** — over a thousand grey cells on
  the side faces of the stepped courses and the gable mass. At every oblique angle the roof reads as a
  grey-brown jumble.
- Upper wall band [7,14): **28.3% stone_bricks residue** on under-eave/edge cells the wall-field
  enumeration missed.

The fill covered the surfaces one enumeration found; the camera finds the rest. The fix is to enumerate
the zone's surface by **exposure**, not by wall-field membership.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A **pure full-shell fill op**: for each structural zone (`structuralZones`, floor-line-bounded),
      enumerate every zone cell with **any of its 6 faces exposed** and set it to the zone's dominant
      material from the map — preserving **declared secondaries** (timber studs/trim runs) and **declared
      sub-regions** (a chimney region keeps its material). Unit-tested on synthetic occupancy (a stepped
      roof with grey side cells → all roof material; a stud run → preserved).
- [ ] Wired into the spray-paint pipeline as a stage (after the E-24 wall-field fill or replacing it —
      implementer's call, recorded), behind the existing named `npm run`. **No hand-edits** (E-24 Rule 1).
- [ ] Run on the cottage: roof-band exposed-surface composition goes **42.7% → ≥90%** roof materials
      (chimney excepted as a declared sub-region); upper-band stone residue **≤5%**. Before/after
      histograms recorded with the same measurement used above (6-dir exposure per band).
- [ ] **Before/after renders at an oblique azimuth the old fill failed on** (e.g. 135° or 225°) saved —
      the grey roof sides visibly gone. The render is the evidence; the histogram is support (E-25 Rule 1).
- [ ] No subject-specific constants (E-25 Rule 3); `npm test` green.
