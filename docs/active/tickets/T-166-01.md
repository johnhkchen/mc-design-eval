the fused judge, on a held set of (concept, build) states. (2) **Gradient exists:** a *clean* build scored

flat at 22–32. **Fails if:** split ties/loses fused (collapse the split — report); the clean×wrong-style
spread stays inside noise (the per-style `expected` is cosmetic — blindness is in the model's reading,
route to a deeper measurement epic); or no build is clean enough to remove the confound (name the
clean-build prerequisite — S-160 geometry or a synthetic fixture).
