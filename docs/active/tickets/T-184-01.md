## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-46** / Story **S-184**. Turn the S-183 corpus into a GO/NO-GO verdict. The promotion gate is
*agreement with human judgment*, so labels are **human** (the reviewer; one-person panel for now).

Build: (1) a clean **pairwise labeling instrument** — beside-concept presentation of the **hard-middle
contestable pairs** (small enough for a focused session, not the full all-pairs matrix); (2) collect the
**human** labels (the human gate — the loop prepares everything up to it; **LLM-proxy fallback** is allowed
but **flagged as not human ground truth**, refute-only); (3) an **agreement + decomposition harness** that
reports term-vs-human agreement (pairwise accuracy / rank correlation) AND the **pack-effect vs
concept-image-effect** decomposition generalized from T-182's C-control.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The recalibrated term agrees with the human labels on the hard middle **and** its signal is carried by the
**concept-image**, not the pack, across the population. **Fails if:** the term tracks the pack at scale (the
T-182 confound generalizes → DO-NOT-PROMOTE, concept-image-conditioning fix — the publishable negative); or
inter-label agreement on the hard middle is low (no ground truth → ill-posed gate, reported); or the term
agrees only on the obvious pairs (validated where it didn't need to be).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A pairwise labeling instrument over the hard-middle pairs; labels collected (human gate satisfied, OR
      the LLM-proxy fallback run and **explicitly flagged non-licensing**).
- [ ] Harness reports: term-vs-human agreement **overall AND on the hard middle separately**; inter-label
      agreement rate; the **pack-vs-concept-image signal decomposition** across the corpus.
- [ ] **Recorded honestly:** does the term read the picture or the pack at population scale? Is the hard
      middle labelable? The GO/NO-GO recommendation for S-185, with the breadth named.
- [ ] `npm test` green; **nothing under `measurements/`** (the freeze is S-185, behind sign-off).

## Notes

- Hard-middle agreement reported **separately** — a term that only nails the obvious pairs is not validated.
- Output is evidence + recommendation, not a promotion.
