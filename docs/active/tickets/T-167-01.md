## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-40** / Story **S-167**. Build the labeled test corpus E-40 validates against and that fixes the
E-39 bake-off's 2-state under-powering. **≥8–10 (concept, build-render) states** spanning the defect axes
(massing/proportion, roof, structural integrity, palette/material, surface relief), including **clean ×
wrong-style pairs** (a clean build vs its matched-style concept AND a same-family wrong-style concept).
Reuse `experiments/eval-alignment/` renders + synthetic-clean fixtures (the clean-wrong-style harness
already uses a `synthetic-gatehouse (fixed)` program).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A single rater can label these states reliably enough to be ground truth: an unambiguous worst-defect
department on most states, and a consistent pairwise "more faithful to its concept" on the wrong-style
pairs. **Fails if:** the rater can't reliably pick the more-faithful build (style fidelity isn't a stable
human signal — the finding that wobbles E-40's premise; report it), or most states lack a single
unambiguous worst department (the dispatch metric needs multi-label — report it).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A versioned corpus JSON (e.g. `experiments/eval-alignment/corpus/defect-corpus.json`): ≥8–10 states,
      each with concept path, render dir, and labels `{worstDepartment, confidence}`; wrong-style pairs
      carry `{matchedConcept, wrongStyleConcept, moreFaithful}`. Carries a `rater` field (single-rater
      honest; no aggregation logic built).
- [ ] Excluded candidate states logged with a reason (single-azimuth / ambiguous worst-defect / chaotic) —
      no padding to hit the count.
- [ ] Clean × wrong-style pairs use synthetic/clean fixtures so the corpus is **not gated on the roof**.
- [ ] A pure loader + schema check; `npm test` green. No model spend required to *read* the corpus.

## Notes

- Single-rater is fine for now; record it as a limitation, not inter-rater agreement we don't have.
- This corpus is shared infrastructure for S-168/S-169 and future eval-validation — keep it self-describing.
