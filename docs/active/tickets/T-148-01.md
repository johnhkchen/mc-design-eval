## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-35** / Story S-148. The frozen instrument passes a build the glance rejects on texture: the
barn that recorded the first composite PASS (T-143-01: 4/4 same-object, v2 PASS, kit-aware PASS) is a
flat mono-fill box beside an articulated concept. The gate measures proportion + block-resemblance +
kit-presence and is **blind** to surface grammar and relief. This ticket adds the lens that sees them,
under the same identity-class discipline the budget work used ([[glance-true-budget-v2]],
T-095/T-101/T-110/T-137/T-144): **aggregation/precondition only — never a re-judge**, the frozen judge
contract (prompts, azimuths, severity grain) unmoved, committed records re-derive or are reported
beside, **version the policy tag not the envelope**.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **A relief/rhythm precondition or lens** in the instrument: measured from the build's own surface
      (rhythm period, proud-cell fraction, field/frame contrast) vs the concept's recognised grammar
      (T-145-01) — **not** a colour metric. A flat mono-fill wall **fails** (or carries a named relief
      residual) when the concept is articulated; an articulated build passes.
- [ ] **Calibrated from committed verdicts**: the flat barn that PASSED (T-143-01) is the anti-anchor —
      it must move to FAIL or a named relief residual under the lens; an articulated reference must
      pass. Calibration evidence committed (a sweep over committed builds), thresholds derived from
      data, **no per-building constants**.
- [ ] **Identity-class proof**: aggregation/precondition only (no judge call, no re-judge); both
      arithmetics reported where a threshold changes; committed records re-derive unchanged or are
      reported beside; the policy tag versioned (prior tag valid forever). The frozen judge contract is
      byte-unmoved (`instrument.diffs: []` where it applies).
- [ ] Tests (the anti-anchor + the articulated pass, monotone re-derivation of committed records);
      the offline checker tolerant of the new field; `npm test` green; `--repro`/`--offline`
      byte-identical.
