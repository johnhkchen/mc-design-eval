## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-53** / Story **S-207**. The test of the hypothesis. With the form-readiness metric corrected (T-206 —
the colonnade reads open again, forcing `close_shell` first), re-run the metered gatehouse climb. This decides
whether the form fix **alone** restores the build (M1 landed) or whether an independent detail-credit cold-start
gap remains (S-208).

**Hypothesis:** once the form closes, the detail hands compound and the build reaches its picture — T-201
already showed detail moves score on a closed form (relief +20, 12→32); the T-205 score-0 floor was an artifact
of the form never closing.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The climb picks `close_shell` first, **the shell closes and stays closed**, detail compounds, and the build
reaches **its picture** on the glance (closed dressed walls, wide arch, dark slate roof at the right pitch,
right orientation/scale), mostly autonomously — **M1 landed.** **Fails if:** it still **stalls at 0 on a
genuinely closed form** (→ confirms the S-208 detail-credit gap, *with* trajectory proof the form was closed);
a **seventh gap** appears (a hand that doesn't read, a missing kit material); or **score variance** makes the
verdict unreproducible (closed dressed build scores 32 one run, 0 the next → accept-signal de-noising needed).
