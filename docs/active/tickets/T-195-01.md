## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-51** / Story **S-195**. The E-50 climb's walls stayed dark because `articulate_walls` only
**recolors** — it can't build **relief**, so the WALL major never cleared. Build a real wall-relief hand:
proud quoins / clinker courses / dressed coursing standing *proud of* the wall plane (construction, not
recolor). Reuse the **E-43 treatment-grammar relief ops** (proud op, quoin/clinker brushes, edges-from-
geometry) as primitives. Recess by exclusion outside declared openings; closure not regressed.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The relief hand makes the gatehouse walls read as **pale dressed stone with proud quoins/coursing** on the
glance (a clear step up from flat recolor) and the **WALL major clears** so the S-191 override keeps it.
**Fails if:** the relief reads **busy/noisy** (amplitude/composition — E-43/E-35 lesson, on the render); the
proud op regresses closure; or the critique still can't *see* relief (scored material not relief → S-196
coverage gap, flagged, not a hand failure).
