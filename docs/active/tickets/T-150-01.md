## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-35** / Story S-150. The generator plots the roof as a **solid triangular prism of roof material
dropped on the wall box** — not how construction works. Grounded in the code:

- `src/view/roof-generate.mjs:271-281` — `for (let y = ... ; y <= top; y++) cells.push({ pos:[x,y,z],
  block: family.field })` fills **every cell** from floor to surface with **roof** material, including
  the two vertical **gable-end triangular faces**. Comment (lines 7-8): "gable-end walls fill to the
  ridge for free."
- `benchmarks/sculpture/recognition/barn.program.json` declares `gableRole: "wall.field.ground"` —
  **declared intent, never consumed** (`gableRole` appears only in validation + fixtures; the generator
  ignores it).
- `src/view/zone-map.mjs:67-69` — the gable cells land in `roofKeys`, so `zoneOf` returns `"roof"`;
  `zone-fill` then recolors the gable to the roof dominant. The skin never owns the gable as wall.
- No overhang: the roof's extent is exactly the wall footprint (`roof-generate.mjs` footprint bbox);
  the `sheet` columns only open the underside, they do not project past the perimeter.

Concept witness (barn run 017): **stone** gable end (vent + ladder), wood-shingle slopes **overhanging**
the walls. The fix is to build the **wall envelope** (incl. gable-end walls) first, then the **roof
covering** as a sloped skin with overhang. Scoped to the **barn** (and gable roofs generally); other
gable subjects benefit but the barn is the proving subject. Deterministic; **no judge run**.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Gable-end-as-wall.** The roof construct (or a sibling envelope step) builds the two vertical
      gable-end triangular faces in **wall material** by **consuming the program's `gableRole`**
      (default to the wall ground field when absent), so they belong to the **wall envelope** — owned by
      the **wall** zone and the wall grammar/skin, **not** `family.field`/roof. `src/view/zone-map.mjs`
      classifies the gable triangle as a wall zone (the sloped planes stay `roof`); zone-fill/skin dress
      it in wall material. Silhouette-neutral: the same cells are occupied, only their material/zone
      change.
- [ ] **Roof-as-covering.** Roof material lands on the **sloped covering + ridge only** — the gable end
      is no longer roof. The slopes' pitch and per-azimuth silhouette are **byte-unchanged** under the
      E-34 ruler (`maskProportions`) and the silhouette gate (no-regress harness; the change must not
      move ridge/eave/pitch).
- [ ] **Eave + verge overhang.** The roof covering projects past the wall perimeter at the **eaves**
      (drip edge) and the **gable verge** (rake) by a **recognized or pack-carried** amount (reuse
      S-147's `eave-overhang` brush / the relief op), **no per-building constant**. Note the interaction
      with E-34's skirt-aware eave detector: an overhanging eave course is *wider than the wall* — verify
      the proportion ruler reads the eave line correctly with the overhang present (architecturally the
      overhang *is* the eave line; record the reading either way).
- [ ] **Hollowness preserved.** The loft under the roof stays hollow (the existing `hollow-carve` path);
      the gable-end walls are part of the shell, not interior fill.
- [ ] **Barn re-run + the glance.** Re-run the barn through the generator (deterministic, `--repro`
      byte-identical) → the gable end reads **stone**, the roof reads as **covering with overhang**.
      Sheet beside the concept in `pr/assets/` (corrected vs prior-flat vs concept), the construction
      fix named. **No judge run** (out of scope — the glance is the proof).
- [ ] `docs/knowledge/design-learnings.md` E-35 section gains the **construction-model** note (prism-on-
      prism → envelope-then-covering); **review.md for T-150-01**; tests for gable-end-wall material,
      roof-covering-only, overhang, and the slope no-regress; `npm test` green; no per-building constants.
