## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-42** / Story **S-172**. Replace the solid roof-prism with a constructed roof. `roof-generate.mjs`
fills a ~72%-of-build **solid material prism** (reads as planks, drowns the walls) and lays a **single
ridge**. Build instead a **covering over the wall envelope** (hollow, material-correct trim) and **one
gable per `masses[]`** (multi-ridge), reusing `gableRecord`/`generateRoof` per mass and the `closureOf`
metric as the no-regress guard.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A covering-over-envelope roof + multi-ridge-per-mass makes the roof read as a roof (not a plank mountain)
and gives multi-mass subjects their correct gables, without regressing wall-track closure. **Fails if:**
removing the prism reopens narrow-roof coverage/closure seams (roof-fit is its own sub-problem — guard +
report), per-mass ridges don't register to the build frame (footprint-registration family), or the roof
reads right but the score doesn't move (cap is elsewhere — report).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Roof built as a covering over the envelope (not a solid fill); the build is no longer dominated by a
      single roof-material prism (census: roof material well under the prior ~72%).
- [ ] Multi-ridge: one gable per `masses[]` — the cottage's two perpendicular gables render correctly;
      barn's prism is gone. Renders beside concept.
- [ ] **No closure regression** vs the wall track (guard with `closureOf`); report any reopened roof-fit
      seam honestly.
- [ ] `npm test` green; frozen instrument untouched.

## Notes

- Reuse `gableRecord`/`generateRoof`/`closureOf` — construction, not a new roof engine. This is the roof
  analogue of the wall track's multi-mass construction.
