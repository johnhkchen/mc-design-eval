## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-32** / Story S-130. Material choice is **diegetic, not optical**: a building is made from
what its place affords ("locally quarried stone and plaster"), TRELLIS textures are an amalgam of
other games' assets (the barn's `flagged-mismatch` kit entries were optics correctly reporting it
cannot decide materials — T-121/T-122), and the vernacular reasoning that picks right materials is
language-space knowledge the model is excellent at and was never asked for. The human edge — taste —
enters **once per style** as ratification (E-32 Rule 4), amortized over every building (and later,
the town: one material story per place = palette coherence for free).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **The formation chain** (BAML functions from T-129-01, composed in a named runner): theme brief
      → **material story** (geology, timber, wealth class, roofing economy, trade) → **palette
      derivation** (role→block with per-role rationale **citing the story**, value-validated with the
      T-086 tooling) → **proportion rules** → **brush-needs list** (resolved against the T-128
      registry) → an **E-31-format style pack** (T-124 contract, `provenance` field populated).
- [ ] **Human ratification gate**: the formed pack is emitted as a **draft** requiring explicit
      ratification before commit/registration (the flow documented; the ratified pack records who/when).
- [ ] **Precedence + no-optics, provable**: the chain has **no GLB-texture input anywhere**
      (grep/review recorded); the pack contract's precedence (concept evidence > pack > vernacular
      default) is honored by construction.
- [ ] **Fixture: re-derive `rustic`** from a theme brief — recorded comparison vs the human-curated
      pack (closeness is the measure of the chain's quality; divergences named, not hidden).
- [ ] **The milestone's input: one genuinely new style formed** (theme brief recorded), ratified,
      committed. Raw replies committed; replays deterministic from the committed pack (E-31 Rule 5);
      `npm test` green.
