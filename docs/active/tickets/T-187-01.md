## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The tolerance lifts **faithful** builds (matchedRight up from 21) **without** lifting **wrong-picture** builds
(matchedWrong stays low), so the re-gate comes back **PICTURE-DRIVEN** with hard-middle agreement ≥ 0.70.
**Fails if:** it lifts everything uniformly (matchedWrong rises → agreement/decoupling regress — the dominant
risk; report matchedWrong explicitly); the +6 gap is irreducible (voxel-vs-art divergence entangled with
picture-divergence → the *scale* can't be widened by a tolerance; localize to the gate/NOISE/corpus-size, not
the term); or `gh-wrongpack` is a synthetic-grounding artifact (fixture fix, named, not a term change).
