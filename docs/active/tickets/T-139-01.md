## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-34** / Story S-139. `maskProportions` (`src/form/silhouette-proportion.mjs`, ~line 125)
anchors the eave at *the topmost row whose extent ≥ `eaveWidthFrac` (0.98) × the max row extent*
(`PROPORTION_DEFAULTS`, line ~45). The E-33 cottage carries a realized plinth band (y3–4, 1–2
blocks wider than the walls): the plinth IS the max extent, no wall row clears 0.98 × it, the eave
line lands on the plinth top, and everything above reads as roof — recorded ridge:eave 5.5 /
roofShare 0.8182 vs corrected ≈1.7 / 0.45 vs targets 1.4145 / 0.293 (T-138-02 review, finding 4).
The armed proportion gate then inverted the workshop's gradient: the model aimed the correct
wall-raise 5/6 rounds and the one accepted move (`storeys:3`) went the wrong way. Same instrument
family as the T-118 protrusion-polluted apex. The module is PURE (no GL/IO/Date/random, runs under
the `src/**/*.test.mjs` glob) and its detection rules are documented in `packs/README.md` as frozen
op parameters — never subject-tuned.

      declared op parameter set, documented in `packs/README.md` alongside the existing rules, with
      the chosen mechanism justified in design.md). No subject-conditional code, no per-building
      constants.

      where the rules differ, **both rulers are reported** side by side. Committed records remain
      untouched and valid; the frozen judge contract, azimuths, and thresholds unmoved.

      masks) pinned as a fixture; tests prove the corrected eave line (≈1.7 ridge:eave family, not

      constituents, recorded-fallback trigger) preserved and tested.
