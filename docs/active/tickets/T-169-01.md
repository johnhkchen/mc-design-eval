## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-40** / Story **S-169**. The referee for the style-distance term (T-168-01), on the S-167 corpus.
Build on `experiments/eval-alignment/{clean-wrong-style,bakeoff}.mjs`. Settle three things and record them
honestly; then **recommend** (do not execute) frozen-instrument promotion.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

With the new term: (1) the clean × wrong-style fixture **craters** (matched ≫ wrong-style, outside the ±12
noise); (2) the score **agrees with the human pairwise labels** on the corpus's contested wrong-style
pairs; (3) the ≥8–10-state bake-off can now actually discriminate split vs fused. **Fails if:** it craters
but **disagrees** with the human (over-penalizes a close style → recommend graded re-calibration, NOT
promotion); agrees on easy pairs but not the contested middle (captures only gross errors — say so); or the
bake-off still can't separate split from fused at 8–10 states (the split is marginal — recommend collapse).
Whichever way it lands is the result.
