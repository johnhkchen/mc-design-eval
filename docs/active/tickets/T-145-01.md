## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-35** / Story S-145. The build's program (Stage 3, `docs/knowledge/pipeline-philosophy.md`)
under-specifies the facade: it names bands and dominant blocks, never the rhythm, fields, quoins,
overhang, or per-face placement on the faces the single concept view never shows. A flat box is the
program being silent. The 2026-06-14 ratified narrowing (philosophy §Stage 2 + design-learnings):
the **textured GLB** may be read at multiple angles for **spatial articulation layout** (where the
studs/openings/pilasters/courses fall, and the rhythm) — feeding recognition, **never** material
identity; palette stays diegetic, from the brief. GLBs already exist on disk ([[trellis-glb-path-works]]);
TRELLIS bakes detail as flat texture on a near-smooth mesh, so this yields *layout* evidence, not
relief depth (depth is idealised downstream in S-146/S-147). Model calls on the `claude -p`
subscription shim — light tier (`--model` from `src/config.mjs`) for scoped per-face detectors, strong
for the design judgement; never the metered API.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **A facade-grammar extension to the building program**, schema-validated against the style pack:
      per-face **rhythm** (pilaster/stud spacing as period+phase or count), **panel fields** (the
      infill region between frame members), **quoins** (corner runs), **course lines** (y-rows),
      **eave overhang** + per-storey **jetty** depth, and **openings on a rhythm**. The pack sanctions
      which idioms/materials are admissible (no grammar names an off-pack idiom). No per-building
      constants — every number is recognised or pack-carried.
- [ ] **A multimodal recognition step** (typed function on the subscription shim) that reads concept
      + textured-GLB multi-angle renders at the gate azimuths and writes the grammar. **Each face
      records its evidence source** (concept vs textured-GLB) and the GLB rows are tagged
      *spatial-layout evidence*. A conformance check proves the grammar carries **no material
      decision** sourced from the GLB (material identity stays diegetic — the ratified rule).
- [ ] **Textured-GLB multi-angle renders** produced or reused at the gate azimuths (reuse the on-disk
      GLB; the render seam recorded). If a textured-GLB render must be generated, TRELLIS/Modal needs
      `dangerouslyDisableSandbox: true`; secrets stay in gitignored `.env`.
- [ ] **Honest fallback**: if GLB recognition proves unreliable on the unseen faces (amalgam texture,
      baked-flat relief), the front-face concept carries the grammar and the back/sides are idealised
      from the pack — **recorded, not forced** (a named record state, not a silent default).
- [ ] Tests: schema-validation fixtures; a recognition fixture (recorded reply → parsed grammar) that
      replays byte-identically offline; reply-policy (T-114) on malformed replies. `npm test` green;
      `--repro`/`--offline` byte-identical; no per-building constants.
