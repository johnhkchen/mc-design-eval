## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-49** / Story **S-199**. The T-198 metered climb stalled because the score-driven accept-gate
(`acceptsRound`, `src/workshop/climb-gate.mjs`) **rolled back `close_shell` on a picture-score tie** (16→16)
even though it closed the gatehouse shell (closureOf **0.615 → 1.000**). The fix: make a **form-readiness gain
credit-able** — the form-analog of the E-50 department-dominant override (which already keeps a tool that
clears a department major through a whole-build scalar regression).

The override path already exists (`departmentDominant`, `deptItemCounts`, the net-minor guard — T-191/E-50).
Add a **form clause** with the same discipline:

- **Keep** a tool iff it raises form-readiness — `closureOf` (reuse T-197's `eaveRingClosure`, do **not**
  invent a second metric) up by a margin toward the form-ready threshold (0.9) — **even at a picture-score tie
  or regression**,
- **AND** it adds **no new department major**,
- **AND** it does **not regress a real major** / does not grow a targeted dept's total item count (the E-50
  net guard — department-trust was safe *only* with this check; form-trust needs the analog).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The form clause **keeps** `close_shell` on the real T-198 tie (counts reproduced as a fixture) and **rejects**
a deliberately-bad closing move (floods interior / regresses roof / adds a major). **Fails if:** it rubber-
stamps the bad move (the net/no-new-major guard leaks — tighten as E-50's CG15 did); or it's too narrow and
still rolls back the legitimate close (no behaviour change); or the keep is glance-indefensible (closure↑ the
glance calls worse → the clause must be form-AND-glance-defensible, not blind closure-maximizing).
