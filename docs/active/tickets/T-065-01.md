## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-19**, a documented design-debt cleanup (T-061 review concern #4). Thin-feature voxelization
(`src/form/glb-thin.mjs` `voxelizeGlbThin`, T-059) **lifts thin subjects** (bow-and-arrow form IoU
0.473→0.526, koi up) but **over-thickens solid forms** — E-18's combined build ran it on **all 7**,
regressing solids (dancing-man **−0.10**, also heart/mushroom/pineapple/moai) and ~**2× occupancy**. The
form-type-routing rule (*thin/organic → thin-preserve; solid/bulky → plain `voxelizeGlb`*) was **named but
never wired**. The over-thickened shell on solids is also spurious bulk (a cleanliness cost).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A **per-subject routing tag** (on the `SUBJECTS` list / a small subject-keyed config) marking each
      subject thin vs solid; a selector that picks `voxelizeGlbThin` for **thin** (bow-and-arrow, koi) and
      `voxelizeGlb` for **solid/bulky** (heart, mushroom, pineapple, moai, dancing-man). No new algorithm —
      just route to the right existing voxelizer.
- [ ] Unit-tested: the selector returns the thin voxelizer for a thin-tagged subject and the plain one for
      a solid-tagged subject.
- [ ] Applied ×7: form IoU + occupancy **before (universal thin) / after (routed)** — solids **recover**
      (dancing-man back toward 0.914), thin subjects **keep** their gain, occupancy drops on the solids.
- [ ] `npm test` green.
