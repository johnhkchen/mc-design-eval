## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-55** / Story **S-212**. The bar. The E-38 finding ([[workshop-progress-decoupled-from-quality]]):
*you cannot validate an eval or demonstrate an agent climbing one without a quality-VARIED, human-rankable test
set.* T-211 proved the current acceptance rule disagrees with the glance — the centered arch scored `8/0/48`
and the **median** discarded the 48, rolling back the build that looks most like the concept.

Build the missing instrument: a corpus of **climb-state builds spanning a real quality range** (the states are
already on disk from T-208/T-211: seed colonnade → closed box → +gable → +centered arch → +dressed relief) with
a recorded **human glance-rank**. Then replay each through the current accept-rule (per-move median + floor-only
batch) and report **keep/rollback vs the glance rank** — the agreement metric every S-213 candidate is judged
against.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The corpus is **human-rankable** (a clear glance order, seed worst → dressed-arched best) and the current
accept-rule **measurably disagrees** with it (discards the +48 arch), reproducibly. **Fails if:** the builds
are **not rankable** (humans can't separate them — all near-D-, the E-38 finding recurring → the gap is the
build quality range, not the accept-rule; STOP and report — the most important possible finding); the
disagreement **isn't reproducible** (the median discard was a fluke, not structural); or the corpus is too
narrow to be a fair bar (name it).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Quality-varied climb-state corpus assembled (reuse on-disk T-208/T-211 trajectory builds + renders: seed,
      closed, +gable, +centered-arch, +dressed) with a **recorded human glance-rank** ordering.
- [ ] A small **replay harness** runs each build through the current acceptance rule (per-move median +
      floor-only batch) and reports **agreement vs the glance rank** (where keep/rollback contradicts the
      glance — the `8/0/48`-arch case quantified).
- [ ] **Recorded honestly:** are the builds rankable? is the disagreement reproducible? If not rankable, the
      ticket STOPS here and reports (redirect upstream), not a fabricated bar.
- [ ] Zero/minimal metered spend (replay committed builds; glance-rank is human). `npm test` green; **frozen
      instrument untouched** (the glance-rank validates the creation-loop accept-rule, never the frozen gate).
