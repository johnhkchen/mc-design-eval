## Context (self-contained)

concept's warm **cream** — the role was chosen by **name**, never validated for **value**. The judge named
the residual `palette@upper`. Fix: pick the **value-true** block per role against the concept swatch in
CIE-Lab ([[value-true-palette-codesign]]; [[concept-image-not-color-value-preview]]).
