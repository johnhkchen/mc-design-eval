## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-39** / Story **S-165**. Make Layer A per-style. First half: recognition emits an explicit `style`
on the program, and `DiagnoseBuild` selects its `expected` profile by that style. Register `rustic` and
`saltcrag` as distinct suites. (The genuinely-different second style is T-165-02.)

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Keying Layer A's `expected` on a declared `style` makes the *same build* yield *different* expected
roof/wall/opening under two styles — the within-family gradient. **Fails if:** recognition can't surface a
`style` distinct from the pack id (style-id is its own sub-problem), or rustic vs saltcrag `expected` come
out near-identical (they are reskins, not different grammars — then T-165-02's genuinely-different style is
what actually tests the claim, and that is the honest scoping).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Recognition program carries an explicit `style`; `DiagnoseBuild` selects the suite by it; no
      per-subject constants (honor the recognize.mjs self-grep discipline).
- [ ] Fixture: the same (concept, render) under `style=rustic` vs `style=saltcrag` yields different
      `expected` text (or, if near-identical, the ticket records that reskins don't differ and points at
      T-165-02 as the real test).
- [ ] `npm test` green; frozen instrument untouched.
