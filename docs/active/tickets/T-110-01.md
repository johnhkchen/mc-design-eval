## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-28** / Story S-110. The church — the E-25 generalization subject — has never reached the gates.
Its record (`benchmarks/sculpture/reconstructed/church.json`) names two causes:

1. **`chain-refused`**: the skin coverage gate fails `band0 stone=0.327 < 0.5` — but the committed
   census decomposition shows band0 = `polished_basalt` 1225 + `stone` 681 + glass/trim of 2082 cells:
   **91.5% stone-family**. The material assignment painted the *role* across two stone blocks; the gate
   censuses the *literal name* `stone`. Third instance of the **kit-blind-gate class** (T-095:
   kit-renamed bands censused 0; T-101: dominant-only rejected the styling) — a measurement-identity
   bug at a naming seam.
2. **`roof-program-fallback`**: the whole-mass roof fit was rejected (honest fallback). The church has
   **two roofs** — tower cap + nave pitch (named in its T-103 component record); one whole-mass fit
   cannot satisfy both.
