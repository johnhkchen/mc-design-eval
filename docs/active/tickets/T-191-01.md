## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-50** / Story **S-191**. Make the **creation-loop accept-gate** follow the glance. T-190-01: the gate
rolled back the glance-correct grey roof — `recolor_roof` cleared the ROOF major (deptMajors ROOF 1→0) but
the whole-build scalar regressed 60→48 (attention-shift promoted pre-existing WALL+OPENING majors, neither
touched by the tool; + vote noise 52/40/48 vs 60/60/52) → rejected at the regression branch.

Implement the **department-dominant override** in `acceptsRound` / the climb runner: keep a tool that
**cleared a major in a department it targets** AND **introduced no new major in any department it targets**,
even on a whole-build scalar regression. **Creation-loop gate only — NOT the E-46/E-47 promotion term;
`measurements/` untouched.**

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The override **keeps** the grey roof (climb re-run: round 3 sticks, trend continues past 60) **AND rejects a
genuinely-bad change** (adversarial fixture: clears a targeted major while degrading its own target).
**Fails if:** the "no new **major** in targeted dept" guard leaks — a fix clearing one major but adding new
*minors* in its own target (net degradation) survives → tighten to net-minor; the override doesn't fire
(mis-modelled → re-localize); or major/minor is too vote-noisy to drive keep/reject (→ noise-reduction co-lever).
