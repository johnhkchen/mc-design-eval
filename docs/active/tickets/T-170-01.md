## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

`DiagnoseBuild` can reliably tag each `CritiqueItem` with `kind ∈ {add, replace, remove}` — distinguishing
a wrong-material `replace` from a right-material-missing-detail `add` — so the BO11 over-cap (a
right-base-material item with a missing detail, currently classed `wrong-style`) flips to `absent` and stops
capping. **Fails if:** the judge can't separate `replace` from `add` consistently (report the confusion; the
tag is then no better than the structural rule), or the fixture re-pin reveals the prompt change altered
unrelated behaviour (scope it, don't paper over).
