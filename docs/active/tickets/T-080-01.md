## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-23** / Story **S-080** — the **program path**. Voxelizing a TRELLIS mesh gives a **solid** mass;
the milestone needs a **hollow** cottage (shell + structure) to put rooms in. Carving is a deterministic
program; *what is safely hollowable* is a scoped LLM call (a right-sized-model exemplar — T-082-01).
**Precondition:** T-084-01 has already produced a **watertight shell** (roof + walls sealed) — hollowing
relies on that enclosure, and must keep it (you cannot enclose an interior behind a hole-y wall).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Mark the hollowable interior**: a detector (the light-tier `hollowable-mass` exemplar from
      T-082-01, or pure-geometric — fully-enclosed voxels not on the exterior skin and not load-bearing
      structure) identifies removable mass.
- [ ] **Deterministic carve**: a program removes the marked interior, **preserving the exterior skin** and
      keeping **structural members** (corner posts, chimney shaft, anything the skin needs). Pure,
      unit-tested on synthetic occupancy (skin cells survive; enclosed cells removed; structure retained).
- [ ] **Exterior unchanged**: re-render the exterior post-carve and confirm the **resemblance verdict does
      not regress** — hollowing is invisible from outside (same exterior render before/after).
- [ ] Record block count before/after (the cavity size) and the exterior-held proof.
- [ ] `npm test` green.
