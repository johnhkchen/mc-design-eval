## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-18** closes E-17's two glb-voxel gaps: **speckle** (T-058-01 `segmentMaterials` → clean
materials) and **thin form** (T-059-01 `voxelizeGlbThin` → thin members survive). This ticket **combines
them and re-measures** across the 7 subjects so the consolidation (T-061-01) is a pure read.

The combined build, per subject: `voxelizeGlbThin` (thin preservation) → value-true color (E-10) →
`segmentMaterials` (clean materials) → compile to `DesignArtifact` → render. Score on the same axes the
E-17 sweep used: **form IoU vs the GLB**, the **speckle metric** (T-058-01), **value ΔE**.
