## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-33** / Story S-135. The workshop's conformance gate checks regularity, never
proportion-vs-concept — so the cottage's proportion defect survived all six rounds **even though the
model's critique correctly named it in four of them** (T-127 ledger). No round ever received a
numeric target. The glance's first complaint is the dimension no check owns.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Proportion metrics** (pure, unit-tested on synthetic silhouettes): from a render's
      silhouette — **ridge:eave ratio, roof share of elevation, footprint aspect** (per mass where
      the component/mass record names masses) — compared against the **concept's** silhouette (the
      concept is the contract; the conditioned sketch is the recorded fallback reference when the
      concept view obscures a ratio). E-22-era silhouette machinery reused.
- [ ] **Wired into the workshop's per-round gate** beside regularity: declared tolerances; a revision
      that worsens a ratio beyond tolerance **rolls back, recorded**; every round's ledger entry
      carries the ratios — the model's critique gets a number to aim at. **Not a judge call; never
      replaces the gate** (E-33 Rule 2); the workshop↔judge isolation scan still passes.
- [ ] **Witness proof**: run on the committed T-127 cottage chain fixture — the check **names the
      proportion defect** the judge-side records describe (the squat upper storey / shallow read),
      with the ratio numbers recorded.
- [ ] **No judge runs**; replay byte-identical; tolerances and metric definitions documented in the
      pack-conformance docs; `npm test` green.
