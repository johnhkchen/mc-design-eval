## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-35** / Story S-145, second ticket. T-145-01 shipped the facade-grammar schema + recognition
pass but only produced a **synthetic fixture** (`recognition/facade/fixture-house`); **no real subject
carries a facade grammar**, and the committed `relief/barn-grammar.json` facade is both thin and
**mis-rolled** (`memberRole: "frame.timber"` on a stone barn). The "prove the look" spike (2026-06-14,
[[look-proven-on-cottage-relief]]) hand-authored a **storey-aware** grammar and got recognizable
results: the **cottage** read as Tudor half-timber (dark_oak_log studs framing white_terracotta plaster,
**constrained to the upper/plaster storey** via the brush `zoneOf`, period 4), and the **barn** grew
stone-brick piers framing cobble panels (`wall.dressing` member, **not** timber). The lesson: relief
unconstrained is a timber cage (studs over the plinth, through the roof); **the grammar must carry the
storey band + rhythm**, and recognition must emit that — so the hand-authored grammar comes from the
model. GL + the model are available now (E-36).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Live facade recognition emits a STOREY-AWARE grammar for the real subjects** (cottage + barn,
      `--ticket T-145-02`, subscription shim): each facade face carries per-face **rhythm** AND the
      **storey band / zone** the relief applies to (the spike's `zoneOf` becomes a recorded grammar
      field — e.g. cottage: the upper plaster storey only; barn: the wall field below the eave). Member
      / field **roles correct per subject** (cottage: `frame.timber` studs + `wall.infill.upper` field;
      barn: `wall.dressing` piers + `wall.field.ground` field — fixing the fixture's mis-roll).
- [ ] **The compiler honors the storey band.** `facadeArticulationPlan` (or the apply seam) threads the
      grammar's band into the brushes' `zoneOf`, so studs/piers land **only in their storey** (no plinth
      cover, no roof punch — the spike's failure mode is structurally prevented).
- [ ] **Render proof (judge-free).** The recognized grammar applied via `applyArticulation` reproduces
      the spike's look — cottage half-timber, barn piers — rendered beside each concept in `pr/assets/`.
      (The barn render rides on S-159's watertight seed to look clean; the cottage proves it on the
      clean workshop build now.) Spatial-layout-only from the textured GLB, never material (the ratified
      §Stage 2 rule).
- [ ] Recognition replays byte-identically offline; the synthetic fixture path still passes; `npm test`
      green; no per-building constants (the band + rhythm are recognized, not hard-coded).
