## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-44** / Story **S-179**. Close the E-43 treatment-grammar leak T-176-01 reported: the
edges-from-geometry vocabulary handles eave/ridge/reveal cleanly but **cannot name** two element-specific
edges —

- **The raking verge** — a *sloped line* down the gable edge (per-column along the pitch). The column-keyed
  `top.row` model reads it as a heavy end band, not a clean rake.
- **The voussoir arch head** — a *curve*. `deriveOpeningEdges` exposes `isArch` and the reveal generalizes,
  but a voussoir arc needs an opening-local curve derivation the footprint-corner model lacks.

Add the derivations/brushes so the **same** treatment grammar names both. **Goal + glance, not algorithm** —
a per-column pitch walk and an arc brush are the leading candidates, not the mandate. Recess by exclusion;
closure not regressed.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The grammar gains a raking-verge edge and a voussoir head such that the gatehouse's verge board and arch head
**read on the render** as crisp element edges (not a heavy end band / a void), via the same vocabulary as
corners/eave/reveal. **Fails if:** the sloped-line / arc derivation needs element-local geometry the
footprint model can't supply on a gable or multi-ridge roof (then the **edge classifier**, not the brush, is
the sub-problem → name it); or the verge/head reads busy or regresses `closureOf`.
