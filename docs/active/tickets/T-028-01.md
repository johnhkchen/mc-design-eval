## Context

Epic E-11 / story S-028. Seed craft pass B, a `stage(state, intent) → state` over the **material-locked**
state. Assign `relief` (Z-depth) by detecting feature types and applying the legal-geometry moves:

- **Recesses/windows** → inset −1 (self-shadow). Carve by exclusion per the memory rule (don't bury a
  block behind a fill).
- **Trim / cornices / frames** → pop +1 (cast shadow under the lip).
- **Horizontal lines** (cornice, eave, base) → add a lip/overhang so the course below sits in shadow.

Feature regions come from `intent` (the plan's focal/trim annotations) and/or simple detection over the
material/occupancy. Writes `relief`; locks it; must not alter the locked `occupied` or `material`. This is
the proof that a pass composes on a prior pass's locked output.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A relief stage assigns Z-depth (inset/pop/lip) and writes `relief`, leaving `occupied` and `material`
      (both locked) untouched — lock enforcement verified.
- [ ] **Tested:** a fixture with marked recess/trim/horizontal regions gets −1 / +1 / lip respectively;
      recesses are carved by exclusion (no buried blocks).
- [ ] The full chain (massing → material → relief) compiles to a `DesignArtifact` that passes AJV and
      renders **measurably less flat** than the massing-only or material-only states (a relief-coverage or
      Z-variance metric increases).
- [ ] `npm test` green.
