## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-41** / Story **S-170**. The proof. Re-run `experiments/eval-alignment/corpus-referee.mjs` with the
`kind`-emitting Layer A (T-170-01) and report whether the typed tag removes the E-40 over-cap. Recall the
E-40 collapse (matched=2, wrong=0/2) and the wrinkle: a *materially-faithful* matched build is required for
the crater to separate; the gatehouse build is itself material-wrong, so its "matched" condition will stay
capped (correctly) until E-42.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

With `kind`, the corpus's truly-matched-and-faithful states recover above the floor (the over-cap is gone),
genuine wrong-style stays capped, and the crater separates **if** a faithful matched build exists. **Fails
if:** matched-faithful states still floor (the cap logic, not the classifier, is the bug); OR `kind` is
emitted unreliably (the over-cap persists for a different reason); OR there is no faithful matched build to
demonstrate separation — in which case the report explicitly **hands off to E-42** (build faithfulness) and
does not fake a crater.
