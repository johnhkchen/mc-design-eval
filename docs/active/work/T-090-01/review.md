## The deviation a reviewer should check

Design D5 originally kept cobble in roof preserve; run 1 passed every numeric gate while the render
still showed the jumble — the visible grey was 185 scattered *preserved* cobble fragments, and the
stone cells the fill recolors are mostly on camera-invisible faces (soffits, covered slots). The fix
followed the ticket's own text: chimney as a **declared sub-region**, cobble/bricks out of roof
preserve *and* splat. This is a genuine semantic change to the cottage policy, justified by the
material map's role bindings; the numbers-passed-render-failed sequence is recorded in progress.md.

## Open concerns / known limits

1. **Interior cavities**: `exposedVoxelEntries` includes interior-cavity skins (spec'd per ticket;
   10-cell delta on this artifact). A future hollowed-interior build will need exterior-reachable
   flood discrimination before the fill — flagged in the module header.
2. **`-y` underside cells** inflate the exposure census (render-invisible but counted); recorded basis
   (`skin: "exposure"`) makes cross-record `total` comparisons a step change vs pre-T-090 records.
3. **Chimney detector fragility**: the protruding-stack rule assumes the chimney is the only thing
   above the highest roof plane. A finial, flagpole, or twin-gable build with unequal gable heights
   would mis-scope the region. Acceptable for the cottage; revisit at the church subject.
4. **Wall blotches in the after-render** (dark splat studs over plaster) are a pre-existing
   GLB/concept splat behavior — confirmed identical in the old committed artifact — and are NOT this
   ticket's scope, but they are now the most visible remaining defect at oblique angles; a candidate
   for the E-25 backlog.
5. **Plaster-zone guard basis**: `surfacePlasterByZone`/`stripOffZonePlaster` still measure the
   projection skin (scope discipline). The exposure fill upstream recolors off-zone surface plaster
   anyway (run 2: 0 stripped), but the throw's blind spot on unprojected faces is documented here.
6. **Concurrency**: T-087-01 was wiring `surface-pattern.mjs` in parallel; this ticket's spray-paint
   edits are in §0c/§5b/§6 seams. If both landed, a rebase/merge sanity pass over the runner is
   warranted (the lock serializes commits, not semantics).
