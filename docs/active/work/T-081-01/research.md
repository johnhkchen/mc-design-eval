# Research — T-081-01 nxm-floorplan-infill

Descriptive map of what exists for **filling the hollow cottage with an N×M floorplan**. Epic E-23 /
Story S-081 — the **program path crossing into design**. No solutions here; just the substrate, the
boundaries, and the constraints the design must respect.

## The job, located

T-080-01 produced an **empty shell**: `docs/active/work/T-080-01/hollow-cottage-artifact.json`
(26×27×32, 5465 voxels, the interior mass carved to air, exterior byte-identical to the solid cottage).
This ticket **fills the N storeys** with floors, dividing walls and interior doorways generated by a
program the LLM **authors/steers** from the **below/plan view**. The gate **changes**: the interior has
**no reference** (concept + GLB are exterior shells) — "break into rooms" is *invention*, so the gate is
**plausibility**, not resemblance.

## Substrate that already exists (consume, do not refork)

### The view layer (T-078-01, `done`)
- `src/view/occupancy.mjs` — `artifactOccupancy(artifact)` / `occupancyFromCells(cells)` →
  `Occupancy { bounds, dims, size, cells:Map<"x,y,z",block>, has(x,y,z), block(x,y,z) }`. The one adapter

  - `storeyBands(occ,{floorFillThreshold})` → `{bands:[{yStart,yEnd,dominantBlock,fill}], floorLines:number[]}`.
    **`floorLines` is the storey-line primitive S-081 builds on** (shared with S-079).
  - `openings(occ, dir)` → `[{bbox,kind:"door"|"window",cells}]` — enclosed-air = window, ground-touching
    = door, per side elevation. **The exterior-opening read.**
  - `airComponents(mask)` — 4-connected air components tagged by which borders they touch (enclosed =
    `!top&&!bottom&&!left&&!right`). The **one** enclosed-vs-border rule, reused by openings/holes.
  - `roofRegion`, `wallFields` (S-084 consumers; not central here).

## The cottage, measured (the real numbers the design must fit)

Running the pure reads against the hollow artifact:
- **dims** 26×27×32; **footprint** 26×32 bbox = 832, **area 662** (irregular — a gabled plan, not a full
  rectangle). **bbox** x∈[-13,12], z∈[-16,15], y∈[0,26].
- **`floorLines` = [0, 14]** → **two storeys**: ground (y 1–13, above the y=0 foundation) and upper
  (y 15–~22, above the y=14 floor line), roof above. The storey count the gate must match is **2**.
- **bands**: y0 `dark_oak_log` (foundation), y1–15 `stone_bricks` (walls), y16–22 `spruce_planks`
  (roof), thin caps above.
- **`openings()` = [] on all four side faces** — see constraint below.
- enclosed solid mass after hollowing = 24 (the surviving structure columns); the interior **air cavity**
  is the volume to partition.

## Assumptions & constraints (load-bearing for design)

1. **No reference for the interior → plausibility gate, not resemblance.** Nothing to compare rooms to;
   the gate validates *structure*: rooms valid/non-overlapping, reachable, floors at storey lines, storey
   count matches, grid fits envelope, openings align with the shell. Report **pass/fail per constraint +
   one named residual** (Rule 7 — quantify, don't hide).
2. **Fill is exterior-safe by the same digest as the carve.** Place blocks **only on interior columns**
   (inside the perimeter wall) → `exteriorHeld` is true → the resemblance verdict provably can't regress.
   The runner throws if `held` is ever false. This is the safety contract, reused not rebuilt.
3. **Append-only, no air op.** Interior is air after the carve; the floorplan is **additive** placements.
   Doorway openings are made by **NOT placing** the wall cell (`[[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]`), never by
   an air block or a delete.
4. **Palette-constrained materials.** Floor + wall blocks must be in the cottage's 6-id manifest, or the
   live AJV gate rejects the artifact ([[voxel-palette-must-be-design-doc]]).
5. **`openings()` returns [] here — a real residual.** The front-most ortho march hits the *outer skin*
   everywhere (windows are recessed/glazed; the ray never sees an enclosed front-face air gap), so the
   geometric opening read finds nothing on this cottage even though it has real doors. ⇒ the
   exterior-front-door position the interior doorways must align "behind" cannot be read geometrically;
   the **LLM (seeing the elevation render) must supply it** as a steering param — the program-author
   pattern's reason to exist. When no exterior opening is available, "openings align with shell" becomes
   the **named residual**, not a hard fail.
6. **The program partitions; the LLM steers.** The rubric assigns *authoring a generator* to **strong**.
   The metered call is the **floorplan reasoning** (rows N, cols M, materials, door hints); the **bulk
   placement is the program's job** (AC: "not hand-placed"). Generator + gate must be **pure/deterministic
   + unit-tested on synthetic shells**; only the reasoning is metered.
7. **The upper storey is narrower than the ground footprint** (gabled roof). Per-storey placement must gate
   cells to "inside the shell **at that storey's height**", not to the ground footprint — an enclosed-air
   slice per storey, reusing the `airComponents` enclosed rule, keeps walls/floors from poking through the
   roof.
