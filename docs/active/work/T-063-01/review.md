## Why a relative floor (the load-bearing design choice)

moai's three 6-connected components measure 3147 / 1560 / 1352 cells — the spurious masses are **0.496× and
0.430×** the principal mass. They are *large* (duplicate statues, not debris), so no absolute small-island
floor can drop them without also nuking legitimately small parts. A floor relative to the largest component
(`minFraction = 0.5`) is the only size-based rule that drops moai's halves while keeping a genuine equal-sized
separate part (e.g. a detached arrow). moai is the **only** multi-component subject in the committed 7, so the
threshold acts on exactly one build; the other six are exact no-ops.

## ⚠️ Critical issue for human review — moai form IoU fell 0.593 → 0.399

The ticket's AC #3 hypothesized form IoU would **rise** "as the spurious silhouette mass goes away." It
**fell**, and the reason is structural, not a bug:

- `judgeIoU` (e18-remeasure.mjs) scores the build's render silhouette against the **GLB's own** rasterized
  silhouette at the 3Q view. **The moai GLB *is* the hallucination** — TRELLIS reconstructed three statues +
  bridging bars from a 3-view contact sheet. So the "ground truth" silhouette already contains the spurious
  mass. Pruning the *build* to one component makes it cover **less** of that corrupted reference → lower IoU.
- The new render confirms the kept largest 6-connected component is itself a **tangle of partial statues joined
  by the bridging bars** (the bars keep parts 6-connected). Geometric component pruning removes the two
  fully-detached masses (the AC headline: frac→1, stray→0) but **cannot reconstruct a clean single statue** from
  a mesh that never contained one cleanly.

**Implications a reviewer should weigh:**
1. For moai, **form IoU vs the GLB is not a meaningful quality signal** — the reference is corrupt. The stray /
   component metrics (frac, strayCount) are the honest signals here, and they improved decisively.
2. The committed pruned moai build is geometrically cleaner (one component, half the cells) but **visually not
   a clean moai**. If the goal is a presentable moai, the real fix is **upstream**: regenerate the GLB from a
   **single-view** concept image (root cause per the E-19 analysis), then this pruning is a no-op safety net.
   That is a separate ticket, not this one.
3. **Decision point:** is a geometrically-clean-but-still-messy moai with a *lower* IoU an acceptable state for
   E-19's "stray-geometry fix," or should the moai artifact be reverted to the unpruned build (higher IoU
   against the corrupt reference, but 3 components) pending the upstream GLB regen? This review recommends
   **keeping the pruned build** — it satisfies the ticket's stated geometric ACs and the IoU metric is known to
   be invalid for this subject — but flags the choice explicitly.

## Other open concerns / notes

- **Thin threshold margin.** `minFraction = 0.5` sits only 0.004 above moai's 0.496 ratio. Deterministic for
  the committed GLBs, but a regenerated moai GLB could shift a mass across 0.5. The threshold is a parameter,
  documented; a future caller can raise it.
- **AC-#4 "verified bug" was already mitigated.** The ticket said the gated secondary was "computed but never
  placed"; in fact `augment: true` already augmented internally (committed heart had 7 distinct = 2 secondary).
  The change made was the *correct* one regardless — `palette: aug` makes the snap-candidate set identical to
  the discipline-guard set by construction (single source of truth) and is behaviour-preserving (manifests
  byte-identical). Reported as a hardening, not a fictional fix.
- **`cleanliness-baseline.{md,json}` left frozen** as the T-062-01 "before" snapshot; the "after" lives in each
  `summary.json` `stray` block (which carries both before and after, satisfying AC #5 self-containedly).
  Re-running the baseline would now report the pruned numbers — intentionally not done to preserve the before.

  from it. Clean direction preserved.
