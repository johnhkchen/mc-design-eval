## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-30**. The committed verdict/record pins are load-bearing — instrument-diffs, epic tables, and
head-to-head baselines all cite them — and two T-116 incidents proved nothing structurally protects
them: (1) an attempted "cheap" reskin re-cut turned out to **re-run the judge**, re-rolling three
styled verdicts (the cottage 135° same-object hold flipped — the known budget-edge flap); it was
correctly reverted and every pin restored byte-identically, with the fresh verdicts preserved
narratively (T-116 concern 6 — residual ROUTED, not closed). (2) A missing `--` in
`npm run kit:extract --subject=barn` silently swept all three legacy kit pins (caught, restored from
HEAD). Protection currently depends on agent vigilance; it must be construction.

## Goal

Two structural guarantees: (a) a **read-only distillation mode** — every runner that can rebuild
records from committed outputs does so through a path that is **incapable of spawning the judge or
overwriting pins** (by construction, unit-tested: the judge seam is absent/stubbed-to-throw in this
mode); (b) a **pin-rotation policy**, recorded in `docs/knowledge/`: canonical verdicts/records change
only inside a ticket that explicitly owns verdicts, one judge run per view, with the retired pin named
in the commit — plus **guarded writes**: a runner refuses to overwrite a committed pin unless invoked
with an explicit rotation flag (the `kit:extract` sweep becomes impossible to do silently). Close
T-111 residual 4 *through* the policy (the stale skin pins rotated or explicitly retained, recorded).

## Builds on
