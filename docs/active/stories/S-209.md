## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-54**, the lead fix. E-53's T-208 proved the cold-start batch escape works (the climb leaves 0, +20, a
trustworthy glance-agreed signal) but the glance-good **+20 dressed batch was rejected** by the form-integrity
guard. Root cause, deterministic (`T-208-01/closure-probe.mjs`):

```
after close_shell        cl = 1.000
after apply_gable_roof   cl = 1.000
after relief_walls       cl = 0.068   (NOT a bounds/floor artifact)
```

`relief_walls` stands the wall **proud** (depth-1 outward) of the program footprint. T-206's `eaveRingClosure`
measures occupancy **on the exact footprint ring**, so a proud-dressed wall reads as *off-ring = "no wall"* —
exactly as an open colonnade does. The metric that must read an *open colonnade* open also reads a *proud-dressed
closed* wall open. That collapse is the only thing between the T-208 escape and a kept dressed gatehouse.

The fix: make `eaveRingClosure` tolerant of **±1 proud displacement** — census the **wall plane** (a column is
"closed" if it has a wall cell on the footprint ring *or one step proud of it*), not only the exact ring —
**without** regressing T-206's invariant that an **open colonnade still reads open**. The distinguisher: a
proud-dressed closed wall and a proud open colonnade differ by *interior fill / continuity*, not by the ring.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The relief-tolerant metric reads a **proud-dressed closed wall closed (≥0.9)** AND an **open colonnade open
(<0.9)** AND a genuinely-reopened shell open — and on the live build, the T-208 +20 dressed batch is **kept** on
a stayed-closed form. **Fails if:** tolerating ±1 proud displacement also forgives a real ±1 *gap*, so a
colonnade (or a one-ring-thin wall with gaps) now reads closed — T-206's invariant breaks (then proud-relief vs
open-colonnade are NOT separable on the ring±1 alone and need interior-fill evidence — the harder fix, name it);
or the ±1 census mis-handles the declared aperture (the intentional opening) and double-counts it; or fixing
relief re-introduces the T-202 over-correction (colonnade reads closed again).

## Tickets

- **T-209-01** — make `eaveRingClosure` relief-tolerant (census the wall plane ±1 proud, not just the exact
  footprint ring); reuse the one `closureOf` authority. Unit-tested on the **four** fixtures that pin the seam:
  open colonnade → open (<0.9, T-206 invariant held); proud-dressed closed wall (the T-208 relief build) →
  closed (≥0.9); genuinely-reopened closed shell → open; and the close_shell/relief sequence from
  `closure-probe.mjs` → stays ≥0.9 after relief (the 1.000→0.068 collapse is gone). `npm test` green; frozen
  instrument untouched.
