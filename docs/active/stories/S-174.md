## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-43**. *Diverge before converging.* Before committing to one way of getting readable articulation,
spike several candidate approaches on the gatehouse, render each beside the concept, and let the
impressively-strong one win on the glance. The compositional treatment grammar (E-43 background, candidate
**A**) is the leading idea, not the mandate — it competes with **B** curated style pattern-book, **C**
reference/concept-driven placement, **D** iterative critique-amplification, and any candidate the spike
surfaces. A hybrid is a fine winner.

This is a **spike**: build each candidate *rough and fast* — enough to render and judge, not production
quality. The point is to find the strong approach cheaply, not to finish one.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Spiking 3–4 candidates on the gatehouse will produce a **clear glance-winner** — one approach (or hybrid)
visibly closes more of the gap to the concept's articulation (arch, quoins, trim band, recessed field) than
the others. **It fails if:** they're all roughly equivalent at the glance (then the lever isn't the
*approach*, it's amplitude/budget — and that's the finding, redirecting S-175); OR they all stay token/flat
(then articulation is gated by something upstream — geometry resolution, scale — name it); OR the strongest
also reads *busy/noisy* (then the ceiling is taste/amplitude, not method).
