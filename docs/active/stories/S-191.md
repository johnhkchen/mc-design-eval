## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-50**. Make the creation-loop **accept-gate follow the glance.** T-190-01 proved the gate rolls back
the glance-correct grey roof: `recolor_roof` cleared the ROOF major (deptMajors ROOF 1→0) and the human
glance confirms the dark roof is closer to the concept — but the whole-build scalar regressed 60→48
(attention-shift: pre-existing WALL+OPENING majors got promoted; neither department was touched by the tool;
+ vote noise) and `acceptsRound` rejected at the regression branch.

Implement the **department-dominant override**: keep a tool that **cleared a major in a department it
targets** AND **introduced no new major in any department it targets**, even on a whole-build scalar
regression. This is the *creation-loop* gate (`acceptsRound` / the climb runner), **not** the E-46/E-47
promotion term; `measurements/` untouched.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The override **keeps** the grey roof (re-running the gatehouse climb, round 3 now sticks and the trend
continues past 60) **AND rejects a genuinely-bad change** on an adversarial fixture (a tool that clears a
targeted major while degrading its own target). **Fails if:** the "no new **major** in a targeted dept" guard
**leaks** — a fix that clears one major but adds several new *minors* in its own target (net degradation)
survives (department-trust misplaced → tighten to a net-minor guard, or the signal needs finer than
major/minor); or the override **doesn't fire** (the case is mis-modelled → re-localize); or major/minor is
too noisy across votes to drive a keep/reject (then noise-reduction — more votes / a noise-aware rule — is
the co-lever, [[diverge-before-converge-experiment-freedom]]).
