## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-27**, the headline. Every failing resemblance verdict on every subject names **`form @ roof`**:
the roof is sampled from the decimated mesh into a stepped, spiky blob (146 of the styled cottage's 265
protrusions are in the roof band), then skinned faithfully — clean-colored noise. The walls prove the
alternative works: surfaces the pipeline *re-authors from geometry* pass their checks. The roof is the
last major surface still inherited. And a real Minecraft roof isn't a painted blob anyway — players build
slopes from **stair courses** with slab transitions.

## Goal

**Replace the sampled roof with a generated one.** Fit roof parameters against the GLB's roof component
(eave line, pitch, overhang, ridge height/direction; gable first, hip where the fit demands), **record
the fit error**, regenerate the roof as clean voxel geometry built the Minecraft-native way — **stair
courses** along the slope, slabs at half-step transitions, full blocks where pitch demands — placed with
correct states via the proven T-097 path, inside the S-102 cage (silhouette IoU vs GLB held; protected
chimney preserved; auto-rollback on regression). If a roof can't be fitted within tolerance, the
regularized sampled roof stays and the failure is named (E-27 Rule 1's honest fallback) — never an
invented shape.

## Builds on

- S-103 component record (the roof planes + eave geometry to fit); S-102 cage (acceptance harness);
  T-097 fixture-state path (stair/slab placement); the E-26 kit (which block family the courses use).

## Run-the-test ticket

  gatehouse: fake rafters gone at the source (roof-band protrusions ~0), stair-course slopes, fit error
  + cage metrics recorded, before/after renders at the failing azimuths.
