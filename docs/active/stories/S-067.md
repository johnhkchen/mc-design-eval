## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-20** breaks the Phase-1 **"facade only"** limit by producing a **full 3-D building** through the
matured GLB→voxel + surgical pipeline. Facade-only existed because text→JSON can't hand-place a whole
building (it narrates and overflows); voxelizing a TRELLIS mesh now places the bulk automatically. This
story is the foundation: a **building** subject and its GLB.

`vConcept` so far handles facades (front elevation) and sculptures (freestanding 3/4 object). A building
is a third framing: a **whole structure in the round** — four sides, roof, depth. The concept must be a
**single complete building in a single 3/4 view** with bold block-scale architectural detail (massing,
roof, openings, trim) — and explicitly **ONE building, ONE view**: the moai's duplicate-mass mess came
from a multi-view contact sheet, which would here produce multiple buildings + hallucinated connectors.

## Goal

A full-building `vConcept` mode: a design doc for a whole structure + a Nano-Banana 3/4 concept of one
complete building (all sides + roof, bold detail, single subject/single view). Provision its **TRELLIS
GLB** and **validate TRELLIS reconstructs a building** (a bulky-angular form — confirm it doesn't fail
like the thin sword, and honestly note any lost fine detail).
