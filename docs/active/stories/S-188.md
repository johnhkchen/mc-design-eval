## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-48**. Pull the un-pulled lever: make the workshop loop run **render → picture-reading critique →
revise → re-render** on the gatehouse, using the E-47 picture-anchored `DiagnoseBuild` as the climb gradient
(the *creation-loop* critique, not the frozen instrument). The machinery exists — structured per-department
critique (E-39) and a critique→amplify loop (E-43/T-176) — but has never run as a **sustained autonomous
climb** with a picture-reading gradient.

Two things this story must get right beyond "wire it up":
1. **An honest accept-gate.** A round is accepted only if it moves toward the concept on the glance-proxy
   (the picture-critique), never just because it changed. Recess/relief by exclusion ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]).
2. **A restraint/stopping rule.** T-176's amplitude loop **overshot** (hd3, glance-overruled to hd2 — see
   [[e47-concept-image-conditioned-term]] lineage). The climb must converge, not oscillate.

Its real deliverable is the **eyes-vs-hands inventory, discovered by running**: per round, what does the
critique name, and does the loop have a lever to act on it?

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The loop runs a multi-round picture-driven climb on the gatehouse and produces a clear inventory of what it
*can* and *cannot* act on. **Fails if:** it cannot act on most of what the critique names (eyes-but-no-hands,
the E-33 result — then S-189's scope is large, report it); it oscillates (overshoot → the restraint rule is
the sub-problem); or the critique's verdicts are too coarse to steer (it says "roof wrong" but not *how* →
the critique must emit a direction, not just a tag).
