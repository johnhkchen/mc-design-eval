## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-48**. S-188 ran the climb and produced the **eyes-vs-hands inventory** — the critiques the loop
correctly named but had **no construction lever** to fix. This story builds the highest-leverage missing
hands, so the next round of the climb (S-190) can actually move them. **Build what the climb stalled on, not
a speculative list.**

The known prime candidate (the one genuine divergence on today's gatehouse): **roof color** — the build's
roof is `dark_oak` (faithful to the *program*) while the concept's is **grey stone**. That is a
recognition/material-map fidelity gap, not a construction defect — the covering roof is structurally correct
(T-177-01). Proportions (E-33/E-34 levers, partially wired) and missing detail (E-43 amplify) are the likely
other gaps; S-188's inventory decides the actual set and order.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Each hand built lets the loop **move a defect it previously could only name** — verified by the critique
firing then clearing on that department after the lever is applied. **Fails if:** a lever fixes the named
defect but **regresses another** on the glance (the fix is not local — the cage/closure guard is the
sub-problem); or the gap is deeper than a lever (e.g. proportions need a geometry rebuild the loop can't
reach — the E-33 "eyes but no hands for geometry" wall, named precisely, handed up).
