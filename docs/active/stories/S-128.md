## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-32**. The project owns a dozen-plus build techniques — gable/hip/pyramid roof generators,
arches, timber-frame grammar, opening dressing, spray-paint ops, hollowing, floorplans, weathering
candidates — but they accreted as epic-specific fixes with heterogeneous interfaces. The strategic
insight: a **brush** (parametrized, composable, unit-tested, preview-carded build technique) is the
unit of capability, building a *large* library of them is relatively trivial compounding work that AI
coding does well, and composition over a big library is what unlocks the model as a builder. The E-31
idiom registry (T-124) is the seed; this story makes the contract universal.

## Goal

(a) The **brush contract**: parameter schema, composition interface (what a brush consumes/emits so
brushes chain), test requirement, and the **preview card** (the brush rendered on a synthetic subject
— the catalog's visual unit). (b) The **registry as the single door**: every existing technique
registered under the contract (idiom generators, spray/paint, dressing, hollow, floorplan); a
**catalog page** rendering every brush with its card. (c) Migration is behavior-preserving: existing
committed records re-verify byte-identical through the registration (the T-113 discipline).

## Builds on

- E-31 T-124 idiom registry (the seed); the E-23..E-30 generators and ops (the inventory); preview
  rendering via the fixed lens.

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-128-01` — contract + registry + full inventory registered + catalog with cards; legacy
  byte-identity proofs; the brush count is the baseline the factory milestone measures growth
  against.
