## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-40**. The test asset the whole epic validates against — and the fix for E-39's under-powered
2-state bake-off. Assemble a **≥8–10-state, multi-department** corpus of (concept, build-render) states
spanning the defect axes (massing/proportion, roof, structural integrity, palette/material, surface
relief), and — critically — including **clean × wrong-style pairs** (a clean build paired with both its
matched-style concept and a same-family wrong-style concept).

Capture **ground-truth labels** per state:
- the **worst-defect department** (for the bake-off dispatch metric), and
- for the wrong-style pairs, a **pairwise "which is more faithful to its concept"** judgement.

Single-rater for now (the project owner), recorded honestly as a single-rater limitation — the record
carries a `rater` field so multi-rater agreement is a no-migration add later (mirrors spec §10).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A human can label these states reliably enough to be ground truth: the worst-defect department is
unambiguous on most states, and the wrong-style pairs are a *genuinely contestable but decidable* call (the
rater consistently prefers the matched-style build). **It fails if:** the rater **cannot** reliably pick
the more-faithful build on the wrong-style pairs (then "style fidelity" is not a stable human signal — the
premise of E-40 wobbles, and that is the finding, reported not buried); OR most states have no single
unambiguous worst department (then the dispatch metric needs a multi-label scheme, not a single department).
