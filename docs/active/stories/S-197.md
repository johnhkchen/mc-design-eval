## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-51**. The reviewer's 2026-06-17 finding, confirmed by the T-194/T-195 renders: **the gatehouse is an
open colonnade, not a closed building** (closureOf ~0.05). The carve hand (T-194) and relief hand (T-195) are
both mechanically correct but **cannot read**, because there is no closed wall for a doorway to be carved into
or for relief to sit on. *You can't carve a doorway into a wall that's already full of holes.*

Two coupled fixes:

1. **Form first — close the shell.** A hand/stage that builds a **dense, closed wall shell** from the
   recognition program's **masses[] / absolute footprint** (not the ragged GLB-voxelized occupancy) — the
   standing [[wall-construct-needs-dense-shell]] finding (replace-via-occupancy walls climb only on dense
   shells; sparse → colonnade). The result is a closed gatehouse the detail hands can act on.
2. **Climb ordering — form before detail.** The climb must do **coarse form (massing / close-the-shell)
   before fine detail (carve openings, relief, banding)**. Detail tools (`carve_arch`, `relief_walls`,
   `band_eave`) are **gated on form-readiness** (e.g. closureOf above a threshold), so the loop can never again
   carve a doorway into a colonnade or dress an open frame. This is the reviewer's task-ordering note made
   structural, not a one-off.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The form-first stage turns the sparse gatehouse into a **dense closed shell** (closureOf rises from ~0.05
toward watertight), and with detail gated on form-readiness, the carve + relief from T-194/T-195 then **read**
against a real wall on the re-climb (T-198). **Fails if:** the program footprint can't produce a dense shell
on this seed (the close-the-shell is the deeper geometry wall, [[cottage-gate-and-volume-gate-are-one-fix]]
territory — name it); or closing the shell **regresses** the roof/form the climb already won (the form stage
isn't composable with the prior climb state); or the ordering gate is too coarse (blocks a legitimate detail,
or lets one through on a still-open form).
