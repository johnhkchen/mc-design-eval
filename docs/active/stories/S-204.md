## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The roof reaches its picture: dark slate colour lands, and the pitch comes to ~1.35 so the framing scale flag
clears. **Fails if:** the **pitch lever doesn't exist** (bringing ridgeToEave to 1.35 needs roof-geometry
control the generator doesn't expose → roof reaches its picture on colour only, pitch named as a limit); the
recolor regresses a previously-cleared major (attention-shift → the E-50 override should keep it, verify); or
the slate block reads wrong-value on the glance (value-true selection needed, not just the named family).
