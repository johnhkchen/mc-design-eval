## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-47**. S-186/T-186-01 made the style-distance term **read the picture** — picture-anchored
expectation collapsed the E-46 pack confound ~4× (`packEffect 45→12`), flipped hard-middle term-vs-label
agreement `0.20→1.00` (τ −0.14→+1), and reversed every E-46 inversion (`ct-wrongpack` 0→16). **But the
re-gate landed DO-NOT-PROMOTE (MIXED/INCONCLUSIVE):** the decomposition is `conceptImageEffect 18 >
packEffect 12`, and that **+6 lead sits inside the ±12 noise band** — 6 points short of the strict
PICTURE-DRIVEN bar (`conceptImageEffect > packEffect + NOISE`).

T-186-01's RE-GATE §3 named *why* precisely: **matched builds compressed `53→21`.** Picture-anchoring grades
blocky Minecraft renders against concept **art**, and a faithful voxel build still "diverges" from the
painting in 2–3 departments (it is blocky; the art is not) — so even the right answer floors near ~20,
shrinking the dynamic range the margin needs to clear the noise. The ordering is correct; the **scale** is
squeezed. (A second, smaller residual: `gh-wrongpack` stayed floored at 1 while its cottage twin recovered to
16 — possibly the synthetic GATEHOUSE_PROGRAM grounding, possibly a real render-vs-art gap.)

This story restores matched headroom via a **voxel-vs-art tolerance** so a blocky-but-faithful element is not
flagged wrong-style, checks `gh-wrongpack`, then **re-runs the same E-46 gate** (corpus + harness unchanged).
Term stays in `src/`; `measurements/` untouched.

## The crux (lead with how it fails — anti-hedge)

The tolerance must lift **faithful** builds (matchedRight back up from 21) **without** lifting **wrong-picture**
builds (matchedWrong must stay low) — it has to distinguish *"a blocky rendering of the right thing"* from
*"the wrong thing."* **It fails if:** the tolerance lifts everything uniformly (matchedWrong rises too →
re-compresses / destroys the 1.00 agreement and the decoupling T-186 won — the dominant risk); or the +6 gap
is irreducible because voxel-vs-art divergence is genuinely entangled with picture-divergence (then the
*scale* can't be widened by a tolerance — the finding points at the gate/NOISE or the corpus size, not the
term); or `gh-wrongpack`'s floor is a synthetic-program grounding artifact (a fixture fix, not a term fix).

## Falsifiable claim

The voxel-vs-art tolerance restores matched dynamic range so the re-gated decomposition comes back
**PICTURE-DRIVEN** (`conceptImageEffect > packEffect + NOISE`) **while** hard-middle agreement stays ≥ 0.70
(ideally holds at 1.00), matchedRight rises, matchedWrong stays low, and the crux cells keep their T-186
direction (`*-wrongpack` up, `*-samepack-*` down). A clean PICTURE-DRIVEN re-gate is what licenses the
(human-signed-off) promotion E-46/E-47 deferred.
