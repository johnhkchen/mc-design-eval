## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-29**, the headline. Eleven epics of evidence say the same thing: **every surface re-authored
from fitted geometry passes; every surface inherited from the voxelized TRELLIS blob fails** (E-27:
spikes −5×, zero→three same-object azimuths the day the roof became generated; E-28: the named
residuals are precisely the not-yet-generated parts). The repair path works but pays an exorcism per
surface. The inversion: **the blob is evidence, not substrate** — fit the components, generate the
build, never copy a blob cell.

## Goal

A **generate-first provision mode**: from concept + GLB, fit the full component set (footprint, storey
heights, wall slabs, roof forms incl. S-112's caps, opening groups — fit errors recorded), then
**generate** the entire build from parameters + kit (walls, roofs, arches, openings via the E-27/E-29
vocabulary), with the voxelized mesh used **only** as the cage's fit target (per-azimuth IoU, closure)
— zero blob cells in the artifact (E-29 Rule 1, machine-checked). Then the **head-to-head**: cottage,
gatehouse, church through generate-first, judged by the frozen gates beside their T-111 repair-path
results — same instrument, receipts committed, whatever it shows.
