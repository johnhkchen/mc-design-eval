## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-33**. The cottage workshop ledger (T-127) is the cleanest model-capability finding the
project has produced: the model **correctly critiqued the proportion defect in 4 of 6 rounds** — and
never fixed it. Two named, unwired seams explain why: `adjust-params` was never aimable at geometry
(pitch, heights, footprint — the parameter surface didn't expose them as revisable), and
`re-recognize` (the model asking to re-read a part of the concept) was reached for in round 3 and
**does not exist** (T-126 review). Eyes right; no hands.

## Goal

Wire both levers: (a) **`adjust-params` reaches geometry** — the T-133 measured-parameter surface
(pitch, ridge/eave heights, footprint, per-mass) becomes revisable in workshop rounds, bounded by the
conformance gates (regularity + S-135 proportion) and replayed deterministically from the ledger like
every other action; (b) **`re-recognize`** — the model can request re-recognition of a named part
(with its critique attached as context), the re-sampled fragment validates against the pack and
replaces that part of the program, ledgered raw-replies-and-all. Proof: on the T-127 cottage fixture,
a workshop run **converts the round-4-class critique into an accepted geometry revision** that the
S-135 check confirms.
