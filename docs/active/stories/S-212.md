## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-55**, the bar. The E-38 finding ([[workshop-progress-decoupled-from-quality]]) named the missing
instrument: *you cannot validate an eval or demonstrate an agent climbing one without a quality-VARIED,
human-rankable test set.* The whole E-48→E-54 arc optimized the climb against an acceptance rule that — T-211
proved one last time — **disagrees with the glance** (the centered arch scored `8/0/48` and the **median**
discarded the 48; the build that looks most like the concept was rolled back).

So before fixing the accept-rule, build the thing to judge it against: a small corpus of **climb-state builds
spanning a real quality range** (seed colonnade → closed box → +gable → +centered arch → +dressed relief — the
T-211/T-208 states are already on disk as renders) with **human glance-ranks**. Then measure: does the current
acceptance rule (per-move median + floor-only batch) **agree with that ranking**? Quantify the disagreement.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A quality-varied corpus of climb-state builds is **human-rankable** (a clear glance ordering exists, seed worst
→ dressed-arched best), and the current accept-rule's keep/rollback **disagrees** with it in a measurable,
reproducible way (it discards the glance-better +48 arch). **Fails if:** the builds are **not rankable** —
humans can't separate them on the glance (all near-D-, the E-38 finding recurring → the real gap is the build's
quality range, not the accept-rule; a major, honest result); or the disagreement **isn't reproducible** (the
median's discard was a one-run fluke, not structural → re-examine before spiking fixes); or the corpus is too
small/narrow to be a bar (name the coverage gap).
