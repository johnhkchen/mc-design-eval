## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-32**. Material choice is **diegetic, not optical**: a cottage is locally quarried stone and
plaster because that's what a cottage *is*; TRELLIS textures are an amalgam of other games' assets
(the barn's `flagged-mismatch` kit entries were optics correctly reporting it cannot decide
materials — T-121/T-122). The vernacular reasoning that picks right materials is **language-space
knowledge the model is excellent at** ("what is a tithe barn cost-effectively built from?" → oak
frame, fieldstone plinth, plank roof) — it was just never asked, because material decisions were
routed through optics. The human edge — taste — enters once per style as ratification, amortized over
every building of the style (and later, the town: one material story per place = palette coherence
for free).

## Goal

**Style formation as a productized chain** (BAML functions, S-129): theme brief in ("a fishing
village on a cold coast") → **material story** (local availability: geology, timber, wealth class,
roofing economy, trade goods) → **palette derivation** with per-role rationale citing the story →
**proportion rules** → **brush-needs list** (against the S-128 registry) → an **E-31-format style
pack** out — **human-ratified before commit** (the taste gate). Precedence enforced: explicit concept
evidence > pack assignment > vernacular default; **GLB textures never read** (provable — the chain
has no texture input).
