## Goal

Epic **E-13**, the scale study (option b). Build two hero subjects — **moai** (angular) and **pineapple**
(organic) — at **small ~16** and **large ~48**, so fidelity-vs-scale is visible against their ~32 builds
(S-036). The question: how does build fidelity (relative to the concept) track resolution, and does it
differ for angular vs organic forms?
