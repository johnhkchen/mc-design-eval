## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-28**. The church — the E-25 generalization subject — has **never reached the gates**. Its
reconstructed record (`benchmarks/sculpture/reconstructed/church.json`) names two causes:

1. **`chain-refused`** — the skin coverage gate fails `band0 stone=0.327 < 0.5`. But the recorded census
   decomposition shows band0 is **91.5% stone-family**: `polished_basalt` 1225 + `stone` 681 of 2082
   cells. The material assignment painted the *role* ("stone walls") across two stone blocks; the gate
   censuses the *literal block name* `stone`. This is the **kit-blind-gate class** for the third time
   (T-095: kit-renamed bands censused 0; T-101: dominant-only census rejected the styling) — a
   measurement-identity bug at a naming seam, not insufficient stone.
2. **`roof-program-fallback`** — the whole-mass roof fit was rejected (honest fallback, sampled roof
   kept). The church has **two roofs** — a tower cap and a nave pitch (named in its T-103 component
   record); a single whole-mass fit can't satisfy both.

## Goal

Unblock both, by their measured causes: (a) the coverage census composes **role-family identity** at its
naming seam — the intended role's block *set* (from the material map / kit), not one literal name —
under E-28 Rule 1's strict exception discipline: monotone (everything passing still passes), **both
fractions reported** in new records, committed records untouched, reasoning written for the reviewer.
(b) The roof fit re-attempted **per component**: tower and nave fitted separately against their GLB
regions, each with the tolerance-or-named-fallback contract. Then the church chain runs through skin →
kit extraction → both gates **for the first time**, every stage outcome named.
