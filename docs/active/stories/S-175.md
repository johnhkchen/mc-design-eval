Epic **E-43**. Build the approach S-174's spike chose, properly, to **readable amplitude**, and prove it on
the gatehouse. The implementation depends on the winner:

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The chosen approach, built to readable amplitude, makes the gatehouse articulation read in the render — a
clear step up from the token build (quoins ~4 cells) — without regressing closure. **It fails if:** readable
amplitude reads **busy/noisy** (amplitude/composition wrong — judged on the render, not the score); the
recess **reopens holes** (`closureOf` regresses → recess needs a watertightness guard); or the chosen
approach doesn't generalize past the gatehouse's specific geometry (note it for S-176).
