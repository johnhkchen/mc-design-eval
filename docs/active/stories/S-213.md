## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-55**, the diverge. With the quality-varied rankable test set + the current-rule disagreement quantified
(S-212), spike candidate acceptance rules and judge each by how well its keep/rollback **agrees with the glance
ranking**. The two failure modes T-211 isolated point at two specific candidates:

- The **per-move median discards a strong minority read** (the centered arch's `8/0/48` → median 8, the 48
  lost). Candidate: a **non-median aggregator** that doesn't throw away a strong minority — max-of-votes, mean,
  or an upper quantile.
- The **batch escape only fires at the score floor** (`score ≤ 0`) and disengages the moment a form move credits
  off 0. Candidate: **batch-while-improving** — let detail moves compound whenever the compound climbs, not only
  at the floor.

Diverge-before-converge ([[diverge-before-converge-experiment-freedom]]): implement ≥2, judge on the bar, let
the strong one (or a hybrid) win. Specify the goal+bar+judge, not the algorithm.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

At least one candidate accept-rule **agrees with the S-212 glance ranking** materially better than the current
per-move-median+floor-batch rule (it keeps the glance-better +48 arch and the dressed compound) **without
rubber-stamping**. **Fails if:** the only way to agree with the glance is to **keep everything** — no rule both
keeps the +48 arch *and* rejects a deliberately-worse build (then the accept-rule problem is ill-posed on a
gradient-less judge, and the fix is the *judge*/scorer, not the rule — name it); or the candidates **trade**
(each fixes one T-211 failure but reopens the other — median fix lets noise through, batch-while-improving
rubber-stamps); or no candidate beats the current rule on the bar (the median was already as good as the rule
class gets → architecture-ceiling territory, hand to S-214).

## Tickets

- **T-213-01** — implement ≥2 candidate accept-rules in the climb-gate (a non-median vote aggregator and
  batch-while-improving; both pure, opt-in, default OFF so frozen comparison runs are unperturbed); score each
  against the S-212 bar (agreement with the glance rank) AND against a **deliberately-worse build** (must reject
  it — the no-rubber-stamp falsification, unit-tested like T-208 CG-B2/B8). Pick the strongest (or hybrid);
  record the contest. `npm test` green; frozen instrument untouched.
