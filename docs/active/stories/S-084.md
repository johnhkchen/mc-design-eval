## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-23**, the **program path** for the surface failures we *witnessed*. The cottage diagnostics and the
E-20 building exposed two concrete defects the milestone must beat:

- **Roof not watertight** — the front-face projection showed roof rows speckled with non-roof blocks, gaps
  between plank courses, and the E-20 crown "dissolves into a chaotic grey jumble." It is not one clean wood
  surface.
- **Wall skin not coherent** — stray `spruce_planks`/`dark_oak_log` embedded in the stone field (the "random
  homes"), and `.` columns with no front voxel (holes).

These are **geometric/topological** coherence problems (holes, seams, stray blocks), distinct from E-18's
*color* speckle. They are deterministic to fix — and they are the **prerequisite for hollowing**: you cannot
enclose an interior behind a hole-y wall.

## Goal

Deterministic seal/fill + strip ops, driven by detectors (the S-082 roof-patch detector + a wall-intrusion
detector), producing a clean, **watertight shell**:

1. **Watertight roof** — the roof region is **100% covered in the roof material**; holes and seams between
   courses are filled; **stray non-roof blocks stripped**. No openings.
2. **Coherent wall skin** — wrong-material intrusions in a wall field are **stripped** (re-assigned to the
   field's material via the structural read / S-079 paint); **holes in the skin sealed**.
3. **Watertight shell** — after (1)+(2), a **flood-fill from outside cannot reach the interior** (the shell
   fully encloses the volume) — the invariant S-080's hollow depends on.
