## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-49**. The T-198 metered climb refuted M1 with a precise diagnosis: a **gate-arbitration conflict**.
The agent picked `close_shell` first (honoring the T-197 ordering gate) and it closed the gatehouse shell
(closureOf **0.615 → 1.000**) — but the score-driven accept-gate (T-191/E-50, `acceptsRound`) scored it a
**tie (16→16)** and **rolled it back** ("no shrink"): a closed grey box reads no closer to the concept *at the
diagnose-score's resolution* than an open one. So the accept-gate **rejects the form-fix the ordering gate
demands** → the shell never stays closed → detail never unlocks → the climb stalls at the open colonnade.

The fix: **a form win must be credit-able.** Extend the accept-gate so a genuine **form-readiness gain**
(closureOf↑ toward the form-ready threshold) is **kept** even when the whole-build picture-score ties or dips —
the **form-analog of the E-50 department-dominant override** (which already keeps a tool that clears a
*department* major through a scalar regression; [[wall-construct-needs-dense-shell]],
e48-e49-build-climb memory). Closing the shell is a real gain the picture-scalar is too coarse to see.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The form-credit term **keeps** `close_shell` on the real T-198 tie (so the shell stays closed and detail can
unlock) **and rejects** a deliberately-bad "closing" move. **Fails if:** credit-for-form becomes a **rubber
stamp** — it keeps a move that floods the interior / buries the roof / regresses a real department major (the
E-50 lesson: department-trust was safe *only* with the net-minor guard; form-trust needs the analogous
guard); or the term is too narrow and still rolls back the legitimate close (no behaviour change); or it keeps
a closure↑ the **glance** says is worse (a closed box hiding a feature the concept shows open → form-credit
must be glance-defensible, not blindly closure-maximizing).
