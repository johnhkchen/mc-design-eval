## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-20**, the **higher-resolution build**. With a whole-building GLB in hand (S-067), voxelize it
into a `DesignArtifact` — but at a **deliberately higher scale** than the ~32-block sculptures, so the
building has real architectural resolution (a larger footprint, more blocks: the "full-fledged, not a
facade" payoff). Color and clean it with the **matured E-19 pipeline**: value-true snap within the
design-doc **flat** palette (no busy blocks), material-region segmentation (no speckle), and stray-voxel
pruning (no floating/duplicate geometry).

One caveat to respect (a measured finding): **scale↔fidelity is not monotonic for angular forms** — a very
high scale can make an angular mass *worse* (the model/voxelizer under-resolves), so pick the scale that
**reads best**, don't just maximize block count.

## Goal

Voxelize the building GLB at a chosen high scale → a clean `DesignArtifact` (flat palette, segmented, pruned
per E-19), rendered. Record block count, form IoU vs the GLB, and the cleanliness axes (speckle,
distinct-block, off-palette). The large, clean, in-the-round building artifact the surgical pass refines.

## Builds on

  stray-pruning) + the `vConcept` scale convention (`src/sculpture.mjs`).
