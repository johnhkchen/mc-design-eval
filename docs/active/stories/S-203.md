## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-52**, the defining feature. A *gatehouse* without a gate isn't its picture. The E-49 capstone (T-201)
showed `carve_arch` producing a **ragged notched carve** (columns 3,4,8,9 notched) that the aperture-coherence
gate **correctly refuted**, falling back to `frame_arch` which left a **too-narrow framed slot** — the runner
itself logged "passage too narrow for an arch head — needs a wider opening (a rebuild)". The 1-wide passage
cannot be *widened by carving*; it needs a **rebuild to a wide arched opening.**

The fix: a hand that **rebuilds** the declared gate aperture to its intended wide arched form — coherent
head / jambs / sill, voussoir arch (reuse S-179), framed and dressed — passing the aperture-coherence gate,
with **closure held everywhere except the declared aperture** (which is intentionally open).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The rebuild hand produces a **wide arched gate** that passes the aperture-coherence gate and reads as an arch
on the glance, closure held everywhere but the aperture. **Fails if:** rebuilding the aperture drops closure
below the form-ready threshold and the climb **re-locks detail / oscillates** (then S-203 and S-202 are coupled
and must be co-designed — the aperture is declared-open, closure-except-aperture must hold); or the wide opening
**breaks structural integrity** (the lintel/arch can't span → the rebuild needs a load path the generator
lacks, name it); or the arch reads as a ragged hole again (the coherence gate refutes the rebuild too → carving
declared openings is a deeper limit than E-51's charter assumed).
