## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-17**'s one genuinely new build technique — the rung the sweep needs and the loose end E-16 left.
The E-16 **GLB-voxel build** wins on *form* (voxelizing the TRELLIS mesh captures the koi's curve, the
heart's lobes — silhouette IoU jumps), **but its color came out speckled**: each voxel sampled the TRELLIS
texture independently, so adjacent cells get scattered colors. The build has the right *shape* but reads
busy/dirty up close — form solved, material not, and silhouette IoU is blind to it.

This story builds the **material-clean pass**: turn that per-voxel noise into **clean material regions**.
The pieces all exist to reuse — (1) cluster the voxel colors into a small palette (median-cut in Lab, as
`src/color/palette-extract.mjs` `medianCutLab` does); (2) snap each cluster to a **value-true block**
(E-14, `src/color/cielab.mjs` `nearestLab` + the block-Lab table); (3) reduce spatial noise (a voxel takes
its neighborhood's dominant block) and apply **E-11 material passes** (`src/sculptor/material.mjs`:
same-hue set, height-varied) so surfaces read as *deliberate texture* rather than *noise*. The result is
the **R2 `+material-clean`** build.

## Goal

A `materialCleanVoxel(build) → DesignArtifact` pass: cluster + value-true-snap + spatial-denoise +
(optional) E-11 material texture, producing a cleaned GLB-voxel build. Demonstrate it **reduces color
noise** (a value-ΔE / distinct-block-count drop) without harming form (IoU steady). Apply across all 8
subjects → the R2 rung. Pure clustering/snap logic unit-tested (GL-free).
