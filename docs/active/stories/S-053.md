## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-16**, terminal link. The epic asked three measured questions with the real TRELLIS GLBs: does a
3-D **target** unlock the E-15 form loop (Arm A, S-049); is the voxelizer's **form** better than
text→JSON (Arm B, S-051); and is **GLB-voxel + surgical region tweaks** the capable combination (the
synthesis, S-052). This story collects the answers into one honest head-to-head and journals it — the
same discipline as the E-14 (value) and E-15 (form) consolidations: a measured before/after number, the
residual shown not hidden.

## Goal

For koi + heart, a **three-way head-to-head**: **text→JSON build** (the E-13 baseline) vs **GLB-voxel
build** (S-051) vs **GLB-voxel + surgical** (S-052) — each with its **form IoU** (vs the GLB target where
applicable, and vs concept for comparability) and **judge categorical**, in one before/after table.
Journal a **GLB-grounded form (E-16)** section in `docs/knowledge/design-learnings.md`: did the 3-D
target move the E-15 loop on text→JSON builds? did voxelization beat text→JSON on form? did surgical
tweaks on the voxel set add capability — and at what iteration cost? what's the residual? Hand the best
renders + the head-to-head to E-12 (`pr/assets/`) as the "image→3D, grounded and refined" beat.
