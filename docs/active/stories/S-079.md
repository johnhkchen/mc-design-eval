## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-23**, the **judgement path** (Path B, craft). The cottage's half-timbered face failed: the E-21
material map was correct (`white_terracotta` plaster + `dark_oak_log` timber over a stone base), but the
feature placer collapsed the plaster into stone (215 → 8 plaster blocks) because plaster and stone share
`placementRule: "walls"` and 3-D feature space has no storey axis. On a **painted wall**, that distinction
is a horizontal stripe — trivial.

## Goal

The "4 cans" surface-paint tool, the analogue of walking up to a wall with spray paint:

1. Project a face to the 2.5-D grid (S-078).
2. Show the LLM the **projected build face beside the concept's same face**; it paints materials onto the
   build face using **only the enforced palette** — the design-doc manifest ∪ E-21 concept-justified
   additions (the "4 cans": the tool exposes no other colors, so off-palette paint is structurally
   impossible — kills the 91-block bloat by construction).
3. **Back-project** the paint to the front-most surface voxels (ortho/45° only — unambiguous). A corner
   voxel visible from two faces takes the concept-matching paint; occluded interior voxels keep the
   colorimetric fallback.
4. **Per-face accept-if-closer** vs the concept face (the E-22 **resemblance gate** per face; P14-safe — a
   paint that doesn't move the face toward the concept rolls back). Faces fit context where the 57k-voxel
   region did not.

## Goal (concrete)

The cottage face, repainted: the plaster band returns, the half-timbered rhythm reads, the resemblance gate
on the front face improves (the 215→8 regression reversed). A material the concept shows but the build
missed may be **added back** (E-21's concept-justified-growth right), still within the can metaphor.
