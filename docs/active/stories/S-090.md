## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-25**. The E-24 zone-fill (S-085) establishes each zone's dominant material on the **wall fields**
— but a building's exposed surface is more than its wall fields. Measured on the committed cottage
artifact (2026-06-10): the roof is **96.7% spruce projected straight down**, yet the roof band's full
exposed surface is only **42.7% spruce — 33.5% stone bricks + 10.2% cobblestone** remain on the side
faces of the stepped courses and the gable mass. The upper wall band similarly keeps **28.3% stone-brick
residue** on under-eave and edge cells the wall-field enumeration missed. Result: a build that looks
skinned from the one angle the fill considered and grey from every other.

## Goal

Extend the zone-fill to **every exposed face of every zone**: enumerate the zone's surface by 6-direction
exposure (not by wall-field membership), and fill each exposed cell with the zone's dominant material,
preserving declared secondaries (timber studs, trim) and declared sub-regions (a chimney). After this, no
camera angle can find an un-skinned surface.

## Builds on

  floor-lines); `src/view/occupancy.mjs` (cell enumeration).
