## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-53**, the test of the hypothesis. With the form-readiness metric telling the truth again (S-206 — the
colonnade reads open, so `close_shell` is forced first), re-run the metered gatehouse climb. This decides
whether the form fix **alone** restores the build, or whether an independent detail-credit gap (S-208) remains.

**The hypothesis:** once the form actually closes, the detail hands compound and the build reaches its picture —
no detail-credit change needed. T-201 already showed detail moves *do* score on a closed form (relief +20,
12→32); the T-205 score-0 floor was an artifact of the form never closing. This story tests that directly.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

On the corrected metric, the climb picks `close_shell` first, **the shell closes**, detail compounds, and the
build reaches **its picture** on the glance — closed dressed walls, wide arch, dark slate roof at the right
pitch, right orientation/scale — mostly autonomously: **M1 landed.** **Fails if:** the climb still **stalls at
0 on a genuinely closed form** (then the detail-credit cold-start gap is real and independent → confirmed and
named for S-208, *with* the trajectory evidence that the form was closed this time); the form closes but a
**seventh gap** appears (a hand that doesn't read, a material the kit lacks → name it); or **score variance**
makes the verdict unreproducible (a closed dressed build scores 32 one run, 0 the next → the accept *signal*
needs de-noising independent of the form fix).
