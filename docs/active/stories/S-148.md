## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-35**. The headline defect: **the frozen instrument passes a build the glance rejects on
texture.** The barn that recorded the project's first composite PASS (T-143-01) is a flat brown
roof-tent on near-blank walls beside a concept of cobble infill, stone pilasters, brick quoins, and a
plinth. Stage 6 measures silhouette proportion, block-resemblance, and kit-presence — and is **blind**
to surface **grammar** (repeating motifs) and **relief** (proud/recessed of the wall plane). An
instrument that can't see the gap can't require it to close.

## Goal

The frozen instrument gains a **relief/rhythm precondition or lens** so a flat mono-fill wall **fails**
when the concept carries articulation, and an articulated build passes. Measured from the build's own
surface (rhythm period, proud-cell fraction, field/frame contrast) vs the concept's recognised grammar
— **not** a colour metric. Calibrated from committed verdicts: the flat barn that PASSED is the
**anti-anchor** (it must move to FAIL or a named relief residual under the lens); an articulated
reference must pass. Identity-class discipline ([[glance-true-budget-v2]], T-095/T-101/T-110/T-137/T-144):
**aggregation/precondition only — no re-judge**, both arithmetics where a threshold changes, the frozen
judge contract (prompts, azimuths, severity grain) unmoved, committed records re-derive or are reported
beside; **version the policy tag, not the envelope**. Parallel to the brush work; **proven in S-149**.
