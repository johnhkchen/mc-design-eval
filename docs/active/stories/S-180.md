## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-45**. Before re-calibrating anything, locate the defect. T-178-01 proved the style-distance term
floors the matched build (WALL:replace 6/6, OPENING:replace 6/6 against its **own** concept, identical to its
wrong-style twin, `replaceContrast −0.20`). But "the measure is the gate" has two distinct loci with
different fixes (see E-45 background):

- **R — the reading:** Layer A (`DiagnoseBuild`) emits `replace`/wrong-style on an element whose material
  **matches** the concept (a genuine mis-read → fix the judge contract).
- **S — the scoring:** Layer A emits the right semantic content but `itemStyleClass`/`kind` mis-buckets it
  `wrong-style`, and/or the **single-`replace` hard cap** floors an otherwise-faithful build (→ fix the
  scalar to a graded distance).

This is a diagnostic spike on **already-committed evidence** — no new metered votes needed (the per-vote
Layer A outputs are in `experiments/eval-alignment/results/corpus-referee-faithful-covered.json` and the
`run-votes6.log`). Classify, don't fix.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The matched-build `replace` tags split cleanly into R vs S, naming which locus (or both) to fix.
**Fails if:** the tags are ambiguous (Layer A's `present`/`missing`/`expected` text doesn't say enough to tell
a mis-read from a right-read — then the judge contract under-specifies and *that* is the finding); or the
split is **all-R** (the reading is the whole defect — re-calibrating the scalar won't help, refuting S-181's
scoring path before it's built); or **all-S** (the judge reads fine, it's purely the cap).
