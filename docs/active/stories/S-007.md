## Goal

Epic **E-08**, second link. Stress-test whether the principles discovered on the Taj generalize to a
**radically different reference**: Hōryū-ji (`references/horyu_ji.JPG`) — a vertical, multi-storey
**wooden pagoda** with stacked tiered roofs, dark timber/plaster palette, massing nothing like a domed
mausoleum. This is the hardest test of:

- **P13 (3-D reference → flat facade):** the reference *is* a freestanding tower. Does "borrow the
  reference's RHYTHM, not its 3-D standalone parts; a facade is ONE connected plane" still yield a
  coherent single-elevation build, or does a pagoda defeat it?
- **P12 (craft from reference, color from brief):** the timber palette is dark and near-monochrome; the
  brief demands COLORFUL. Does color still come from the brief, not the wood?

One experiment: run the current champion config grounded on Hōryū-ji, A/B-judge both rounds, record what
held and what didn't. A principle that fails to generalize gets **scoped in the journal**, not waved past.
