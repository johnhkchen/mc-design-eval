## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-25**. The benchmark roster has a measurement-quality problem and a coverage gap:

- **Moai is a bad measurement subject.** Its concept art shows **three statues in one frame on a black
  background**; the TRELLIS mesh came out as two fragmented masses; the build was judged "different
  object" largely due to debris and fragmentation that say nothing about the pipeline's design capability.
  A weird concept makes every downstream verdict ambiguous — the subject is retired, by decision
  (2026-06-10), not quietly dropped.
- **No challenge subject.** Cottage and gatehouse have both been used to develop the pipeline; passing on
  them can't prove generalization. The epic's milestone needs a building the pipeline has **never been
  tuned on**.

## Goal

(a) **Retire moai** from the resemblance roster with a recorded rationale (registry comment + journal);
its scale-study history stays untouched. (b) **Add the challenge subject: a village church with a square
bell tower** — two attached masses, two roof forms (pitched nave + pyramidal tower cap), ≥3 material
zones (stone walls, dark roof, timber door, light trim), bulky throughout (no thin freestanding features
— the TRELLIS thin-subject limit). Its concept must pass a **concept sanity checklist** before
registration: single building, clean background, one canonical 3/4 view, distinct material zones,
readable silhouette. Once registered, the concept is **immutable** (E-25 Rule 2).
