## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-31**. Every epic since E-16 inherited the TRELLIS mesh's lumpiness — wobbling walls, ragged
footprints, noise that fourteen epics of downstream machinery sanded but never removed (E-27 measured
276 attached spikes / 23.9% ragged columns entering at voxelization). Minecraft's prior makes the fix
cheap: the game has roughly **eight legal surface orientations** (the 6 axes + 45° roof planes), every
wall is an axis-aligned plane, every footprint is a rectilinear polygon. Snapping the mesh to that
grammar **before** anything reads it makes builds straight by construction instead of straightened by
surgery.

## Goal

A deterministic **GLB conditioning stage**: decimate to a coarse face set → **snap face normals to the
Minecraft grammar** (6 axes + 45°) → detect the dominant mirror plane and apply the better half →
fit a **rectilinear footprint**. Output: a **conditioned form sketch** — clean planes, applied
symmetry, rectangular footprint, gross proportions (storey heights, roof pitch class) — the artifact
recognition (S-125) reads instead of the raw mesh. Pure, unit-tested on synthetic and real GLBs;
subject-agnostic; no fit tolerances downstream (the sketch informs, it is never a target to match).

## Builds on

- The GLB manifest + existing mesh loaders (`trellis-glb` path); the E-27 decomposition's plane-fit
  machinery (reused where it serves); the E-30 sub-speck tolerance (conditioning composes after it).
