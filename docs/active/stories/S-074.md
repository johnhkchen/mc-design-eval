## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-21**, terminal. The epic replaced "nearest mean color" as the sole material authority with a
**concept-grounded, LLM-defined material map** (S-071) assigned by **geometric feature** (S-072) and
**refined against the concept** (S-073) — to fix the failure where colorimetry **collapsed stone brick and
cobblestone** (near-tone, but a deliberate material distinction). This story measures whether the
distinction is restored and the build reads clean *and* true.

## Goal

Apply the full concept-grounded material pipeline to the **gatehouse + cottage** (the stone-brick-vs-cobble
headline) and **2 sculptures**; produce a **before/after vs the colorimetric (E-19) build**: does the
intended near-tone distinction **return** (the right materials present where the concept put them, not
merged)? Does it read **clean** (E-19 region coherence held — no speckle) **AND true** (material identity
matches intent)? Judge categorical + a journal **concept-grounded materials (E-21)** section (the failure,
the LLM-map fix, the feature assignment, where it helps vs over-reaches) + E-12 handoff.
