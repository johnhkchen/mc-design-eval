## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-51**. The reviewer authorized **letting the loop carve openings** (2026-06-17) — the E-50 plateau's
arched-gate gap was unbuildable because the blanket no-air-op rule forbids carving the aperture. Implement the
charter **narrowing** and the hand:

- **Narrow the no-air-op rule to an aperture-coherence gate.** Carving (removing material) is allowed **only
  to form a *declared* opening** (a door/gate/window in the recognition program). Everything else stays
  no-air-op ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]] still holds outside declared apertures).
- **The aperture-coherence gate** (replacing the blanket ban for openings): a carved opening must be a
  **coherent dressed aperture** — continuous head/jambs/sill, framed, no ragged edges or stray voids — and
  **closure must hold on every surface except the declared aperture** (precedent:
  [[generate-first-wall-watertightness]]'s opening-coherence gate).
- **The hand:** carve a declared opening to its intended width/shape and dress it (head/jambs/sill; reuse the
  S-179 voussoir arch for arched heads). Prove the **wide arched gate** on the gatehouse.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The loop carves the gatehouse gate to a wide **arched, dressed** aperture that reads on the glance, with
closure held everywhere except the aperture. **Fails if:** carving produces ragged voids / reopens holes —
the coherence gate can't tell an intentional opening from a hole ([[reference-is-spec-not-substrate]]); then
**the charter relaxation is refuted** — openings revert to recess-only and wide arches stay an explicit known
limit (a real result that bounds the rule). Also fails if the carve breaks closure on a *non*-aperture surface
(the narrowing leaked — re-scope the "declared opening only" guard).
