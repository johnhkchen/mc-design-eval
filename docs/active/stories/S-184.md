## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-46**. Turn the S-183 corpus into a validated (or refuted) verdict. Two deliverables:

1. **The human labels** — the promotion gate is *agreement with human judgment*, so the labels are **human**
   (the reviewer is the labeler; one-person panel for now). Build a clean **pairwise labeling instrument**
   (beside-concept presentation of the hard-middle contestable pairs — small enough for a focused session,
   not the full all-pairs matrix). The labeling is a **human gate**; the loop prepares everything up to it.
   *Fallback (flagged):* an LLM-proxy panel labels — explicitly **not** human ground truth, a weaker gate
   that can refute but not license promotion.
2. **The agreement + decomposition harness** — computes (a) **term-vs-human agreement** (pairwise accuracy /
   rank correlation on the labeled pairs) and (b) the **pack-effect vs concept-image-effect decomposition**
   generalized from T-182's C-control (does the recalibrated term's signal track the PACK or the CONCEPT
   IMAGE across the population?).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The recalibrated term **agrees with the human labels** on the hard middle **and** its signal is carried by the
**concept-image** (not the pack) across the population. **Fails if:** the term tracks the pack at scale (the
T-182 confound generalizes → concept-image-conditioning fix, DO-NOT-PROMOTE — the publishable negative); or
inter-label agreement on the hard middle is low (no ground truth → the gate is ill-posed, reported not
buried); or the term agrees with humans on the *obvious* pairs but not the contestable ones (validated only
where it didn't need to be).
