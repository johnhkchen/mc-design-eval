## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-47**. The follow-on to E-46's DO-NOT-PROMOTE (T-185-01). The recalibrated style-distance term is
**PACK-DRIVEN** on the S-183 decoupling corpus (`packEffect 45 ≫ conceptImageEffect 8`, hard-middle agreement
0.20 against labelable ground truth). The term grades the build against a **pack-derived** style spec, so it
measures pack-material agreement, not whether the build looks like its concept image. The exact seam and the
candidate repairs are in `docs/active/work/T-185-01/LOCALIZATION.md`.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Conditioning the wrong-style judgement on the **rendered-build-vs-concept-image match** (not pack/material
agreement) makes the term **PICTURE-DRIVEN** on the same corpus — and the re-run E-46 gate licenses the (still
human-signed-off) promotion. **Fails if:** the picture effect rises only by re-coupling to the pack (no true
decoupling — check the crux cells move the *right* way); matched builds regress; or the re-gated decomposition
stays PACK-DRIVEN / INCONCLUSIVE / the hard middle agreement misses 0.70.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The defect from `LOCALIZATION.md` is fixed at the seam (`styleProfileBlock`/`diagnoseRenderArgs` and/or
      `styleFidelityScore`): the expectation the build is graded against is **concept-image-anchored**, with the
      pack supplying material vocabulary only.
- [ ] Spike ≥2 candidate approaches; judge on the re-gated decomposition (the glance), not a tuned constant.
- [ ] **Re-gate:** re-run `experiments/eval-alignment/style-agreement-run.mjs` (S-183 corpus, VOTES≥6) →
      **PICTURE-DRIVEN** (`conceptImageEffect > packEffect + NOISE`), hard-middle agreement ≥ 0.70, crux cells
      move correctly (`*-wrongpack` rise, `*-samepack-*` fall). Record the numbers at full strength.
- [ ] **No autonomous freeze:** any promotion remains behind the corpus gate AND human sign-off
      ([[pin-guard-is-structural]]); `measurements/` untouched until then. The T-185-01 staging path (a guarded
      `promote.mjs` the human runs) is the template if the re-gate licenses a GO.
- [ ] Honest recording: if the fix does not flip the decomposition, that is the result — do not promote on a
      split.
- [ ] `npm test` green.
