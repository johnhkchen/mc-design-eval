## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-33**. The concepts' barn/cottage roofs read steep (~55–60°); the stair block's native slope
is 45°. If the roof vocabulary tops out there, steeper pitches are **inexpressible** and every build
comes out squat regardless of how well proportions are read — likely the single largest contributor
to "the form is off." Minecraft builders solve this with **mixed full-block/stair courses** (the 2:1
stepping family and friends); the brush registry (T-128) has the door for exactly this kind of
addition, and the factory's draft conventions (T-131) define how it's specified.

## Goal

**One new brush through the door**: steep-pitch roof courses — parametrized by pitch class above 45°
(2:1 at minimum; the implementer states which classes are realizable and which are refused by
construction), state-correct (`unmapped` empty), composable with the existing roof family (ridge
caps, verges, gable ends, dormers), conformance-compatible (courses still read even), preview-carded.
Realized on the re-seeded barn program where the measured pitch demands it.
