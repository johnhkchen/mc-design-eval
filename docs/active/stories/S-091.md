## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-25**. Three witnessed shell defects, all geometric/topological, none caught by any current gate:

1. **Floating debris.** The committed cottage artifact contains **23 connected components — 126 cells off
   the main mass** (one 54-cell chunk, one 20-cell chunk, plus salt), visible as floating fragments around
   the silhouette in every render. The retired moai subject was dominated by leftover rail/beam debris.
2. **Shell voids.** The gatehouse build has a gaping dark cavity in its upper-right that its concept does
   not show — a missing-mass hole, distinct from legitimate openings (the arch, windows).
3. **Plan-only closure.** S-084 verified the roof watertight *from the sky*; at oblique angles sky shows
   through gaps between roof course steps. Closure was never checked from the directions viewers
   actually look.

## Goal

A deterministic **shell-integrity stage**: (a) strip off-main connected components (keep the largest;
small thresholded exceptions must be declared, none expected for buildings); (b) detect cavities/voids
that the concept's silhouette does not show and patch them with the zone dominant, while **preserving
openings the concept does show** (door, windows, arch); (c) verify closure from **all six axis directions**
— every boundary face of the enclosed volume is skinned, except declared openings.
