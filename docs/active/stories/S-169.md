## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-40**. The referee — does the style-distance term (S-168) actually fix the E-39 negative, and does
it agree with a human? Two things to settle on the S-167 corpus:

1. **Crater re-run.** Re-run `experiments/eval-alignment/clean-wrong-style.mjs` with the new term: the clean
   build must now score **far below** its matched twin, outside the ±12 noise — the spread E-39 lacked.
2. **Agreement + power.** Score the S-167 corpus with the new term and check it **agrees with the human
   labels** on the contested wrong-style pairs; re-run the split-vs-fused bake-off on the now ≥8–10-state
   corpus (E-39's was 2 states).

Then make a clear, evidence-backed **recommendation** on whether to promote the style-distance term into the
**frozen instrument** — recommend, do not silently execute (a frozen-instrument change is a deliberate,
re-pinned, separately-approved step; the de-freeze lesson).

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

With the new term, the clean × wrong-style fixture craters AND the score agrees with the human's pairwise
labels on the corpus's contested pairs. **It fails if:** it craters but **disagrees** with the human
(over-penalizes a close style → mis-calibrated, recommend a graded distance, not promotion); OR it agrees
on easy pairs but not the *contested middle* (then the term captures only gross style errors — say so);
OR the bake-off still can't separate split from fused even at 8–10 states (then the split's value is
genuinely marginal — recommend collapse). Whichever way it lands, the recommendation follows the evidence.
