## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-46**. Build the labeled corpus's *substrate*: ≥8–10 styled build states across multiple subjects
(gatehouse, cottage, barn, …) and styles, with a deliberate factorial that **decouples PACK from CONCEPT
IMAGE** so the T-182-01 pack confound becomes testable instead of baked in (E-46 background). The corpus must
include the **crux cells** —

- **match:** build B · its true pack · the concept B was built for → intended HIGH.
- **same-pack, wrong-picture:** build B · its true pack · a concept B does **not** match → intended LOW (the
  decisive "reads the picture" cell).
- **wrong-pack, right-picture:** build B · a foreign pack · the concept B matches → pack-sensitivity probe.
- **cross:** off-diagonal builds populating the matrix.

— and the **hard middle**: *partially*-faithful states (one wrong material, slightly-off proportions, missing
dressing) where a human could reasonably rank either way. Built by replay (reproducible); a manifest records,
per state, its (build, pack, concept) and its *intended* faithfulness label.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The corpus decouples the axes — every crux cell is constructible and the manifest lets the S-184 harness
measure pack-effect and concept-image-effect separately. **Fails if:** the same-pack/wrong-picture cells are
unconstructible (recognition always re-derives a matching pack → pack and concept inseparable *by
construction* — a structural confound, the deeper finding); or the "hard middle" states aren't actually
contestable (all obvious → the corpus can't embarrass the term). Build a state and inspect it on the render
before trusting its intended label.
