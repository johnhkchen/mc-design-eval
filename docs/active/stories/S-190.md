## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-48**. The payoff — with the gradient wired (S-188) and the highest-leverage hands built (S-189),
run the **sustained picture-driven climb** on the gatehouse and answer the epic's question: does the build
**measurably approach its picture, round over round, under the loop's own steam**, in a way a **human agrees
with on the glance**? And where is the **ceiling**?

This is the M1 capstone evidence: one house that looks like its picture, reached by the loop — not by
hand-authoring.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A multi-round run shows a **monotone-ish lift toward the concept** (the picture-critique trend improves and
the beside-concept renders visibly close the gap), **a human glance agrees** on a sampled before/after, and
the lift is **mostly autonomous** (little per-round human nudging). **Fails if:** it plateaus early (the
remaining gap is hands we haven't built → name them for E-49); it oscillates (accept-gate/restraint
insufficient); the human glance **disagrees** with the critique's "improvement" (the gradient still isn't the
glance — back to E-47's family); or the lift only happens with a human steering each round (not an autonomous
climb — report how much human was needed).
