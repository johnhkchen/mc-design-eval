## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-34**. The E-33 milestone's headline defect (T-138-02 review, finding 4): `maskProportions`
in `src/form/silhouette-proportion.mjs` defines the eave as *the topmost row whose horizontal
extent ≥ `eaveWidthFrac` (0.98) × the max row extent*. When a build carries a plinth/skirt band
wider than its walls (the cottage: y3–4, 1–2 blocks wider), the skirt IS the max extent, no wall
row qualifies, and the eave line lands on the skirt top — everything above reads as roof. Recorded
cottage ridge:eave 5.5 / roofShare 0.8182 vs corrected ≈1.7 / 0.45 vs targets 1.4145 / 0.293. The
armed proportion gate inverted the loop's gradient: the model aimed the correct wall-raise 5/6
rounds and the one accepted move went the wrong way.

## Goal

**The skirt never reads as the eave.** Bottom-anchored bands wider than the wall column above them
are excluded from eave anchoring; the wall column owns the line. Frozen op parameters (declared,
never subject-tuned); identity-class discipline in full: monotone proof (skirt-free masks derive
byte-identical ratios), both rulers reported wherever a record re-derives, committed records
untouched, the frozen judge unmoved. The cottage artifact becomes the pinned regression fixture.
