## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-25**, terminal. Everything before this story builds a pipeline that *should* produce
concept-faithful buildings; this story is the proof that it **does**, on three subjects at once —
including one (the church, S-094) that the pipeline has never been tuned on. The epic's binding rules
apply in full: the deliverable is the **multi-angle contact sheet** a human can recognize; the concept is
immutable; the challenge subject enters as a registry entry only; no angles dropped; no hand-edits; gaps
named honestly.

## Goal

One named `npm run` per subject takes **cottage, gatehouse, and the church** from concept to build
end-to-end — shell integrity (S-091), concept-derived zone map (S-092), full-shell zone-fill (S-090) with
E-24's value-true blocks and coherence, secondaries, and the **multi-angle same-object gate** (S-093) —
and all three **pass**: same-object at every azimuth, ≤2 named minor gaps each. The church run proves
generalization: a reviewer can verify the pipeline contains **no reference to the church beyond its
registry entry**. Fresh re-runs reproduce all three results.
