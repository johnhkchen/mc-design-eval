## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-52**, the M1 capstone. With the three geometry fixes in place — the form metric invariant to proud
detail (S-202), the wide arched gate by rebuild (S-203), and the roof at slate colour + correct pitch (S-204) —
re-run the **metered gatehouse climb** and judge it on the glance. This is the proof the E-49 capstone (T-201)
could not deliver: that run broke the gate-arbitration deadlock and got a closed dressed-stone mass with a
gabled roof, but missed its picture on the arch, roof colour, and pitch — and the form metric collapsed under
relief. All four of those are now addressed.

Expected live result: `close_shell` sticks (form-credit, E-49) and **stays** read-closed through the relief
pass (S-202), the wide arched gate reads (S-203), the roof lands dark slate at ~1.35 pitch (S-204), the framing
eyes stay quiet — the glance reads the gatehouse's picture.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

On the three fixes, the re-run metered climb reaches the gatehouse's picture on the glance — closed dressed
walls, **wide arched gate**, **dark gabled roof at the right pitch**, right orientation/scale — mostly
autonomously, **without oscillating** (the form metric no longer turns the gate against the build). **Fails
if:** the fixes don't compose live (one undoes another — e.g. the arch rebuild re-locks detail); the climb
**oscillates** despite S-202 (the metric fix didn't fully take); or a **sixth gap** appears (interior/ROOM, a
missing kit material, proportion beyond pitch) → name it at full strength as the input to E-53 (generalize). A
precisely-named sixth gap is a complete result.
