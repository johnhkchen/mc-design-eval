## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-44**. T-176-01 generalized the E-43 treatment grammar's edges-from-geometry to the roof and
openings and found it **composes cleanly for the eave band, ridge cap, and opening reveal** — but **leaks on
two element-specific edges the wall corner/top/row model cannot name**:

- **The raking verge** — a *sloped line* down the gable edge (per-column along the pitch), not a flat row.
  The column-keyed `top.row` model treats the gable-end columns as a heavy end band instead of a clean rake.
- **The voussoir arch head** — a *curve*. `deriveOpeningEdges` exposes `isArch` and the reveal generalizes,
  but a voussoir arc needs an opening-local curve derivation the footprint-corner model doesn't have.

Add the missing derivations/brushes so the **same** treatment grammar names both, and prove the gatehouse's
verge board and arch head **read** on the render. **Specify the goal + the glance, not the algorithm** — the
clean way to derive a sloped line and an arch curve is open; a per-column pitch walk and an arc-brush are the
leading candidates, not the mandate.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

The treatment grammar gains a raking-verge edge (sloped per-column line) and a voussoir head (arc) such that
the gatehouse's verge board and arch head **read on the render** as crisp element edges (not a heavy end
band / a void), with the same vocabulary that handles corners/eave/reveal. **Fails if:** the sloped-line /
arc derivation needs element-local geometry the footprint model still can't supply on a gable or multi-ridge
roof (then the **edge classifier**, not the brush, is the sub-problem — the E-43 "hard geometry" risk made
concrete); or the verge/head reads busy or breaks closure (`closureOf`).
