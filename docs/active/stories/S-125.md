## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-31**. The strategic diagnosis: a human resembles concept art by **recognizing** forms and
substituting canonical realizations — "that lumpy thing is a gable roof" — not by measuring the mesh.
Recognition is robust to exactly the noise that broke fourteen epics of fitting, and it is a
vision-language task the model is demonstrably good at (the E-21/E-26 map and kit rationales prove the
model *sees* correctly even when the output contract wasted it). This story puts the model's reading
back at the center — the thing the project is named for.

## Goal

The model reads the **concept + conditioned sketch** (S-123) and emits the **building program** in
pack vocabulary (S-124): idiom instances with parameters — e.g. roof: `gable, pitch 45°, 2 dormers`;
walls: `timber-frame, jettied upper storey`; base: `stone plinth`; openings: `rhythm + treatment` —
schema-validated against the pack, realized by the registry into a complete clean-by-construction
build. Mis-recognitions are expected and are the **workshop's** job to catch (S-126), not a fit
tolerance's. Recognition is re-sampleable inside the workshop (E-31 Rule 1); the **accepted program is
committed** and replays deterministically (Rule 5). Runs on the `claude -p` subscription shim, strong
tier.
