## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-42**. The long-deferred roof work (old S-150 / T-160-03). `roof-generate.mjs` fills a **solid
material prism** — ~72% of a build's blocks in the saltcrag census — which reads as a single material
(planks) and drowns the walls; and it lays a **single ridge**, so the cottage's two perpendicular gables
can't be expressed. Replace with **a covering over the wall envelope** (not a solid fill) and **one gable
per `masses[]`** (multi-ridge), reusing the proven `gableRecord`/`generateRoof` per mass.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Building the roof as a covering over the envelope (hollow, not a solid prism) and laying one ridge per mass
makes the roof read as a roof (not a plank mountain) and gives multi-mass subjects their correct gables —
without regressing the wall-track closure. **It fails if:** removing the prism reopens narrow-roof
coverage/closure seams the wall track fought (then roof-fit is its own sub-problem — guard with the closure
metrics, report); OR the per-mass ridges don't register to the build frame (the footprint-registration
family again); OR the roof reads better but the build's score doesn't move (the cap is elsewhere — material,
S-171, or the measure).
