## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-28**. The E-27 roof program fits and generates the **slopes** — and the verdicts prove it
(cottage 135°/225° now same-object). The two still-failing cottage views name what the program doesn't
yet generate: 45° major `form @ roof across the whole top` + major `massing @ upper storey gable ends`;
315° major `form @ roof` with minor `eaves/edges` (record:
`benchmarks/sculpture/reconstructed/cottage.json`). A pitched roof seen from its gable end is mostly
gable triangle, verge, and eave termination — none of which the slope generator constructs; they remain
whatever the blob left there.

## Goal

Extend the roof program with **fitted end-of-roof construction**: the **gable-end triangle** (infill
plane fitted to the component's end faces against the GLB), **verge/rake lines** (the sloped edge
courses, stair/full-block per pitch), and **eave terminations** (clean course ends, overhang faces) —
all with fit errors recorded, generated via the E-27 construction vocabulary, inside the cage
(per-azimuth silhouette IoU vs GLB, closure, protected chimney, auto-rollback). Out-of-tolerance fits
keep current geometry with the failure named (E-28 Rule 2).

## Builds on

- E-27 roof program + shaped vocabulary (the generators this extends); T-103 component records (end-face
  geometry); T-102 cage (acceptance); the reconstructed cottage record (the named target views).

## Run-the-test ticket
