## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-24**. The per-face gate **accepted** the cottage skin on a marginal `0.25→0.40` while the
upper-storey wall was **91% un-plastered** — a number rubber-stamped an un-skinned surface. This is the
recurring fooled-gate failure ([[render-aliasing-not-material-speckle]]): the gate scored *something*, but
not "is the intended dominant material actually applied?"

## Goal

A **coverage-aware gate**: before the per-face resemblance hill-climb counts, the gate checks that each
structural zone's **dominant material is established** — the intended material covers ≥ a threshold of the
zone's wall-field surface. If the dominant is **under-applied**, the face/skin **fails** regardless of the
marginal resemblance delta. So an un-skinned (or 9%-skinned) wall can **never** pass.

The coverage check is a **gate precondition**, not a tie-breaker: resemblance refines a skin that already
has its base coat; it cannot substitute for one.

## Builds on

- The E-22 per-face / resemblance gate, S-085 (the dominant per zone), E-23 `structuralZones` + the
  material map (the *intended* dominant to measure coverage against).

## Run-the-test ticket
