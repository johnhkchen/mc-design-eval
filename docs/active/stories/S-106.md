## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-27**. The user's prediction this story tests: *skinning succeeds when the major components are
parametric and defined rather than one big blob.* Today the skin/grammar/kit pipeline re-derives
structure from noisy occupancy at every stage (zone bands from floor-line scans, frame lines from
geometry heuristics, roof courses from whatever the blob's top looks like) — which is how voxelization
noise kept getting faithfully skinned. After S-104/S-105 the major components carry **definitions**
(generated planes, edges, fields, opening groups) — re-derivation is both unnecessary and wrong (E-27
Rule 4: components are the contract).

## Goal

The E-24/E-26 skin pipeline **consumes component definitions**: roof courses follow the generated roof
planes (not the sampled top); frame lines come from component edges (eaves, rakes, corners as defined);
wall fields are the slabs' faces; zone maps are re-derived/re-pinned on the rebuilt geometry where bands
shifted. Re-measure the **church's skin-gate refusal** (band0 wall-field stone 0.33 < 0.5 — the E-25
provision finding) on its reconstructed shell: the expected unblock, or a named measured residual.
No behavior change for subjects/stages lacking a component record (graceful fallback to occupancy
derivation, recorded when used).
