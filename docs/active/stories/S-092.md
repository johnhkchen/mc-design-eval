## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-25**. The zone *machinery* (floor-lines, zone-fill) now works — but the zone *assignments* come
from a built-in building prior (base=stone, upper=plaster, roof=wood), not from the concept. Two measured
consequences:

- The **cottage** concept is a half-timbered building on a low stone **plinth**: cream plaster + dark
  timber on **both** storeys, stone only at the base course and corners. The prior assigns the entire
  ground storey to stone (57.9% of the base band's exposed surface). Perfect execution of this map still
  produces a *different cottage* than the concept's.
- The **gatehouse** map invented a brown base band on an all-stone building.

Key insight from the skin saga: the splat **cannot fill** a zone (9% conversion — quantization collapse),
but the concept image **can locate** where bands begin and end and what each band's dominant is. Use the
concept for what it's good at: *boundaries and assignments*, with the deterministic fill doing the
application.

## Goal

Derive the zone map **from the concept image**: build a height-band color profile of the concept's
building region, align it to the structural floor-lines, and emit per-band `{yRange, dominantRole,
secondaries}` resolved against the E-21 material roles. The prior becomes a fallback when the concept is
unreadable — never an override. Cottage map must show plinth + two plaster/timber storeys; gatehouse must
show no brown base.

## Builds on

- E-21 material map (roles); E-24 S-086 value-true selection (role → block); `structural-read.mjs`
  floor-lines (the y-axis the profile aligns to); `src/color/image-grid.mjs` quantization.

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-092-01` — pure concept band-profile extractor + floor-line alignment, unit-tested on synthetic
  images; run on cottage + gatehouse concepts; resulting maps match the concepts' actual banding; consumed
  by the zone-fill with zero subject-specific code.
