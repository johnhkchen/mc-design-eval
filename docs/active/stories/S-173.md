## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-42**. The proof that closes the loop with E-41. With material faithfulness (S-171) and a
constructed roof (S-172), there is finally a build that is faithful to its concept. Feed it back through
E-41's crater re-run (`experiments/eval-alignment/corpus-referee.mjs` with the `kind`-emitting Layer A):
the matched-faithful build should now score **well above** the same build against a wrong-style concept —
the crater E-39/E-40 could never show because every "matched" build was itself unfaithful.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

A materially-faithful, properly-roofed matched build scores far above its wrong-style twin under E-41's
style-distance term — the crater finally **separates** (spread well outside the ±12 noise). **It fails if:**
it still doesn't separate (then the gate is the term's *distance scale*, not the build — hand back to a term
re-calibration, and that is the finding); OR the faithful build lifts matched but the wrong-style twin also
lifts (the term under-penalizes — re-calibrate); OR faithfulness can't be reached for any subject (then the
build pipeline, not the measure, is the standing wall — name it plainly).
