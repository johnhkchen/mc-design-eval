# Epics

Epics are the top of the work hierarchy: **Epic → Story → Ticket**. Each epic is a large, spec-grounded body of work that will be broken into stories (`docs/active/stories/`), which in turn break into RDSPI tickets (`docs/active/tickets/`).

Epics are planning artifacts. Unlike tickets, they are **not** scheduled or phase-advanced by Lisa (the `.lisa.toml` `[dirs]` only scans tickets/stories/work). They exist to scope and sequence the work and to anchor stories to the spec.

Source of truth: [`docs/specification.md`](../../specification.md).

## Dependency graph

E-01 is the foundation (the artifact contract is the spine, §5). E-02–E-05 build the four instrument layers around it. E-06 runs the Phase-1 3×3 matrix once those layers exist. E-07 is the deferred Phase-2 model sweep. E-08 is the autonomous optimization loop: it *discovers* improved prompting techniques (on top of E-03's runner, judged by E-04) and feeds the promoted champions into E-06's fair comparison. E-09 swaps the geometry engine — LLM design reasoning → concept art → 3D mesh → voxel → the same DesignArtifact (§5) — keeping the instrument (E-02 render, E-04 rubric, §6 validators) while retiring text-JSON's geometry ceiling; its voxelizer core is built portable so other repos (e.g. plant-model-studio) can reuse it. E-10 is the real-block color layer: a CIE-Lab nearest-block engine + a block→Lab table that extracts the canonical block palette from a concept image and maps colors to real blocks — grounding concepts (imaginary blocks → real, detail → resolution-capped) before image-to-model, and serving as E-09's portable voxelizer color core. E-11 is the keystone: the **staged sculptor** — block out gray massing, lock it, then add material/relief/curves/detail as pluggable passes over a locked substrate, framed by massing-first and render-review-iterate. It is the input-agnostic toolkit that makes the final build good whatever the form source (concept image now, GLB/scan later); the craft-pass library and the E-09 voxelizer plug into it. E-13 demonstrates breadth — the `vConcept` archetype (term → concept → build) turned loose on sculptural subjects — and *measures* the concept→build fidelity frontier. E-14 acts on what E-13 measured: it activates E-10's CIE-Lab engine in the live path as the **shared value-true palette contract** between the concept and build stages, closing the *value* drift (the moai's faithful-form/too-dark-block gap) from both ends at once — the color half of the gap, leaving the form half to E-11/E-15. E-15 closes the **iterate** bookend E-11 left open: a **region-scoped observe→tweak→re-observe→accept-if-improved** loop over the compiled `DesignArtifact` (input-agnostic — facade or 3-D sculpture), driven first by deterministic scoped passes (the proven cage) then an LLM form-edit lever behind the same accept-gate, scored by a silhouette-IoU form metric. It attacks the **form** half (organic line-loss: the koi S-curve, the heart aortic loop) the way E-14 attacked color, and exposes a per-region **form-target interface** where a future GLB hint (E-09) plugs in unchanged. E-16 supplies that GLB: real TRELLIS image→3D meshes (made from our concept images) used three ways — as a **form target** that plugs into E-15's seam (does a 3-D target unlock the loop the flat concept couldn't?), as a **geometry source** voxelized into a `DesignArtifact` (is the voxelizer's form better than text→JSON?), and — the synthesis — a **GLB-voxel build refined by surgical region tweaks against the GLB itself**. It is the overnight chain that closes the image→3D arc and tests "GLB voxel set + surgical regions" as the capable configuration, head-to-head vs text→JSON. E-17 brings the whole arc together: an **ablation sweep** taking all 8 sculptural subjects up one canonical technique ladder (text→JSON → glb-voxel → +material-clean → +surgical), scored identically at every rung, so the **marginal contribution of each improvement** (value, form, material, polish) is finally legible in one scorecard + a per-subject march-of-progress. It also adds the one new lever the sweep needs — a **material-clean pass** that fixes E-16's per-voxel color speckle (form was solved by voxelization, material was not). E-19 is the **cleanup epic**: regain the text→JSON path's clean color/material on the GLB-voxel build by clearing the backlog of documented-but-unfixed issues (stray-voxel/component pruning — no SAM; a texture-variance-aware *flat* palette; PCA gradient banding; the `aug` secondary-palette wiring fix; per-subject thin-routing) on top of an honest cleanliness metric (a fixed speckle score + a stray-voxel count). E-18 closes the two gaps E-17 left: **speckle** (material-clean only narrowed it → proper **material-region segmentation**, region-fill instead of per-voxel snap) and **thin form** (bow-and-arrow voxelizes worst → **thin-feature-preserving voxelization**; sword can't get a GLB at all — TRELLIS hard-fails on the thin blade). Its boundary finding: the pipeline is **form-type-routed** — bulky/organic forms → image→3D (voxelize), thin/angular forms → text→JSON (which already serves sword well). E-20 is the payoff that **breaks the Phase-1 "facade only" limit**: text→JSON could only ever do a single face (it can't hand-place a whole building — it narrates and overflows), but the matured pipeline (TRELLIS bulk placement + E-19 clean materials + E-15 surgical regional refinement) makes a **full-fledged 3-D building** feasible. It produces a flagship building at higher resolution (larger, more blocks), refined to a **Strong+** standard, rendered in the round, shown against the old facades — the capability leap, demonstrated and measured. Runs after E-19. E-21 fixes a material failure the building exposed: mean-color CIE-Lab matching **collapses near-tone-but-distinct materials** (stone brick vs cobblestone — same average color, a deliberate concept distinction), and colorimetry provably can't separate them. The fix is **concept-grounded, LLM-defined materials**: a multimodal LLM reads the concept → a material map (`role → block`, incl. near-tone pairs), assigned to the voxels by **geometric feature** (corners → cobble, walls → brick — no SAM), then **refined against the concept**. It runs after E-19 and before E-20's coloring step, so the full building gets intended material zoning, not a merged grey. (SAM-3D for pixel-accurate region textures stays deferred.) E-22 fixes the **lens, then the gate**. The scale-64 gatehouse scored clean on every block metric (`speckle 0.001`, `distinct 4`, `offPalette 0`) yet rendered as grey static — diagnosed as **texture minification aliasing**: at high voxel density each 16px block texture is squeezed to ~6px and the viewer point-samples it (`NearestFilter`, no mipmaps) with no antialiasing, so a *clean build photographs as noise*. The deeper problem is that the gate was **gameable by accident** — an agent satisfying the metrics did its job, but the metrics were the wrong target. So E-22 (a) makes the render a **faithful photograph** (supersample + mipmaps, no shrinking the build to dodge aliasing), and (b) replaces green-metric sign-off with a **reference-anchored resemblance gate**: the build's render placed beside the **concept image** and the **GLB mesh render** at one shared view (a saved triptych), scored perceptually *and* judged categorically ("same object / drifted / different"), with the references immutable. Its **Rules of engagement** are binding anti-cheat constraints — the verdict is the triptych not a number, the resolution floor holds, every claim cites a fresh render, the gate can't be weakened to pass, and an honest residual gap beats a faked pass — so agents can neither game a metric nor quietly defer the goal. Runs after the building chain so it photographs the matured builds (though the lens fix benefits every upstream verdict). E-23 opens the **2.5-D interaction sector** — the realization that the LLM is bad at reasoning over a 57k-voxel cloud (the surgical loop overflowed context; the cottage's plaster face collapsed because 3-D feature space has no storey axis) and good at looking at a *view*. So it hands the model a view matched to the task instead of the voxels, with **two paths off one feature zone**: **programs where they help** (the LLM authors/steers a generator — hollowing the mass, an N×M floorplan filling N storeys) and **LLM judgement where they can't** (spray-painting materials on a projected wall with the enforced palette — the "4 cans" — to restore the half-timbered face). It reads from any angle (ortho + 45° + full 3-axis) but paints back only on the well-conditioned projections, and a **structural read** (footprint / storeys / openings) is the shared primitive both paths stand on. It is also the project's first deliberate step across **craft → design**: the interior has no reference, so the floorplan is *invented* and judged by a **plausibility gate** (valid reachable rooms, aligned openings) rather than the resemblance gate. And it is where multi-model **agentic engineering** begins — narrow view-scoped detectors (roof-patch, hollowable-mass) run on a **lighter model tier** via the subscription shim. The milestone — **a hollow, accurate-looking cottage with an N×M grid infill** — is strong enough to force both paths and both gates at once, and the epic is held to the **failure modes witnessed this session**: a **watertight roof** (fully wood, no seams/openings/strays), a **coherent wall skin** (no random intrusions or holes) over a **watertight shell** that is nonetheless **hollow enough for interiors** (S-084 seals before S-080 carves), and material placed by **splatting a same-angle textured-GLB / concept render** grid-quantized onto the face (reusing the facade `image-grid` technique). The front-face POC already confirmed the core mechanism on real cottage voxels — project → paint a band → back-project landed clean, reversing the 215→8 plaster collapse. E-24 closes the gap E-23 exposed between *can* and *durably does*: skinning the cottage proved the concept-color **splat cannot establish a zone's dominant material** (it converted only 9% of the upper-storey wall to plaster; a structural **zone-fill** took it to 77% — but only when applied inline by hand, which `npm run` doesn't reproduce). So E-24's thesis is **zone-fill the dominant, splat/judge the details — durably, value-true, coverage-gated**: a deterministic base coat of each zone's dominant material (base=stone, upper=plaster, roof=wood) with the splat/LLM demoted to secondaries (timber, trim); blocks chosen **value-true** in CIE-Lab (cream, not pink `white_terracotta`); surfaces made **coherent** (clean roof courses, no stray salt); and a **coverage-aware gate** that *rejects* an under-applied skin so a marginal number can never rubber-stamp a 91%-un-skinned wall again. Its binding rule is **durability** — every result is produced by a named `npm run` end-to-end and reproduces on re-run; an artifact that exists only because an agent hand-edited it is not a result. The milestone re-skins the cottage + gatehouse end-to-end, reproducibly, value-true, past the un-foolable gate. E-25 is the promise the whole building chain has been building toward: a **working concept→model pipeline** whose output a human can put next to the concept art and recognize, **from any angle**. A full audit (2026-06-10) found that every *persisting* defect is a surface or view the pipeline never enumerated — the roof was 96.7% wood from the sky but only 42.7% on its exposed surface (grey side-faces at every oblique angle), the cottage carried 23 connected components of floating debris, the gatehouse had a shell void its concept doesn't show — or a parameter a *prior* hard-coded instead of reading from the concept (the zone map assigns the whole ground storey to stone where the concept shows a half-timbered storey on a stone plinth). So E-25 (a) extends the zone-fill to **every exposed face of every zone** (roof sides included), (b) adds a **shell-integrity stage** (one component, voids repaired, closure verified from all six directions — not just the sky), (c) derives the **zone map from the concept** (band boundaries aligned to floor-lines, dominants read from the image; the prior demoted to fallback), and (d) widens the gate to a **multi-angle same-object verdict** — 4 fixed azimuths, per-view judgement, a contact sheet as the deliverable. Its stronger rules bind the goal to what a human can *see*: the verdict is the sheet (no number substitutes), concepts are immutable once registered, **no subject-specific hand-tuning** (subjects enter as registry entries only), no dropped angles. The roster is refreshed — moai retired (its three-statues-on-black concept made every verdict ambiguous) and a **challenge subject added** (a village church with a square bell tower, behind a concept sanity checklist) — and the terminal milestone is the generalization proof: cottage, gatehouse, *and the never-tuned-on church* each produced by one `npm run` end-to-end, all passing the multi-angle gate, reproducibly. E-26 supplies the **missing construction ingredient E-25's skin work exposed**: the concept's *style kit*. The cottage concept visibly depicts smooth sandstone panels, stripped logs, planks, trapdoor shutters, and fence-infilled windows — instantly identifiable to a viewer — yet none reach the build, because (five whys, 2026-06-10) the E-21 map contract asks *which block matches this color?* never *which block is this?* (its own rationales prove the LLM saw the materials and was forced to guess `white_terracotta` for sandstone), the pipeline's per-voxel-color spine gives fixtures no identity to survive into (the schema and renderer already support block states end-to-end — nothing ever emits them), and the image-shaped gate names "palette" gaps, never "missing shutters," so no epic was ever asked to supply details. Root cause: **the kit of depicted ingredients has no representation in the pipeline contract** — construction can't place what was never extracted; the gate can't demand what was never named. E-26's inversion is **recognize, don't match**: a multimodal LLM enumerates the concept's bill of materials name-true (value-verified in CIE-Lab; color-snap demoted to a recorded fallback), a **placement grammar** binds each entry to structural feature instances (frame lines, fields between them, each opening), the **fixture path is proven** end-to-end (trapdoors/fences/stairs/slabs with states; closure treats a dressed opening as dressed, not a hole), every door/window is **dressed** per the kit, and a **kit-aware gate** runs beside the multi-angle gate so a missing ingredient is a named gap a coarse skin can't hide. Terminal milestone: the styled cottage — sandstone panels, stripped-log frame, shuttered fence windows — from one `npm run`, both gates passing, with gatehouse + church through the same untuned path. E-27 removes the last inherited surface. The E-25/E-26 milestones proved a pattern: every surface the pipeline **re-authors from geometry** (wall fields, frame lines, dressed openings) now passes its checks, and every surface **inherited raw from the decimated TRELLIS mesh** fails — the judges name `form @ roof` on every failing subject, and measurement shows why: the styled cottage carries 265 attached spike/fin cells (146 in the roof band — the fake criss-crossing rafters) and 21.7% ragged columns, **already present at the shell stage** (276 / 23.9%), entering at voxelization and then faithfully painted by the skin. So E-27's thesis is **the reference is the spec, not the substrate**: a **regularization cage** (morphological de-spiking under a no-regress harness — per-azimuth silhouette IoU vs the GLB, closure held, protected regions, auto-rollback — the E-15 cage finally given its 3-D target), a **component decomposition** (blob → named roof planes, wall slabs, masses, opening groups), **roof-as-program** (eave/pitch/overhang/ridge fitted least-squares to the GLB, fit error recorded, the roof *regenerated* as Minecraft-native **stair-course** construction), a general **shaped vocabulary** (stair runs, slab steps, **arches** — the gatehouse arch and church openings rebuilt as fitted constructs), and **component-aware skinning** — the E-24/E-26 skin/grammar/kit pipeline consuming component *definitions* instead of re-deriving structure from noisy occupancy (expected to unblock the church's band0 skin-gate refusal). The instrument stays frozen; the terminal milestone re-judges cottage + gatehouse + church on reconstructed forms with the unchanged gates — the claim under test being that clean parametric components move the verdicts, and that skinning succeeds when a build is defined parts rather than one big blob. E-28 finishes what E-27's verdicts now isolate. The reconstruction worked — spikes down ~5× on every subject, three azimuths judging same-object (from zero), the gatehouse arch gap major→minor — and the remaining FAILs are **named, specific form gaps**: the cottage's two failing views name `gable ends` and `eaves` (the slope fit passed; the *ends* of the roof were never generated), the gatehouse's three name `ridge line`, `upper roof edges`, and `chimney-like protrusions` the GLB doesn't show; and the church is blocked by a **measurement-identity bug** — its base band is 91.5% stone-family (`polished_basalt` 1225 + `stone` 681 of 2082) but the coverage gate censuses the literal name `stone` (0.327 < 0.5), the kit-blind-gate class for the third time, plus a whole-mass roof fit that can't satisfy its two roofs (tower + nave). So E-28: **gable-and-verge fit** (fitted gable-end triangles, rake lines, eave terminations), **ridge-and-silhouette fit** (fitted ridge cap courses, upper-edge terminations, refit-or-remove of protrusions absent from the GLB silhouette — cage-wrapped, chimney protected), and the **church unblock** (role-family census identity under the strict T-095/T-101 gate-seam discipline — monotone, both fractions reported — plus per-component roof fits), converging on a **closure milestone**: all three subjects re-judged once, instrument-diff committed, testing for the first full multi-angle passes — or honestly named residuals with the fit errors that explain them. E-29 concludes the arc the GLB integration opened: eleven epics of records say every surface **re-authored from fitted geometry passes** and every surface **inherited from the voxelized blob fails** — so the epic **inverts provisioning**: fit the components from concept + GLB, *generate* the build from parameters + kit, and use the blob only as the cage's fit target — **it never becomes the build** (zero blob cells, machine-checked). It folds in the three gaps E-28 named, each needed by both paths: the **hip/pyramid cap** generator (the church tower's fallback — "a pyramidal cap is not a ridge pair"), a single **material-vocabulary authority** (grammar and dressing disagreed about ~170 cells' block set, the construction-side twin of the thrice-fixed census-identity bug — one role→block-set contract, conformance-tested across every stage and gate), and **judge-reply robustness** (a malformed reply is not a verdict: bounded re-ask with every reply committed, REFUSAL only at budget exhaustion, verdict re-rolling still impossible — the church's 225° unparsed REFUSAL is the regression). The headline story runs generate-first **head-to-head** against the repair path on the legacy three, frozen instrument, receipts beside receipts; the terminal milestone registers a **fourth subject** (candidate: an L-plan coaching inn with a jettied upper storey) and takes it through generate-first **on its first run** — the claim under test being that what took the cottage eleven epics should now take one. Milestone targets are pinned from T-111-01's closure verdicts before scheduling. E-30 acts on E-29's sharpest finding: *first-run generalization is gated by the weakest input-prep lens, not the strongest generator* — the barn registered clean, ran the repair chain untuned to honest verdicts, and generate-first **never started** because the zone lens refused its concept (`no-field-cells`: the cobble field quantizes into stone_bricks at ΔL 2.082 — the E-21 near-tone collapse, long fixed in the material map, still living in the T-092 lens). So the headline is the **role-aware zone lens** (bands classified against the committed map's role palette, near-tones disambiguated as a binary Lab decision; the barn's null records flip; no downstream contract relaxed). Folded in, the rest of the E-28/E-29 ledger: the **roof-form seam** attacked analysis-first (a per-azimuth roof-region diff instrument — gable ends / ridge / slopes / eaves vs the GLB — names what reads wrong *before* any rung is built; then targeted refits for the cottage gable-end residual and the gatehouse ridge/315°-regression); **pin protection** (a read-only distillation mode that cannot spawn the judge by construction, guarded pin writes that refuse silent overwrites, and a recorded pin-rotation policy — the reverted reskin re-roll and the kit-sweep incident as fixtures); and **registration hardening** (a pre-spend lens smoke so a lens-refusable concept is caught before the TRELLIS budget, plus a declared sub-speck tolerance in glb-smoke). The terminal milestone runs the stalled proof — `generated:barn` end-to-end against the already-pinned bar (kit PASS, ≥2/4 same-object, ≤10/2) — and re-judges the legacy three once after the roof refits, pins rotated under the new policy.
