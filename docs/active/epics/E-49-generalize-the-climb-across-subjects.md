## Background (epic stub — shape it from E-48's ceiling)

**Milestone rung: M3 approach — "a street that belongs together" begins with *more than one* build that
climbs.** E-48 proves the picture-driven climb on **one** subject (the gatehouse). "Better *generations*"
(plural) lives here: the same loop, unattended, across **several** subjects and styles.

This is deliberately a **stub** — its stories are not pre-written, because E-48's **ceiling report** (S-190:
where the gatehouse climb plateaus, what hands it still lacks, how much human it needed) decides what
generalization actually requires. Drafting tickets now would pre-commit to a shape we don't yet know
([[diverge-before-converge-experiment-freedom]]). Scope it after E-48 lands.

## The known generalization gates (candidates, not a plan)

From the arc's standing findings — to be confirmed/reprioritized by E-48's ceiling:

- **Multi-mass construction.** The cottage is two perpendicular masses; the loop's constructor is single-box
  ([[cottage-gate-and-volume-gate-are-one-fix]]). A street needs L-plans, wings, multi-ridge roofs.
- **Kill the hardcoded `SUBJECTS` map.** Drive purely from recognised programs so new subjects enter as
  registry entries, not code (the standing "price of admission — volume & longevity" item).
- **Unattended longevity.** E-48 measures *how much human* the one-subject climb needs; generalization
  requires it to run across many subjects with little-to-none — the M4 Commissioned Village honesty.
- **Per-subject hands.** E-48's ceiling names hands the gatehouse lacked; other subjects/styles will expose
  others (steep pitches, dormers, jetties — the brush library compounds).

## How this epic can fail (provisional)

- **The one-subject climb doesn't transfer** — the gatehouse hands are subject-specific and each new subject
  needs bespoke levers (no compounding). Then the brush/recognition abstraction is the real sub-problem.
- **Multi-mass is a wall, not a lever** — the single-box constructor can't be generalized incrementally and
  needs a rebuild. E-48's ceiling will indicate whether this is in the path.

## Done when

To be defined from E-48's ceiling — provisionally: the picture-driven climb runs unattended across ≥3
subjects of ≥2 styles, each measurably approaching its picture on the glance, with the per-subject human
intervention quantified and the brush/recognition reuse (compounding) measured.
