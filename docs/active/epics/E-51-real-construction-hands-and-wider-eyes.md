## Background (read this first — the plateau named the gap, and it's the same gap)

**Milestone rung: M1 finish line, for real — "the gatehouse reaches *its* picture, not just a recognizable
one."** E-48 proved the loop climbs; E-50 proved the accept-gate can follow the glance (the department-dominant
override kept the glance-correct grey roof through a live scalar regression). But the E-50 completion run
**plateaued**: roof reached its picture (form + colour), **walls stayed dark, no arched gate, scale off.**

The plateau named the gap, and it is one gap wearing three hats: **the loop's hands are mostly *recolor* and
*additive stamps*, not *construction*; and its eyes don't see *orientation* or *scale*.**
- **Walls:** `articulate_walls` recolors a flat plane — it cannot build the **relief** (proud quoins/clinker)
  the concept shows, so the rollback was glance-defensible (a recolor genuinely isn't pale *dressed* stone).
- **Arch:** a real wide arched gate needs the aperture **carved/widened** — forbidden by the blanket no-air-op
  rule, so the loop literally could not build it.
- **Eyes:** the reviewer's glance caught roof-orientation (90° vs the gate) and scale; the critique flagged
  neither — and the climb cannot stall on what it cannot see.

This is the **recolor-vs-construction** theme ([[facade-grammar-recolor-vs-construction]]) resurfacing at the
climb level: the *machinery* works (autonomous climb + a gate that follows the glance); what's missing is
**construction-grade hands and wider eyes.** Governed by `docs/knowledge/project-direction.md` +
`docs/knowledge/anti-hedge-directive.md`; frozen instrument untouched.

## The charter change (reviewer decision, 2026-06-17): let the loop carve openings

The blanket **no-air-op / recess-by-exclusion** rule ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]) has protected closure
since the mesh-spike era — but it also makes real arches and windows unbuildable. The reviewer's call: **let
the loop carve openings.** Scoped as a **narrowing, not an abolition** (the way E-36 narrowed the freeze):

- **Carving is allowed ONLY to form a *declared* opening** (a door/gate/window in the recognition program) —
  not to bury blocks, not to carve arbitrary holes. Everything that isn't a declared aperture is still
  no-air-op.
- **An aperture-coherence gate replaces the blanket ban for openings:** a carved opening must be a *coherent,
  dressed* aperture (continuous head/jambs/sill, framed, no ragged edges or stray voids), and **closure must
  hold on every surface except the declared aperture itself** (which is intentionally open). Precedent: the
  generate-first opening-coherence gate ([[generate-first-wall-watertightness]]).
- **The risk this guards (anti-hedge):** carving reopens the holes/spikes the whole project fought
  ([[reference-is-spec-not-substrate]]) — ragged voids, broken closure, the colonnade. The coherence gate is
  what makes the relaxation safe; if it can't keep carved openings clean, the charter change is refuted and
  openings go back to recess-only (wide arches then stay an explicit known limit).

## Task-ordering principle (reviewer, 2026-06-17 — applies to the climb AND to ticket scoping)

**Form before detail.** Carving a doorway, adding relief, banding eaves — these are *detail* operations that
only read on a *ready form* (a closed, dense shell). The climb must sequence coarse→fine, not do everything at
once up front; detail tools are gated on form-readiness (S-197). And **tickets stay one-stage** — the reviewer
flagged T-196 bundling eyes + re-climb as "one ticket stuck running many stages"; the re-climb is now gated
behind the form stage so it runs in order, and new work is scoped one focused stage per ticket.

## How this epic can fail (state it up front — anti-hedge)

- **Carving reopens holes.** The aperture-coherence gate can't distinguish an intentional dressed opening from
  a ragged void — closure regresses, the spike/colonnade era returns. Then the charter relaxation is refuted
  (openings revert to recess-only) — a real, valuable result that bounds the rule.
- **The relief hand still doesn't read.** Proud quoins/clinker get built but the glance still says flat (the
  amplitude/coverage is wrong, or the critique still can't see relief) — back to the E-43/E-35 relief lessons.
- **Wider eyes mis-fire.** An orientation/scale-aware critique flags false positives (calls a correct roof
  rotated, or a right-sized build too big) — the new coverage is noise, not signal; report and re-localize.
- **The hands work but the climb still plateaus** — a *fourth* gap appears (the gap list isn't closed). Then
  name it; M1 on this subject is genuinely harder than three hands.
- **Scope creep past M1.** This epic closes the *gatehouse*; resist generalizing here (that's E-49). One
  subject to its picture first.

## Done when

The loop can carve a declared opening into a coherent dressed aperture (wide arched gate reads, closure held
everywhere else) and build real wall relief (pale dressed stone reads); the critique sees orientation + scale;
and a re-run gatehouse climb reaches *its* picture on the glance — pale dressed walls, arched gate, dark
gabled roof, right orientation/scale — mostly autonomously. Or a failure above is reported at full strength
(carving refuted → openings recess-only; a fourth gap named). Frozen instrument untouched.
