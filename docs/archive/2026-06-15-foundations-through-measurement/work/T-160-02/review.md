## Verdict on the falsifiable claim (lead with it)

> *Driving the existing articulation passes from the pack's declared wall roles — applied as relief over
> the T-160-01 envelope, replacing the patch path — will make the wall read as **construction** and climb
> (or hold) where the flat recolor plateaued, **without drowning the build**.*

**PARTLY CONFIRMED, and the failure modes the ticket pre-named both landed — measured, not hidden.**

1. **"Reads as construction" — CONFIRMED in the render (the ticket's named judge).** The cottage, monotone
   brown after T-160-01, now shows a **stone base + lighter plaster upper + dark corner quoins** —
   eye-confirmed in `cottage-skin-beside.png`. The barn shows a stone base course + corner quoins under
   the roof. The skin is real construction, not a recolor, **where the envelope closes**.
2. **"Without drowning" — CONFIRMED.** Bands stay multi-material by construction; limewash was tuned to a
   **0.5 accent coat** (not the full-face whitewash a `coverage:1` first cut produced). The witness band
   balances cobblestone 73% / limewash 14% / quoins+plinth 12%. WS11 asserts no single block > 80%.
3. **"Climb (or hold) on the eval" — DID NOT climb on the target; the eval does not reward the wall skin.**
   This is **finding (a)**: the eval is **blind to wall relief when a bigger cap dominates**. Cottage held
   far better than T-160-01 (**−2 vs −7**) but stayed capped by **roof/massing/overhang**; the visible
   per-storey + quoin skin moved the score not at all. → **routes to S-161** (the render is the judge here,
   exactly as the ticket said for relief). 
4. **The witness regressed (−37) — that is the SPARSE-SHELL ENVELOPE gap, NOT the skin** (finding (c)-
   adjacent). `barn--saltcrag-skin-beside.png` shows a **gappy colonnade of detached posts**: construct_walls
   rebuilt the workshop build's **22%-filled** shell into a colonnade (T-160-01's named limitation), and a
   skin on see-through columns is wasted. The skin did **not** flood (not finding (b)); the envelope upstream
   of it never closed. The absolute-footprint registration T-160-01 named is the real unlock for sparse shells.

   worst-defect axis when a build has two near-equal caps; a render-anchored tie-break is a measurement
