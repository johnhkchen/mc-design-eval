## Open concerns / known limitations (Rule 5)

1. **Resemblance went sideways, honestly.** Triptych judge: both subjects `drifted` (cottage gap
   material-zoning@roof; gatehouse form@walls/roofline); cottage front evidence 0.35 → 0.30 (the
   sandstone band is *further* from the concept under the per-face metric; renamed blocks also depress
   the named-set score — set 0.5). E-24 delivers coverage/value-truth/coherence/reproducibility; the
   resemblance ceiling is E-25/E-26 form-and-zoning work. The numbers are recorded everywhere, not
   smoothed.
2. **`polished_basalt` is a value win with a texture cost** (vertical striping vs coursed brick) — the
   same identity-vs-color tension the T-086 review named for tuff. If a human prefers identity, the
   switch policy's margin/floor constants are single-sourced in `src/color/value-select.mjs`.
3. **Per-face acceptance is coverage-only (deterministic)**; the resemblance delta is evidence, not a
   gate input — required for the double-run hash to be meaningful (GL output must not steer the
   artifact). This *narrows* T-088's `acceptWithCoverage` composition to its GL-blind branch; if a future
   ticket wants the delta to gate again, it must give up byte-reproducibility or pin GL.
4. **The salt strip judges the projection skin while fill + census moved to the exposure shell** (T-087
   instrument lags T-090) — an obliquely-visible speck can survive. Bounded (the base coat already filled
   the shell), but the strip should adopt `skin:"exposure"` in a follow-up.
5. **Duplicated policy constant:** the cottage `ZONE_POLICY` now lives in spray-paint.mjs AND the
   durable-skin registry (deliberately, to keep off T-090's file mid-flight). Once T-090 settles, a
   shared policy module is the missing-DAG-edge fix the T-087 review already requested for zone-fill.
6. **Thin-sample roles keep unvalidated priors** (gatehouse cobble 3 cells / door planks 11; cottage
   chimney bricks) — absence of a value judgement, not a pass.
7. **The gatehouse "E-23 baseline" is a counterfactual replay** (no splat-only skin ever shipped for it);
   the record labels it as such.
8. **Concurrent-ticket hygiene:** T-090's regenerated spray-paint records were uncommitted in the
   worktree the whole session; every commit here was path-scoped around them. They remain for T-090 to
   commit.
