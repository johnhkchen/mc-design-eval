An English yeoman farmstead family on pastured lowland. They clear fieldstone from their own
pasture, cut dressed stone at a small local quarry, hew heavy structural timber from a managed
oak coppice, and saw lighter boards at their own sawpit; lime is burned for rendering the upper
walls. The household is comfortable but careful — worked material is spent only where structure
or weather demands it, a small batch of kiln-fired material is reserved for what must survive
the hearth, there is no slate, and no window glass larger than a small lattice pane. The cottage
and the tithe barn are both buildings of this one place and draw on the same materials.
