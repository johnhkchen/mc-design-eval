## The defect, visible in the committed records

The cottage recognition program (`benchmarks/sculpture/recognition/cottage.program.json`) says it
out loud: *"The sketch read 4 storeys (~4.8 blocks) and a 27-block height — mesh noise; the concept
plainly reads two storeys, so I substitute a regular 2-storey shell at storeyHeight 4."* The sketch
measured an eave of **19.3 blocks**; the committed cottage seed builds an 8-block eave
(`workshop/cottage/program.json`: shells height 8, roofs eave 8 / ridge 17). The barn is closer
(seed eave 9 / ridge 21 vs sketch eave 10 / ridge 21, d 24 vs 26) — its squatness is pitch-class
quantization (rustic has exactly one class) plus the eave/footprint nibbles. The VLM eyeballs and
overrides; the pack quantizes; the measured source is consumed only as prompt context
(`src/recognition/prompt.mjs` `sketchDigest()` — lines 37–60, prose digest into the BAML prompt).

## The measured source: form-sketch/v1 (T-123)

- Generated by `src/form/form-sketch.mjs` (`buildSketch`), runner `benchmarks/sculpture/form-sketch.mjs`,
  committed at `benchmarks/sculpture/form-sketch/<key>.json` (+ `.md`, `-sheet.png`). Subjects:
  barn, cottage, church, gatehouse.
- **Units are mixed and must be converted.** `params.sampleScale` (48) is the voxel-cell scale;
  `params.registryScale` is the subject's working block scale (`durable-skin.mjs` SUBJECTS
  `generated.scale`: cottage **32**, barn **48**). Fields named `*Blocks` are already in registry
  blocks (`eaveBlocks = (eaveLayer+1) × registryScale/sampleScale`); everything else
  (`footprint.planDims`, `polygon`, mass `bbox`, layers, cells) is in **cells at sampleScale**.
- Relevant measurements:
  - `proportions.eaveBlocks` / `heightBlocks` (fractional allowed: cottage 19.3 / 27.3; barn 10 / 21).
  - `proportions.storeyCandidates`: `[{n, perStoreyBlocks, plausible}]` (cottage: only n=4 @4.8
    plausible; barn: n=2 @5 and n=3 @3.3 plausible).
  - `pitch`: `{class, dominantTiltDeg, riseCells, runCells, ridgeAxis, eaveLayer, ridgeLayer}`
    (barn 45°; cottage 35.54°). Median-smoothed top profiles per T-123 (TRELLIS facet normals lie).
  - `footprint`: `{polygon, isRectangle, planDims}` — barn [48,26] (rect), cottage [40,48] (L/cross).
  - `proportions.masses[]`: `{id, role: "primary"|"protrusion", bbox{minX..maxZ}, yRange, pitch?}`.
    Both barn and cottage have `massCount: 1` — exactly one primary; protrusions are mesh noise.

## The parameter surface: building-program/v1 (T-125)

`schema/building-program.schema.json`, gates in `src/recognition/program.mjs`. Strict AJV
(`additionalProperties: false` at every level — any new field requires a schema edit). The
dimensional parameters per mass:

- `rect {x0,z0 ∈ [-64,64], w,d ∈ [3,64]}` — footprint extents (registry blocks).
- `storeys ∈ [1,4]`, `storeyHeight ∈ [2,6]` — eave height is **derived**: `eaveY = storeys×storeyHeight`.
- `roof.pitchClass` — a rise/run ratio, gated to `pack.proportions.pitchClasses`
  (rustic `[1]`; saltcrag `[2, 1, 0.5]`). `roof.ridgeAxis` is the qualitative orientation.
- **Ridge height does not exist in the schema** — derived in compile:
  `ridgeY = eaveY + max(1, round(pitch × floor((perpSpan−1)/2)))`, perpSpan from the eave-expanded
  footprint (`compile.mjs:199–226`).

Gates: `parseBuildingProgram`/`assertBuildingProgram` (schema), `validateProgramAgainstPack`
(`program.mjs:155`) — pack roles, roof idiom ∈ ROOF_LAYOUTS, **pitchClass ∈ pack classes** (check 3),
**storeyHeight ∈ pack band [3,4]** (check 4), opening-lane feasibility per wall at min spacing
(check 6 — shrinking a rect can make a lane infeasible), dormer fit (7), mass connectivity via
`rectsTouch` (8). These are the **model-reply** gates (bounded re-ask loop, T-114 pattern).

## The seams and their ratified constraints

- `compileProgram(program, pack)` (`src/recognition/compile.mjs`) is **ratified PURE of the sketch**
  (header: "consumes exactly (program, pack); never the sketch... the committed program is a CLOSED
  replay input"). Quantity must therefore enter *the program*, not the compiler.
- `seedWorkshopProgram({program, pack, budget})` (`src/workshop/seed.mjs:102`) — compile → budget
  override (PATTERN_BOOK_BUDGET 6) → `assertWorkshopProgram` → `realizeProgram` → `runConformance`.
  Deterministic; byte-identical `serialized` for same inputs.
- `recognitionRels(key, packRel)` / `chainRels(key, packRel)` (`seed.mjs:54–81`) — the ONE place
  record paths derive; pack-namespaced (`packNs`: default rustic = bare key).
- Pattern-book chain (`benchmarks/sculpture/pattern-book.mjs`): stage 1 sketch sha-receipt
  (`verifySketch`), stage 2 re-parses the committed program through the live gates and
  **byte-compares** the recompiled draft to `recognition/<key>.artifact.json`, stage 3 seeds and
  **byte-compares** to `workshop/<runKey>/program.json` in `--repro`. Any change to seed derivation
  on the existing paths breaks the committed pins. T-119 pin-guard (`preflightPins`,
  `guardedWriteRecord`, `--rotate-pins`) guards every record write, preflighted before spend.
- Generalization: subjects come from the durable-skin SUBJECTS registry filtered by
  `def.glb && def.generated?.scale` (the recognize predicate); E-25 Rule 3 self-grep pins that no
  subject key appears in a runner source.
- Renders: `renderViews(artifact, MULTI_ANGLE_GATE.azimuths)` (`src/view/multi-angle.mjs`), used by
  `recognize.mjs` `renderEvidence()` — sha-receipted **evidence, never gating**; render absence is
  recorded, never fatal; GL bytes are excluded from reproducibility decisions (E-24/E-28 precedent).

## Constraints & assumptions surfaced

1. **Schema ceilings cap expressible eave**: storeys ≤ 4 × storeyHeight ≤ 6 → max 24 blocks
   (covers cottage's 19.3). Pack band [3,4] is narrower — S-133 explicitly demotes pack proportions
   to "the fallback tier", but `validateProgramAgainstPack` check 4 still hard-errors outside the
   band; any measured program carrying storeyHeight 5 cannot pass that gate unmodified.
2. **Pitch is quantized by pack vocabulary** (check 3). Rustic offers only class 1 (45°): measured
   35.54° (cottage) and 45° (barn) both snap to 1 — the *pitch* render delta for rustic subjects is
   nil until S-134 widens the vocabulary; massing (eave/footprint) is where T-133's visible change is.
3. **Prior pins must remain valid** (AC 4): re-seeding cannot overwrite `recognition/*.program.json`
   (stage-2 byte-compare) or `workshop/<key>/program.json` (stage-3 byte-compare) — new programs
   must be new records at new paths.
4. **Sketch mass naming is coarse**: both subjects have exactly one primary mass; the cottage's
   2-mass program decomposition has no per-mass sketch counterpart. "Per mass where the sketch
   names masses" must degrade to whole-building measurements when massCount is 1.
5. The seam consumes only committed inputs (sketch + recognition program + pack) — **a fully
   model-free, GL-free-except-evidence path**; replay can be exact.
6. No judge runs (T-138 owns verdicts), no per-building constants, `npm test` green.
