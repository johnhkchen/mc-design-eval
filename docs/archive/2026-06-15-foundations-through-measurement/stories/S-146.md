## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-35**, the crux. Skinning today is **recolor-on-fixed-geometry** over a voxelized smooth box:
`src/view/face-paint.mjs` is explicit — "PAINT IS A RECOLOR, NOT A MOVE … There is NO air op"; and
`src/view/zone-fill.mjs` assigns one dominant block per structural zone. Recoloring a box can never
make a pilaster proud of its infill or an eave overhang the wall. **Relief must be construction.** The
one shipping relief idiom proves the mechanism works: `src/view/clinker.mjs` emits courses that "sit
PROUD of the wall plane … in front of EXISTING zone wall cells … no board can protrude past the rake
line — by construction"; `jetty` projects past the wall line the same way. Recessed relief is done by
**exclusion** ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]): build the pilasters proud and leave the field at the
base plane, so the field reads recessed — no air op needed.

## Goal

A shared, tested **surface-relief op** (through the registry door) that emits proud cells in front of
existing shell cells and recesses fields by exclusion, generalised from `clinker`/`jetty` — parametrised
by face, row/column rhythm, depth, material. PURE (no GL/IO/Date/random), byte-stable placement order,
idempotent on its own output (the clinker charter). **In-plane silhouette preserved by construction** —
proven under a no-regress harness against the E-34 proportion ruler (`maskProportions`) and the
silhouette gate: relief lives in front of the plane, never past the rake, so neither the ruler nor the
silhouette verdict can regress. The 2.5-D layer gains a *read* of relief (the per-cell depth that
`surface-grid.mjs` already records is finally consumed), never a paint-time air op.

## Builds on

- E-34 ruler + silhouette gate (the no-regress targets); `clinker`/`jetty` (the precedent).
