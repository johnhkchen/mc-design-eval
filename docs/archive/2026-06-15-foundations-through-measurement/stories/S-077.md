## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-22**, terminal. S-075 fixed the render lens (the scale-64 static was minification aliasing, not
the build); S-076 built the resemblance gate (triptych concept | mesh | minecraft + perceptual score +
categorical judge). This story applies both to the headline builds and reports honestly.

**Why this matters:** builds judged before E-22 were photographed through the aliasing lens, so their
*visual* verdicts are suspect. Re-photographing through the fixed lens may flip some — better (static
clears) or worse (real material drift that the noise was hiding). Both are honest results (Rule 7).

## Goal

Re-photograph and re-judge the headline builds through the fixed lens + the resemblance gate, and report
which read as the **same object** as their references and where the residual gap is — no green-metric
stand-ins (Rule 2), no shrinking builds to pass (Rule 3), every claim citing a fresh render (Rule 4).

## Acceptance / scope (carried into T-077-01)

- The fixed render + resemblance gate applied to **gatehouse + cottage + 2 sculptures**; triptychs saved.
- A per-subject verdict: `same object | drifted | different object` + the **named residual gap** (region +
  attribute) for anything short of "same object" (Rule 7 — quantify, don't hide or defer).
- A **before/after** for the gatehouse: the scale-64 build photographed through the **old** lens (static)
  vs the **fixed** lens (clean) — *same artifact* — proving the static was the lens, not the build.
- Any gap the gate attributes to **material drift** (not the lens) is recorded as a **finding routed back
  to E-21**, not fixed here (E-22 photographs and judges; it does not edit materials).
