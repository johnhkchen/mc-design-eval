## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-24**. Even with the zone-fill (S-085), the cottage **roof reads chunky/speckled** and walls carry
**stray-material salt** (a lone cobble in a plaster field). S-084 made the roof **watertight** (100%
coverage), but coverage ≠ a clean *pattern*: the plank courses are noisy and stray blocks break a zone's
material field.

## Goal

Raise **surface-pattern** quality (beyond S-084's coverage):
1. **Clean roof courses** — the roof reads as regular **stepped plank courses** (the concept's banding), not
   a chunky jumble; smooth the course pattern within the watertight roof.
2. **Strip stray-material salt within a zone's field** — a single off-dominant block surrounded by a zone's
   dominant material (a cobble island in a plaster wall) is re-assigned to the field, unless it's a
   legitimate secondary (a timber stud, which forms a run, not an isolated speck).

Distinct from S-084 (which seals holes/strays for *watertightness*); this is about the **read of the
surface pattern** once the zone-fill base coat is down.

## Builds on

- S-085 (the zone-filled base coat to clean), E-23 `roofRegion` / `structuralZones`, S-084 surface-coherence
  ops (the seal/strip machinery to extend).
