## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-30**. The barn exposed two registration gaps (T-116 concern 2): (1) the S-094 concept sanity
checklist gates **geometry** (single building, bulky, clean background, ≥3 zones) but not **lens
readability** — the barn passed the checklist, spent the TRELLIS/GLB budget, and was *then* refused by
the zone lens (`no-field-cells`); the refusal would have been visible from the concept alone. (2)
glb-smoke's strict single-component gate **over-fires on sub-speck debris** — the barn's GLB failed it
at every scale on **one floating mesh cell** (largest-component fraction ≥0.9813 throughout); the
ticket had to accept a named deviation with a conditioned-evidence probe instead.

## Goal

Registration that cannot waste a spend: (a) the checklist gains a **pre-spend lens smoke** — the
(fixed, S-117) zone lens + kit-extract dry-run against the concept **before** TRELLIS; a refusal is
cheap, named, and blocks registration (regenerate or pick another concept *before* any GLB exists —
concepts are immutable only **after** registration, so this is checklist-compatible). (b) glb-smoke
gains a **declared sub-speck tolerance**: components below a declared cell-fraction budget are
reported and stripped by the standing shell stage rather than failing the gate; the strict
single-component check holds above the tolerance. The barn's two deviations become regression
fixtures: its concept passes the new smoke (post-S-117), its GLB passes the tolerant gate.

## Builds on

- S-094 checklist (T-094 — the discipline this extends); S-117 (the smoke must run the fixed lens —
  hence the dependency); the barn's `concept-checklist.md` + glb-smoke records (the fixtures);
  shellStage component strip (the existing remediation the tolerance leans on).
