## Goal

Epic **E-10**. Map a concept image to a **real-block grid at a fixed resolution**: sample the image to an
N-wide grid and match each cell to the palette (the canonical palette from S-021, or the design doc's
declared one) → a 2-D facade in real blocks. This demonstrates the two fixes concretely — every cell is a
real, obtainable block (no imaginary blocks) and detail is capped at the grid resolution. Then check
**palette adherence** (§9) and compare the **extracted vs declared** palette (do they agree?).

## Builds on

- **S-021** (palette extraction) + S-020 engine + S-019 table; the existing render harness (E-02) to
  visualize the grid; the palette validator (§9).
