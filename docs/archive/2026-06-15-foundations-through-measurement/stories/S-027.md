## Goal

Epic **E-11**, seed pass A — **fake shading through materials**. Over the locked massing, assign each
surface a same-hue block *set* (2–3 IDs) mixed for texture noise, and **vary the block choice across
height** so light breaks differently top to bottom. This is the "mix stone/cobble/andesite for depth and
age" move, and an upgrade of E-10's single-block match to a hue-family set. It writes `material` on the
build state and locks it.

## Builds on

- The locked massing (S-025); the E-10 color engine + block→Lab table (nearest *set*, not just nearest
  one); the spine's stage interface + locks.

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-027-01` — material-noise pass (hue-family set per surface, vary by height).
