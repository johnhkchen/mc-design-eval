## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-34**. Three vocabulary/calibration gaps the E-33 milestone measured (T-138-02 review,
concern 6 + finding 3):

1. **Unnamed lens.** Program-level ratios (`measured-program`'s silhouetteRatios, computed from
   program parameters) and occupancy-level ratios (`maskProportions` over realized voxels) can
   disagree by 3× — the cottage proved it — and no record names which lens produced its numbers.
2. **Uncalibrated tolerance.** `PROPORTION_DEFAULTS.tolerance = 0.15` was declared, never derived;
   E-33 left three subjects' committed before/after data to calibrate from.
3. **Flattened pitch targets.** Pitch read from the conditioned sketch inherits TRELLIS's
   stair-step flattening (both E-33 sketches measured ≤45°, so the T-134 steep door went unused
   even where the concept reads steeper). The E-33 honesty rule already says the concept is the
   contract and the sketch the fallback — pitch targets must follow it.

## Goal

**Every proportion number carries its lens, its tolerance carries its calibration evidence, and
pitch targets cite their source with concept precedence.** Workshop-side only (creation is free);
calibration is cross-subject from committed E-33 records, never per-building; the frozen judge
contract unmoved.
