## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-34**. The aggregate gap budget (`MULTI_ANGLE_GATE.gapBudget: 2`,
`src/form/multi-angle-gate.mjs`) was declared before any build survived to be judged on gaps and
has never been calibrated. Three epics of committed verdicts now show it failing the very builds
the glance accepts: barn T-127, saltcrag T-132, both T-138-01 barns — all decided **4/4
same-object with all-minor gaps** (the glance passes) yet recorded budget-FAIL at 8/2; meanwhile
the T-138-02 cottage (2/4 same-object, 7 minor + 4 major — the glance fails) is
indistinguishable in the PASS/FAIL column. **The reviewer decided on 2026-06-12: the budget must
be rationalized to track the desired look** — the glance is the bar (`docs/knowledge/milestones.md`:
"if a build passes the gate but fails the glance, the glance wins" — the budget currently inverts
this). The judge contract itself (prompts, azimuths, gap grain) is untouched: only the verdict
aggregation over already-returned gap lists changes, so committed gap lists stay comparable and
no verdict is ever re-judged.

## Goal

**A severity-aware, identity-first budget policy (v2), derived from the committed verdicts and
frozen after calibration**: every committed glance-passing verdict class passes v2, the
glance-failing class fails it; both arithmetics (legacy ≤2 beside v2) reported in every record
going forward; the calibration evidence is a re-derivation sweep over committed gap lists — never
a re-judge.
