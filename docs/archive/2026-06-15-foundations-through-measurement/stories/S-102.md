## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-27**. The voxelized TRELLIS mesh arrives ragged: the styled cottage's shell-stage artifact
carries **276 cells with ≥4 of 6 faces exposed** (attached spikes/fins — the fake "rafters" in every
render) and **23.9% of columns have a ≥3-block height cliff** vs a neighbor. The component strip (T-091)
removes only *disconnected* debris; nothing shaves attached noise, and the full-shell zone-fill then
paints it convincingly. The E-15 lesson governs the fix: free-form voxel edits **regress without a 3-D
target** — so every smoothing step here is caged against the GLB.

## Goal

A deterministic **regularization stage with a no-regress cage**: morphological open/close on the voxel
shell (erode-dilate shaves spikes/fins; dilate-erode fills pits), where each step is accepted **only if
the cage holds** — per-azimuth silhouette IoU vs the GLB within declared tolerance, six-direction closure
preserved, **protected sub-regions** (chimney, declared thin features) untouched. Rejected steps roll
back automatically. Generic over subjects; the protrusion census and ragged-column rate are the
before/after evidence.

## Builds on

- T-091 shell integrity (closure check, component strip — this stage runs after it); the GLB
  mesh-render path (cage silhouettes); the protrusion/raggedness census (E-27 background measurements).

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-102-01` — pure morphological ops + cage harness, unit-tested on synthetic spiky shells; run on
  cottage/gatehouse/church shells; protrusions and ragged columns reduced, zero cage regressions,
  before/after renders + metrics.
