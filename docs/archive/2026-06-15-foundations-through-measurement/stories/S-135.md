## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-33**. The workshop's conformance gate checks regularity (courses even, symmetry, palette) —
never **proportion-vs-concept**. Result, measured live (T-127): the cottage's proportion defect
survived all six rounds even though the model's critique named it in four of them — no round ever
received a numeric target. The glance's first complaint (squat massing, shallow pitch) is precisely
the dimension no check owns.

## Goal

A **deterministic proportion check** in the workshop's per-round gate, beside regularity: silhouette
ratios — ridge:eave, roof share of the elevation, footprint aspect (and per-mass where the component
record names masses) — computed from the build's render vs the **concept's** silhouette (the concept
is the contract; the sketch is the fallback reference — E-33 honesty note). Machinery we own from the
E-22 era. Declared tolerances; a round's revision that worsens a ratio beyond tolerance is rolled
back, recorded; the round ledger carries the ratios so the model's critique gets a number it can aim
at. **Not a judge call; never replaces the gate** (Rule 2).

## Builds on

- E-22-era silhouette analysis (the machinery); T-126 workshop conformance seam (where it wires in);
  T-133 measured programs (what it verifies).

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-135-01` — the ratio metrics (pure, unit-tested on synthetic silhouettes) + workshop wiring +
  tolerances declared; run on the T-127 cottage fixture: the check **names the proportion defect the
  judge-side records describe**. No judge runs.
