## Goal

Epic **E-11**, seed pass B — **depth through legal geometry**, the marquee move against our oldest ceiling
(flat fields). Over the **material-locked** state, assign Z-depth: inset windows/recesses one block back
(self-shadow), pop trim/cornices/frames one block forward (cast shadow), add a lip/overhang under
horizontal lines (cornice, eave, base) so the wall below sits in shadow. Writes `relief`; locks it. By
operating on the *material-locked* state, it proves a pass composes on a **prior pass's** locked output —
the spine's whole point.
