## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-29**. The styled church fails at the **settle** stage: after 4 grammar+dressing re-runs the
chain still wants `frame 14 / foreign fill 170` (T-110 record), and the kit-presence gate names
`frame lines 169/544 cells missing` (placed as `polished_basalt`). Diagnosis from the T-110 review:
**grammar and dressing disagree about ~170 cells' material vocabulary** — two stages hold different
opinions about which blocks realize the same role (the value-true substitution maps the church frame
to basalt in one stage but not the other). This is the construction-side twin of the census-identity
bug fixed on the gate side (T-110: role-family coverage; third instance of the kit-blind class). The
root cause is structural: there is **no single authority** for role→block-set — each stage composes
map + kit + value-substitution on its own.

## Goal

One **material-vocabulary authority**: a pure module that composes material map + kit + value-true
substitutions into the canonical `role → block-set` (dominant + preserve + fixtures per role), consumed
by **every** construction stage (zone-fill, grammar, dressing, settle) and both gates. A **conformance
test** pins each consumer to the authority (a stage composing its own vocabulary fails the test). The
proof: the church styled chain **settles** — grammar and dressing converge without tuning — and kit
presence no longer loses frame lines to a naming split.

## Builds on

- T-110's role-family census (`ownCoverage` metric — the gate-side half of this contract); the E-26
  kit + E-21 map + T-086 value-true substitution (the three sources the authority composes); the
  settle fixpoint machinery (T-101).
