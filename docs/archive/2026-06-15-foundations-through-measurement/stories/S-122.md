## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-30**, follow-on. The T-121 milestone isolated the single highest-leverage roof-form defect,
agreed by both receipt types (judge gaps and GLB-anchored diffs): **the generated cross-gable ridge is
built ~4 blocks low — mean delta −4.015, unchanged through T-118's instrument repair**
(`roof-diff/…`, `generated/cottage.json`; the barn's first run shows the same low-ridge signature —
major `form @ roof` on all four azimuths, roof-diff 4416px / 35% roof share, and the contact sheet
visibly shows a flattened roof). T-118 fixed the *reading* (`fitRidgeLine` evidence repair); the
*construction* still places the ridge below the fitted apex. This is a narrow generator bug with a
standing instrument that can verify the fix region-by-region — the rare known-small fix with a known
payoff site.

## Goal

The generated ridge is built **at the fitted height**: diagnose precisely where the −4.015 is lost
(fit value vs generator consumption vs rung acceptance), fix it in the pure cores, verify with the
roof-diff instrument (ridge-region delta → ~0), then **one owned re-judge** of the affected subjects
under the pin-rotation policy.
