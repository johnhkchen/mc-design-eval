## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-36** (de-freeze the creation loop). Pin-guard (`src/form/pin-guard.mjs`) decides a write is
refused when the path is **"tracked in git"** (`decidePinWrite`: "tracked = path is committed (a
pin)"). So a **draft build artifact becomes frozen infrastructure the moment it is committed** — the
exact wound the pipeline philosophy warns of ("pinned first drafts became infrastructure"). Live proof
(2026-06-14): regenerating the barn merely to look at it was refused on
`generated/barn/{base-artifact,component-plan,grammar-artifact,artifact}.json`; the guard refused the
*improved* build because its bytes differ from the frozen-bad one. And a guard refusal on a draft write
got swallowed and re-surfaced as a confusing failure-record error, masking the real (benign) chain.

The measurement integrity the guard protects is real and must stay: the **pinned judge's verdict
records**, **ratified packs**, committed **baselines/milestones**, and `retired-pins.json`. The fix is
to guard *those*, not "everything committed."

## Goal

Pin-guard fires on a **curated instrument allowlist** — judge verdict records, ratified packs of
record, committed baseline/milestone measurements, the retired-pins registry — and **nothing else**.
Draft creation artifacts (`generated/*`, `workshop/*` ledgers + final-artifacts that are not yet the
subject of a committed verdict, `recognition/*` programs, chain intermediates) regenerate and overwrite
**with no `--rotate-pins`**. The swallowed-error mask is removed so a guard refusal never hides the real
chain behavior. The dividing line (E-36): **frozen once MEASURED; draft until then.**
