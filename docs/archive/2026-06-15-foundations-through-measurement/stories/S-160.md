## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-38**. The 2026-06-15 stage proved **replace-beats-patch**: replacing the GLB-voxelized roof
with a parametric gable climbed barn +43 / gatehouse +26 (verified), while every wall *patch* tool
plateaued, and the cottage stayed gated because its walls have **missing columns** a patch cannot add.

This story applies the proven move to **walls (then massing)**: construct clean parametric walls — with
a regular opening rhythm — from the **recognized footprint/storeys** (already on disk:
`benchmarks/sculpture/recognition/<subject>.program.json`), discarding the noisy voxel walls. This is
the pipeline philosophy (build from recognition, don't fit the mesh) and is the most likely lever to
unstick the cottage and climb all three building types.

Reuse: `experiments/eval-alignment/{defect-eval.mjs, autonomy-loop.mjs, roof-climb.mjs}`. The eval is the
trusted measure; the new tool is a *wall construction* brush analogous to the gable.

## Tickets

- **T-160-01 — parametric wall construction from the recognized footprint** *(first; in flight)*. The
  loop chose **Option B** (occupancy-derived footprint, morphologically closed → perimeter; recognition
  drives only opening rhythm + storey heights) over building from the program's absolute coords, to
  sidestep unscoped footprint-registration. Sound call — registration was a flagged risk.
- **T-160-02 — construction-centric wall skin from pack roles** *(next; depends on T-160-01)*. Skin the
  clean envelope as **construction, not recolor**: drive the existing articulation brushes (quoin,
  course/clinker, limewash, plinth, opening-dressing) from the declared wall roles, as relief —
  **replacing** the loop's blunt patch/fill tools (`seal_walls`, hardcoded `wallField`). Embodies the
  epic's principle: give the agent *fine construction tools*, not *large simplified fixes* that bury what
  worked. Witness case: the saltcrag barn, whose rich declared vocabulary collapsed to a flat plane.
- **T-160-03 — multi-ridge roof for multi-mass subjects.** SCOPE GUARD: T-160-01/02 are *walls only*.
  The cottage is **2 perpendicular gables**, but the autonomy loop's roof is single-`ridgeAxis`. So even
  with clean walls the cottage roof stays single-gable — **if the cottage doesn't climb after the wall
  work, the gate may be the roof, not the walls.** Don't misread that as failure. Lay one gable per
  `masses[]` entry (the roof analogue of multi-mass construction), reusing `gableRecord`/`generateRoof`.
- **T-160-04 — footprint registration from the absolute program rect** *(after T-160-02)*. T-160-01's
  named finding: sparse/ragged shells (barn, 20–22% filled) stay a **colonnade of posts** because
  morphological close can't bridge straight-run gaps; closing them needs the program's **clean rectangle
  registered to the build frame**. Geometry counterpart to T-160-02's materials — 02 makes a dense
  envelope read as construction, 04 makes a sparse envelope exist at all. Barn is the witness.
- (later) thread all brushes into the loop, re-run the volume batch, measure whether 3/3 climb.
