## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-37**. ~50 of the 58 `benchmarks/sculpture/` directories are sediment from retired epics
(E-09→E-24): `glb-voxel-*`, `e18-build`, `e19-build`, `sweep-ablation`, `form-revise-ab`,
`glb-formtarget-ab`, `material-correct`, `secondary-palette`, `palette-discipline`, `value-select`,
`concept-materials`, `surgical`, … plus the standalone chain runners superseded by the unified chain.
They sit in the live tree and are the "strewn about" feeling — and the thing a thin-context agent can't
tell apart from canonical code. The decision (reviewer): **archive, don't delete** (history preserved,
reversible).

## Goal

Move the retired-epic artifact trees **and** the superseded standalone runners (`styled-milestone`,
`challenge-milestone`, `generated-milestone`-as-a-terminal-chain, `pattern-book`-as-a-separate-chain)
to **`_archive/`** — **after checking every committed test/record reference** (E-37 Rule 2: a move is
refused if a reference can't be updated). The folding calls: **kit recognition folds into the Stage-4
skin** (it already feeds it — keep, don't archive); **reconstruction/regularize (E-27/28) archives** —
superseded by generate-first ("the blob never becomes the build"). `_archive/` stays in git history;
the live spine must not import it (enforced in S-157).
