## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-38**. The differentiator is the **measurement**, not prettier builds — so the eval's
credibility is the asset. Today `defect-eval.mjs` is validated only on **coarse structure** (a human
agreed a roofless holey shell is worse than a solid walled one) and shown **reliable** on a fixed render
(std ≤ 2). Its open gap is the **contested middle**: does it agree with a human on *close* pairs, where
the call is genuinely subjective? An eval that only ranks obvious gaps is a defect detector, not a
quality measure.

S-160/S-162 will produce builds that **span quality** (climb trajectories across many subjects) — the
raw material for a hard human-ranked set. This story uses them to stress the metric.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

On a reviewer-ranked set of **close** pairs (builds within a narrow human-quality band, NOT
obvious-good-vs-broken), the defect-eval will agree with the human ordering on a **majority** of pairs.
**It fails if:** agreement on close pairs is at chance (then the eval captures only coarse structure and
the "quality measure" claim is overstated — say so, and localize which defect classes it's blind to); OR
the human can't reliably rank the close pairs either (then the pairs aren't actually contested and the
fixture is too easy — rebuild it harder, don't claim a pass); OR agreement is high only because the eval
and the human both anchor on one dominant defect (then it's still a defect detector, not preference —
report the distinction). The embarrassing outcome (eval disagrees with the human on the hard middle) is
the *most* portfolio-worthy: it is exactly "I tested my own metric hard enough to find where it fails."

## Tickets (to be cut once S-160/S-162 have produced a quality-spanning build set)

- Assemble a close-pair fixture from climb trajectories (pairs the human marks as a genuinely hard call),
  with a blind human ranking captured as ground truth.
- Score every pair with the untouched `defect-eval.mjs`; report pairwise agreement on the hard set
  separately from the easy set; name the defect classes where it diverges from the human.

## Notes

- Do NOT tune the eval to the human set inside this story — that would launder the validation. Measure
  first; any correction is a *subsequent*, separately-reported change (the stage's "measure your own
  metric" lesson).
- One reviewer (the project owner) is the ground truth for now; note it as a single-rater limitation
  rather than claiming inter-rater agreement we don't have.
