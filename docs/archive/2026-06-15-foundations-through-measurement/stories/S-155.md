## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-37**. The same subject is scattered across `workshop/`, `generated/`, `styled/`, `challenge/`,
`recognition/`, `pattern-book/`, `reconstructed/` — different bytes, no single source of truth. And
E-36 just established that **draft artifacts must regenerate freely while frozen measurement records
stay pin-guarded** — a distinction that is currently invisible in the file tree. The fix is **location
encodes status**: an agent should know draft-vs-frozen-vs-dead by *where a file lives*, not by knowing
its history.

## Goal

Three homes, status by location:
- **`builds/<subject>/`** — the canonical draft: `program.json`, `seed-artifact.json`, `ledger.json`,
  `final-artifact.json`, `renders/`. Freely regenerated (the E-36 free zone).
- **`measurements/`** — frozen records (gate verdicts, baselines, milestones). Pin-guarded — **the E-36
  instrument allowlist becomes "everything under `measurements/` (+ ratified `packs/`)"**, by location.
- **`_archive/`** — superseded (populated by S-156).

Migrate the **live** chain outputs into `builds/`. **Migrate the frozen measurement records into
`measurements/` under identity-class discipline** (E-37 Rule 1): paths change, **bytes never** —
baselines are not re-banked, every committed verdict re-derives unchanged, the judge contract is
unmoved, monotone proof committed.
