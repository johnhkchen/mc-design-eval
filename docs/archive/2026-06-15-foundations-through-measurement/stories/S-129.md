## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-32**. The project's early facade era — the era whose output looked good — ran its model calls
as **typed BAML functions** (`baml_src/`: conceptart, facade, materialmap, judge, review, revise;
`@boundaryml/baml` still in `package.json`, `npm run baml:gen` still wired). The geometric era
sidelined that into ad-hoc prompt+AJV seams scattered through runners. "Design once, reuse many"
applies to prompts exactly as to brushes: a typed function with fixtures is testable, reviewable, and
reusable; an inline prompt string is none of those.

## Goal

The new design seams become **BAML functions**: idiom **recognition** (T-125), workshop **critique**
(T-126), **vernacular reasoning** (the material-story author, for S-130), and **concept
decomposition** (the backlog factory's core, for S-131) — each with input/output schemas, pinned
fixtures/tests, raw replies committed (the T-114 ledger pattern). **Transport rides the `claude -p`
subscription shim — never metered API keys**: BAML is authoritative for schemas/prompts/tests; if
BAML-native transport can't proxy the shim, a thin adapter executes through the existing
`sdk-binding` seam (the facade-era `ClaudeStub` precedent — research decides, constraint is hard).
The **frozen judge is exempt** — its contract is instrument surface (E-32 Rule 2). Ad-hoc prompts in
the new runners are retired.
