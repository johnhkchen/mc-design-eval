## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-30**. The fourth subject (tithe barn) stalled the generate-first bootstrap at its first step:
the T-092 zone lens refused the concept with `no-field-cells` — the barn's **cobblestone field
quantizes into `stone_bricks` at ΔL 2.082, leaving zero cobble-dominant rows**
(`benchmarks/sculpture/zone-map/barn.json`, T-116). This is the E-21 near-tone collapse — *fixed in the
material map years of epics ago* (the barn's own map preserves brick ≠ cobble at exactly that ΔL) —
still living in the zone lens, which classifies bands by raw color-dominance quantization. Downstream,
kit-extract requires derived bands and the generated runner requires kit (receipts: `kit.mjs:122`,
`generated-milestone.mjs:476`); the contracts were correctly not relaxed.

## Goal

Make the zone lens **role-aware**: band classification resolves concept rows against the **committed
material-map roles** (the role palette with near-tone pairs kept distinct), not against a raw quantized
color table — the lens sees what the map already encodes. The barn's field becomes a cobble-dominant
band; its refusal record flips to a derivation; `zoneMapRecord`/`kitRecord` flip from `null` (the
church-flip precedent) and kit-extract runs. **No contract relaxed** (E-30 Rule 1): kit still requires
bands; the generator still requires kit. Legacy zone maps re-verify **byte-identical** through the
change, or every diff is named and justified (the T-113 behavior-preserving discipline).
