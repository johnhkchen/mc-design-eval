## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-23**, the substrate. Every editing lever so far worked on raw 3-D voxels and didn't fit the LLM
(the surgical loop overflowed context at 57k blocks; the cottage face failed because 3-D feature space has
no storey axis). The fix is to hand the LLM a **view**, not the voxels. This story builds the shared layer
both downstream paths stand on.

## Goal

Two pure capabilities + one rendered one:

1. **View layer.** Read/render the build from **ortho + 45° + arbitrary 3-axis** angles (using the E-22
   fixed lens — supersampled, faithful). *Reading* from any angle is in scope (cheap, more coverage).
2. **2.5-D projected surface grid.** For the **well-conditioned ortho/45° projections only**, produce a 2-D
   cell grid where each cell carries the front-most surface voxel along its ray + **depth + normal** (the
   ".5"). This is the canvas the spray-paint tool (S-079) writes on; back-projection is unambiguous here
   (arbitrary-oblique paint-back is explicitly **out** — see E-23 scope).
3. **Structural read.** From the occupancy (pure/geometric, no GL): **footprint**, **storey bands** (the
   horizontal material/floor lines), and **opening positions** (doors/windows). The shared primitive — the
   storey line serves *both* material banding (S-079: where plaster starts) *and* floor heights (S-081:
   where storeys sit).

## Builds on

- The E-22 render lens (`render/src/render.mjs`, supersample), `BUILDING_VIEW_3Q`, the voxel occupancy
  helpers in `src/form/`, and the silhouette/projection math already in `src/form/glb-silhouette.mjs`.

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-078-01` — implement the projection grid (cell+depth+normal, ortho/45°), the multi-angle view reader,
  and the structural read (footprint/storeys/openings); pure cores unit-tested on synthetic occupancy;
  one live multi-angle render of the cottage as the GL proof.
