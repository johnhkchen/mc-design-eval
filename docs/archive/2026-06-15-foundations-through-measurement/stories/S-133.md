## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-33**. The pattern-book builds pass the glance on identity and fail it on proportion — squat
massing, shallow pitch. Mechanism (T-127/T-132 records): recognition *names* idioms well but
*eyeballs* dimensions (VLMs are weak metric estimators), and the pack quantizes the rest
(storey-height defaults, coarse pitch classes). Meanwhile the conditioned sketch (T-123) carries
**measured** eave/ridge heights, footprint aspect, and pitch — and is consumed only as recognition
*context*. The mesh is bad at materials and good at proportions; we currently use it for neither.

## Goal

**Identity from language, quantity from geometry** (E-33 Rule 1): the building program's dimensional
parameters (ridge height, eave height, footprint extents/aspect, pitch) are **sourced from the
conditioned sketch's measurements**; recognition keeps naming the idioms and their qualitative
attributes; pack defaults demote to **recorded fallbacks** used only for dimensions the sketch cannot
measure. Re-seeded programs for cottage and barn carry the concepts' actual steepness and massing —
verified by the S-135 ratio check, **no judge runs** (S-138 owns verdicts).
