## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-35**. A construction-model defect, separate from surface texture: **we plot the roof as a
triangular prism sitting on a rectangular prism** — not how a building is actually constructed. Real
construction raises the **wall envelope** first (the rectangular walls *and* the triangular **gable-end
walls**, both in wall material — the wall continues up into the triangle), then lays a **roof covering**
(sloped planes + ridge, overhanging the walls at the eaves and the gable verge) as a thin skin over it.
Our generator inverts this:

- `src/view/roof-generate.mjs:271-281` fills a **solid triangular prism of ROOF material** (`family.field`)
  from the band floor to the surface height — every cell, including the two vertical **gable-end
  triangular faces**. Its own comment says "gable-end walls fill to the ridge for free."
- The barn program **already declares** `gableRole: "wall.field.ground"` (`recognition/barn.program.json`)
  — but **nothing consumes it**; the roof construct has no gable-end-wall logic. So the gable triangle
  that the concept shows in **stone** comes out as **planks**.
- `src/view/zone-map.mjs:67-69` classifies the whole roof region (gable triangle included) as the
  **roof** zone, so zone-fill recolors the gable to the roof dominant — the skin never gets to dress it
  as wall.
- The roof sits **flush** on the wall footprint — **no eave drip-edge, no gable verge/rake overhang**
  (the concept barn has both).

The barn concept (run 017) is the witness: stone gable end with a vent + ladder, wood-shingle slopes
overhanging the walls. Our barn: one brown mass, gable and slopes alike, flush. This is half of why the
barn reads as a shed and not a barn — and it is upstream of the facade-grammar/relief work (you can't
dress a gable end the skin doesn't own).

## Goal

**The parametric generator builds the way construction works: wall envelope first (including gable-end
walls in wall material), roof covering second (a thin sloped skin over it, with eave + verge overhang).**
The barn is the proving subject. Specifically: consume the already-declared `gableRole` so the gable
triangle is **wall** (owned by the wall zone + grammar, not the roof), keep roof material on the **sloped
covering + ridge only**, and add **recognized/pack-carried eave + verge overhang** (reusing S-147's
`eave-overhang` brush). The roof slopes' pitch and silhouette stay put (E-34's straight ruler reads
them); the gable material/zone change is silhouette-neutral; the overhang is a deliberate, recorded
geometric addition. Deterministic re-run + sheet beside the concept — **no judge run** (the glance is the
proof here).
