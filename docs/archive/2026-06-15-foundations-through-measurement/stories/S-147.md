## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-35**. The articulation idioms are **missing** from the registry (`src/pack/idiom-registry.mjs`,
18 brushes): no `pilaster`, no `quoin`, no `infill-panel`, no `eave-overhang`. `timber-frame` paints
frame **lines** (floor-line beams, corner posts, eave beams) but stops short of the **repeating
stud+panel infill** that gives a half-timber wall its read. And `jetty`/`dormer`/`plinth` constructs
exist in `src/form/idiom-constructs.mjs` but are "not [wired into the program path] at all." So even
when S-145's recognition names the grammar, there is nothing in Stage 4 to build it.

## Goal

The missing idioms land as registry brushes ([[brush-door-export-not-allowlist]], E-32 — composition,
tests, preview card; never widen an allowlist), built on **S-146's relief op**: `pilaster` (proud
vertical strip on a rhythm), `quoin` (corner stepped accent), `infill-panel` (repeating stud+panel
within a field — the thing `timber-frame` stops short of), `eave-overhang` (soffit extension at the
roofline). Plus the existing `jetty`/`dormer` constructs **wired into the program path** so the
recognised grammar (S-145) actually builds. Vocabulary authority holds ([[vocabulary-authority-one-composition-point]]):
role→block-set composed at the one point, conformance sweep green. No per-building constants.

## Builds on

- **S-146** (the relief op these idioms emit through); S-145 (the grammar they consume); E-32 registry.
