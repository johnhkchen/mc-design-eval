## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-33**. The cottage never reached the judge (T-127): the per-view coverage precondition failed
it because **band1 (the upper storey) has zero visible cells at the contract elevation** — the eaves
and jetty occlude it. A census that fails a view on a band the camera **cannot see** is a
**measurement-identity bug** — the fourth instance of the class (T-095: kit-renamed bands censused 0;
T-101: dominant-only rejected the styling; T-110: role-family blind). The fix class is established
and disciplined; the camera and thresholds do not move.

## Goal

The per-view coverage census becomes **visibility-aware**: for each gate view, a band's census
denominator includes only cells **visible from that view** (the projection machinery already
computes per-view visibility); a band with no visible cells from a view is recorded
`not-visible-from-view` and excluded from that view's precondition — it still must pass from views
that *can* see it. Discipline in full (E-33 Rule 3): **monotone proof** (every previously passing
view still passes — replay test over committed records), **both arithmetics reported** in new
records, committed records untouched and valid, thresholds/azimuths/judge contract unmoved. Result:
the cottage's judge call is unblocked **without touching the camera**.
