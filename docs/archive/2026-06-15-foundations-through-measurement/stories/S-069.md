## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-20**, the **quality bar**. The high-resolution building (S-068) is placed and clean, but
TRELLIS+voxelization gets the *bulk* right while fine architectural detail (window reveals, cornices, roof
edges) can stair-step or read soft. This story pushes the build to a **high standard** using the E-15
**surgical revision loop** with the building's **GLB as the form target**: observe a region → diagnose →
bounded tweak (procedural or LLM) → re-observe → accept-if-improved (P14-safe; non-improving edits rolled
back; accepted regions lock).

"High standard of passing round" = the **categorical judge** (Weak/Competent/Strong/Exceptional) reaches
**Strong or better**. Iterate bounded rounds toward that bar — and if it tops out below Strong, **report
where and why** (finding where quality tops out is the project's actual purpose, not a failure to hide).

## Goal

Run the E-15 loop (GLB target) region-by-region on the building, bounded, until the categorical judge
reaches **Strong+** — or the topping-out point is measured and explained. Record the per-region edits
(kept/rolled-back), the judge verdict per round, and the form IoU trajectory. P14-safety holds throughout.
