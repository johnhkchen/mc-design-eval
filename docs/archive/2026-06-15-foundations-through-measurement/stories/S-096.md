## Background (self-contained)

Epic **E-26**. The cottage concept visibly depicts **smooth sandstone** panels, **stripped logs**,
planks, **trapdoor shutters**, and **fence-infilled windows** — instantly identifiable to a viewer — yet
the material map (`benchmarks/sculpture/material-map/cottage.json`) holds 7 full-cube color-role guesses
(`white_terracotta` for the sandstone panels, which renders pink). The E-21 contract asked *which block
matches this color?*; it never asked *which block is this?* — even though the concept is voxel art
depicting real, nameable blocks, and the map's own rationales prove the LLM saw them clearly.

## Goal

A **style-kit extractor**: the multimodal LLM enumerates the concept's depicted ingredients **name-true**
— each entry `{block, role, formClass: cube|fixture|rail, whereUsed}` — validated against the
`minecraft-data` vocabulary, **value-verified** against the concept swatch in CIE-Lab (verification
failure = flagged review item, never a silent fallback), and saved as a **reviewable kit artifact**
beside the immutable concept. Color-snap survives only as the recorded fallback for surfaces the
extractor declares unidentifiable.

## Builds on

- E-21 material-map machinery (the read it upgrades); E-25 T-092 concept band profile (one
  concept-reading seam — the kit extends it, never duplicates it); E-14/E-24 CIE-Lab verification
  (`src/color/block-table.mjs`); `minecraft-data` vocabulary.

## Run-the-test ticket

- `T-096-01` — extractor + kit schema + value verification; run on the cottage: the kit names smooth
  sandstone, stripped logs, planks, trapdoors, fences (matching what a viewer sees); gatehouse kit
