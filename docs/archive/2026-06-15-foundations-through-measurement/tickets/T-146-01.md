## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-35** / Story S-146 — the crux. Skinning is **recolor-on-fixed-geometry**: `src/view/face-paint.mjs`
("PAINT IS A RECOLOR, NOT A MOVE … There is NO air op") and `src/view/zone-fill.mjs` (one dominant
block per zone). Recoloring a voxelized smooth box can never make a pilaster proud of its infill.
**Relief is construction.** The mechanism already ships: `src/view/clinker.mjs` emits courses PROUD of
the wall plane, **in front of existing zone wall cells**, "no board can protrude past the rake line —
by construction"; `src/form/idiom-constructs.mjs` `jetty` projects past the wall line the same way.
Recessed relief is by **exclusion** ([[facade-recess-by-exclusion]]): proud pilasters + a field left
at the base plane reads recessed — no air op. The E-34 ruler (`src/form/silhouette-proportion.mjs`
`maskProportions`) and the silhouette gate must not move when relief is added.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **A shared surface-relief op** through the registry door ([[brush-door-export-not-allowlist]]):
      emits proud cells in front of existing shell cells (generalised from `clinker`/`jetty`) and
      recesses fields by exclusion (no air op). Parametrised by face, row/column rhythm, depth,
      material. **PURE** (no GL/IO/Date/random), byte-stable placement order, **idempotent on its own
      output** (re-run emits nothing) — the `clinker` charter.
- [ ] **In-plane silhouette preserved by construction**, proven under a no-regress harness: with relief
      added, the E-34 proportion ratios (`maskProportions`) and the per-azimuth silhouette are
      **byte-unchanged** (relief lives in front of the plane, never past the rake). The harness is the
      acceptance gate — relief that moves the ruler or the silhouette is rejected.
- [ ] **The 2.5-D layer reads relief**: the per-cell depth `src/view/surface-grid.mjs` already records
      is consumed by a relief-aware read (so the workshop can *see* proud/recessed structure), and it
      is documented that relief is a **construction-stage** capability, **never** a workshop paint
      air-op (the no-air-op rule stands for paint).
- [ ] A **preview card** for the op; tests covering proud emission, recess-by-exclusion, idempotence,
      and the no-regress harness. `npm test` green; `--repro`/`--offline` byte-identical; no
      per-building constants.
