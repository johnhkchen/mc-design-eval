## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-27** / Story S-103. The E-25/E-26 finding: surfaces the pipeline *re-authors from geometry*
(wall fields, dressed openings) pass their checks; surfaces *inherited from the decimated mesh* (roofs,
massing) fail every judge. To re-author the major masses, the blob must become **named components with
geometry**. The structural read (`src/view/structural-read.mjs`) supplies per-cell primitives (floor
lines, wall fields, openings, roof region) but no component *geometry* — nothing downstream can fit a
roof plane or an arch to a per-cell label set.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A **component-record schema + decomposition core** (pure, deterministic, unit-tested on synthetic
      shells): segment a shell into named components, each with explicit geometry —
      **roof planes** (normal, extent, eave edge, ridge candidate), **wall slabs** (plane + bounds),
      **attached masses** (separable volumes + junction surface — a tower vs its nave), **opening
      groups** (apertures with head/jamb geometry). Derived from the structural read + GLB plane fits;
      tolerant of both raw and regularized (T-102) shells.
- [ ] **Per-subject records committed**: cottage (2 roof planes, wall slabs, chimney as protected mass),
      gatehouse (walls, roof, the arch opening group), church (tower + nave masses with their two roof
      forms) — each **visualized** (component-colored render or overlay) so a human can check the
      segmentation at a glance.
- [ ] The record is the **single contract** downstream consumers use (E-27 Rule 4) — schema documented;
      no consumer-specific variants.
- [ ] No subject-specific constants (registry-only); behind the named `npm run` chain; `npm test` green.
