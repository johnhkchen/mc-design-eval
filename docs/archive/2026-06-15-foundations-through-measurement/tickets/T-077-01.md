## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-22**, terminal / Story **S-077**. T-075-01 fixed the render lens (the scale-64 static was
minification aliasing, not the build); T-076-01 built the resemblance gate (triptych concept | mesh |
minecraft + perceptual score + categorical judge). This ticket applies both to the headline builds and
reports honestly.

Builds judged before E-22 were photographed through the aliasing lens, so their *visual* verdicts are
suspect; re-photographing may flip some better (static clears) or worse (real drift the noise hid). Both
are honest results.

**Rules of engagement (E-22, binding):** verdict = the triptych, not a number (Rule 2); no shrinking builds
to pass (Rule 3); every claim cites a fresh render (Rule 4); a residual gap is **quantified and reported**,
never hidden or deferred (Rule 7).
