## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-27** / Story S-106 — the prediction under test: *skinning succeeds when the major components
are parametric and defined rather than one big blob.* Today every skin stage re-derives structure from
noisy occupancy (zone bands from scans, frame lines from heuristics, roof courses from the blob's top) —
which is how voxelization noise kept getting faithfully skinned. After T-104/T-105 the major components
carry **definitions**. E-27 Rule 4: components are the contract; re-derivation where a definition exists
is a bug.

Known downstream target: the **church** never reached kit extraction — its skin chain refuses at the
T-088 gate (band0 wall-field stone **0.33 < 0.5**, the E-25 provision finding) on the blob shell.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Consumption seams** in the E-24/E-26 skin pipeline: roof courses follow the **generated roof
      planes** (not the sampled top); frame lines come from **component edges** (eaves, rakes, corners
      as defined); wall fields are the **slab faces**; zone maps **re-derived/re-pinned** on rebuilt
      geometry where bands shifted (the T-095 review's re-pin protocol, open concern #4). Each seam
      unit-tested with a component record vs the occupancy fallback.
- [ ] **Graceful fallback, recorded**: a subject/stage without a component record uses today's
      occupancy derivation unchanged — existing committed records re-verify byte-identical.
- [ ] **Full re-skin of reconstructed cottage + gatehouse + church** through the styled chain (grammar,
      dressing, settle included); kit presence still PASSES on the cottage (the E-26 claim must survive
      reconstruction).
- [ ] **Church band0 re-measured** on its reconstructed shell: the skin-gate refusal point passes, or
      the residual is named with its measured cause (honest finding — the milestone decides).
- [ ] Behind the named `npm run` chain; no subject-specific constants; `npm test` green.
