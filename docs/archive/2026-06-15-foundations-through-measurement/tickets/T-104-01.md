## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-27** / Story S-104 — the headline. Every failing resemblance verdict on every subject names
**`form @ roof`** (E-25 challenge + E-26 styled milestones). The roof is sampled from the decimated mesh
into a stepped, spiky blob (146 of the styled cottage's 265 protrusions are in the roof band — the fake
criss-crossing rafters), then skinned faithfully. The walls prove re-authoring works. A real Minecraft
roof is **built**: stair courses along the slope, slabs at transitions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Roof fit core** (pure, unit-tested on synthetic roof specs): from the component record's roof
      planes (T-103), fit **eave line, pitch, overhang, ridge height/direction** against the GLB roof
      region (least-squares or equivalent); gable first, hip where the fit demands; **fit error
      computed and recorded**. A fit outside declared tolerance → the regularized sampled roof stays and
      the failure is **named** (E-27 Rule 1 fallback — never an invented shape).
- [ ] **Roof generator** (pure, unit-tested): regenerate the roof volume from the fitted parameters as
      clean voxel geometry built Minecraft-native — **stair courses** along the slope (correct
      facing/half states via the proven T-097 path), **slab** half-step transitions, full blocks where
      pitch demands; block family from the E-26 kit (cottage: spruce). Render `unmapped` must be empty.
- [ ] **Replacement under the cage** (T-102): swap the sampled roof component for the generated one;
      per-azimuth silhouette IoU vs GLB within tolerance, closure holds, the **chimney protected**
      (passes through, re-seated on the new roof if its base moved); auto-rollback on regression.
- [ ] **Run on cottage + gatehouse**: roof-band protrusions from 146/165 to **≈0**; before/after renders
      at the azimuths the gate failed (135°/225°/315°); fit errors + cage metrics in the record.
- [ ] Behind the named `npm run` chain; no subject-specific constants; `npm test` green.
