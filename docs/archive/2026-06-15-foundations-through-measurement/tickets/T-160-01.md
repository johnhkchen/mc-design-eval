## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-38** / Story **S-160**. Apply the stage's proven **replace-beats-patch** move (which climbed
barn +43 / gatehouse +26 by replacing the voxel roof with a parametric gable) to the **walls** — the
confirmed gate on the cottage, whose walls have *missing columns* no patch can add.

Read first: `docs/findings/2026-06-15-measurement-and-autonomous-climb.md`. Reuse:
`experiments/eval-alignment/{roof-climb.mjs, defect-eval.mjs, autonomy-loop.mjs}` and
`src/view/{occupancy.mjs, facade-articulation.mjs}`. Recognized programs (footprint/storeys/openings):
`benchmarks/sculpture/recognition/<subject>.program.json`.

## Falsifiable claim (anti-hedge — lead with how it fails)

Constructing walls **parametrically from the recognized footprint** (a clean envelope with a regular
opening rhythm), *replacing* the voxel walls, will let the **cottage climb** on the defect-eval where
every patch tool left it flat — and will **not regress** barn/gatehouse. **It fails if:** the cottage
stays flat (then the gate is massing/proportion, not walls) OR barn/gatehouse regress (the construction
is worse than their voxel walls) OR the recognized footprint doesn't align to the voxel build's frame
(then footprint-alignment is the real sub-problem, and that is the finding). Report wherever it lands,
with renders.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A pure **wall-construction brush**: given the recognized footprint + storey/eave heights, build a
      clean wall envelope (solid faces, floor→eave) with a **regular opening rhythm** (windows + a door),
      replacing — not patching — the existing voxel walls. Unit-tested like the other brushes.
- [ ] Wired as the wall tool in `autonomy-loop.mjs` (replacing the patch-based `seal_walls`), keeping the
      toolset **minimal** (the stage's lesson: more tools made results worse).
- [ ] Re-run the autonomous volume batch (cottage, barn, gatehouse); record the trajectory + render each
      final build **beside its concept** (judge-free). Report the per-subject climb honestly.
- [ ] **Recorded honestly:** name what still reads wrong; if the cottage still doesn't climb, say so and
      localize the real gate. The render is the evidence, not the score alone.
- [ ] `npm test` green; no per-building constants; the eval (`defect-eval.mjs`) untouched (it is the
      trusted measure — changing it is a separate S-161 concern).

## Notes

- The eval is reliable on a fixed render (std ≤ 2); trust its *relative* verdict, verify big deltas by
  eye (the stage caught two over-reads this way).
- Keep it simple: a single strong construction move, measured — not a pile of tools chasing a number.

## STEER (2026-06-15, evidence: `experiments/eval-alignment/DIAGNOSIS-multimass-and-volume.md`)

A structure read of the recognition programs sharpens the gate. **The cottage is 2 perpendicular masses**
(main 18×28 ridge-z + cross-wing 8×15 ridge-x); barn/gatehouse are single-mass. The loop's constructor
is **single-box** (one `eaveY`, one `ridgeAxis`, one gable) — it *structurally cannot* place two gables.
So the real gate is **multi-mass representation**, not "missing columns." Build the wall (and roof) brush
to **iterate `masses[]` from the recognition program** and construct each mass — not one box.

Corollary that does double duty: the program already carries `rect` / `storeys×storeyHeight` /
`roof.ridgeAxis` / wall `role`s. **Read those instead of the hardcoded `SUBJECTS` constants**; deleting
that map is what turns "3 hand-tuned subjects" into "any recognized subject" (the volume leg, S-162).
Caveat: gatehouse has **no** recognition program — it must get one or drop from the roster. If the
program's frame doesn't align to the voxel build's frame, footprint-registration is the real sub-problem
(report it, per the falsifiable claim above).
