## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-29** / Story S-113. The styled church fails at the **settle** stage: 4 grammar+dressing
re-runs still want `frame 14 / foreign fill 170` (`benchmarks/sculpture/styled/church.json`, T-110),
and kit presence names `frame lines 169/544 cells missing` (placed as `polished_basalt`). Root cause
per the T-110 review: **grammar and dressing disagree about ~170 cells' material vocabulary** — each
stage composes material map + kit + value-true substitution **on its own**, and on the church they
compose differently. This is the construction-side twin of the gate-side census-identity bug (T-110
fixed coverage with role-family `ownCoverage`; third instance of the kit-blind class). Sequenced after
T-111-01 (it edits stages the closure milestone runs).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A **material-vocabulary authority** (pure module, unit-tested): composes material map + kit +
      value-true substitutions into the canonical `role → block-set` (dominant ∪ preserve ∪ fixtures
      per role, per zone/band). One composition point; deterministic; recorded in run records (the
      authority's output is part of the artifact lineage).
- [ ] **All consumers migrated**: zone-fill, placement grammar, opening dressing, settle, the coverage
      gate, and kit presence consume the authority — none composes its own vocabulary. A **conformance
      test** enumerates the consumers and fails if any stage bypasses the authority (the structural
      guarantee, not a convention).
- [ ] **Behavior-preserving where stages already agreed**: committed cottage/gatehouse records
      re-verify **byte-identical** through the migration (`--offline`/`--repro` asserts).
- [ ] **The church proof**: the styled church chain re-run — **settle converges** (no
      `foreign fill` residue; convergence count recorded) and kit presence **recovers the frame lines**
      (169/544 missing → 0 missing-by-naming; any remaining absence is a real placement gap, named).
- [ ] No subject-specific constants; no gate threshold/azimuth/judge changes (vocabulary identity only,
      the T-095/T-101/T-110 discipline); `npm test` green.
