## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-31** / Story S-123. Every epic since E-16 inherited the TRELLIS mesh's lumpiness and paid to
sand it downstream (E-27: 276 attached spikes / 23.9% ragged columns entering at voxelization).
Minecraft's prior makes the fix cheap and *upstream*: roughly **eight legal surface orientations**
(6 axes + 45° roof planes), axis-aligned walls, rectilinear footprints. Snap the mesh to that grammar
**before** anything reads it — straight by construction, not straightened by surgery. The output is a
**sketch for recognition** (E-31 Rule 3): it informs the model's reading and gross proportions; it is
**never a target to fit against** — no downstream tolerance may reference it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Conditioning core** (pure, unit-tested on synthetic meshes): (a) decimate to a coarse face
      set (declared target, ~100 faces); (b) **snap face normals to the Minecraft grammar** — the 6
      axis directions + the 45° roof family — with faces merged/regularized after the snap; (c)
      **dominant mirror-plane detection** and better-half application (symmetry score recorded; no
      mirror applied below a declared confidence — asymmetric subjects stay asymmetric, recorded);
      (d) **rectilinear footprint fit** (axis-aligned polygon, snap tolerance declared).
- [ ] **The conditioned form sketch** schema: cleaned planes, applied symmetry, footprint, gross
      proportions (storey-height candidates, roof pitch class, mass count) — serialized, committed,
      **visualized** (a render or plot a human can check against the GLB at a glance).
- [ ] **Run on all four registered GLBs** (cottage, barn, gatehouse, church): sketches committed with
      their visualizations; the church's two masses survive conditioning as two masses; the barn's
      footprint comes out a clean rectangle.
- [ ] **No downstream coupling**: nothing in this ticket adds a fit tolerance against the sketch or
      mesh (grep/review recorded) — consumers read it as evidence only (E-31 Rule 3).
- [ ] Deterministic (`--repro` byte-identical); registry-only; no per-building constants (per-style
      none needed here — conditioning is universal); `npm test` green.
