## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-27** / Story S-102. The voxelized TRELLIS mesh arrives ragged and nothing downstream fixes it.
Measured (2026-06-10) on `benchmarks/sculpture/styled/cottage/shell-artifact.json`: **276 cells with ≥4
of 6 faces exposed** (attached spikes/fins — the fake "rafters" in every render; 165 in the roof band)
and **23.9% of columns with a ≥3-block height cliff** vs a 4-neighbor. The T-091 component strip removes
only *disconnected* debris; void repair fills holes, not bumps; the full-shell zone-fill then paints the
noise convincingly. Governing lesson (E-15): free-form voxel edits **regress without a 3-D target** —
every smoothing step here must be caged against the GLB.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Pure morphological ops** (unit-tested on synthetic spiky shells): open (erode→dilate, shaves
      attached spikes/fins) and close (dilate→erode, fills pits) on the voxel shell, parameterized by
      structuring radius; plus the **protrusion census** (cells by exposed-face count) and
      **ragged-column rate** as pure metrics.
- [ ] **The cage** (pure harness, unit-tested): a candidate step is accepted only if (a) per-azimuth
      silhouette IoU vs the GLB render stays within a declared tolerance at all 4 gate azimuths, (b)
      six-direction closure (T-091) still passes, (c) **protected sub-regions** (declared: chimney, thin
      features) are untouched. A failing step **rolls back automatically and is recorded** — never
      silently kept.
- [ ] Wired as a pipeline stage after shell integrity, behind the named `npm run` chain (no hand-edits;
      no subject-specific constants).
- [ ] **Run on cottage, gatehouse, church shells**: protrusions (≥4-face) and ragged-column rate
      reduced — record before/after numbers against the 276 / 23.9% cottage baseline, with declared
      targets stated by the implementer and the residual named (Rule: honest gaps). Zero cage
      regressions across all runs.
- [ ] **Before/after renders** at an oblique azimuth per subject — the spike noise visibly reduced; the
      render is the evidence, the census is support. `npm test` green.
