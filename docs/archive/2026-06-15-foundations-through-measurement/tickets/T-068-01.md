## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-20**, the higher-resolution build. Voxelize the whole-building GLB (T-067-01) into a
`DesignArtifact` at a **deliberately higher scale** than the ~32-block sculptures (a larger footprint,
more blocks — the "full-fledged" payoff), then clean it with the **matured E-19 pipeline**: value-true
snap within the design-doc **flat** palette (no busy blocks), material-region **segmentation** (no
speckle), **stray-voxel pruning** (no floating/duplicate geometry).

Respect the measured caveat: **scale↔fidelity is non-monotonic for angular forms** — a very high scale can
make an angular mass read *worse*. Try a couple of scales and **keep the one that reads best**, not the
biggest block count.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The building GLB voxelized at a **high scale** (markedly more blocks than the sculptures) → a
      `DesignArtifact` that passes the AJV gate; **a couple of scales tried, the best-reading kept**
      (recorded which + why).
- [ ] Cleaned via E-19: value-true within the design-doc **flat** palette (zero off-(augmented)-palette,
      no busy-texture blocks), material-region segmented, stray-pruned (principal mass; no floating
      islands).
- [ ] Rendered at a building-appropriate view; **block count, form IoU vs the GLB, and cleanliness axes
      (speckle / distinct-block / off-palette)** recorded (`benchmarks/.../building/`).
- [ ] `npm test` green.
