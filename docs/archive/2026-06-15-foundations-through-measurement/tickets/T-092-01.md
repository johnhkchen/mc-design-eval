## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-25** / Story S-092. The zone machinery works (floor-lines, fill), but the zone **assignments**
are a hard-coded building prior (base=stone, upper=plaster, roof=wood). Measured consequences
(2026-06-10):

- The **cottage concept** (`benchmarks/sculpture/runs/014-vConcept-a-cottage/concept.png`) shows a
  half-timbered building on a low stone **plinth** — cream plaster + dark timber on **both** storeys,
  stone only at the base course/corners. The prior assigns the whole ground storey to stone (57.9% of the
  base band's exposed surface is stone_bricks). Perfect execution of this map still yields a different
  cottage than the concept's.
- The **gatehouse** map invented a brown base band on an all-stone building.

Known constraint: the splat **cannot fill** a zone (9% conversion — quantization collapse,
band misalignment), but the concept **can locate** band boundaries and dominants. Read assignments from
the concept; let the deterministic fill apply them.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A **pure concept band-profile extractor** (unit-tested on synthetic images): segment the concept's
      building region by height into color bands; align band boundaries to the structural **floor-lines**
      (the geometric y-axis — never dominant-block bands, which are corrupted by the noise being fixed).
- [ ] **Role resolution**: each band's profile color resolves to an E-21 material role (and through
      T-086's value-true selection to a block). Output: `{bands: [{yRange, dominantRole, secondaries}]}`.
      The built-in prior remains only as a **fallback** when the concept region is unreadable — recorded
      when used, never overriding a readable concept.
- [ ] **Cottage**: the derived map shows a low stone plinth + plaster/timber on both storeys (matching the
      concept), not a full stone ground storey. **Gatehouse**: all-stone walls, no brown base. Both maps
      saved and diffed against the prior-based maps.
- [ ] Consumed by the zone-fill with **zero subject-specific code** (E-25 Rule 3); concept images
      immutable (E-25 Rule 2); `npm test` green.
- [ ] An end-to-end cottage run with the derived map; before/after render — the ground storey reads
      half-timbered over a plinth.
