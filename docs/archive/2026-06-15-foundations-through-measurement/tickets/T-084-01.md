## Context (self-contained)

Epic **E-23** / Story **S-084** — the **program path** for the surface defects witnessed this session.
Cottage diagnostics + the E-20 building showed: a **roof that isn't watertight** (stray non-roof blocks,
gaps/seams, the crown a "chaotic grey jumble") and a **wall skin that isn't coherent** (stray
`spruce_planks`/`dark_oak_log` in the stone field — "random homes" — and `.` columns with no front voxel —
holes). These are geometric/topological coherence problems (distinct from E-18's color speckle) and they are
the **prerequisite for a safe hollow** (T-080-01): you cannot enclose an interior behind a hole-y wall.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Watertight roof op**: using the roof region (structural read) + the S-082 roof-patch detector, the
      roof becomes **100% covered in the roof material** — holes/seams filled, **stray non-roof blocks
      stripped**. Measured: roof-cell coverage → 100%, stray count → 0, no openings.
- [ ] **Coherent wall-skin op**: wrong-material **intrusions** in a wall field are stripped (re-assigned to
      the field material), and **holes** in the skin are sealed. Measured: intrusion count down, skin-hole
      count → 0.
- [ ] **Watertight shell check**: a **flood-fill from outside the bounding box cannot reach any interior
      cell** — the shell fully encloses the volume (the invariant T-080-01 depends on). Reported pass/fail.
- [ ] The seal/fill + strip ops and the flood-fill watertight check are **pure/deterministic and
      unit-tested** on synthetic occupancy (a holed shell seals; strays strip; a breached shell fails the
      check); the detectors are the metered calls.
- [ ] Run on the cottage; record before/after coverage + the watertight result; `npm test` green.
