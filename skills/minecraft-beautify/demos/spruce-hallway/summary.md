# spruce-hallway: decor pass

- **Brief:** this is the service corridor of a spruce-taiga hillside base. It has a dark spruce timber frame on a pale stone footing, and iron lanterns hang on the bay rhythm. The west wall is the machine side, where the live line runs as a showcased channel. The east wall is the storage side, with recessed panels and shelf niches.
- **Palette:** dominant spruce (planks, plus stripped and bark logs for the frame). Supporting stone: stone bricks for the base course, polished andesite for the floor field, chiseled stone bricks for the post footings. Accents are lanterns on chains, barrels, pots and candles. The relationship is dark wood / pale stone / black iron.
- **Changes:** the corridor now has posts every 4 blocks, giving 5 bays. Each frame line has a post and an upside-down stair corbel on the west wall, a proud post on a stone footing on the east wall, and a beam with a ceiling rib above. In the floor, a log band crosses each frame line. Between frames, the floor field is andesite with plank borders. The west side of the floor keeps its original protected plank strip. Each bay has a stone kick course and a thin trapdoor shelf over the line, with sparse props. The east bays alternate between recessed trapdoor panels and slab-ledge niches with barrels and a pot. Each ceiling coffer is recessed one block and holds a hanging lantern. The 6 ad-hoc torches are gone.
- **Function:** `check.mjs in out` returns `ok`. It reports 0 violations across 40 protected cells and 18 keep-clear cells, and no new functional blocks next to the circuit. The corridor can be walked end to end, and the minimum clear width is 4. The generator also refuses to write any cell at x=1 below y=3.
- **Readings I chose:** the hallway is buried in the hillside, so I grew the structure by 1 in +x and +y, from 7x6x21 to 8x7x21. The new space backs the east recesses and the coffers. Growth in −x was impossible because it would shift the protected coordinates, so the west wall has corbels and a shelf rather than recesses. That asymmetry is deliberate: one side is for the machine, the other for storage.
- **Time:** about 25 minutes. That covers 3 render rounds: v1 (structure and materials), v2 (shelf and props), v3 (stone footings). Each round improved on the one before, so none was reverted.
- **Files:** `beautify.mjs` (the generator), `views.sh` (the 4 eye views), `out.nbt`, `check.json`, `check-before.json`, and `renders/` (before-/after-*, plus earlier rounds in `iterations/`).
