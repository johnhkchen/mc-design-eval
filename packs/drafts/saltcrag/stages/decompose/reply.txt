**What's new vs. owned:** the four work items are the techniques the registry genuinely lacks — a directional weather-face finish (limewash on the seaward elevation only), a clinker board-lap rhythm (the tarred gable can't read as a flat fill), corner quoining (`opening-dressing` only dresses openings, never building corners), and a true thatch construct (depth + eave overhang + ridge roll, which a `hay_block` gable swap doesn't give). Ridge slate, roof coursing, lintels, chimney, footings, doors/windows, turf, and shell prep are all covered by existing brushes — noted, not duplicated. I left `timber-frame`, `dormer`, `jetty`, `arch`, and `roof.pyramid` out entirely: saltcrag is rubble-and-clinker, not half-timbered, and its heads are flat salvage, so it doesn't need them to read as itself.
