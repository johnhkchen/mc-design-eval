West Crissy Field in the Presidio of San Francisco, on the bay shore a short walk east of the Golden Gate Bridge: the historic US Army buildings at the west end of the old Crissy airfield, now a climbing gym, a swim school, a bike shop and offices, and the old Fort Point Coast Guard Station (1915) on the beach with its boathouse, now the Greater Farallones visitor centre. It must read, at a glance, as the Presidio: a tidy, low, historic military post by the water, white and cream painted walls, red and grey roofs, green trim, flagpole and lawns, with the Golden Gate Bridge's orange in the distance (not in these modules).

Scale: 0.5 blocks per metre (a storey is about 2 blocks). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, nothing 1.21.4+). These are the real buildings in a real-place diorama; placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each building IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION: north is low z, east is high x, as in the footprint maps below, and keep its walls to the map (# = the building, . = open ground inside the plot, which may get paving, a lawn, a path or planting). Several footprints sit at an angle to the grid: follow their diagonal walls as clean, regular block steps and set the roof ridges along the long axis. Each module stands at y 0 on a flat 1-block ground of smooth stone. No villagers. Light every building so no mob spawns. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height.
