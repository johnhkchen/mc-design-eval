The module sits right on the terrain and its paths, Campfire Circle and Field Station are good; as the foreground of the hero view it still reads too much as plain lawn. Fix, keeping the box, the ground heights (especially the outer 2 blocks of every edge) and every feature's position:
1. Planting is far too sparse: keep mown lawn only on THE EAST MEADOW, the east end of THE BIG LAWN and the picnic terraces; everywhere else in the park (and the plain grass north-west of the Field Station) lay dense drifts of native coastal planting in irregular sweeping bands following the paths' curves: tall grass and ferns, azalea and flowering azalea bushes (leaf blocks), moss blocks and moss carpet, dead bushes, sweet berry bushes, and wildflowers in colour bands (allium, cornflower, oxeye daisy, poppy, dandelion), 50-70% of the non-lawn park surface, soft grey-green, ochre and purple from above.
2. Trees are round green blobs: make them Monterey cypress and pine: dark spruce / dark oak leaves in wide, flat-topped, layered, wind-swept crowns on short thick trunks, in clumps of 3-6, plus a few tall pines.
3. THE PICNIC PLACE should read clearly: 3-4 terraces stepping down the slope with pale concrete retaining seating walls, timber picnic tables and benches on each terrace, colourful umbrellas (a fence post with a banner or a wool/carpet canopy), planting beds between.
4. THE EAST TUNNEL PORTAL: make it clearly visible: a smooth light-concrete face with two dark tunnel mouths, lit, the Parkway's two carriageways (dark asphalt, white dashed lines, a concrete median barrier) coming out of it eastward in a cut lined with concrete retaining walls, the park's planting spilling over the top.
5. The OVERLOOK terraces on the high south edge: curved, stepped concrete seating terraces (3-4 steps) with timber bench tops facing north, planters of grasses, a promenade with lamps.
