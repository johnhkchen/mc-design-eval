The Main Post of the Presidio of San Francisco, the old US Army post's heart on the Main Parade Ground: the Walt Disney Family Museum and its sister barracks on Montgomery Street, the Presidio Officers' Club and the Presidio Visitor Center. It must read, at a glance, as THIS place: a row of long, identical, three-storey 1890s red-brick Army barracks with steep red hipped roofs and dormers, each fronted by a full-length white two-storey porch (white posts, white railings, a white-trimmed upper gallery), facing a big green lawn; the white Spanish-revival adobe Officers' Club with red clay-tile roofs.
