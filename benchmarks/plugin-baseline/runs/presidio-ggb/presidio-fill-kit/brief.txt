Generic buildings of the Presidio of San Francisco (the old US Army post at the south end of the Golden Gate Bridge), to stand in for the many small buildings of a real-place diorama: officers' and family housing, garages, sheds, a barracks block, a larger hall, and a concrete coastal gun battery. Together they must read, at a glance, as the Presidio: a tidy historic military post among cypress and eucalyptus, white and cream painted wood and stucco walls, red clay-tile and dark grey roofs, green or white trim, brick chimneys, porches. Each is set (centred) on a real building's footprint, so each must be a complete, finished small building that looks right from every side.

Scale: 0.5 blocks per metre (a storey is about 2 blocks, so windows are single blocks and roofs are stairs and slabs). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, nothing 1.21.4+). Each module stands at y 0 on a flat 1-block ground of smooth stone (keep the ground course only under the building plus at most a 1-block apron; the rest of the plot's y 0 may stay air). Front door on the south side (high z). No villagers. Light every building so no mob spawns. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height. Make them varied (not one recoloured box): different roofs, porches, materials.
