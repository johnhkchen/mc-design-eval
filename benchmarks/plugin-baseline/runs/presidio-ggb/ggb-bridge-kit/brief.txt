The Golden Gate Bridge (1937), San Francisco: its south tower standing in the water on its pier, and the suspension span: the road deck with its orange stiffening truss, and the two great main cables sweeping down from the tower tops in catenaries with their rows of vertical suspender ropes. It must read, at a glance, as THIS bridge: everything International Orange (use orange and red terracotta and concrete: red_terracotta / orange_terracotta for the steel body, red_concrete / orange_concrete for highlights; no wool), the tall Art Deco tower with two legs joined by stepped portal struts, the cables' long curve, the suspender lines. These are kit modules: placement code drops each one onto the real bridge line and repeats the deck section along it, so every dimension and coordinate below is exact; keep to them.

Scale: 0.5 blocks per metre (the 227 m tower is 113 blocks above the water). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). The bridge runs north-south: the deck runs along z (north is low z, east is high x). No villagers. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height.

VERTICAL DATUM (all four plots): y 0 is the water's surface. Placement drops the y 0 layer (the sea stays there), so build NOTHING that matters at y 0: the tower's pier and fender start at y 1. Do not lay a ground course in any plot; every cell not part of the bridge stays air.
SHARED CROSS-SECTION (identical in all four plots, in each plot's own x): the two main cable lines are 14 apart and the deck is 15 wide, from the west cable line to the east cable line inclusive. In plots 1, 2 and 3 the west cable line is local x 0 and the east one local x 14; in plot 0 they are local x 16 and x 30.
