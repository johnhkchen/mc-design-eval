The module sits right on the terrain and the edges blend; but as the foreground of the hero view it still reads as a generic lawn park. Fix, keeping the box, the ground heights (especially the outer 2 blocks of every edge) and every feature's position:
1. The west W band is built as a big black parking lot with white stall lines: it is the PRESIDIO PARKWAY, a divided highway (two carriageways of 3 lanes, dark asphalt, white dashed lane lines, solid edge lines, a concrete median barrier between them) descending in a cut to the WEST TUNNEL PORTAL. Remove all parking stalls. Make the portal clearly visible: a smooth light-concrete face with two dark arched/rectangular tunnel mouths, lit, with concrete retaining walls lining the cut and the park's planting spilling over the top.
2. Planting is far too sparse: almost every park surface is flat mown grass. Keep mown lawn only on THE BIG LAWN and the mown loop in Outpost Meadow; everywhere else in the park lay dense drifts of native coastal planting in irregular sweeping bands that follow the curves of the paths: tall grass and ferns, azalea and flowering azalea bushes (leaf blocks), moss blocks and moss carpet, dead bushes, sweet berry bushes, and wildflowers in colour bands (allium, cornflower, oxeye daisy, poppy, dandelion), so that 50-70% of the park's non-lawn surface is planted and reads soft grey-green, ochre and purple from above.
3. Trees are round green blobs: make them Monterey cypress and pine: dark spruce / dark oak leaves in wide, flat-topped, layered, wind-swept crowns on short thick trunks, in clumps of 3-6, plus a few tall pines.
4. THE OUTPOST needs more: around the tall timber lookout add 3-4 big log climbing structures (crisscrossed stripped logs, log piles, balance logs, a lower second lookout deck, rope nets of chains/fences), boulders of stone/tuff, sand and gravel play ground, and the 1-wide water runnel.
5. Paths: the long sweeping curved decomposed-granite paths should be 3 wide with smooth curves and lamps every 8-10 blocks; the CLIFF WALK along the north bluff needs a clear timber railing and its two jutting overlook decks with benches.
6. The OVERLOOK terraces on the high south edge: curved, stepped concrete seating terraces (3-4 steps, slabs/stairs) with timber bench tops facing north, planters of grasses between them, a broad promenade with lamps.
