The arch, pylons, anchorage and approach read very well on the site; FORT POINT (plot 0) is the weak part, it reads as a ragged, half-ruined brick shell rather than the massive, intact casemate fort:
1. The outer walls look broken and jagged (notches, stray single blocks, uneven tops). Make them solid, continuous, vertical brick walls with a clean, level top line, one parapet course at the top all the way round.
2. The three tiers of arched gun casemates do not read. On the water-facing north and west fronts, make three clear horizontal rows of regularly spaced openings (every 2 blocks along the wall, one row per tier at y 1, 3 and 5), each a dark recessed opening with a granite sill course under each tier, so the rows read from a distance.
3. Give the landward (south and east) fronts a granite base course and a clean row of narrow loopholes, with the arched sally port clearly framed in granite.
4. The parade ground (the open courtyard) should be visible from above as a clean paved square with arcaded galleries (arches) on its inner faces at each tier.
5. Keep the lighthouse on the top tier at the north-west corner, dark with a lit lantern.
KEEP EXACTLY: the fort's footprint, position and height (its walls to the map, top y 8, lighthouse to y 14, nothing above y 15); and do NOT change anything of plot 1 (the pylons, arch, deck, anchorage, cables, approach).
