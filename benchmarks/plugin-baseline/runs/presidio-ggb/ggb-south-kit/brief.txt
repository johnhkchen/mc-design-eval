The south end of the Golden Gate Bridge (1937), San Francisco, where it comes ashore over Fort Point: the brick Civil War fort at the water's edge, the bridge's steel arch leaping over it between two Art Deco concrete pylons, the massive south anchorage where the main cables go down into the ground, and the approach viaduct climbing onto the bluff. It must read, at a glance, as THIS place: the dark red-brown brick fort with tiers of arched gun ports, and the International Orange steel arch over it (bridge steel in red_terracotta / orange_terracotta with red_concrete / orange_concrete highlights; concrete in light grey or pale cream). These are kit modules that placement code drops onto the real site, so every dimension and coordinate below is exact; keep to them.

Scale: 0.5 blocks per metre. For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). Build each module IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION: north is low z, east is high x (the bridge runs along z, the Golden Gate's water is north and west). No villagers. Light everything so no mob spawns. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height.
