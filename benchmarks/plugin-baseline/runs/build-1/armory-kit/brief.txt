The Seattle Center Armory (the Center House; 1939, rebuilt 2012) and Chihuly Garden and Glass beside it (2012): the Armory is a long, solid 1930s concrete hall, three to four storeys, cream with chocolate-brown horizontal bands and strip windows, a big glazed curtain wall on its south side looking at the Space Needle, inside a huge food hall under steel roof trusses and skylights; east of it a few low pavilions (shops, café). Chihuly Garden and Glass: the Glasshouse, a tall glass-and-steel hall (a long pointed vault of glass panes on a white steel frame, like a conservatory) with an enormous red-orange-yellow glass flower sculpture hanging inside it; and the Galleries, a low dark-grey gallery building with a green roof and the CHIHULY sign band over its glass entrance.

Scale: 1.4 blocks per metre. For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, nothing 1.21.4+). These are the real neighbours of the Space Needle at Seattle Center; placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each building IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION: north is low z, east is high x, as in the footprint map below, and keep its walls to the map (# = the building, . = open ground inside the plot, which may get paving, steps or planting). Each module stands at y 0 on a flat 1-block ground of smooth stone. Use the photographs only as inspiration for character and colour. No villagers. Light every building so no mob spawns. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height.

Plots along x, each standing at y 0:
 plot 0, x 0..220, z 0..148 (221 x 149), up to y 41: THE ARMORY AND ITS PAVILIONS (map SEA1270134763): the west block is the Armory (cream/brown banded concrete, strip windows, the south face a tall glass curtain wall with the food hall visible inside, roof monitors with skylights, a flat roof); the smaller eastern pieces are low modern pavilions (glass, white panels, flat roofs).
 plot 1, x 221..252, z 0..46 (32 x 47), up to y 22: THE GLASSHOUSE (map SEA1270134470): a long pointed glass vault (glass panes on a white or light-grey steel frame of iron bars/quartz ribs), and inside it, hanging from the ridge along most of its length, the huge glass flower sculpture: a cloud of red, orange and yellow stained glass and glass panes, with a few lights.
 plot 2, x 253..296, z 0..104 (44 x 105), up to y 17: THE GALLERIES (map SEA1270099651): a low dark-grey (deepslate tiles, gray concrete) flat-roofed gallery with a green roof (moss, grass), a glass entrance under a projecting canopy with a sign band (light letters on dark) on its north end, and a few colourful glass sculptures (stained glass, end rods) at its doors.
