San Francisco City Hall (Arthur Brown Jr., 1915): the Beaux-Arts civic palace of the Civic Center, its dome taller than the
United States Capitol's. A long, symmetrical two-storey granite palace on a rusticated base, its fronts lined with a GIANT
ORDER of fluted Doric columns standing proud of a recessed wall of tall windows, an entablature and a balustraded attic
over them; a pedimented central portico on the east (Polk Street) front; projecting corner pavilions; and rising from the
centre the great dome on a tall drum ringed by paired columns, the dome itself leaden grey with fine ribs, gilded trim and
a lantern whose cupola and finial are gilded. Light grey granite everywhere else, with gold accents only on the dome and lantern.
It must read, at a glance, as THIS building: the colonnaded palace front and the tall grey dome with its gilded lantern over it.
