# Farmhouse-Lean: Recognizable Elements

1. **Two-storey farmhouse** → rough stone walls (stone bricks, mossy stone bricks) at x: 0-18, z: 0-14, y: 0-10
2. **Steep pitched roof** → roof preset (slate/dark wood), 45° pitch
3. **Brick chimney** → brick/red stained glass vertical stack, x: 6, z: 4, y: 5-14 (goes above roof)
4. **Shuttered windows** → trap doors (wooden) on stone, 2×1 pattern on both storeys
5. **Timber porch/lean-to** → oak/spruce stairs + slabs, x: 14-18, z: 10-14, y: 0-2
6. **Potager garden wall** → stone brick fence x: 22-38, z: 2-28, forms rectangle around raised beds
7. **Raised beds** → composter blocks (repeating) or dark oak fences bordering grass paths, 3×3 grid
8. **Gravel paths** → gravel + light gray concrete blocks in crosshatch pattern, main axis at z: 14
9. **Centre feature** → crafting table or cauldron (well) at z: 14, x: 30
10. **Crops** → wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot (in block form) per bed
11. **Fruit trees** → oak/spruce logs + leaves against garden wall, x: 35, z: 4-8
12. **Cold frame** → dark oak trapdoors + glass pane roof, x: 34, z: 20
13. **Tool shed** → 4×4 small structure with wooden door, x: 28-31, z: 22-25, y: 0-3
14. **Rose/bean arch** → oak fence gate + trapdoors woven, x: 30, z: 10 (entry to garden)
15. **Lanterns** → lantern blocks at corners and gate posts
16. **Gate to garden** → oak fence gate at x: 20, z: 14 (fence line crossing)
