Owner's comment on the Seattle Center render: the surrounding buildings look rough. For MoPOP specifically, fix these (keep the footprint, the plot, the monorail street and train, and 1.21.1 blocks):

1. The forms read as separate hemisphere domes sitting side by side. Gehry's MoPOP is a few continuous, billowing sheet-metal masses that lean, overhang and fold into one another. Merge neighbouring lobes so they flow together with creases where they meet; give each mass a leaning, asymmetric profile (taller on one side, rolling over at the top like a wave or a crumpled sheet), and let some overhang the walkways below. No hemisphere caps.
2. The purple mass is a vertical-striped cylinder with a flat pink roof. Make it a curved, leaning, crumpled purple form whose top rolls over into a rounded crown: no flat roof slab, no pink rim, no straight vertical drum walls.
3. The surfaces are speckled with randomly scattered blocks. Clad each mass as shingled metal: one main block per colour with a close second tone laid in regular horizontal seam courses (for example purple concrete with purple terracotta courses; yellow concrete with gold-block seams; light grey concrete with iron-block seams for the silver; red concrete with red terracotta; light blue concrete with cyan terracotta). No random dithering.
4. Keep the strong colours (purple, gold, silver, red, blue). The silver masses should read silver-bright, not white.
