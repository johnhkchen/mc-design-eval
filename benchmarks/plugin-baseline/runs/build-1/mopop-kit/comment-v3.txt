Owner's comment on the render: "what's with those hemispheres in the foreground?" v2 turned the domes into rounded egg/jelly-bean blobs; from the air they still read as inflated balloons, not Gehry. Fix the FORM (keep footprint, plot, monorail street and train, colours, the seam-course cladding, 1.21.1 blocks):

1. Gehry's MoPOP is made of SHEETS, not inflated volumes. Build each colour as a tall curved wall (a shell 1-2 blocks thick) that rises from the ground and peels over at the top like a breaking wave or a curled sheet of metal, with its top edge overhanging the side it curls toward. No rounded caps, no egg tops, no closed ellipsoids.
2. Where two sheets meet, make a sharp crease or a ridge line, and let one sheet tuck behind or overlap the next; leave gaps and slots between sheets (glass or dark recesses in the gaps) so the composition reads as several folded pieces, not one lump.
3. Vary the heights strongly: the purple/blue mass is the tallest, nearly vertical on its outer face and folding inward at the top; the gold and red pieces are lower and lean outward; the silver piece is a crumpled cluster with sharp ridges by the monorail.
4. Roofs between and behind the sheets are lower, flat or gently curved in grey, so the colourful sheets stand proud of them (from the air you should see the sheet edges as lines, not blobs).
