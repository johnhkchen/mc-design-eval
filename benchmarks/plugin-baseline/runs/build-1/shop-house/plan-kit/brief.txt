A small shop house for a shopping district: one building on its own plot, with a redstone shop on the ground floor and a family's home above. At most 3 storeys counting the shop. Its plan is L-shaped (or whatever shape makes room) so the plot keeps green space: a vegetable garden behind the house with beds, a path, a fence and a gate, reachable from the home. The base building is generic, a plain, well-made shop any trade could move into, so it can be repeated along a street, varied and reskinned: a few clear material roles (walls, frame or trim, roof, shopfront), a shop name board and display that are easy to change. This copy's tenant is a farmer who sells pork chops (a butcher's: pork, baked chicken, steaks), so dress the shopfront and its display for that.
