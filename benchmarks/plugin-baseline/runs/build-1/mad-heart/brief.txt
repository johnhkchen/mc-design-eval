BUILD: mad-heart: a mad scientist's home inside an anatomically realistic human heart. Outside it must read as a real heart, not a valentine: the lopsided cone tilted with its apex down and to one side, the aortic arch curving up and over the top, the pulmonary trunk in front of it, the superior and inferior vena cava entering on the right, coronary arteries and veins running over the surface, fat along the grooves. Inside, the four chambers are the rooms (the thick-walled left ventricle as the main lab), with the valves as doorways between them; mad-scientist clutter throughout: tubes, glowing vats, tesla coils, a cluttered desk, bookshelves, specimen jars.
SIZE: about 30 wide (front, x), 26 deep, 36 tall including the great vessels; 1:1 scale, a player is 2 blocks tall.
