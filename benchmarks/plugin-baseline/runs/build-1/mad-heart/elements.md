# mad-heart — element map

Screen axes used in the code: `u` runs along the street from the viewer's left to right (world `x = 29 - u`), `y` up,
`z` front (north, the street) to back. The viewer's left is the patient's right, so the right atrium, the superior
and inferior venae cavae are on the viewer's left and the apex points down and to the viewer's right.

Written after the first form passes, then kept in step with the build (the first draft of this list was in my head, not
on disk: see gaps.md).

| # | what a viewer recognises | the real Minecraft thing it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The heart itself: lopsided cone, apex down and right, rounded atrial bulge on the left, flat foot** | A lofted body (per-height ellipses, `hull.mjs`) of `red_terracotta` / `pink_terracotta` / `netherrack` / `nether_wart_block` in drifting patches (ventricles lighter, atria darker). Terrace edges are chamfered with `mangrove_stairs` (`smooth.mjs`, ~190 stairs). No dome brush: nothing in the toolkit makes a tilted flattened cone. |
| 2 | **Aortic arch curving up and over the top, three branches off it** | A swept tube of `red_wool` / `red_concrete` (radius 3.4, hollow), rising from the root, over the top and down the back; brachiocephalic, carotid and subclavian stubs capped with `waxed_oxidized_cut_copper` collars, a copper coil of `waxed_copper_block` + lit `copper_bulb` inside each and a real `lightning_rod` above. |
| 3 | **Pulmonary trunk in front of the aorta, splitting to both lungs** | A swept tube of `blue_wool` / `blue_concrete` from the right ventricle up and back, with the left and right pulmonary arteries leaving it under the arch. |
| 4 | **Superior and inferior vena cava entering on the right** | `blue_concrete` / `blue_terracotta` / `blue_wool` tubes on the viewer's left: the SVC rises out of the atrium as a chimney (copper collar, `lightning_rod` mast inside), the IVC drops to the ground as the entrance tower. Copper flanges (`waxed_oxidized_cut_copper`) at three heights. |
| 5 | **Coronary arteries and veins on the surface, fat along the grooves** | Ridges standing one block PROUD of the muscle (not recolour): arteries `red_wool` (LAD, circumflex, right coronary, posterior descending), veins `blue_concrete` (great and small cardiac, coronary sinus, middle cardiac). Fat is lumpy `sandstone` / `yellow_terracotta` / `sponge` / `yellow_concrete_powder` along the grooves and the base. Pulmonary veins, auricle ribs, and the left/right auricles are ellipsoids and ridges in the same palette. |
| 6 | **Four chambers as rooms** | Hollow rooms 2+ blocks inside the wall: **right atrium** = three storeys of one tall hall (vestibule, library/study, mezzanine); **right ventricle** = the vat gallery along the front; **left ventricle** = the thick-walled main lab (4-thick free wall), floor y=12, ceiling about y=21, balcony at y=16; **left atrium** = bed-and-brews loft on the back. |
| 7 | **Valves as doorways** | Tricuspid: 2x3 doorway RA to RV, bone-white `bone_block` annulus, `quartz_stairs` leaflets with `chain` chordae. Mitral: doorway LV balcony to LA, bone ring. Pulmonary and aortic valves: bone-white rings at the mouths of the pulmonary shaft and the aorta, each with a `ladder` up the vessel. Plus a "Gerbode shortcut" (a septal hole) from the RA mezzanine to the LV balcony so the four rooms form one walkable circuit (checked by `walk.mjs`). |
| 8 | **Tesla coils** | Two in the lab: `polished_blackstone` base, `waxed_copper_block` core wound with `copper_bulb` (lit) and `waxed_oxidized_copper`, a toroid of `waxed_exposed_copper`, `end_rod` spark and a `lightning_rod`; a 24-high copper-and-bulb mast through the library and out of the SVC; coils in the arch branches. |
| 9 | **Glowing vats** | 3x3 glass sleeves (`lime_` / `light_blue_` / `cyan_stained_glass`) round `verdant_froglight` / `sea_lantern` / `ochre_froglight` cores with `slime_block` bubbles, `copper_grate` base and lit `copper_bulb` lid. Two copper-ringed portholes in the front wall show the first vat to the street. |
| 10 | **Cluttered desk, bookshelves, specimen jars** | Desk: `dark_oak_slab` top on `dark_oak_trapdoor` panels and `chiseled_bookshelf` drawers, with `lectern` (book), `brewing_stand`, `cauldron`, lit `candle`s, `skeleton_skull`, `lantern`, `potted_crimson_fungus` and a stair chair. Bookshelves: whole walls of `bookshelf` and `chiseled_bookshelf`. Jars: 3x3x4 `lime_` / `light_blue_stained_glass` shells holding `nether_wart_block`, `slime_block`, `red_nether_bricks` organs on a `polished_deepslate` plinth; single `honey_block` / `slime_block` jars among the books. |
| 11 | **Tubes** | Pipes of `waxed_oxidized_copper` with `tinted_glass` sight sections and lit `copper_bulb` junctions along the vat gallery ceiling, `chain` drops to the vat lids. |
| 12 | **The way in** | Inferior vena cava tower: warped double door (`warped_door`) in a `waxed_cut_copper` surround, signboard brush board ("DR. V. CORDIS / CARDIAC LAB / KNOCK TWICE", `B.sign`), two red `B.banner` wall banners, two `dark_oak_fence` lamp posts with `lantern`s, a paved stoop. Inside, a square spiral of `stone_brick_stairs` rises 7 blocks to the right atrium. |
