The villagers' quarters for the plantation estate (the Greek Revival mansion built earlier): a Minecraft villager village of cabins housing about a hundred villagers, laid out in neat rows along lanes, oriented toward the big house so it stays in sight (the village's main lane runs west toward where the estate sits).
Function: every villager needs a bed it can claim, so about 100 beds in all, in small family cabins (around 25 cabins of 4 beds: one-room or two-room plank-and-log cabins with a porch, a chimney, a door, windows, beds with two clear blocks over them). Build ONE cabin design as a helper with a few variations (mirrored, porch side, wood, roof colour) and stamp it along the rows, instead of furnishing every cabin as its own part.
Fit: family life, gardens, poultry and a small informal economy. Each cabin has a fenced garden plot (farmland with crops, a composter, a water source), shared chicken coops and runs with fences and hay, a well, a small market square with stalls where the villagers' job-site blocks sit (composter, smoker, barrel, loom, fletching table, cartography table and the like, so villagers can take up trades), benches and a shade tree. Lanes of dirt path, lanterns on posts so no mob can spawn, a few big trees. Keep the whole village within about 96 x 96 blocks.
