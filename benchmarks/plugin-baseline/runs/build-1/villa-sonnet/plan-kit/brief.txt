BUILD: villa-sonnet: a Roman villa (domus/villa urbana), single storey around open courts. Recognisable elements: a symmetrical street front with a recessed entrance (fauces) between shuttered shop rooms; the atrium behind it, open to the sky, with a shallow rain pool (impluvium) under a square opening (compluvium) in the roof; the tablinum (study) facing the entrance across the atrium; a peristyle garden court behind, ringed by a covered colonnade of white columns, with formal planting, low hedges, a fountain and statues; rooms off both courts: the triclinium (dining room with couches on three sides), cubicula (small bedrooms), a kitchen (culina) with a hearth; terracotta tile roofs sloping inward to the courts, cream plaster walls with a red painted dado, mosaic floors, bronze lamps and potted plants.
SIZE: about 36 wide (front, x), 48 deep, 9 tall to the roof ridges; 1:1 scale, a player is 2 blocks tall.
