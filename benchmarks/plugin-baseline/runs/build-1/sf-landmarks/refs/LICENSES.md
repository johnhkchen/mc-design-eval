# Reference photo licences

All photos are from Wikimedia Commons. Licences were read from each file’s own imageinfo/extmetadata or ImageObject metadata, not the Commons footer. Metadata evidence and download URLs are preserved in `licenses.json`. Checked 2026-10-18.

Downloaded using supported 1920 px width thumbnail URLs and resized to 1600 px on the long side (Commons rejects arbitrary 1600 px thumbnail widths). All files are JPEGs; no crops. If displaying a photo, retain its author, source and licence links and note further edits. CC BY-SA adaptations must follow the linked share-alike terms.

Coverage limitation: no verified rear view of the Painted Ladies was found. The street-slope view shows facade depth and the sidewalk; it is not a rear elevation. Some detail photos include people or parked cars. The Palace frontal view has a tree partly screening the lower rotunda.
