# Element map — what each recognisable thing becomes in Minecraft
1. Palas: calcite block ~45x16, dark-slate gable roof via roof() preset (civic overridden gable/steep), stepGable brush on gable ends, rows of pointed `arch` openings glazed with glass_pane + quartz surround, balcony = corbel stairs + slabs + quartz_wall balustrade.
2. Stair turret: `cylinder` r3 in calcite to y63, `roof tower-cap` cone to y75 with gold_block finial; arrow-slit panes.
3. Slender corner/gable turrets: `cylinder` r2 + tower-cap cones (dark slate), corbelled bases of upside-down stairs.
4. Main tower: square calcite shaft, gallery = projecting slab ring on upside-down-stair corbels with quartz_wall railing, pyramid roof.
5. Gatehouse: `bricks` block, quartz trim, `parapet crenellated`, `arch` gate opening with iron_bars portcullis and spruce doors, lanterns, wall banners (Bavarian lozenge: light_blue/white rhombus banner blocks).
6. Gate towers: two round calcite `cylinder` r4 with tower-cap cones.
7. Courtyards behind curtain walls: calcite crenellated walls, stone_brick / gravel floors, well (cobble wall + chain + bucket-less), lanterns.
8. Knights' House & Bower: gable block with stepGable, square tower with pyramid cap.
9. Rock crag: heightfield of stone/andesite/tuff/cobble with retaining walls (stone_brick stairs/walls), approach stairs to the gate.
