BUILD: neuschwanstein: Neuschwanstein Castle, the Bavarian fairy-tale castle, on its rocky ridge. Recognisable elements: the tall white-limestone Palas (the main palace block) with steep dark-slate roofs, stepped and pointed gables, rows of arched windows and a balcony; the slender round stair turret beside the Palas rising far above everything, capped with a tall pointed spire; many slender round turrets with pointed slate cone caps on corners and gables; the square main tower with its upper gallery; the red-brick Gatehouse at the front with white trim, a crenellated top and an arched gate, flanked by two round white corner towers with cone caps; the upper and lower courtyards between them behind curtain walls; the Knights' House and the square Bower; the whole thing standing on a steep grey rock crag with retaining walls, so the castle reads tall and vertical.
SIZE: about 110 wide (front, x), 50 deep, 75 tall to the top of the highest spire, including the rock base; 1:1 scale, a player is 2 blocks tall.
