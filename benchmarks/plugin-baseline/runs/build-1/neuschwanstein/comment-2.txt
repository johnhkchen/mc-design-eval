Next pass, in this order:
1. THE BACK CLIFF. The south side is a flat sheer grey wall, the biggest blank area on the build. Make it a real crag: rock buttresses and gullies, strata ledges, overhangs, a few clinging pines, and a zig-zag service path with a small postern door into the lower court.
2. THE THRONE HALL APSE. Build it as a real semicircular apse projecting outward from the Palas wall, with its own conical slate roof and gold finial, tall round-arched windows around its curve, and inside it the raised gold throne platform in the curved end.
3. THE PALAS BACK FACADE. The south elevation still reads as a grid. Give it the same pilaster bays and per-storey Romanesque window groups as the front, and add a projecting oriel tower (a bay window rising three storeys on corbels, with its own small cap).
4. THE FRONT APPROACH. From the street view the stair-turret spire is barely visible behind the gatehouse. Make it read from the front: thin the gatehouse roofline in front of it if needed, and add a winding approach road up the crag to the gate with a low parapet wall and lanterns.
5. COURTYARD LIFE. A fountain in the upper courtyard, lanterns, banners, a cart, flower boxes.
Keep everything else as it is.
