Owner's comment on the Seattle Center render: the surrounding buildings look rough. Fix these on the Pacific Science Center, keeping the footprint, the plot, the pools and the arches' positions, in 1.21.1 blocks:

1. Every wall top is crenellated (merlons all round), so it reads as a castle. Remove all crenellations: Yamasaki's walls end in a clean, flat white parapet.
2. The walls are flat grey with plain pilasters. They should be WHITE (white concrete, smooth quartz, calcite) with Yamasaki's tracery: slender vertical ribs (a block proud, every 3-4 blocks) that rise and branch into tall pointed Gothic arches in the upper third of each wall, and an arcade of pointed arches at the ground floor along the courtyard. Use quartz pillars/walls and stairs to draw the ribs and arch heads.
3. The five lattice arches over the pools are right in place but chunky. Make them slender white pointed arches with open lattice tracery between their two legs (a diamond/rib net near the crown), legs one block thick.
4. The roofs are bare grey. Use a light roofing block (light grey or white concrete, smooth stone) and add the walkways and railings along the pools, the flower-shaped fountain bowl in the main pool, and the lamp globes hanging from the arches.
