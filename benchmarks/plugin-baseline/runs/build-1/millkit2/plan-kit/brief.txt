A second modular housing kit for the same Southern agricultural settlement (a cotton mill village, about 1880-1930, on a Create-mod server; cotton, tobacco and corn; the plantation house and the first mill-village row are nearby). It complements the first kit (shotgun house, saddlebag duplex, hall-and-parlour cottage, kitchen garden, chicken coop, well yard, company store): new house types, a few two-storey ones, and the shared yards a village of families needs. Build it as ONE sample row of modules on the SAME fixed plot grid, so each plot can be cut out as its own module file and mixed with the first kit in any layout.
THE GRID (exact, identical to the first kit): a dirt-and-gravel lane runs along x at z 0..4 (with a lantern post every 12 blocks at the lane's edge, at the same x as the first kit); plots are 12 wide (x) and 14 deep (z 5..18); plot i covers x = 12*i .. 12*i+11. Each module stands inside its plot with its front on the lane side (north), its door or gate at the plot's middle (x = 12*i+5..6), and the plot's two side columns (x = 12*i and 12*i+11) kept as a low fence or hedge line or open path so neighbours join cleanly. 8 plots in a row (96 wide):
 plot 0: dogtrot house (two one-room pens under one gable roof with an open breezeway between them, front porch)
 plot 1: camelback shotgun (a shotgun house with a second storey over the rear rooms only; an inside stair up to it)
 plot 2: two-storey I-house for the mill overseer's family (one room deep, two rooms wide, central stair hall, two-storey front porch, end chimneys)
 plot 3: four-room pyramid-roof mill cottage (square plan, hipped pyramid roof, central chimney serving all four rooms, porch across the front)
 plot 4: boarding house for single mill hands (two storeys, a shared kitchen and table below, bunk rooms above reached by a stair, a long front porch)
 plot 5: shared work yard: smokehouse (with a smoker and hanging hams of barrels/trapdoors), woodpile under a lean-to, wash kettle (cauldron) and a bench
 plot 6: pecan orchard and bee yard (a few trees, bee nests or beehives, a picnic table, a swing)
 plot 7: 1920s mill bungalow (low hipped or front-gable roof, deep porch on tapered posts, brick chimney), the newest house on the street
Houses: board-and-batten, clapboard or painted brick on brick or stone piers, tin or shingle roofs (use different woods and roof colours from the first kit so a mixed street varies), porches, real windows; beds for a villager family (each with two clear blocks above, its head against a solid wall, reachable from the door), a table, a stove (furnace or smoker) by the chimney. Stairs a player walks to the top without jumping (flight()); chimney smoke from a fire inside the flue (chimney()). Simple, sturdy, lived in; each house a little different. Lit so no mob spawns.
