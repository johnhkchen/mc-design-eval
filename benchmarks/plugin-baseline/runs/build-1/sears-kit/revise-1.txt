The owner's review of v1, house by house (plot numbers as in plan.md). Keep the Alhambra (plot 1) and the Craftsman (plot 7) as they are.
- Plot 0 Magnolia: the roof has an open hole at the back (a bed shows through from above): close it. The two chimneys are far oversized: make them ordinary stacks (1x1 flue in a 2-wide or 3-wide brick stack) set into the end walls. Add the three front dormers the plan gives it. The portico stair at 6,11,9 and 9,11,9 ends a step short (fit): flush the top step.
- Plot 2 Vallonia: it reads as a steep wooden A-frame; it must read as a low Craftsman bungalow. Rebuild its roof: a LOW side gable (about 1 up in 2 across), dark shingle, that sweeps on down over a full-width front porch at the same pitch, with a wide shed dormer on the front slope; tapered wooden porch posts on stone piers; exposed rafter tails under the eaves. One and a half storeys, not a tall gable wall facing the street.
- Plot 3 Puritan: make the gambrel read: a steep lower slope (nearly vertical, 2-3 courses) breaking to a shallow upper slope, on both sides, with the long shed dormer across the front; replace the tall white entry block with a small entry hood on two columns. Close the 1-wide gap in the entry porch.
- Plot 4 Cornell: it reads one storey; it must read as a two-storey foursquare: two full storeys of walls with the upper windows under the eaves, the low hipped roof and hipped dormer on top, and a full-width one-storey porch with square columns across the front.
- Plot 5 Stanford / Cape Cod: add the two gabled dormers on the front slope; the central chimney's hearth needs its mouth open into the room at floor level.
- Plot 6 Winona: the chimney stands in the middle of the front; move it to a side wall (an exterior chimney with the hearth inside). Keep the porch under the front gable.
- Fit: the campfire at 88,1,18 is shut inside its stack: give it the hearth mouth into the room.
Every house keeps its plot, its slab-ramp way in, its beds and its rooms; change only what is listed.
