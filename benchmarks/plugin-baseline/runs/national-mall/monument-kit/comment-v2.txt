The obelisk is the problem; keep the grounds, flag ring, plaza, paths and trees exactly as they are. Fix the obelisk:
1. The upper two thirds are striped (alternating bands of two blocks): the real shaft is one SOLID, uniform shade. Above the colour line use ONE block only, everywhere (calcite, or smooth sandstone if calcite is too close to the lower part), no bands, no pattern, no mixed blocks. The lower third stays smooth quartz. The colour change must be one crisp horizontal line about 19 blocks above the plaza and nothing else.
2. It reads as a telescoping tower because the shaft steps in at the colour line. Do NOT step in there. Make the shaft one clean 7x7 prism from the plaza to the pyramidion, tapering only by chamfering its four corner columns: remove the corner column of each edge from about 36 blocks up, so the top half reads 7 wide with clipped corners (a gentle taper), never a visible ledge.
3. The pyramidion is a stepped spire with a needle: make it a simple, short, smooth four-sided point (about 7 blocks high from 64 to 71): stairs on all four faces climbing to a single block apex, same shade as the upper shaft, a tiny dark tip only. Keep the two small dark windows per face just below it and the red aircraft lights.
4. Floodlight the base: warm lights hidden in the plaza around the shaft so it glows at dusk.
