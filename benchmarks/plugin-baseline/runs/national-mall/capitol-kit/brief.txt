The United States Capitol (1793-1868, dome by Thomas U. Walter 1855-66), the east end and climax of the National Mall: the hero of the render.

Scale: 0.42 blocks per metre (a storey is about 1.5 blocks: windows are single openings or slits, columns are walls or fences one block thick, roofs are stairs and slabs; keep big forms crisp and true to the real proportions, which matter more here than small detail). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, nothing 1.21.4+). These are real landmarks of the National Mall in Washington DC, for a hero aerial render at golden hour and dusk, seen from a few hundred blocks away and above, looking along the Mall: they must read at a glance as THESE buildings, by silhouette, colour and their one or two signature features. Build each IN SITE ORIENTATION exactly as in its map: north is low z, east is high x; the Mall runs east-west (along x), the Capitol at its east end, the Washington Monument at its west. Placement sets each module on its real footprint without turning. Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass. Light every building and landscape so no mob spawns (lanterns, hidden light sources, lit windows); at dusk the monuments are floodlit white, so put warm light at their bases. No villagers, no signs with text.

Plots along x, each standing at y 0:
 plot 0, x 0..35, z 0..95 (36 x 96), up to y 45: THE US CAPITOL — A long white neoclassical building running NORTH-SOUTH (along z), its WEST FRONT facing the Mall (low x). Three parts: the central block with the GREAT DOME at the middle of the footprint (z centre about 47), the Senate wing to the north (low z) and the House wing to the south (high z), joined by short connecting corridors, the whole about 96 long. THE DOME is the signature and must be perfect at this scale: a square base, then a tall round colonnaded drum (the peristyle: a ring of white columns, about 16 across), an attic storey with windows, then the ribbed, cast-iron bell-shaped dome (about 12-13 across at its base, white, with subtle ribs of light grey), a second smaller colonnaded tholos/lantern on top, and the bronze Statue of Freedom (a dark figure 2-3 blocks tall) at the very top: dome and statue reach about y 39 above the base. The wings: white marble, two to three storeys over a raised rusticated basement, rows of tall windows between Corinthian pilasters and colonnades, a balustrade along every roofline hiding low grey/copper roofs, each wing with a pedimented columned portico on its east face (high x) and a long colonnade on its west face. The central block's west side: a projecting centre with a columned loggia above the terraces (the West Front), with the inaugural west steps leading down to the terraces (the terraces themselves are in the grounds kit, against this west face at x 0 of this plot). The east face (high x): the great East Portico, a deep double row of columns under a pediment, at the top of a broad flight of steps. All white (white concrete, smooth quartz, calcite, quartz pillars, diorite trim for shadows); windows dark with warm lit panes at dusk; floodlights at the base so it glows white at night.
