Good wings and footprint; keep the wings, their porticoes and colonnades, the footprint and the height. Fix the dome and the roofs:
1. The dome reads squat and stepped like a beehive. Rebuild it as Walter's dome: a tall round peristyle drum ringed by free-standing white columns (a continuous colonnade of 1-block quartz pillars with dark voids behind, standing proud of the drum on a round base), an attic band of small windows above it, then a TALL, smooth, bell-shaped dome (round in plan, about 12-13 across at its base, its profile rising steeply then curving in: use stairs and slabs for a smooth curve, white, with thin light-grey ribs running up it), a small colonnaded lantern (tholos) on top, and the dark bronze Statue of Freedom on it. The dome should be the tallest, most graceful thing, rising high above the wings (to about y 39). It should never look like a stepped pyramid.
2. The light wells in the wings show orange/sand floors from above: roof them over (flat light grey roofs or glass skylights with grey frames), no orange.
3. The green blocks on the roofs (the small green pavilions) read like stray copper: remove them or make them light grey roof lanterns.
4. Light the West Front and the dome's drum warmly at dusk (hidden floodlights), so the dome glows.
