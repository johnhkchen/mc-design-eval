The real corona is three tiers, each tier a tall band whose four walls lean OUTWARD as they rise (an inverted, stepped pyramid: each tier wider at its top than at its bottom, and each tier starting a little wider than the top of the one below), clad all over in a bronze lattice screen. Rebuild it so: a recessed dark glass base storey; then three tiers of about 4 blocks high each, every tier stepping out by 1 block per 2 courses on all four sides (use upside-down cut copper stairs on the outer faces so the walls read as leaning out), the top tier the widest, with a flat roof. Clad the tier walls in a uniform warm bronze lattice: waxed cut copper with a regular pattern of small dark openings (trapdoors or dark glass panes behind iron bars) over every face, glowing amber at dusk from lights behind the screen.
