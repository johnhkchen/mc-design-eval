The Brooklyn Bridge (1883, John A. and Washington Roebling), New York: the great suspension bridge over the East River from Manhattan to Brooklyn. It must read, at a glance, as THIS bridge: two massive grey-buff granite and limestone towers, each pierced by TWIN POINTED GOTHIC ARCHES; four pale main cables sweeping between them; and the famous web of cables: the vertical suspenders crossed by the DIAGONAL STAYS that fan out from each tower top; the elevated wooden promenade running down the middle above the two roadways. These are kit modules: placement code drops each one onto the real bridge line and repeats the deck section and the approach section along it.
