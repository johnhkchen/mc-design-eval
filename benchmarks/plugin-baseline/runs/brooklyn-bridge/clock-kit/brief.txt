The Clock Tower Building, 1 Main Street, DUMBO, Brooklyn (1915, built for Robert Gair's carton company; condominiums since 2000): a white reinforced-concrete loft building on the waterfront crowned by a slim four-faced CLOCK TOWER. It must read, at a glance, as THIS building: a big, plain, pale-cream concrete block of about 15 storeys, its facades a regular grid of large industrial windows between flat concrete piers and spandrels; on top, the square tower with a huge round clock face on each side (white dial, black hands and numerals, glowing at dusk) under a small cornice and pyramidal cap.

Scale: 0.7 blocks per metre (a storey is about 2.5 blocks). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). These are real structures of the Brooklyn waterfront in New York for a hero AERIAL render at golden hour: the camera looks from high over the East River and Brooklyn Bridge Park toward the Brooklyn Bridge with the Lower Manhattan skyline behind, so silhouettes, tops and the faces toward the river matter most. Placement code sets each module exactly where the real one stands, so every dimension and coordinate below is exact; keep to them. Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass. No villagers. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height. Light it for dusk (lamps on the bridge decks and promenade, warm windows in some lofts, floodlit stone) so no mob spawns on a walkable surface.

THE PLOT'S FRAME: DUMBO's own street grid: north (low z) is true north, toward the East River and Water Street; Main Street runs along the west edge (low x), Plymouth Street along the south (high z) and Washington Street to the east (high x).
