Empire Stores and Jane's Carousel, DUMBO, Brooklyn: the waterfront heart of Brooklyn Bridge Park between the two bridges. EMPIRE STORES (1869-1885): a long row of seven joined brick coffee and sugar warehouses, five storeys, on Water Street facing the river: deep red-brown brick walls, rows of round-arched and segmental-arched window openings with heavy iron shutters (dark green / black) pinned open, granite sills and lintels, stone quoins, a stepped and corbelled roofline over each warehouse bay, and since 2017 a cut through its middle to a green roof garden. JANE'S CAROUSEL (1922 Philadelphia Toboggan carousel; pavilion by Jean Nouvel, 2011): a vintage painted carousel in a clear GLASS BOX pavilion on the riverside lawn, a flat black-edged roof over a tall transparent cube, the carousel's lights glowing inside at dusk. They must read, at a glance, as THESE: the long red-brick arcade of shuttered openings beside the river, and the glowing glass box.
