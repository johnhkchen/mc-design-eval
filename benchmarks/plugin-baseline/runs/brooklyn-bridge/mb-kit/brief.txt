The Manhattan Bridge (1909, Leon Moisseiff, with Carrère and Hastings), New York: the steel suspension bridge just upriver of the Brooklyn Bridge, the one framed at the end of Washington Street in DUMBO. It must read, at a glance, as THIS bridge: two tall STEEL TOWERS painted pale blue-grey, each a pair of riveted legs with lattice X-bracing between them and an ornamental Beaux-Arts crown of arched cross-girders with ball-topped finials; four cables; a deep double-deck truss (roadways on top, subway tracks below); and the massive granite anchorages, the Brooklyn one pierced by the tall vaulted ARCHWAY over a cobbled plaza.
