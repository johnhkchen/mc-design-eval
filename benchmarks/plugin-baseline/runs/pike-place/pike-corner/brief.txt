The market's south and east blocks: the Economy Market and Corner Market at the foot of Pike Street, the Sanitary Market across Pike Place from the arcade, and a small Post Alley building.

Scale: 1.4 blocks per metre. For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, nothing 1.21.4+). These are real buildings of Pike Place Market in Seattle, for a hero render at dusk. Placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION, as in the footprint maps: Pike Place, the market street, runs along z just east of the Main Arcade (north-west end at low z, Pike Street at high z); 1st Avenue is further east (high x, uphill); Elliott Bay is west (low x, downhill). The ground climbs steeply from the waterfront up to Pike Place: y 0 of each plot is the lowest ground under that footprint, and the GROUND map gives the ground height above y 0 per cell. Buildings on the bluff show extra lower storeys on their downhill (low x) side: build down to the ground there, never floating and never buried. Build only on the footprint cells (#). Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass (lit stalls, shop interiors near the windows). Make it read at dusk: warm lights in windows and stalls, lanterns, neon where the real building has it.
