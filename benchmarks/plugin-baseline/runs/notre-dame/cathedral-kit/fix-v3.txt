Rebuild the east end (the apse and the choir east of the transept) and make the south and north flanks read:
1. APSE ROOF. The apse's roof is a polygonal half-cone in dark lead, hipped round the curve from the main ridge, with a
   balustrade at its foot and a small cross or finial at the ridge's east tip.
2. CLERESTORY. Tall pointed windows (2 lancets under a round oculus, dark stained glass, deep reveals) in every bay of the
   upper apse and choir, between slender piers.
3. FLYING BUTTRESSES. Real ones: from tall outer piers capped with pinnacles (stepped, ending in a spike or a lightning
   rod), a single long sloping arch (stairs and slabs, one or two blocks thick, open underneath) spans over the aisle roof
   to the clerestory wall at each bay. Around the apse they radiate. Open air under every arch: you must see sky through
   them. No lattice grid.
4. RADIATING CHAPELS at ground level round the apse: a ring of low chapels, each with a large pointed tracery window
   (dark glass, stone mullions), a small gable over it (a steep stone triangle with a crocket edge), and a lead lean-to roof
   behind; a pierced parapet runs along their top.
5. FLANKS. Same bay rhythm along the nave and choir: aisle windows with gables, buttress piers with pinnacles, flyers.
6. RELIEF. No flat runs of more than 4 blocks on any visible face: string courses, reveals, piers proud by a block,
   dark recessed glass. Sandstone family as now (sandstone, smooth/cut/chiseled sandstone, a little end stone or birch for
   highlight), dark lead roofs.
Work on the east end and flanks only; diff should leave the towers' boxes untouched.
