The Lower Plaza of Rockefeller Center with its skating rink and Paul Manship's gilded PROMETHEUS (1934), the ring of flags around it, and the Channel Gardens that run up to Fifth Avenue between La Maison Francaise and the British Empire Building: the open heart of the complex, seen from above right in front of 30 Rock. These are LANDSCAPE modules on the real ground.

Scale: 0.9 blocks per metre (a storey is about 3.5 blocks). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). These are real buildings of Rockefeller Center in Midtown Manhattan, New York, for a hero AERIAL render at golden hour and dusk: the camera looks down from high over Fifth Avenue, so ROOFS, setbacks and the tops of the buildings matter as much as the street fronts. Placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION, as in the maps: the Manhattan grid is square to the axes; uptown (north) is low z, downtown (south) high z; Fifth Avenue is east (high x), Sixth Avenue (Avenue of the Americas) west (low x); the cross streets (48th, 49th, 50th, 51st Streets) run along x. The ground is nearly flat: y 0 of each plot is the lowest ground under it and the GROUND map gives the ground above y 0 per cell. Build only on the footprint cells (#). Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass (lit lobbies, shop windows). No villagers. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height. Make it read at dusk: warm light in a scattering of office windows (not all), lit lobbies and shopfronts at street level, floodlit crowns and signs where the real building has them; light every roof and terrace so no mob spawns.
