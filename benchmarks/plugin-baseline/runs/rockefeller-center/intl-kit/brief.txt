The Fifth Avenue front of Rockefeller Center: the International Building (1935) with Lee Lawrie's bronze ATLAS in its forecourt, and the two low six-storey buildings that flank the Channel Gardens, La Maison Francaise and the British Empire Building (1933). Seen from the air from over Fifth Avenue, it is the foreground of the hero view.
