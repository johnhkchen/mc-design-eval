The low Fifth Avenue wings, the roof gardens and the two Channel Gardens buildings read well: keep them, every plot, footprint and height exactly. Plot 0's TOWER does not:
1. The tower's big faces are blank sandstone walls (only the edge columns have windows). Every face of the International Building tower, all four sides from the wing roofs to the crown, must carry the Rockefeller Center fluting top to bottom: continuous 1-wide limestone piers proud of the wall, alternating with 1-wide recessed dark window strips (gray/black stained glass panes, gray_terracotta spandrels every storey, about 1 window in 5 lit warm), exactly like 30 Rock and like the wings below it. No blank wall areas anywhere on the tower.
2. Its setbacks should read as clean tiers: each setback ledge capped with a slab/stair coping and a low parapet, floodlit from the ledge (light blocks hidden in it) so the upper faces glow at dusk; the crown a flat roof with a low penthouse.
