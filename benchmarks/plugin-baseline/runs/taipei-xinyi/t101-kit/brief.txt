Taipei 101 (C.Y. Lee & Partners, 2004; 508 m to the spire tip, 101 floors): the hero of the render, seen from Elephant Mountain at dusk. It must read at a glance as THIS tower: a bamboo stalk or pagoda of EIGHT identical stacked segments of eight floors each, every segment a square inverted frustum whose green-blue glass walls flare OUTWARD as they rise, so each segment overhangs the foot of the one above; it stands on a tapering pedestal with a giant coin medallion on each face, carries ruyi ornaments, and ends in a stepped pinnacle and a slim spire. It is built here as TILING MODULES, three plots: the pedestal and podium mall on the real footprint, ONE segment (compose stacks it eight times, so its foot must sit exactly on its own top), and the crown and spire (compose sets it on the eighth segment). The three must line up: the tower's square is centred in the 76 x 76 boxes; the pedestal's top and every segment's foot are the same 62 x 62 square centred in that box (box x and z 7..68), every segment's top and the crown's foot are the 76 x 76 square. Glass: a fine green-blue grid (cyan and light blue stained glass panes in a frame of light gray / gray metal mullions, or cyan/light blue stained glass blocks with prismarine accents), with a darker band at every floor line. Dusk lighting as the real building: white-gold light strips running up every segment's four flared corners, a glowing band at the top ledge of each segment, a scatter of lit office floors, the observatory floors and the crown lit brightly, red aircraft lights on the spire.

Scale: 1.4 blocks per metre (a storey is about 5 blocks). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). These are real buildings of the Xinyi district of Taipei, Taiwan, for a hero render at DUSK from the Elephant Mountain trail, about 1.8 km to the SOUTH-EAST and 200 blocks up: the camera looks north-west across the district, so the SOUTH and EAST faces and the ROOFS and crowns are what it sees most; the street fronts matter too. Placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION, as in the maps: north is low z, south high z, east high x, west low x; Xinyi Road runs along x north of Taipei 101. The district is flat (the GROUND map gives the ground above y 0 per cell). Build only on the footprint cells (#). Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass (lit lobbies, shop windows). No villagers. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height. Make it read at dusk: warm and cool light in a scattering of windows (not all), lit lobbies and shopfronts at street level, floodlit crowns and red aircraft lights on the towers where the real building has them; light every roof and terrace so no mob spawns.

THE TOWER PEDESTAL (floors 1 to 26) stands in the south-east of the footprint at plot x 96..171, z 108..183 (76 x 76): a truncated pyramid, the full 76 x 76 square at the ground tapering INWARD evenly to a 62 x 62 square (plot x 103..164, z 115..176) at its top course, y 157, which is a solid flat roof (the first segment sits on it); green-blue glass with the same grid as the segments, a tall glazed entrance hall on the north and east faces. On each of its four faces, centred, near the top (about y 128..154): a GIANT COIN MEDALLION, a round disc about 22 across and 2 deep standing proud of the wall, gold or bronze (gold block, yellow/orange terracotta, raw gold), with a square hole in its middle, lit from below. THE PODIUM MALL (Taipei 101 Mall) fills the rest of the footprint west and north of the pedestal: six storeys of glass and pale stone, its roof stepping in terraces from about y 70 at its north-west block down to about y 35 along the street edges, roof gardens and skylights, the main entrance on Xinyi Road (north, low z) under a glass canopy, lit shop windows all round at street level, the mall's sign. Nothing above y 161 here.
 plot 1, x 197..272, z 0..75 (76 x 76), up to y 46: ONE SEGMENT (stacked eight times) — one of the eight identical segments, 8 floors, 47 tall (y 0..46). Its FOOT (y 0) is the 62 x 62 square centred in the box (x and z 7..68): a solid floor course, so the segment stands on the pedestal and on the segment below; its walls lean OUTWARD evenly to the full 76 x 76 square at y 44..46 (one block out about every 6 courses, as a smooth stepped flare on all four faces), and its TOP (y 46) is a solid flat ledge over the whole 76 x 76 square (the next segment's foot stands in its middle, so the outer ring of the ledge shows as the pagoda overhang). Eight floors of green-blue glass grid with the floor lines; the four corners are NOTCHED (a double sawtooth step at each corner, as the real building), and at the foot of each face, centred, a RUYI ornament (a cloud-scroll head over a short stem, about 7 wide and 9 tall, pale metal: iron block, quartz, light gray concrete, 1 deep, standing proud); the top ledge carries a lit band (sea lanterns or shroomlight behind glass) all round, and white-gold light strips run up the four flared corners. Some office windows lit, most dark green glass. Keep the inside hollow behind the glass (a floor slab every 6 blocks is fine).
