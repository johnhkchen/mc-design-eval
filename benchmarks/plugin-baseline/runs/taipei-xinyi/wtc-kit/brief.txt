The Taipei World Trade Center group west of Taipei 101 along Xinyi Road: the International Trade Building (1988, 34 floors, 143 m), Exhibition Hall 1 (the long low hall), the Grand Hyatt Taipei and the Taipei International Convention Center (TICC). They stand left of Taipei 101 in the camera's view.

Scale: 1.4 blocks per metre (a storey is about 5 blocks). For a public catalog for a Minecraft 1.21.1 server: only blocks that exist in 1.21.1 (no pale oak, no "iron_chain" (it is "chain" in 1.21.1), nothing 1.21.4+). These are real buildings of the Xinyi district of Taipei, Taiwan, for a hero render at DUSK from the Elephant Mountain trail, about 1.8 km to the SOUTH-EAST and 200 blocks up: the camera looks north-west across the district, so the SOUTH and EAST faces and the ROOFS and crowns are what it sees most; the street fronts matter too. Placement code sets each module on its real footprint exactly as it stands (no turning), so build each IN ITS SITE ORIENTATION, as in the maps: north is low z, south high z, east high x, west low x; Xinyi Road runs along x north of Taipei 101. The district is flat (the GROUND map gives the ground above y 0 per cell). Build only on the footprint cells (#). Exterior only: no interiors beyond what shows through glass (lit lobbies, shop windows). No villagers. Nothing may leave its plot or exceed its stated height. Make it read at dusk: warm and cool light in a scattering of windows (not all), lit lobbies and shopfronts at street level, floodlit crowns and red aircraft lights on the towers where the real building has them; light every roof and terrace so no mob spawns.
