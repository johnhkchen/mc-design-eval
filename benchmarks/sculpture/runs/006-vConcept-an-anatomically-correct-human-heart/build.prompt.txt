## Finalized design document
# Design Document — Freestanding Sculpture: Anatomically Correct Human Heart

## 1. Read of the subject
A human heart is a **fist-sized, lopsided muscular cone**, not the symmetric valentine. Essential masses: a fat **rounded body** (the ventricles) tapering to a blunt **apex** that tilts down-left, two soft **atrial bulges** on top, and a **crown of great vessels** erupting from the base. The two unmistakable cues are the **arching aorta** (the candy-cane curve looping up and over) and the **cluster of branching tubes** at the top. Those, plus the tilted teardrop body, make a viewer say "heart, the real one" — not the cartoon.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
- **Body (ventricles):** ~14 tall × ~14 wide × ~12 deep ovoid, asymmetric — right side flatter, left rounder. Apex offset to one side so it leans, never bilaterally mirrored.
- **Vessel crown:** ~10–12 blocks of stacked tubes above the body; the aortic arch is the tallest element, carrying the **longest dimension to ~32** (apex-to-arch-top).
- **Silhouette:** FRONT — teardrop body with a vessel tangle on top, aorta hooking right. SIDE — clearly front-heavy, vessels leaning back. 3/4 — the arch reads as a true loop with a hole through it (the recognizing angle).
- **Footing:** vessels are cut flat at the base and seated on a **2-block plinth disc** of polished stone so it sits stable on the turntable, base-weighted, never top-heavy.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
Near-complementary, warm-dominant:
- **Dominant — `red_terracotta`** (muscle): muted, fleshy, less neon than concrete.
- **Supporting — `red_concrete`** (oxygenated aorta/arteries) and **`light_blue_terracotta`** (vena cava, pulmonary veins): the cool blue pops the warm mass via complement.
- **Accent — `yellow_terracotta`** (coronary fat/vessels, sparingly tracing the body's grooves).
- **Plinth — `polished_andesite`** (neutral, recedes).

## 4. Surface & motifs
- **Coronary grooves:** single-block channels of `yellow_terracotta` snaking down the ventricle front — bold, block-scale, the "anatomy" tell.
- **Vessel rings:** each great vessel is a clean 3–4-wide tube; blue vs. red separation reads instantly.

## 5. Block-budget plan (~32 longest edge)
Apex→arch-top 32: plinth 2, ventricular body 14, atria 4, vessel crown + aortic arch 12. Body footprint ≈14×12 — compact, readable from every side.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~32 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~32(x) × 32(y)
  × 32(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.
