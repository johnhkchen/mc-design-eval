## Finalized design document
# Moai Statue — Freestanding Block Sculpture (Design Document)

## 1. Read of the subject
A moai is fundamentally a **monolithic head-and-torso column**: an oversized, blocky head fused to a narrow, armless body. Instant recognition lives in three features — the **heavy straight brow ridge**, the **long wedge nose**, and the **jutting chin/lips**. A faint stone integrity (one continuous mass, no neck taper) sells "carved volcanic tuff" rather than "robot." If those carry, a viewer names it at a glance from any side.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
One stacked column, footed for stability:
- **Base/plinth** (y0–4): a 12×10 buried-foot pedestal so the tall mass never reads as floating or top-heavy.
- **Torso** (y4–26): a slab ~14 wide × 8 deep, gently leaning back.
- **Head** (y26–48): ~16 tall, the dominant volume — head is ~45% of total height (moai are head-heavy).

Ratios: head ≈ 0.45 H, torso ≈ 0.45 H, plinth ≈ 0.10 H. **Front:** symmetric, brow + nose + chin cascade. **Side:** the defining profile — sloped forehead, protruding nose/lips, flat back. **3/4:** brow and nose cast the silhouette that confirms the read.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Monochromatic stone harmony** — a moai is one carved rock; restraint reinforces monolith-ness.
- **Dominant:** `gray_concrete` (whole body/head — flat, even volcanic gray).
- **Supporting:** `andesite` / `stone` (mottling at base and weathered flanks for tonal break).
- **Accent (sparing):** `deepslate` in the deep eye-sockets and under-brow shadow — value contrast, not hue, carves the face.

(Named-block value can render dark — gray_concrete reads convincingly stony.)

## 4. Surface & motifs
- **Brow-shadow band:** a 1-block `deepslate` recess line under the brow across the whole face — the strongest recognition motif.
- **Weathering mottle:** scattered single `andesite`/`stone` blocks on lower torso and plinth, bold at block scale, evoking eroded tuff.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the 48-block longest edge (height): plinth **5**, torso **22**, head **21**. Footprint stays compact at ~14×10. Nose projects ~3 blocks forward; brow ~2. Eyes are 2×2 deepslate voids set under the brow — no detail finer than a block.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~48 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~48(x) × 48(y)
  × 48(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.

## Realize the document with craft

- Keep the object SOLID and connected — no detached floating parts unless the subject truly has
