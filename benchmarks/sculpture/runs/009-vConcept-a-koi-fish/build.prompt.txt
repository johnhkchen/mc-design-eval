## Finalized design document
# Design Document — Freestanding Koi Fish Sculpture

## 1. Read of the subject
A koi is a fat, muscular carp frozen mid-swim. Three masses define it: a **deep, rounded body** (widest just behind the head), a **broad blunt head** with a downturned mouth, and a **wide flowing tail fin**. The instantly-namable cues are the **gentle S-curve of the swimming body** and the **Kohaku color pattern** — crisp red-orange blotches on white. Get the arch and the two-tone patches and a viewer names it on sight; without them it's just "a fish."

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Longest dimension ≈32 blocks along the nose→tail axis. Budget: head 7, body 16, tail fin 9. The body is a cigar that swells to ~10 blocks tall × 8 wide at the shoulder, tapering to a 3-wide wrist before the tail flares back out to ~11 tall. Impose a shallow **S-bend** in plan (head nudged left, tail swung right) so the 3/4 and top views read as *motion*, not a sausage. **Front view:** tall teardrop with pectoral fins flaring like ears. **Side view:** the signature arch + fanned tail. The fish rests belly-down on a small **base of stacked blue blocks** (3-block "splash") so it never floats and won't tip.

## 3. Color palette (apply color theory)
**Kohaku two-tone — warm/neutral split** for maximum legibility:
- **Dominant white:** `white_concrete` (clean body ground).
- **Supporting red-orange:** `orange_concrete` + a few `red_concrete` for the boldest patches — the koi blotches.
- **Accent black:** `black_concrete` for eyes and lip line.
- **Base:** `light_blue_concrete` water splash (cool complement makes the warm fish pop).
Restrained, high-contrast, period-true to a Kohaku.

## 4. Surface & motifs
Two block-scale motifs: **(a) the patches** — irregular 3–5-block orange islands on white, asymmetric front-to-back so every angle differs; **(b) fin ridging** — tail and dorsal stepped in 1-block ribs to read as fin rays. Eyes are single black blocks with one white pixel-block highlight.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Nose→tail 32 (head 7 / body 16 / tail 9); body girth ~8×10; fins add ~4 each side; base 3 tall. Compact, turntable-ready.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~32 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~32(x) × 32(y)
  × 32(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.

- Keep the object SOLID and connected — no detached floating parts unless the subject truly has
