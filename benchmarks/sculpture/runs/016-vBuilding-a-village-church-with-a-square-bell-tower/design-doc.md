# Design Document — Village Church with Square Bell Tower

## 1. Read of the building
A modest **rural parish church**, English/Northern-European vernacular, late-medieval idiom. The instant-recognition signature is the silhouette: a **tall square bell tower** rising hard above a long **steep-gabled nave**, topped by a stubby spire. Tower + nave + a pitched roof with a cross reads "church" before any detail registers — this is the gestalt to protect at every angle.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Two masses joined in an **L/T**: a long **nave** (rectangular box, gabled) and a **square tower** at one gable end. Footprint ≈ **48 (length) × 14 (depth)**; nave wall height ≈ 12, ridge ≈ 18. Tower is **10×10**, rising to ≈ 30, with a short 4-block pyramidal spire — the dominant vertical (tower height ≈ 2× nave height) keeps it church-like, not barn-like.
- **Entry (west):** through the tower base — arched double door.
- **North & south elevations:** long nave walls, rhythmic windows + buttresses.
- **East:** gable end with a single tall window (the "altar" end).
- **Roof:** steep **gable** over the nave (reads from every side), **pyramidal spire** on the tower. Both masses sit on a 1-block stone plinth — heavy base, stable footing.

## 3. Color palette (analogous + restraint)
Stone-and-timber earth harmony, low chroma:
- **Walls (dominant):** `cobblestone` — grey, weathered, masonry read.
- **Roof:** `dark_oak_planks` — warm dark mass, strong gable contrast against grey.
- **Trim/quoins/buttresses (supporting):** `stone_bricks` — crisp edges, corner emphasis.
- **Accent (sparing):** `polished_andesite` window framing; a single `gold_block` cross finial atop the spire — one bright point, hierarchy preserved.

## 4. Surface & architectural detail
- **Buttresses:** 1-block-deep `stone_brick` pilasters every 6 blocks along the nave walls — chunky, voxel-survivable, gives shadow rhythm.
- **Windows:** tall 1×3 recesses framed in `polished_andesite`, repeated on the rhythm between buttresses; one large east window.
- **Cornice:** single `stone_brick` band where wall meets roof; **quoined** tower corners.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the ~48 longest edge: **nave ~32**, **tower ~10**, plinth/buttress overhang ~6. Roof ~40% of visible mass (dark oak); walls cobblestone; openings ~15% of wall area — one compact, readable structure.
