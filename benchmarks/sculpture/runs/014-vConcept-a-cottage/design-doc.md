# Tudor Cottage — Design Document

*(Authored from the concept image for T-071-01 / E-21 — the cottage's `design_doc` prior. The concept
image is the primary signal; this doc names the visible materials so the material map has a prior.)*

## 1. Read of the building
A **half-timbered Tudor cottage** — a cosy two-storey village house. A heavy **stone ground floor**
carries a lighter **timber-framed upper storey** of dark beams over cream plaster panels, under a
steep **dark plank gable roof** with a tall stone-and-brick **chimney**. The eye reads three stacked
material bands: stone base, striped timber/plaster middle, dark roof.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Roughly square footprint, ~16–20 blocks wide, two storeys, an L/gabled massing with the upper storey
**jettied** (overhanging) slightly over the stone base. Steep gable roof, ridge well above the eaves;
a chimney rises through the roof on one flank. Longest dimension ≈ 24 blocks tall.

## 3. Color palette (warm timber + cream + cool stone — near-tone pairs throughout)
- **Ground-floor walls (base):** `cobblestone` / `stone_bricks` — cool grey coursed stone, the
  load-bearing plinth. (Two near-tone greys, distinct grain.)
- **Upper-storey frame (trim):** `dark_oak_log` — the dark structural timbers, vertical studs + corner
  posts + eaves beams; the building's signature stripes.
- **Upper-storey infill (walls):** light cream plaster — `white_terracotta` (warm off-white, not pure
  white) between the timbers.
- **Roof:** `dark_oak_planks` / `spruce_planks` — dark warm-brown plank courses (a near-tone pair with
  the timber frame — DISTINCT material, must not collapse into the timber).
- **Chimney:** `cobblestone` shaft with a `bricks` cap — warm red-brown brick against cool grey stone.
- **Openings:** `spruce_door` / dark plank shutters in the window reveals; a lantern by the door.

## 4. Surface & architectural detail
Motif 1 — **half-timbering:** regular vertical `dark_oak_log` studs over cream infill across the upper
storey, the defining rhythm. Motif 2 — **stone base coursing:** coursed grey stone with framed window
openings. Roof in stepped dark plank courses; chimney as a bold vertical accent.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Stone base ~6 high, timber/plaster storey ~7 high, gable roof ~10. Near-tone material pairs to keep
distinct: cobblestone vs stone_bricks (base), dark_oak_log vs dark_oak_planks (timber vs roof),
cobblestone vs bricks (chimney).
