# Stone Gatehouse — Design Document

## 1. Read of the building
A **medieval defensive gatehouse** — the fortified entry block of a castle or town wall, c. 12th–14th century. Fundamentally a tall, solid stone **tower pierced by a single passage**. Two features name it at a glance: the **arched gate** tunnelling through its base, and the **peaked gable** rising directly above that arch on the entry face. Massing is one upright stone cube, heavier than it is wide, so it reads as a guard-block, not a house.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
One mass: a rectangular stone keep. **Footprint 28 W × 20 D**, **wall height 30**, **ridge tip at 44** (longest dimension ≈ 48 vertical). Ratio ~1.5:1 tall-to-wide — proud and defensive. The **ridge runs front-to-back**, so the gable **triangle faces the entry (front)**, framing the arch. Front/back elevations show the gable peak + arch; **side elevations** show the long slope of the gable and narrow slit windows. Roof: **steep gable**. Two shallow **corner buttress-pilasters** (2-block-deep) ground all four corners; a 2-high plinth course foots it firmly to the earth. Entry side = gabled front.

## 3. Color palette (monochrome + warm accent)
**Near-monochrome stone harmony** — restraint suits fortification.
- **Dominant walls:** `stone_bricks` (cool grey, the structural body).
- **Texture/banding:** `cobblestone` (plinth + buttress faces, rougher grain).
- **Roof:** `deepslate_tiles` (dark cool grey — recedes, lets the gable read as silhouette).
- **Accent (sparing):** `dark_oak_log` arch voussoirs + door, the one warm note drawing the eye to the gate.

## 4. Surface & architectural detail
Motif 1 — **the arch:** a chunky **6 W × 10 H** opening, ringed by a 1-block `dark_oak_log` voussoir band, repeated front and back. Motif 2 — **banding:** a single `cobblestone` string-course at wall-top and the 2-high plinth, wrapping all four sides. Windows: bold **1×3 vertical slits**, two per side elevation. Buttresses read as corner pilasters.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the ~48 vertical: **plinth 2**, **walls 28** (arch + slits subtracted), **gable+roof 14**. Footprint 28×20 keeps it one compact, in-the-round block.
