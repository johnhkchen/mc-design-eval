# Tithe Barn — Design Document

## 1. Read of the building
A medieval **tithe barn** (12th–15th c. England): a long monastic store for grain paid as tithe. It reads instantly as one thing — a **long, low stone box under an enormous steep roof**, where the roof is taller than the walls. The giveaway features: the **vast wagon doors** punched into one long side (cart-high, the only big opening) and the **near-windowless masonry**. Barn, not church, not house.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
One single rectangular mass — no wings. **Footprint 48 × 16**, eave height **8**, ridge height **18** (so the gabled roof, ~10 tall, is *bigger* than the 8-tall wall — the defining proportion). Length:height ≈ 2.7:1, a grounded horizontal building.
- **Roof: steep symmetrical GABLE**, ridge running the 48-length, ~55° pitch, full triangular gable ends.
- **Long elevations (48-side):** stone wall broken by **two wagon-door bays** (the south is the entry); buttresses rhythm the wall.
- **Gable ends (16-side):** blank stone triangles rising to the ridge — a single slit vent high up.
- **Roof:** two clean planes meeting at a straight ridge.
Sits on a continuous stone plinth course — heavy, stable, earth-bound.

## 3. Color palette
**Analogous earth harmony**, low-chroma — quiet and old, as weathered stone + timber should be.
- **Walls (dominant):** `cobblestone` — irregular, grey, reads as rubble masonry.
- **Roof (supporting):** `dark_oak_planks` — deep brown shingled plane, the dark mass that dominates.
- **Doors & gable timber (supporting/trim):** `spruce_planks` — warmer timber for the wagon doors and ridge.
- **Plinth/buttress accent:** `stone_bricks` — crisper dressed stone at base and buttress faces.
No saturated color; hierarchy by value (dark roof / mid walls / light dressed accents).

## 4. Surface & architectural detail
- **Buttresses:** chunky 2-wide × 1-deep `stone_bricks` piers stepping the long walls every ~10 blocks — the chief repeating motif, giving rhythm and shadow.
- **Wagon doors:** 3-wide × 5-tall `spruce_planks` openings framed by `stone_bricks` jambs and a flat lintel.
- **Roof texture:** `dark_oak_stairs` along the eave course and `dark_oak_slab` ridge cap for crisp edges.
- **Plinth:** one `stone_bricks` banding course wrapping all four sides.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the 48 longest edge: **~44 walls/roof span**, **~2×2 door bays** (×2), **buttresses every 10**; depth 16, height 18. Walls consume the lower third, the gabled roof the upper two-thirds — one compact, readable barn.
