# Freestanding Block Sculpture — "A Dancing Man"

## 1. Read of the subject
A dancing man IS a human figure caught mid-motion: the body thrown off its vertical axis. The essential masses are head, torso, and four limbs — but recognition comes from the *pose*, not anatomy. The instantly-namable features: **one leg planted, the other kicked out**, and **arms flung asymmetrically** (one up, one out). Frozen diagonal energy is the whole point; a symmetric standing figure would read as a statue, not a *dancing* man.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Heroic-cartoon proportions for block-scale legibility: head ≈ 6 tall, torso ≈ 10, legs ≈ 14, total ~30 plus a base, fitting the 32 budget. Torso tilts; hips and shoulders counter-rotate (contrapposto) so silhouette differs per view. **Front:** wide A-frame stance, arms making a jagged line. **Side:** clear lean and a trailing kicked leg — the strongest "motion" read. **3/4:** twist resolves, most dynamic. Stability: the planted leg + a flat **2-block-tall plinth** (6×6) catches the figure's center of mass, so the kicked leg can cantilever without toppling the read.

## 3. Color palette (analogous + accent)
- **Dominant:** `terracotta` (warm clay skin/body) — earthy, human, matte.
- **Supporting:** `red_terracotta` (shirt/torso) and `brown_terracotta` (trousers/legs) — analogous warm earth tones for a unified, lively body.
- **Accent (sparing):** `gold_block` — a hat or shoes, catching the eye and crowning the motion.
Warm analogous harmony reads as energy and flesh; the single cool-bright gold accent gives focus without circus-color chaos.

## 4. Surface & motifs
Two bold block-scale motifs: a **2-block-thick limb stripe** where shirt meets trousers (a clear color belt at the waist), and **blocky 2×2 hands/feet caps** so extremities punch out against the slimmer limbs. Both survive voxelization.

## 5. Block-budget plan (~32 longest edge)
Plinth 2 · legs 14 · torso 10 · head 6 = 32 vertical. Horizontal spread ~14–16 (stance + outflung arm). Limbs 2–3 blocks thick; head 4–5 cube. Compact, readable, in the round.
