## Finalized design document
# Freestanding Sculpture — "A Bow and Arrow"

## 1. Read of the subject
A bow is fundamentally a **tensioned arc**: a curved springy limb pulled into a "D" by a taut string, with a straight arrow nocked across it ready to loose. The instant-read features are the **double-curved bow arc** and the **dead-straight diagonal arrow** crossing it — the contrast of curve-against-line is what names it. Drawn (mid-pull) reads as "bow," not a plain stick.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
- **Bow limb:** the longest mass, ~32 blocks tall, a vertical recurve "D" 2 blocks thick, 3 deep — enough body to read solid from any angle, not a flat cutout.
- **String:** a 1-wide line spanning the limb tips, bowed inward at the grip.
- **Arrow:** ~22 blocks, a straight diagonal shaft crossing the grip, point leading up-forward, fletching trailing low-back — its diagonal breaks the bow's vertical and sells depth in 3/4 view.
- **Base:** a low oval plinth (~8×5×2) the lower limb tip plants into, so the piece stands stable, not floating.
- **Silhouette:** FRONT = D + diagonal arrow; SIDE = the limb's recurve profile; 3/4 = both curve and line resolve — the strongest angle.

## 3. Color palette (apply color theory)
**Warm-wood + metal accent**, an analogous earth scheme that reads as a crafted weapon.
- **Dominant — limb & grip:** `spruce_planks` (rich brown wood).
- **Supporting — arrow shaft + base:** `stripped_oak_log` (lighter tan, separates arrow from bow).
- **String:** `bone_block` (pale taut cord).
- **Accent — arrowhead:** `iron_block` (cold metallic point); **fletching:** `red_concrete` (the single saturated spark that draws the eye to "arrow").

## 4. Surface & motifs
- **Grip wrap:** a 3-block band of `dark_oak_planks` at the limb's center — bold tonal break marking the hand.
- **Fletching vanes:** stepped red blocks forming a clear arrow tail; recurve at both limb tips curls forward for the "ready" pose.

## 5. Block-budget plan
32 limb height · 22 arrow (diagonal) · 18 string span · grip band 3 · base 8×5×2 · arrowhead+fletching ~4 each. Compact ~32×24×8 footprint.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~32 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~32(x) × 32(y)
  × 32(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.

## Realize the document with craft
- Build the essential masses and the recognizable features the document names; honor its
  proportions and its dominant/supporting/accent palette hierarchy.
- Keep the object SOLID and connected — no detached floating parts unless the subject truly has
  them (and then bridge them so they are supported).
