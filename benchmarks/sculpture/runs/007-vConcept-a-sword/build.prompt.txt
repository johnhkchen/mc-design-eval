## Finalized design document
# Design Document — Freestanding Block Sculpture: "A Sword"

## 1. Read of the subject
A sword IS a long straight blade meeting a perpendicular crossguard, capped by a gripped hilt and pommel. The instant-recognition cue is the **cruciform silhouette**: a dominant vertical line crossed near its base by a short bar. The blade's **taper to a point** and the **bright metal/dark handle contrast** seal the read. We display it **point-up, vertically**, the most heraldic and stable orientation for a tabletop piece.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Four stacked volumes along Y:
- **Blade** (≈21 blocks): a 3×1 cross-section column tapering to 1×1 at the tip — flat front/side, a slim diamond in plan so it reads as a blade, not a post.
- **Crossguard** (≈9 blocks wide, 2 tall): the horizontal bar, the only wide mass.
- **Grip** (≈4 blocks): a 2×2 wrapped handle.
- **Pommel** (≈2 blocks): a 2×2×2 knob anchoring the bottom.
Total ≈32 tall. Ratios: blade ≈ 2/3 of height; guard width ≈ ¼ of height. From FRONT the cross reads boldest; from SIDE it thins to an edge-on slab (correct — swords are flat); the **3/4 view** reveals the diamond blade cross-section, proving it's in the round. A **3-wide, 2-thick pommel-grounded footing** plus a small **stone plinth** (5×3×1) keeps it upright, not top-heavy.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Cool metal vs. warm handle — complementary contrast** suits the weapon's steel-and-leather identity.
- **Dominant:** `iron_block` — bright blade steel.
- **Supporting:** `polished_andesite` — blade edge/fuller shading for value depth.
- **Accent (warm):** `dark_oak_log` grip + `gold_block` pommel & guard tips — regal warm pop against cool steel.
- **Base:** `stone` plinth (neutral).

## 4. Surface & motifs
- **Central fuller**: a single vertical `polished_andesite` stripe down the blade's front face — the blade's defining groove, bold at block scale.
- **Guard caps**: `gold_block` at each crossguard end, mirroring the pommel for symmetry.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Longest edge (32, vertical): blade 21 · guard zone 2 · grip 4 · pommel 2 · plinth 1–2. Width capped at 9 (guard); depth capped at 3 (blade base) — a compact, readable cruciform.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~32 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~32(x) × 32(y)
  × 32(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.
