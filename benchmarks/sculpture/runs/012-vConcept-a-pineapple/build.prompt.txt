## Finalized design document
# Design Document — Freestanding Pineapple Sculpture (~16 blocks tall)

## 1. Read of the subject
A pineapple is two masses in tension: a **fat ovoid fruit body** and a **spiky radiating crown of leaves** roughly as tall as the body itself. The instant-recognition cues are (a) the **diamond crosshatch lattice** on a golden-amber skin and (b) the **fan of stiff, pointed green fronds** erupting from the top. Get the silhouette — egg below, flame of spikes above — and a viewer names it before seeing a single block of texture.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Total height ~16. Split **~9 body / ~7 crown**. Body is a vertical ovoid: widest (~7×7) at one-third height, tapering to ~3×3 at the shoulders and a flat ~5×5 footing at the base for turntable stability. Built radially symmetric so FRONT, SIDE, and 3/4 read identically — correct for a fruit. Crown is 6–8 leaf-blades of stepped height (tallest ~7, outer ~3) splaying outward, so the top silhouette is jagged from every angle, never a flat disc. Center of mass sits low in the belly — stable, not top-heavy.

## 3. Color palette (analogous warm + complementary accent)
- **Dominant body:** `orange_terracotta` — warm amber skin, the pineapple's signature.
- **Supporting lattice:** `yellow_terracotta` raised studs marking the diamond grid; analogous warm neighbor gives texture without clashing.
- **Crown:** `green_concrete` leaves — the **complementary** punch against orange that makes both colors louder and screams "pineapple."
- **Accent:** sparing `brown_terracotta` for shadowed lattice seams / the basal ring.

Warm analogous skin + single complementary crown = restrained, high-recognition harmony.

## 4. Surface & motifs
**Motif 1 — diamond crosshatch:** alternate `yellow_terracotta` studs on the `orange_terracotta` field in a staggered grid, one block per "eye," bold enough to survive voxelization. **Motif 2 — bladed fronds:** each crown leaf is a 1-wide stepped stack with a pointed tip, edges jagged.

## 5. Block-budget plan (16 long edge)
- Footing/base ring: 1 (height 0)
- Ovoid body: 8 (heights 1–9), max width ~7
- Shoulder taper into crown: 1
- Crown fronds: 7 (heights 9–16)
Compact ~7×7 footprint; vertical drama in the crown.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~16 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~16(x) × 16(y)
  × 16(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.

  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.
