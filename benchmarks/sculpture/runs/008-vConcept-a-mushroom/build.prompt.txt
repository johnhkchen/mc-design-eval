## Finalized design document
# Design Document — Freestanding Block Sculpture: "A Mushroom"

## 1. Read of the subject
A mushroom is fundamentally **a wide dome perched on a slender stalk** — a top-heavy lollipop silhouette that nothing else shares. The instant-recognition cues are (a) the **overhanging cap** whose rim flares wider than the stem, casting a clear gap of empty air beneath it, and (b) the **classic toadstool livery: a red cap dotted with white**. Pick the *Amanita muscaria* read — it is the single most universally "mushroom" image, so a viewer names it from across the room.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Two stacked volumes on a footing:
- **Cap** — a hemispherical dome, ~22 blocks wide, ~10 tall, belly **overhanging** the stem by ~4 blocks all around so the underside reads as shadow (recall: a dome must overhang or it reads as a pyramid).
- **Stem** — a tapered column ~8 wide at base narrowing to ~6, ~14 tall, slightly bulged near the bottom (the volva foot) for stability.
- **Base** — a low mossy mound, ~12 wide, anchoring it so it never floats or tips.

Total height ≈ **32 blocks**. Front/side read identically (radial symmetry is the point); the 3/4 view shows the cap's circular overhang and the stem's curve — the most flattering angle. Cap:stem height ≈ 1:1.4; cap-width:stem-width ≈ 3:1.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Complementary** (red ↔ green) for maximum pop, the natural toadstool scheme:
- **Dominant:** `red_concrete` — cap.
- **Supporting:** `bone_block` — stem (warm off-white, organic, not sterile).
- **Supporting:** `moss_block` — base mound (the green complement grounds the red).
- **Accent (sparing):** `white_concrete` — the cap spots and the underside gill ring.

Red on green sings; bone stem stays neutral between them.

## 4. Surface & motifs
- **Cap spots:** 5–7 bold `white_concrete` single-block dabs scattered across the dome — the signature motif, big enough to survive voxelization.
- **Gill ring:** a `white_concrete` band on the cap underside where it meets air, reinforcing the overhang.

## 5. Block-budget plan (32 longest edge)
Base mound **2** · stem **14** · cap dome **10–12** · cap rim flare folded into width (22 wide). Footprint ≈ 22×22, compact and readable.

## Scale (honor it)
- Target ~32 blocks along the LONGEST dimension. Bounding box up to ~32(x) × 32(y)
  × 32(z); build a compact, solid object that fills that envelope without sprawling.
- Use `fill`/`box` for the major masses and `line` for struts/edges so you can afford solid
  volume; reserve `voxel` (with block `state` for stairs/slabs) for shaping curves, facets,
  and surface detail. Approximate curves/round forms with stepped stairs+slabs, not flat panels.

## Realize the document with craft

- Keep the object SOLID and connected — no detached floating parts unless the subject truly has
