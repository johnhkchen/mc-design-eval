# Moai Statue — Sculpture Design Document

## 1. Read of the subject
A moai IS a colossal head-and-torso monolith: a single upright slab where the **head devours ~⅔ of the height**. Recognition lives in three features — the **long straight nose** as a vertical ridge, the **heavy overhanging browline**, and the **jutting jaw/chin** that tilts the face slightly skyward. Get those and a viewer names it instantly; miss the brow and it becomes a generic totem.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Three stacked volumes on the y-axis: **buried torso (lower ⅓)**, **head (upper ⅔)**, capped by a flat top. Height ≈16, head depth ≈8, width ≈7 — a deep, front-facing wedge, NOT a thin plate, so the 3/4 view shows real skull mass. Proportions: head 2:1 over torso; nose runs a full ~6 blocks down the centerline. **Front** reads as the iconic face; **side** reads as the sloping forehead + protruding brow + receding chin (the famous profile); **3/4** marries both. Footing: torso flares to a wider ~7×5 base block so it sits planted and bottom-heavy — never top-heavy.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Monochromatic gray harmony** — fitting because moai are carved from one volcanic tuff; restraint sells "single hewn stone." 
- **Dominant:** `gray_concrete` (body mass). 
- **Supporting:** `andesite` and `cobblestone` (weathered texture variation on cheeks/torso). 
- **Accent (sparing):** `deepslate` recessed into eye sockets and under the brow for shadow-depth the silhouette alone can't give.

## 4. Surface & motifs
Two motifs: **the carved face channels** — eye sockets, nostril, and mouth line cut ONE block deep so shadow defines them at block scale; and **stone weathering** — andesite/cobblestone scattered in vertical streaks down the torso, mimicking erosion runoff. Both must be whole-block; finer detail vanishes in voxelization.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the ~16-block vertical edge: **base/torso 5**, **jaw-to-mouth 3**, **nose/cheeks 4**, **brow-to-flat-crown 4**. Depth-8 reserved mostly for the head wedge; brow protrudes 1–2 blocks forward of the eyes. Compact ~7×8×16 footprint.
