# Moai — Freestanding Block Sculpture (Design Document)

## 1. Read of the subject
A moai is a **monolithic head-and-torso figure**: roughly three-fifths head, two-fifths body, no legs. It reads instantly from one cluster of features — a **heavy straight brow shelf**, a **long wedge nose**, **sunken eye sockets**, **thin pressed lips**, and a **jutting blunt chin**. Secondary tells: **elongated rectangular ears** down the sides and **stub hands meeting over the lower belly**. Get the brow-nose-chin axis right and a viewer names it at a glance.

## 2. Form & proportion (in the round)
Stacked volumes, all rectilinear-slab: **base plinth → torso block → neck pinch → head wedge**. Head occupies the top ~19 of 32 blocks, body ~11, plinth ~2. The head is a **front-heavy wedge**, deeper top-to-chin than wide, so the **3/4 view shows the nose-ridge profile** that the front flattens. Side silhouette: a backward-leaning slab with the face overhanging the chest. Ears project ~1 block per side. A **footprint ~12×10** plinth keeps it bottom-heavy and stable — never top-heavy despite the big head.

## 3. Color palette
**Monochrome-with-warm-crown** harmony — fitting volcanic basalt with one cultural accent.
- **Dominant:** Gray Concrete (the carved tuff body — flat, matte, stony).
- **Supporting:** Andesite and Cobblestone Stairs for weathered shading on cheeks/brow and chin underside.
- **Accent (sparing):** Red Nether Bricks for the **pukao topknot**, the single warm note that draws the eye up and signals "Rapa Nui."

## 4. Surface & motifs
Two motifs at block scale: the **brow-and-nose cross** (a one-block-deep nose ridge meeting a recessed brow line, casting the signature shadow) and **carved eye sockets** (1×1 inset pits). Cheeks and torso left **flat and unbroken** so the bold geometry survives voxelization.

## 5. Block-budget plan
Of the 32-block height: **plinth 2, torso 11, neck 1, head 16, pukao 2**. Width tapers 12→10→9 from plinth to head; depth held ~10 so the profile stays massive. Ears and nose spend the lateral/forward budget; everything else stays compact and readable.
