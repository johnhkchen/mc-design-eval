# Element map: desert adobe market hall

Identity: a flat-roofed adobe trading hall in a desert town. Cream sandstone walls, burnt-orange terracotta bands, dark
timber (vigas, posts, lintels). Three bays on a north street front: shop stall, tall arched entrance, shop stall.

Palette: dominant sandstone / smooth sandstone (about 60%), supporting orange terracotta + red sandstone (about 25%),
accent dark oak timber and white quartz stripes (about 15%).
