# Toolkit gaps hit on the desert market hall

- Roof detection fails on any wall with openings of 3 or more blocks: `mcd roof --detect` reported a 0-cell footprint (stall bays leave columns under the 4-solid "tall" threshold, the exterior flood leaks in and the interior is never filled), so `--preset desert` crashed with "Reduce of empty array". I had to pass `--footprint 1,3,15,11 --y 6` by hand. Closest: `--footprint` / `--y`. `detect` should give a readable error, or fall back to the bounding box of the full-height columns.
- Wind tower: the `desert` preset's tower is deepslate bricks with a quartz cap, a 3x3 box with one hole per face. There is no material, slit or viga option on the CLI (`--tower x,z,w,h,top` only). I cleared it and rebuilt the tower by hand (setting each cell). Closest: `--spec roof.json` with `tower.material`, but slits and beam ends are not expressible.
- The preset's deck and coping palette also used `smooth_quartz` and `mud_brick` (outlier flags in `mcd palette`). I swapped them with a hand-written replace loop. Closest: `--material` / `--trim` on `flat-parapet`, but the preset's own tower and coping ignore them.
- Striped cloth awning: no brush. I placed 2 rows of `red_sandstone` / `smooth_quartz` stairs by hand (terracotta and wool have no stairs, so the concept's orange-white stripes are approximated by stair blocks). Closest: `bunting` (wall banners only), `planter` canopy none. A `awning` brush (colour pair, slope, depth, post spacing, valance) is the biggest missing piece.
- Viga ends (protruding beams): no brush. Placed `dark_oak_log` with axis by hand along each wall. Closest: `brackets` (stairs under an overhang), `fins`.
- Rounded adobe corners/edges: no brush. `setbacks`/`parapet` have no "rounded" style; I used buttress piers with slab caps. Closest: `dome` and `arch` (curved but not wall-edge rounding).
- Stepped dome form: `dome` hemisphere at radius 3 and height 2 came out as a squat terraced stack; no option for a ribbed or banded dome, and no ring-colour option (I used the drum block as the only orange band). Closest: `dome({drum})`.
- Decorated pots, composters, barrels, hay bales: no prop brush. Set by hand with `g.set`. Closest: `planter` (flowers in a box) and `fixture` (lanterns).
- Fences: `B.fence()` returns an unconnected fence, so a rail needs a hand-written run with per-cell `north/south/east/west`. Closest: `parapet({style:"balustrade", rail})` (wall/fence on a roof edge only), `railing` rule in `mcd paint`.
- Signs: `signboard` over a 1-row band produced a dark-oak board with tiny text; the `plaque` brush wants 2+ rows. A single-row wide name board has no tool.
- Portal frame: `arch(frame:)` was not usable once I had also carved the stalls and set a lintel; ended with hand-placed corbel stairs. Closest: `surround arch` (not tried on a 2-thick wall).
- Weathering: `mcd paint ... weather light` and `vary subtle` on sandstone introduced grey granite-like blocks (visible stray speckles). Removed. Closest: `vary` with an explicit block list.
- Roof grows the grid or ignores the size cap: tower height + preset coping + dome pushed the build to 11 layers against a "9 tall" brief; there is no `--max-y` that scales rooftop features.
