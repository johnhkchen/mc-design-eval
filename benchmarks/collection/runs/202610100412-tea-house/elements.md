Identity: a small irimoya (hipped-and-gabled) tea pavilion on a raised timber floor, in a mossy garden.
Palette by role: dominant dark tile + dark timber (roof / frame), supporting pale paper (shoji panels) + warm spruce deck,
accent maple red + lantern amber; grey moss-stone in the garden.
