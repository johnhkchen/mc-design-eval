# BUILD SPEC: Neighbourhood Grocery (brick corner-shop, two storeys + parapet)

## 1. Identity
Red-brick Victorian shopfront. It has a dark-oak storefront with striped awnings, a recessed entrance and a green "GROCERY" sign. A flat-roofed apartment storey sits above, with flower boxes and a white stepped cornice and parapet.

## 2. Footprint / height
- 14 wide (x 0–13) × 14 deep (z 0–13), on a 1-block stone-brick plinth/sidewalk (y0) that extends 1 block past the front and sides.
- Total height is 12 (y0–y11).
- The cornice overhangs 1 block on the front and both sides.

## 3. Zones, bays, top
**Vertical (y):**
- y0: plinth.
- y1–4: shopfront (3 clear + awning line).
- y5: white belt course.
- y6–9: apartment storey (sill y6, windows y7–8, lintel y9).
- y10: cornice, overhanging 1.
- y11: parapet.

**Bays (x), ground floor:**
- x0: corner pier.
- x1–4: display window.
- x5–8: recessed entrance.
- x9–12: display window.
- x13: corner pier.

**Bays (x), upper floor:**
- Three windows at x2–3, x6–7 and x10–11, each with a shutter block on both sides.

**Top form:**
- The parapet is flat, with white corner piers and dark-oak infill panels.
- A raised central pediment spans x5–8, 1–2 blocks higher, with brick infill.
- Under-cornice stairs are upside-down.
- Chimney: 2×2 brick stack on the east wall at z3–4, projecting 1 block, rising to y13 with a cobblestone cap.

## 5. Features (x, y, z from front-left ground corner)
- **Awnings:** left x0–4 and right x9–13, at y4. Stripes alternate red and white, one block each. They slope out 2 blocks from the face, stepping up toward the wall.
- **Sign:** x4–9, y4–5, at z0 over the entrance. It is 6×2 with a green field and "GROCERY" lettering.
- **Lanterns:** (3,3,0) and (10,3,0), hung under the belt course.
- **Entrance:**
  - Recessed to z2, 4 wide (x5–8), with a double door at x6–7, y1–2, open.
  - Two blocks of clear glass sit above the doors.
  - A stepped quartz pediment sits at y5–6 over x5–8.
- **Ground windows:** x1–4 and x9–12, y2–3 (2 high), above a 1-block dark-oak bulkhead.
- **Upper windows:** x2–3, x6–7 and x10–11, y7–8. Each has open trapdoor shutters on both sides and a quartz stair lintel stepped up at y9.
- **Flower boxes:** composters at y6, z−1, with flowers at y7. They sit at x1–3, x5–8 and x10–12.
- **Sidewalk props (z−1 to −2):**
  - A melon stack and crates by x1–2.
  - Barrels and flower pots at x3–4 and x11–12.
  - Pumpkins and hay bales at x9–11.
- **Side wall (east):**
  - Three small dark-oak trapdoor windows at y3 and y7.
  - A 2×4 cobblestone/andesite patch at z4–5, y3–7.

## 6. Depth plan
- **Projections:**

- **Recesses:**
