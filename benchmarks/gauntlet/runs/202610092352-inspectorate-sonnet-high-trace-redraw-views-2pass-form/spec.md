# Build spec: The Inspectorate / Permit Office

## 1. Identity
Two-storey Concord civic office in deepslate and oak, with a quartz-and-glass Permit Office shopfront woven into its ground-floor arcade.

## 2. Footprint and height
- The sheet wins over the stated 17×13×11. The front trace gives **26 wide × 19 tall**, counted off the sheet.
- The side trace is 39 deep × 31 tall. Rescaled to the front's 19 tall, that is **about 24 deep**. The footprint is therefore roughly square, as the 3/4 view shows.
- Plinth and cornice are 26 wide. The wall body is 22 wide (x=2–23).

## 3. Zones, bays, roof
**Vertical, bottom to top:**
- y0–1: plinth and base course.
- y2–8: quartz arcade storey.
- y9: string course and sign band.
- y10–13: dark upper storey (windows y10–12).
- y14: dentil frieze.
- y15: projecting cornice, x1–24.
- y16–18: roof and pediment.

**Bays, left to right (x):**
- 2: corner pier.
- 3–7: arch, 5 wide.
- 8: pier.
- 9–16: central arch with doors, 8 wide.
- 17: pier.
- 18–22: arch, 5 wide.
- 23: corner pier.

Upper oak pilasters sit at x=2, 7, 16 and 23. Upper windows are 2 wide, at about x=5–6, 10–11, 14–15 and 19–20.

**Roof:**
- Stepped deepslate-tile hip, 3 courses (y16–18).
- Central pediment x8–17 peaks at y18, with a gold cross at x12–13, y16–17.
- Finial posts at x=3, 8, 17 and 22. Add a copper-capped chimney at the back right.

## 5. Features (x from the front-left corner, y from the ground)
- **PERMIT OFFICE sign:** x8–17, y8–9. Quartz face with a cyan end-cap at each end and gold lettering. It projects 1.
- **INSPECTORATE plaque:** x8–15, y13–14. Carved stone with a chiseled border, between the central pilasters.
- **Double doors:** x12–13, y3–5, with a glass transom at y6.
- **Shopfront panes:** left arch x4–7 and right arch x18–21, y3–7. Thin quartz mullions.
- **Steps:** 3 risers, x10–15, from y1 up to the door sill.
- **Lanterns:** hung on iron brackets at x=1 and 24 (y7). Pier lanterns at x=3, 8, 17 and 22 (y5–6). Hanging lanterns inside the arches.
- **Banners:** cyan with a gold cross at x=0 and 25, y4–6.
- **Planters:** at x=4, 7, 17 and 21 on y3.
- **Complaint box:** just right of the steps at x≈16, y1–2, overflowing with paper.
- **Shutters:** upper window x19–20 closed. The other three windows are dark and unlit.

## 6. Depth plan
- **Plinth:** projects 1 beyond the wall.
- **Corner piers and pilasters:** proud 1.
- **Central bay (x8–17):** the pediment, plaque and sign project 2.
- **Cornice:** overhangs 2.
- **Arcade glazing:** set 2 back behind the piers. Arch reveals are 1 deep.
- **Steps:** project 3.
- **Upper windows:** recessed 1, with sills proud 1.
- **Side walls (24 deep):**
  - Upper storey: oak pilasters between dark windows, 5 bays.
  - Ground floor: open colonnade of dark oak piers with shallow arches.
  - Roof: hip with a flat ridge.
  - Base: mossy deepslate plinth, with the same cornice running round.
