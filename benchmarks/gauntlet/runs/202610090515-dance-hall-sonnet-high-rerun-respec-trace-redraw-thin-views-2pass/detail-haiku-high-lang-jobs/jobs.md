1. **WHERE:** west face (and mirror on east), all window openings (F/N glass in rows 4–12, cols 10–12, 22–24, 29). **WHAT:** `west openings -> sills cut_sandstone_slab; lintels flat cut_sandstone`. **WHY:** the glass is flush with the wall, so the side reads flat. The concept's openings are framed and shadowed.

2. **WHERE:** north face, wing parapet tops (row 16, cols 3–9 and 17–23, the E/B shoulders). **WHAT:** `north rows 16 cols 3-9,17-23 topmost -> cornice stepped cut_sandstone`. **WHY:** the concept steps the wing crowns up toward the fins with a moulded lip, and the build ends in a bare edge.

3. **WHERE:** north face, outer wing pilasters (cols 1–2 and 24–25, rows 3–15, the A/B sandstone). **WHAT:** `north cols 1-2,24-25 rows 4-15 where A -> vary 0.15 chiseled_sandstone`, then `north cols 1,25 rows 0-15 -> pilasters every 24 cut_sandstone out 1`. **WHY:** the concept's end piers are tall, carved and slightly proud. Here they are plain flat sandstone strips.

4. **WHERE:** north face, main door and ground-floor openings (cols 10–16, rows 0–3, and the F windows at cols 3–7 and 19–23). **WHAT:** `north rows 0-3 openings -> frames chiseled_sandstone; lintels hood cut_sandstone_stairs`. **WHY:** the concept surrounds the entrance and the base windows with a stepped, dressed trim that the build lacks.

5. **WHERE:** north face, tower crown (rows 26–29, cols 11–15, B/U/V). **WHAT:** `north rows 27-29 topmost -> coping slab cut_sandstone`. **WHY:** this gives the spire a capped, stepped top like the concept. The build's tip is a bare tower with a bare end.

6. **WHERE:** roof, outer edge of the stepped terraces (rows z 4 and 11, cols 1–25; rows z 33–34). **WHAT:** `roof wall tops -> coping slab cut_sandstone`. **WHY:** the roof shows hard cut-off steps in the 3/4 view. A capped edge ties the roof steps to the wall bands.

7. **WHERE:** north face, ground row below the wings (row 0–1, cols 1–7 and 19–25, the C/B/A base). **WHAT:** `north rows 0 cols 1-7,19-25 -> plinth cut_sandstone 2`, with `all faces rows 0-3 where B -> weather 0.25`. **WHY:** the concept sets the pale walls on a heavier base that darkens toward the ground. Light wear only at the base keeps this restrained.

8. **WHERE:** west face, mid-wall band (row 13, the I strip under the U stair course at row 14). **WHAT:** `west row 13 -> courses 1 chiseled_sandstone`. **WHY:** this adds a thin carved band under the existing stair cornice. It gives the long flank the horizontal rhythm the concept uses.
