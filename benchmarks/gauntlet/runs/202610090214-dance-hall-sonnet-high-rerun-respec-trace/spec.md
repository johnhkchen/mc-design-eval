# Spec: Art Deco Dance Hall

## 1. Identity
Symmetrical stepped Art Deco dance hall: a central spire tower with tall blue and cream fins, flanking stepped wings, and a jeweled gold marquee over a double-door entrance.

## 2. Footprint and height
- The sheet wins. The stated 22×20×16 is squashed against the drawing. The trace grid is authoritative: **52 wide × 60 tall** (26×30 concept blocks at 2×).
- Depth: **40** (20 ×2). The marquee adds about 8 forward of the facade.
- Centre axis is between columns 25 and 26. Everything is mirrored, with column c ↔ 51−c.

## 3. Zones, bays and top (rows counted from ground, y=0)
**Vertical**
- Plinth: y0–3 (black, with a blue band at y3–4).
- Entrance and wing base: y3–8.
- Marquee: y8–20, with the underside at y8.
- Wing windows: y9–25.
- Wing parapet and cap: y28–31.
- Tower shaft: y20–43.
- Spire: y43–60.

**Bays (x)**
- Outer pilasters: 0–5 and 46–51.
- Wing fields: 6–15 and 36–45.
- Blue fins: 16 and 18 on the left, 33 and 35 on the right. The inner fin rises to y46 and the outer to y42.
- Cream fins: 19–20 and 31–32, up to y46.
- Tower: 21–30, with the stained-glass window at 23–28.

**Top**
- The roof steps down diagonally from the tower base (x16, y42) to the wing parapet (x6, y31) in 2-block risers.
- Each wing has a black cap course at y30–31 under a blue band at y33–34, spanning x3–15.
- The spire is an open pointed-arch frame, 10 wide at its base and narrowing in steps to a 2-wide tip at y60. It encloses a 2-wide glass slot with gold blocks at y53–55 and y46–48.
- The rear roof is terraced: three setback tiers rise toward the tower, 2 blocks per tier.

## 5. Features
Coordinates are x from the left corner, y up.
- **Doors:** two 4-wide leaves at x21–24 and x27–30, y0–5, with a cream mullion at x25–26. The dark oak doors sit in a recess.
- **Entrance piers:** fluted columns at x17–20 and x31–34, y0–8.
- **Marquee:** x15–36, y8–20 (22 wide).
  - Gold stepped crown: peaks at x25–26, y19–20 and steps down 1 block per column pair to y16 at the ends.
  - Orange terrazzo band: y11–15.
  - Lit white sign panel: x19–32, y10–13, flanked by teal and orange jewels.
  - Edge: a single gold course.
- **Central window:** x23–28, y20–34. It is a 2×2 checkerboard of blue, tan, olive and grey stained glass, split by a 2-wide cream mullion at x25–26. Chandeliers hang behind it.
- **Gold sunburst:** x23–28, y36–42, a triangular fan with its base at y36.
- **Wing windows:** a pair of tall glass strips, 2 wide and y9–24, at x9–10 and x41–42. They use banded tan, blue and olive stained glass inside recessed cream arched frames.
- **Wing sunbursts:** a small gold sunburst at x9–10, y28–30 and its mirror. Stepped zig-zag cream hoods sit over each.
- **Wing door niches:** black, x9–12 and x39–42, y1–7, with a gold-lit window inside.
- **Outer pilasters:** cream, with reeded relief and a 1-block stepped cap at y28–30.

## 6. Depth plan
- **Facade:** the main face sits at z=0.
- **Marquee and canopy:** projects 8 forward, with a gold stair-stepped top rising toward the tower and a quartz soffit.
- **Tower shaft:** projects 2 forward of the wings.
- **Blue and cream fins:** fins stand 2 proud of the shaft. Blue fins are 1 thick and cream fins 2 thick.
- **Wing windows and door niches:** recessed 2. Create the recesses by not placing front material, not by burying blocks behind a fill.
- **Entrance:** doors recessed 3 behind the piers.
- **Side walls (depth 40):** each carries 4 projecting pilaster groups (2 proud), spaced about 8 apart, with a cream plinth to y8 and small windows in the upper band.
- **Roof:** tiers step back and rise toward the spine.
