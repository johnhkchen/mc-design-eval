# BUILD SPEC: Deepslate Holdings (old Concord granary)

## 1. Identity
A 3-storey deepslate-and-oak granary with a hipped slate roof, a front pediment and a rooftop elevator head. Its right flank is a glazed stair shaft with oak chutes. A quartz-and-glass entrance and sign are woven into the old loading bay.

## 2. Footprint and height
The sheet wins over the stated 17x15x22. The traces give **19 wide x 19 deep x 24 tall**, with the tall proportion kept (19:24 on both the concept and the trace).
- Main wall x0–14 (15 wide). Shaft x15–18 (4 wide).
- Depth z0–18. z=0 is the front wall plane. Projections go to negative z.
- Height: y0 is the plinth base, the ridge is y20, the spire reaches y22 and the tower finial y23.
- The side trace's 39 rows are oversampled vertically. Do not stretch the height to match.

## 3. Zones, bays, roof
**Vertical, bottom to top:**
- y0–1: mossy plinth. Planters stand against it.
- y2–7: ground storey (6 tall, grand). The floor is y2, reached by 2 steps.
- y8–11: storey 2. Windows are y9–11, with an oak sill at y8.
- y12–15: storey 3. Windows are y13–15, with an oak sill at y12.
- y16–17: cornice (a 1-block overhang) and frieze.
- y17–20: roof.

**Horizontal bays:**
- Oak pilasters at x=1, 5, 9, 13, each 1 wide, y2–15.
- Window bays are 3 wide at x2–4, 6–8 and 10–12. Each has a 2-wide window, at x2–3, 6–7 and 10–11. A narrow 1-wide bay at x14 sits against the shaft pier at x15.
- Shaft: oak pier x15, glass x16–17, oak pier x18, y2–18.

**Roof:**
- A hip roof of deepslate tiles with 3 stepped courses and a flat top at y19.
- A central pediment at x5–13, y17–20, with oak-stair diagonal rafters and a 1x2 oak slit window at x9. A lightning-rod spire at x9, y21–22.
- Elevator head at x14–17, z2–5, y19–22, with an oak-fence railing and finial at y23.
- Corner pinnacles at x0 and x14 rise 1 above the eave.

## 5. Features (x from the front-left corner, y up)
- **Entrance portico, x5–12.** Quartz pillars at x5, 7, 10, 12 (y2–5). Glass sidelights at x6 and x11. A double dark_oak_door at x8–9, y2–4 (3 tall). A glass transom at y5.
- **Sign band, x6–11, y6–7.** Two rows of signs: DEEPSLATE HOLDINGS above, COMMUNITY SERVICES below. The surrounding lintel runs x5–12, y6–7.
- **Banners.** Cyan, on x5 and x12 at y3–4.
- **Steps.** Centred at x7–10, z-1 to z-2, with oak-fence rails and lantern posts at x6 and x11.
- **Ground windows.** Arched, 2 wide at x2–3 and 1 wide at x14, y3–6. Oak sills.
- **Upper windows.** Lit with hanging lanterns. Pilaster-foot wall lanterns at x5, 9, 13, y8.
- **Planters.** Moss-and-stone boxes along the plinth at x0–4 and x13–18, z-1. Bushes 3 tall at x1 and x3 (x16–17 also). Flowering azalea at x5–6 and x11–12.
- **Shaft.** A quartz-stair zigzag inside, one flight per storey. Lanterns on the landings.
- **Side chutes.** Oak-stair brackets and landings step out along the side wall, 1 block per storey, over a dark blackstone channel (x14–17 on the side). The side wall also has 2-wide windows at z8–9 on each upper storey and an oak pilaster every 4 blocks.
- **Back.** A plain deepslate wall with a few windows.
