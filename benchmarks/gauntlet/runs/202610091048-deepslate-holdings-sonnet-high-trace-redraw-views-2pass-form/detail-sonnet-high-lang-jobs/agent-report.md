I wrote `rules.txt` and painted twice from `input.nbt`. detail-2 is modestly better than input, but the front elevation got darker and busier.

- **Treatments used:**
  - `weather 0.3` and `vary` on deepslate_bricks.
  - A bracketed polished_deepslate cornice on NORTH and WEST row 17.
  - A deep quartz cornice with a smooth_quartz slab coping on the portico.
  - Hood lintels on the NORTH and WEST windows, with the stair tower excluded.
  - A stone_bricks plinth of height 2 with a mossy gradient up rows 0-2.
  - Polished_deepslate coping on the roof rim, plus `vary` on the tiles.
  - `courses 6 oak_planks` on the WEST timber side.
- **Dropped:** the job 8 quoins. detail-1 painted 16 of them over the oak piers (NORTH col 1 is mostly E there), breaking the pier line. detail-2 omits them and the piers run unbroken again.
- **Better:** the roof now has a defined stone rim and uneven tiles. The base is a stepped moss-streaked footing. The portico lintel projects. The windows have drip hoods, and the pier line is intact.
- **Worse or unsure:**
  - The polished_deepslate cornice and hoods sit on dark wall, so they read as noise more than as a heavy eave band.
  - The WEST courses wrote only 7 cells, so that side still reads mostly as one panel.
- **Files:** `detail-2.nbt` is the pick, with renders in `t2/`. `detail-1.nbt` and `t1/` are the quoin version.
