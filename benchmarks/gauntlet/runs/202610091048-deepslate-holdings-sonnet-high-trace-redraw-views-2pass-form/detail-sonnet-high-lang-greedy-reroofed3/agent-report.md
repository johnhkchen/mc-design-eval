detail-2 is a modest improvement over input, not a transformation. Most of this facade was already crafted. `faces --plain` showed only a few writable cells, and lintels, cornices and frames mostly skipped or went wrong. Detail-1 added frames, hood lintels and sills on every opening, and that wrecked the portico and put stray slabs on the shaft and tower. Detail-2 is a repaint from input.nbt with those removed, and I judged it from the front and east elevations only.

- **Kept (10 jobs, 148 cells):**
  - **Plinth:** weathering plus mossy variation.
  - **Wall fields:** cracked-brick variation on the front, the shaft side and the elevator head.
  - **Elevator head:** polished-deepslate courses every 5 rows.
  - **Windows:** flat polished-deepslate lintels over the upper front windows, and oak sills on the upper side windows.
  - **Oak chute:** plank courses every 4 rows plus plank variation, which breaks up the flat oak slab.
  - **Roof edge:** polished-deepslate slab coping, 24 cells.
- **Dropped:** frames, hood lintels and slab sills on every opening, front and side cornices (only 2 cells wrote), roof `vary` and pilaster `weather` (both wrote 0).
- **Still open:** the stepped front cornice, entrance quartz detailing and pilaster capitals, because the existing ornament blocks every rule there. Detail-2 is untidy in places: the side sills read as stray at the silhouette, and the front lintels are barely visible.

Files are in the run directory: `rules.txt`, `detail-1.nbt`, `detail-2.nbt`, and renders in `t0`, `t1` and `t2`.
