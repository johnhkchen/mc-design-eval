Both rounds are built and saved, and round 2 is the better one. I didn't run `mcd check` on either file.

- **Built:** a 43×41×43 Taj Mahal generated by code. I painted one face and rotated it onto all four sides. It has the brick apron, a plinth with a parapet, and the 25×25 mausoleum with chamfered corners. Each face has two niche tiers, a pishtaq with a stair-stepped pointed iwan carved 3 deep, a door and a jali. The roof carries the drum, onion dome, gold finial, four chhatris and four minarets. Materials follow the spec's map; both builds have no unknown block ids.
- **Block swaps:** `calcite_stairs` and `quartz_wall` don't exist, so I used `quartz_stairs` and `diorite_wall`. The spec allows `diorite_wall`.
- **Round 1 mismatches vs `concept.jpg`:**
  - The dome read yellow and small, because the cream lancet inlay covered too much of it.
  - The minaret lanterns were heavy sandstone blocks, where the concept has slim white ones.
  - The plinth parapet was noisy, a slab-and-block speckle.
  - There were no kiosks beside the pishtaq.
  - The wall and niche tier is darker and greyer than the concept's pale marble.
- **Round 2 fixes:**
  - The dome inlay is now a slim pointed oval, so the dome reads white.
  - Minaret lanterns have corner pillars only, with a quartz cap.
  - The parapet is solid with sandstone posts every 4 blocks.
  - Kiosks now stand at x=14 and x=26 as well as the piers.
- **Better round:** round 2. The silhouette and materials read closer to the concept: white dome, light minarets, clean plinth.
- **Still off in round 2:**
  - The dome is lumpy rather than a smooth stepped onion.
  - The mausoleum body is darker than the concept, and the spec's calcite and andesite map caps how pale it can get.
  - The chhatris and door are small.
