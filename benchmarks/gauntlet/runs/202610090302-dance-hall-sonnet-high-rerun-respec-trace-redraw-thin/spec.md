# Build Spec: Art Deco Dance Hall

## 1. Identity
Symmetrical art deco dance hall. A stepped central tower with a spire and blue fins rises over two lower side wings. A gold-and-jewel marquee projects over a grand entrance.

## 2. Footprint and height
- **Counted from the trace: 26 wide (x 0–25) × 30 tall (y 0–29).** The stated 22 × 16 would squash the tower, so the sheet wins.
- Depth scales with width (20 × 26/22), so **depth ≈ 24 (z 0–23)**.
- The tower is centred on x=13.

## 3. Zones and bays

**Vertical zones (y from ground)**
- y0–2: base and doors
- y3: entrance lintel
- y4–9: marquee
- y10–15: window and wing body
- y15–16: wing cornice and parapet
- y16–21: stepped shoulders
- y22–29: spire

**Tops**
- **Wings:** flat roof at y15, with a parapet to y16 across x3–7 and x19–22. A blue lintel sits at y16 over x4–7 and x19–22. The cornice is a zig-zag: black blocks at x2–3, y14.
- **Shoulders:** a 1:1 diagonal stair of tan blocks, from the fin tops (x8, y21) down to the wing roofs (x3, y16).
- **Fins:** blue, y10–22, stepping up toward the centre. The outer fin steps up at x8, y21. The inner fin peaks at x9, y22.
- **Spire:** x11–15 at y22–26, narrowing to x12–14 at y27–28 and a single block at x13, y29.

## 5. Features

**Entrance (y0–3)**
- Two dark_oak_door leaves, each 2 wide × 3 tall, at x11–12 and x14–15. A cut_sandstone post stands at x13.
- A dark lintel (smooth_basalt) spans x9–16 at y3.
- Flanking pillars at x8–9 and x17–18 are cream with a dark base.

**Marquee (x8–18, y4–9)**
- Bottom band: gold, y4.
- Face, y5–7: cream panels, with teal diamonds at x10, 13 and 16, and red and green jewels at y8.
- Crown: a stepped gold pyramid at y8–9. It is 7 wide at y8, and gold at x10–16 on y9.
- Beaded honeycomb border at the left and right ends.

**Central window (x12–14, y9–16)**
- Stained-glass panes in blue, yellow, brown and orange, in a grid with a cross mullion.
- Navy panes at y15–16.

**Sunburst (x11–15, y17–21)**
- A gold fan with its peak at x13.
- Under it is a tan band at y17, with a terracotta accent at x13.

**Spire (y22–26)**
- A gold_block core at x13, flanked by quartz at x12 and x14 and sandstone at x11 and x15.
- Glass slits at x12, 14 and 15.

**Side wings (centred at x5 and x21)**
- A tall 1-wide × 7-high stained-glass slit at y6–12. It is yellow, orange and brown, with navy at the top.
- A gold sunburst block at y14, above the slit.
- A dark doorway recess at x4–6 and x20–22, y1–3. It is grey inside, with blue plinth blocks at x3 and x7, y1.
- Black base at y0 across x3–9 and x16–22.

## 6. Depth plan
- **Front plane z=0:** the wings, the tower and the pylons.
- **Marquee:** projects 3 forward (z −3 to −1), with a sloped gold top: stairs rising back toward the wall. It rests on the pillars.
- **Fins:** stand proud 1 block forward. The pilasters stand proud 1 block.
- **Central window:** recessed 1 block, using exclusion rather than a buried block.
- **Wing windows:** recessed 1 block.
- **Door recess:** 1 block deep.
- **Pylons:** project 1 block, with buttress piers at 6-block intervals down both sides.
- **Tower:** about 8 deep, as a stepped rear spine.
- **Rear volume:** drops to y≈12 in 3 sandstone tiers stepping down toward the back.
- **Side walls:** quartz with small slit windows at y11–12 and the buttress piers.
