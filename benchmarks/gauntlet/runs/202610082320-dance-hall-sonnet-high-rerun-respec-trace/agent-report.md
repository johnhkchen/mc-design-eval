I built both rounds, `round-1.nbt` and `round-2.nbt`, and round 2 is better. I judged that by eye from renders against `trace.png` and `concept.jpg`. I didn't run `mcd check`.

- **Built:** a 27×31×27 art-deco dance hall, generated by `build.mjs` (`ROUND=1` and `ROUND=2` rebuild the two files). The front profile is extruded 24 deep, with the wing crests stepping up to a flat tower. The tower carries blue fins at x8–9 and x17–18, tan pilasters, a quilted glass window with a 1-wide sandstone cross, and a gold sunburst. The marquee, soffit and recessed double doors sit under it at the entrance, and the spire is open and 3 deep, with a glass slit. The wings have lancet windows, medallions, black caps and a blue lintel, and the sides have sandstone pilasters and slit windows.
- **Round 1 mismatches:**
  - The `arch` brush spire overshot to a tall hollow triangle, not the traced stepped lancet with a tip at y29.
  - A sandstone tread course banded the tower face.
  - There was no roof lip.
  - The side pilasters were too thin.
- **Round 2 fixes:**
  - **Spire:** hand-traced legs, lintel, finial and tip, with the glass slit in the back plane.
  - **Tower face:** quartz restored at the top row.
  - **Roof:** a slab lip on the rear and outer terrace edges.
  - **Sides:** pilasters 2 wide with carved faces, and the slits re-spaced to match.
  - Round 2's silhouette now matches the trace.
- **Brushes:** only `arch` was used (round 1), and I dropped it for the spire in round 2. The massing and wing crests are hand-extruded columns, not the massing brushes.
- **Concept over spec:** the wing lancets are at x4–5 and x21–22 (spec says x5–6), and the terraces are the front profile extruded back, not rear-rising treads.
- **Still off:** the trace's base windows are wider than mine, the marquee jewel detail is sparser, and the quartz reads creamier than the concept's grey-white.
