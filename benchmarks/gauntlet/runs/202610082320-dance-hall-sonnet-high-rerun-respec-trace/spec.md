# Build Spec: Art Deco Dance Hall

## 1. Identity
Symmetrical stepped art deco dance hall. A central tower carries a slender lancet spire and flanking blue fins. Side wings step up toward the centre, and a gold-and-jewel marquee sits over a recessed double-door entrance.

## 2. Footprint and height
- **Sheet wins: 27 wide × 30 tall at the spire tip**, not the stated 22 × 16. Width and height come from the trace grid.
- The tall tower is the design, so don't compress it.
- Depth is not measurable from the elevation. The ¾ view reads roughly square, so use **24 deep** (the stated 20 is the minimum).
- Centre axis is x=13. Everything mirrors about it.

## 3. Zones and bays

**Vertical zones (y=0 at ground):**
- y0–1: plinth.
- y0–3: entrance doors (4 tall).
- y4: canopy soffit.
- y5–7: marquee body.
- y8–9: marquee crown.
- y10–16: stained-glass shaft.
- y17–21: sunburst panel.
- y22: tower shoulder.
- y22–29: spire (open arch frame y22–26, finial y27–29).

**Bays, left to right:**
- Wing: x0–7.
- Fins: x8–9, with the tan pilaster at x10.
- Tower: x10–16.
- Fins: x17–18, with the tan pilaster at x16.
- Wing: x19–26.

**Spire:**
- Open arch frame x11–15, narrowing to x12–14 at y27–28.
- Single-block tip at x13, y29.
- A 1-wide glass slit runs at x13, y21–26.

**Wings:**
- Outer carved pilasters x0–1 and x25–26, top at y12.
- The crest steps up 1 block per column from y13 at x1 to y19 at x7, mirrored on the right.
- A dark blue lintel band runs y16, x3–6. Black end-caps sit at x2–3, y15–16.

**Roof:**
- Tower top is flat at y22.
- Behind the front, flat terraces step UP toward the rear: y16, y18, y20, about 5 blocks per tread.
- Add a stepped parapet lip on each terrace.

## 5. Features (x from the left corner, y from the ground)

- **Doors:** two pairs at x11–12 and x14–15, y0–3. A sandstone pier at x13. Carved pilasters sit either side at x9–10 and x16–17.
- **Canopy and marquee:**
  - Soffit: y4, x8–18, projecting 3.
  - Body: y5–7, x8–18. White sign panels sit at x10–12 and x14–16; the teal diamond is at x13, y5–7.
  - Crown: y8 gold at x9–17, y9 gold at x12–14.
  - The crown steps down toward the ends as gold stairs.
  - Keep the border thin, so the sign reads light, not brown.
- **Central window:** x12–14, y10–16. A blue-grey, olive and orange quilt with a **1-wide cross mullion in sandstone** (full cross, not a 2×2 block).
- **Sunburst:** gold fan at x12–14, y19–21, apex at x13, y21. Frame it with sandstone.
- **Wing lancets:** 2 wide at x5–6 and x20–21, y5–13, in glass quilt with sandstone surround. A small gold sunburst medallion sits above at y14–15.
- **Base windows:** dark, x4–5 and x21–22, y1–3, with a gold sill. Blue band at y1 on x3–4 and x22–23.
- **Fins:** unbroken vertical blue strips, y9 up to y22. The inner fin face is x9/x17, one block taller than the outer.

## 6. Depth plan

- The marquee and canopy project 3 blocks from the façade.
- The doors recess 1 block behind the façade line.
- Each tan pilaster projects 1 block (x0–1, x9–10 and mirrored). Repeat the pilasters at 4-block intervals along the side walls, taller at the front.
- The fins and tower face are flush (depth 0). The fins stand proud of the tower wall by 1.
- The wings sit 1 block back from the tower.
- The spire frame is 3 deep, hollow, with the glass slit in the back plane.
- Side walls: smooth_quartz with 1×2 slit windows between the pilasters.
- Rear terraces drop back in 5-block treads, as in section 3.
