# Temple of the Hidden Spring — Front Elevation

## 1. Lore & setting
An invented highland-desert tradition, the **Maqari**, who settled a sun-scorched plateau where water surfaces only at rare springs. Their temple honors **Say라**, goddess of the hidden spring — water as the divine, withheld and precious. Built of the only abundant material, sun-baked earth, it faces the dawn so first light strikes the portal where pilgrims pour libations.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
A flat, screen-like **pishtaq** facade: a tall rectangular frontispiece pierced by one deep pointed **iwan** (recessed portal-vault). The form is a billboard for the temple behind it — appropriate where the *threshold*, not the interior, is sacred. Massive earthen piers read as cool shade-makers against glare; the deep iwan throws a black pool of shadow, dramatizing entry. Glazed tile is reserved for accents because firing glaze costs scarce fuel — so it signals devotion, like spending water itself.

## 3. Color palette (complementary, with analogous highlight)
- **Dominant — warm ochre brick:** *red sandstone* (the earthen body, ~70%).
- **Supporting — cream:** *smooth sandstone* (framing bands, plinth).
- **Accent — turquoise glaze:** *cyan glazed terracotta* (the iwan and stars — turquoise = water/sky, the goddess made visible).
- **Sparing highlight — gold:** *gold block* (finial only).

Orange-ochre against turquoise is a true **complementary** contrast (warm earth vs. cool water); gold sits **analogous** to the ochre, so it harmonizes without a third clash. Restraint keeps it sacred, not gaudy.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- **The pointed iwan arch** — repeated as the grand portal and two flanking blind niches.
- **Eight-point star (water-source)** — glazed turquoise on the spandrels and parapet frieze.
- **Stepped merlon cresting** — a crenellated skyline echoing the plateau's terraces.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** two-course cream plinth.
- **Supports:** engaged corner piers (half-towers) framing the screen.
- **Cornice:** corbelled muqarnas-stepped band in cream.
- **Crown:** turquoise merlon crest + central gold finial.
- **Entrance:** deep recessed turquoise iwan, the facade's only true relief depth.
- **Niches:** flanking blind arches with turquoise tympana.
- **Proportion:** facade width:height ≈ **3:2**; iwan height = **2/3** of facade; central axis strictly symmetrical; pishtaq frame ≈ **1:1.6**.
