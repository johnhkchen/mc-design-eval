# Temple of the Tidal Crown

## 1. Lore & setting
A monsoon-coast pilgrimage temple to a sea-and-sky deity, raised by a maritime dynasty at the height of its wealth. Like the reference — a perfectly symmetric, garden-fronted monument built as an act of devotion and memory — this facade is ceremonial, axial, and meant to be approached head-on across water. It is a jewel-box, not a fortress.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference speaks a clear grammar: a cubic body with **chamfered corners**, one dominant **iwan** (recessed pointed-arch portal) flanked by **stacked twin niches**, the whole crowned by a great **onion dome on a drum**, ringed by four small **chattri kiosks** and bracketed by **corner minarets**. This works because a single deep central void organizes the whole face, and the bulb dome resolves the rising vertical pull. I keep every move; I only change the skin.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
Where the reference is white marble, the brief demands color. **Complementary harmony, azure ↔ amber, with a crimson accent:**
- **Dominant — lapis_block** (deep azure body)
- **Supporting — smooth_quartz** (cool light to let blue breathe) + **gold_block** (warm amber frames, the complement)
- **Accent — red_nether_bricks** (crimson inlay, ~5% — the spark)

Blue body against gold framing is maximum contrast; quartz keeps it from going garish; crimson punctuates the tympana.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference's **pietra dura** and arch spandrels: gold **ogee-arch jambs** outlining the iwan and every niche; **crimson quatrefoil rosettes** set in each spandrel; horizontal **gold string-courses** every 6–7 rows splitting flat fields; a quartz **pinstripe** running each minaret.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** quartz plinth, ~⅙ of height, with a gold cap course.
- **Body:** lapis, chamfered corners; central iwan recessed **5 blocks** deep, ~⅓ the facade width, rising two-thirds of the wall.
- **Supports:** four **engaged** minarets bonded to the corners (no sky-gap), gold finials.
- **Crown:** lapis onion dome whose belly **overhangs its drum by 2 blocks per side**, topped by a gold finial spire; four chattris at the dome's base.
- **Proportion:** dome height ≈ body height (1:1); iwan width:height ≈ 1:2; minaret:body ≈ 1:1.1. Symmetry absolute about the central axis.
