## Finalized design document
# Temple of the Azure Mandate

## 1. Lore & setting
A memorial-temple of the **Heaven-Facing Order**, raised on a forested mountain stair to honor a unifying sage-founder. Like the reference — a 20th-century mausoleum speaking in imperial grammar — it fuses civic solemnity with sacred ceremony: a hall you *ascend* to, not merely enter.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference reads as **mass below, sky above**: a fortress-like white granite podium of battered, coursed ashlar grounds a soaring **double-eaved blue-glazed hip roof**. The eye climbs cool stone, then bursts into saturated cobalt. The logic is contrast and hierarchy — heavy/light, matte/glazed, neutral/vivid — and it works because the single bold roof reads instantly against pale walls and green hill.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Complementary harmony**, neutral-anchored (cool blue vs. warm gold over white).
- **Dominant — pale granite white:** `calcite` (battered walls, pylons, pilasters).
- **Supporting — cobalt roof:** `blue_concrete` (tiled eaves and ridge mass) — the reference's signature glazed blue.
- **Accent — imperial gold:** `gold_block`, used sparingly for the three lintel inscriptions.
- **Glazed ridge accent:** `blue_glazed_terracotta` at roof crests and ornaments, catching light like real tile.
- **Shadow/base grey:** `andesite` for the broad ceremonial stair.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference: **upturned eave-ends and ridge beasts**, a **carved tablet cartouche** crowning the central bay, **dentil-like bracket courses (dougong)** beneath each eave, and **three gold-charactered lintels** over the openings. Adapted: the bracket course becomes a repeating calcite-and-gold corbel band; the tablet becomes a recessed quartz panel framing a gold glyph.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** broad battered podium with two flanking pylons, ~⅓ of total height.
- **Supports:** four engaged calcite pilasters dividing three bays.
- **Entablature/eaves:** dentil bracket course, then a **double blue roof** — lower eave shading the entrance, upper roof the crown.
- **Crowning element:** the cobalt hip roof with glazed-terracotta ridge, the facade's focal mass.
- **Openings:** three round-arched portals; **central bay widest** (golden-ratio dominant, ~1.6× the flanking arches), each capped by a gold-lettered lintel.
- **Proportion:** podium : wall : roof ≈ **1 : 1.5 : 1**, symmetrical about the central axis.

## Orientation (photographed head-on)

## Scale & RELIEF — use it (this is judged)
- Width up to ~48 (X), height up to ~40 (Y) including the crown. Build big.
- DEEP relief: up to ~16 blocks of depth into −Z, and genuinely use it. Columns/buttresses
  project several blocks PROUD of the wall; the central portal RECESSES several blocks deep;
  cornices, string courses, and the crown step forward and back across MULTIPLE Z-layers. A
  near-flat screen only 4–6 deep is a failure.
- CROWN proportion: the crowning element (pediment / parapet / attic / cresting) must span the
  facade's full width and have real height — a deliberate culmination of the body, never a
  small cap perched on a wide base.
