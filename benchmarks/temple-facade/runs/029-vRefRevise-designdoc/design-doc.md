# The Sunward Gate: Temple-Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
The Sunward Gate is the processional front of the Heliarchs, a sea-trading people of the copper coast. Every solstice, the dawn procession passes under its single vault. The building is not for prayer. It is a triumphal threshold, a gate that celebrates a victory. The reference reads as civic, monumental and confident, so this temple says *arrival*, not seclusion.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference is Neoclassical triumphal architecture: one near-square block cut by a single deep barrel arch. Its power comes from **mass, not lightness**. Two heavy piers carry a thick entablature and a tall blank attic, all in calm horizontal bands. The ornament stays inside framed panels, so the silhouette remains a pure rectangle. That restraint is why it reads clearly from far away, and the build keeps it.

## 3. Color palette: complementary (orange ↔ teal) with a gold accent
- **Dominant: Smooth Red Sandstone** (with its stairs and slabs). A warm sun-orange for the piers and attic body. It replaces the reference's white limestone.
- **Supporting: Dark Prismarine** (with its stairs and slabs). A deep sea-teal for the plinth, impost bands, cornice and archivolt. It is the complement of the orange, so every horizontal band stands out.
- **Relief field: Waxed Oxidized Cut Copper** (with its stairs). Verdigris recessed panels behind the sculpture, a lighter value of the teal.
- **Accent: Gold Block.** Used only for the shield roundels, the keystone and the highlights in the sculpture groups.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- **Shield frieze** (taken from the reference's attic roundels): a row of gold shields on the attic, one every 3 blocks.
- **Pier sculpture groups**: tall relief figures built from red-sandstone stairs on a copper niche, standing on teal pedestals.
- **Framed relief panels** above each group: copper fields with stair-cut scenes, inside teal stair moldings.
- **Coffered vault**: a grid of dark prismarine coffers with gold centers inside the arch.
- **Archivolt**: a stepped teal molding, crowned by a gold keystone.

## 5. Features & proportion
The reference is roughly 1.1 : 1 (height to width). The facade is **45 wide × 49 tall**, with relief up to 6 deep.

| Element | Size | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Base (plinth) | 3 tall | Dark prismarine, projects 1 block |
| Piers | 16 wide each | Hold the sculpture groups (rows 4–20) and the panels (rows 24–31) |
| Impost band | at row 21 | Teal stair band where the arch springs |
| Arch | 13 wide (≈0.29 of the width), crown at row 32 | Archivolt projects 1, vault recessed 6 |
| Entablature | 4 tall | Frieze band, then a dark-prismarine cornice overhanging by 2 |
| Attic | 9 tall (≈1/5 of the height) | Flat-topped crowning block with the shield frieze; red-sandstone slab coping, no pediment |

The silhouette is a rectangle, cut only by the arch.
