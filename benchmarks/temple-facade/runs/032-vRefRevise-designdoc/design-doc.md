# Temple of the Vermilion Tide: Front Elevation

## 1. Lore & Setting
A river-harbour guild raised this gate in a late-classical age to mark the Tide Procession. Each year a painted barge passes under the central arch on the feast of the Vermilion Tide. The face celebrates passage, return, and the sea-guilds' prosperity. It is a ceremonial threshold, not a wall.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
From the reference: a near-square block pierced by one tall round-headed arch; heavy piers that read as load-bearing; horizontal bands (frieze, attic, cornice) dividing the mass into tiers; figural sculpture at eye level; a row of small square panels crowning the attic.

It works because the single large void gives an immediate focal point, the heavy piers make the mass feel authoritative, and the repeated small panels create rhythm without clutter. We keep this grammar and translate it into block-scale relief.

## 3. Color Palette
**Harmony: split-complementary.** A warm red dominant, a yellow-ochre support, and two opposing cool accents (lapis blue and oxidised-copper teal) give a festive, high-contrast face.

- **Red Nether Bricks** (dominant, ~60%): piers, body, attic field.
- **Yellow Terracotta** (support, ~20%): frieze band, arch coffers, plinth stripe.
- **Lapis Block** (accent, ~10%): keystone, tablet frames, figure bases.
- **Oxidized Cut Copper** (crown accent, ~10%): cornice and rosette crown.

The reference's pale stone informs form only. No white or grey blocks appear on the facade.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
- **Figural frieze:** stair-and-slab silhouettes on yellow terracotta, outlined in lapis.
- **Attic tablets:** 3×3 recessed inscription panels in red bricks with lapis frames.
- **Rosette row:** small copper-ringed medallions along the crown, each with a lapis centre.
- **Coffered soffit:** the arch ceiling alternates yellow terracotta and red bricks in a 2×2 coffer grid.
- **Keystone:** one lapis block at the arch crown.

## 5. Architectural Features & Proportion
**Grid: 31 blocks wide × 31 tall** (height ≈ width, as in the reference).

- **Base:** 3-block red-brick plinth with a 1-block projecting stair course.
- **Piers:** two 9-block piers (x 0–8 and 22–30), set 1 block proud of the centre, with recessed panels giving 1–2 blocks of relief depth.
- **Central span:** 13 blocks (x 9–21). The arch opening is 9 wide (x 11–19), about one-third of facade width. Springing at y=12, crown at y=16.
- **Frieze:** y 17–19, yellow terracotta with lapis figures, full width.
- **Attic:** y 20–25, red bricks with tablets and the rosette row.
- **Cornice:** y 26–27, oxidized copper stairs and slabs, projecting 1 block.
- **Crown:** y 28–30, copper rosettes, with one lapis block marking the centre peak.
