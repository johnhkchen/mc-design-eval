# Temple of the Triumphal Dawn

## 1. Lore & setting
A victory-temple of the **Solari**, a sun-worshipping republic that raised a single monumental gate-shrine to honor those who held the pass. Like the reference — a *triumphal arch*, civic and commemorative rather than devotional-enclosed — this is a threshold you walk *through* to be counted among the honored. Era: an imagined neoclassical empire at its confident zenith.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference reads as **one cubic mass pierced by one great void**: a near-square silhouette, a single central archway, broad solid piers, capped by a tall attic band. It works because the lone opening concentrates all drama centrally while the unbroken piers give it weight and gravity. I keep that grammar: solidity, symmetry, one dominant arch, sculpture confined to framed relief panels so the wall stays calm.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
The reference is warm cream limestone read against a complementary blue sky — so I commit to a **complementary scheme with a metallic accent**:
- **Dominant — pale cream stone:** `smooth_quartz_block` (the sunlit Pierre de Paris).
- **Supporting — honey ochre:** `smooth_sandstone` for piers and shadowed relief, the warm tone the stone takes in raking light.
- **Accent (complement) — sky azure:** `light_blue_terracotta` in the attic roundels, echoing the blue that frames the real monument.
- **Accent (honor) — gold:** `gold_block`, sparing, for the crowning device only — gilding the victory.

Warm neutrals carry 85%; blue + gold are jewels, not fields.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference: **circular roundels** ringing the attic, **deep high-relief figural panels** flanking the arch, a **coffered/dentil cornice**, and the spandrel reliefs above the arch. Adapted: four sunken azure roundels along the crown; two recessed relief niches (quartz figures on sandstone ground) framing the entrance; a dentil course of alternating quartz/sandstone under the cornice.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
Reading the reference as roughly **1:1 overall**, arch ≈ ½ the total height, attic ≈ ⅓:
- **Base:** low sandstone plinth, two steps.
- **Supports:** two broad quartz piers, each ~¼ the facade width.
- **Arch:** central, springing at mid-height, the silhouette's heart.
- **Entablature:** projecting dentil cornice, full relief depth ~3 blocks.
- **Crown:** flat attic band of azure roundels, centered gold sun-device — the Solari seal catching first light.
