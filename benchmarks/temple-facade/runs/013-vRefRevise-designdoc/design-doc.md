# Temple of the Azure Meridian — Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & Setting
A river-delta sanctuary of the **Tide-Keeper cult**, raised by a grief-stricken navigator-queen to enshrine the star she steered by. Like its proven inspiration — a domed Mughal mausoleum of perfect symmetry — it is a monument to devotion rendered as geometry: serenity made architectural.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
The reference teaches **bilateral symmetry around a single recessed portal**, a heavy crowning mass balanced by slender vertical accents at the corners, and ornament concentrated in framed panels rather than smeared across the wall. This works because the eye is given one dominant event (the dome), one threshold (the iwan), and calm field between them. I adopt that hierarchy exactly — and reject the reference's *whiteness*, which would collapse a Minecraft build into a flat slab.

## 3. Color Palette — *Cool Analogous with Warm Accent*
- **Dominant:** `warped_planks` / `prismarine_bricks` — deep teal-blue body (the "azure" of the meridian star).
- **Supporting:** `cyan_terracotta` — muted blue-green for recessed panels and the drum, holding the analogous cool family together.
- **Frame & relief:** `dark_prismarine` — denser shadow-line outlining every arch and panel.
- **Accent:** `gold_block` — finial, portal keystone, chattri caps; a single warm complementary spark against the cool field so the crown reads instantly.

This is a confident cool-analogous harmony (blue→teal→green) pierced by gold — bold, not monochrome, honoring the brief while keeping the reference's disciplined restraint.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
From the reference: the **pointed-arch (iwan) frame**, the **pishtaq** rectangular surround projecting beyond the wall, repeated **blind-arch niches** flanking the entrance, and **lotus-finial** crowns. Adapted: niches become 1-deep recesses (carved by omission, never buried), each gold-keystoned.

## 5. Architectural Features & Proportion
- **Base:** a wide plinth, ~⅕ of total height, grounding the mass.
- **Body:** square central block, **height ≈ width** (1:1), pierced by one tall central iwan rising ~⅔ of the body.
- **Corners:** four slender minaret-towers, **height ≈ 1.6× the body**, framing — not crowding — the dome.
- **Crown:** an **onion dome on a tall drum**, belly **overhanging** the drum (≈1.15× drum width) so it never reads as a pyramid; gold finial above. Drum:dome:finial ≈ 1:2:0.6.
- **Chattris:** four small domed kiosks at the body's outer corners, echoing the great dome at ⅓ scale.
