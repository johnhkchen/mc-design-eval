# Temple of the Pearl Meridian — Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
A Mughal-era *dawn-shrine* raised by a grieving sovereign as a memorial to a beloved — a tomb-temple where pilgrims greet the first light. Its serene symmetry and luminous white skin read, like the reference, as devotion rendered in stone: paradise made architectural.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference is governed by **perfect bilateral symmetry** and a **hierarchy of arches**: one colossal central *iwan* (recessed pointed-arch portal) flanked by paired, half-scale arched niches stacked in two registers. A cubic plinth-block anchors a soaring **onion dome on a drum**, four corner minarets pin the composition. It works because the eye climbs an ordered ladder — plinth → arch-screen → dome — every element echoing the pointed-arch motif at shrinking scale.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
Near-**monochromatic white** with a warm complementary accent — calm, with a single grounding contrast.
- **Dominant — luminous white marble:** **Smooth Quartz Block** (the body, dome, minarets).
- **Supporting — soft shadow/inlay grey:** **Diorite** for arch reveals and string-courses, giving relief without color noise.
- **Accent — red sandstone:** **Red Sandstone** for the plinth band and spandrel inlay (the warm terrace tone seen below the marble).
- **Accent — gilded finial:** **Gold Block** crowning the dome — the brass pinnacle of the reference.
Harmony: monochrome + warm complementary accent.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- The **pointed (four-centered) arch**, repeated at three scales as the unifying motif.
- **Pietra-dura framing:** thin Diorite/Red-Sandstone borders outlining each iwan, as the reference frames its great arch in calligraphic bands.
- **Chattri-style finials** atop the minaret heads; lotus-bud **onion dome** with a clear belly-overhang over its drum.
- **Recessed niches** carved by exclusion, never buried — depth from absence of front material.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** broad Red-Sandstone plinth, 1/6 of total height.
- **Body:** cubic Quartz mass; central iwan ~**0.6× the body width**, full-height recess of 3 blocks relief.
- **Supports:** four free-standing minarets at the corners, slender (height ≈ **5× width**), ringed by Diorite balcony bands.
- **Crowning element:** onion dome on a tall drum, dome ≈ **1/3 total height**, capped by the Gold finial — the silhouette's apex.
- **Proportion:** dome-to-facade height ≈ **golden ratio**; arch height ≈ **2× its width**.
