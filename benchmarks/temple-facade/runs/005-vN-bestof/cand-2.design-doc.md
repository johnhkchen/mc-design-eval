# Temple of the Ember Tide

## 1. Lore & Setting
A coastal fire-cult of the **Savalk**, a fictional bronze-age people on volcanic black-sand shores. The temple honors **Vael**, goddess of the dusk tide — the moment the sun "drowns" and the sea glows. Built where lava cliffs meet surf, the facade faces due west to catch the sunset head-on; rituals occur at the daily "ember tide." The climate is hot, salt-laden, and storm-battered, so the front is a thick, sheltering masonry mass — a windbreak as much as a shrine.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
A **stepped, battered (inward-leaning) pylon** flanking a deep recessed portal — the lean sheds storm-wind and reads as stable, eternal mass. Heavy, load-bearing piers (not slender columns) suit rough volcanic stone and seismic ground. A strong horizontal cornice caps the wall to throw rain clear of the face. Silhouette: broad trapezoid crowned by a single flame-finial — earthbound base, aspiring peak, mirroring sun-into-sea-into-sky.

## 3. Color Palette (analogous + complementary accent)
Dominant warm masonry through a sunset gradient — an **analogous** sweep (red→orange) for harmony and heat, broken by one cool **complementary** accent (the sea) so the fire reads as fire.
- **Dominant:** dark volcanic body — `blackstone`
- **Supporting 1:** sun-warmed stone — `red_sandstone` / `terracotta`
- **Supporting 2:** ember glow — `orange_terracotta`
- **Accent (sparing):** dusk-tide sea — `warped_planks` (teal), at thresholds only
- **Highlight:** ritual flame — `glowstone` / `sea_lantern` in the niche

Restraint: cool accent stays under ~10% of the face, concentrated at the doorway.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
- **The Drowning Sun** — a recessed semicircle/stepped disc over the portal, glowstone-lit.
- **Tide-chevrons** — stacked zigzag courses banding the piers, recalling surf.
- **Ember-pitting** — scattered orange inlays in the dark stone, like embers in ash.

## 5. Features & Proportion
Governed by a near **golden ratio (1 : 1.6)**, height to width.
- **Base:** broad 3-block plinth, widest element.
- **Supports:** two battered piers, each ⅕ of facade width.
- **Entablature:** double-corbel cornice, ⅛ of height.
- **Crown:** central flame-finial, 1 unit above the cornice.
- **Entrance:** deep-recessed portal, ⅖ width, twice as tall as wide, with the sun-disc above.
- **Niches:** two flanking lantern slots at mid-height for tide-light.
