# Temple of the Salt Vigil — Front Elevation

## 1. Lore & Setting
A coastal salt-priesthood of the invented **Halreni** people, who read the sea's withdrawal at low tide as the breath of *Maru*, keeper of preserved things — bodies, grain, memory. The temple stands on a windswept salt flat where the tide once reached; pilgrims come to leave the names of the dead pressed into salt cakes. The climate is bright, dry, and salt-bleached, so the architecture answers glare and crystalline light.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
The order is **heavy-based and stepped**, a broad trapezoidal mass that resists wind and reads as permanent — fitting a cult of preservation. Deep-cut niches and a recessed central portal give strong relief shadow, the only "color" the harsh flats lack. Squat, fluted piers (not slender columns) suit cut sandstone and salt-block construction, which carries compression but not tension. The silhouette is a stepped ziggurat-temple crowned by a single beacon — vertical hierarchy pulling the eye up to Maru.

## 3. Color Palette (analogous warm + cool accent)
A restrained **warm analogous** scheme — sand→ochre→rust — evokes sun-baked salt and mineral crust; one cool accent marks the sacred threshold (complementary tension reserved for the holiest point).
- **Dominant — pale sand:** smooth sandstone
- **Supporting — ochre band:** cut red sandstone
- **Supporting — rust relief:** granite
- **Accent (sacred only) — sea-green:** prismarine bricks at the portal and beacon
- **Shadow/line — deep brown:** polished deepslate for outlines and joints

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
- **The receding wave** — a stepped chevron frieze running the entablature, echoing the tide that reveals Maru.
- **Salt-cake lattice** — a square coffered grid in the flanking niches, where pilgrims' offerings sit.
- **The single eye** — one prismarine roundel at the apex, the god's open watch.

## 5. Features & Proportion
- **Base:** wide three-step plinth, deepslate-edged.
- **Supports:** four fluted sandstone piers, ochre-banded at mid-height.
- **Entablature:** chevron frieze + heavy deepslate cornice.
- **Crown:** stepped attic rising to a prismarine beacon-eye.
- **Entrance:** tall recessed portal framed in sea-green, deep relief.
- **Niches:** two flanking coffered recesses.
- **Proportion:** facade **golden-ratio** wide-to-tall (≈1.6 : 1); portal occupies the central **1/3**; plinth : shaft : crown ≈ **1 : 2 : 1**.
