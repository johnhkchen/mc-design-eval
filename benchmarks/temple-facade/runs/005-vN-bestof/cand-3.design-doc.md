# Temple of the Salt-Sun — Design Document

## 1. Lore & Setting
The **Maktari** are a desert-littoral people of an invented bronze-age tradition, living where a salt pan meets red sandstone cliffs. Their temple honors **Veth, the Salt-Sun** — a solar deity who "draws water up to make it pure." The facade faces east to catch first light across the flats; pilgrims arrive at dawn when the low sun ignites the stone. Climate is arid, glaring, wind-scoured — so the building is heavy, shaded, and inward, with deep reveals that throw hard shadow.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
A **battered (inward-sloping) trapezoidal massif**, pylon-like, because rammed sandstone and mudbrick want a wide base and resist with mass, not span. Ritual demands a single processional axis: one tall central portal flanked by symmetrical engaged piers, drawing the eye to the dark threshold where the god "lives in cool shadow." Deep-set niches and a heavy projecting cornice exist for **shade** — every horizontal ledge is a sun-break in a place with no forgiving light. Relief depth is shallow but emphatic, read by raking sun rather than color contrast alone.

## 3. Color Palette (analogous warm + cool accent)
Dominant **red-gold sandstone** (terracotta) — the cliffs themselves. Supporting **sun-bleached cream** (smooth sandstone) for cornice and column shafts, lifting the silhouette against sky. Supporting **burnt ochre** (red sandstone) in the battered base, grounding the mass. Sparing accent: **deep turquoise** (warped/cyan-glazed) at the portal and the solar disc — the *complement* of the warm field, reserved for the sacred water-and-sky the salt god rules. Restraint reads as holiness; the lone cool note carries all the meaning.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
- **Radiant disc** — turquoise sunburst crowning the portal, repeated small in the cornice frieze.
- **Stepped chevron** (salt-crystal zigzag) — banding the entablature and pier capitals.
- **Vertical fluting** on the engaged piers — structural reed-shadow, echoing irrigation channels.

## 5. Features & Proportion
Whole facade **golden-ratio** wide:tall ≈ 1.6 : 1. Battered **base** = ⅕ height. Four **engaged piers** rise ⅗ height to a **cornice** (⅙ height) with chevron frieze. Central **portal** = ½ the width of one bay, twice as tall as wide, recessed three blocks deep. Flanking **niches** hold shadow. Crowning **solar disc** centered above the cornice, the silhouette's single apex.
