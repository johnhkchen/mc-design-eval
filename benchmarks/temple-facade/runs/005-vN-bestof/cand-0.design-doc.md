# Temple of the Ember Tide

## 1. Lore & Setting
The **Vashkri**, a salt-merchant people of an invented archipelago, raised this temple to **Sulha, goddess of the ember tide** — the bioluminescent red bloom that lights their harbor at dusk and signals safe passage home. The climate is hot, humid, and storm-battered; the temple faces west, head-on to the setting sun and the incoming tide, so the facade is the deity's own watching face. Stone is scarce here, so the Vashkri quarried **fired clay and copper** — materials their kilns and forges already mastered for sea-trade.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
The language is **stepped and frontal**: a broad symmetrical mass rising in three setbacks toward a single crowning flame-finial — a silhouette that reads from far out at sea. Deep relief (recessed niches, projecting pilasters) throws hard shadow in low coastal light, so the face stays legible at dawn and dusk when worship happens. Heavy battered base and small high openings answer storm-wind and flood: ritual, structure, and climate converge on the same form.

## 3. Color Palette — *analogous warm, single cool accent*
- **Dominant — fired terracotta** (`terracotta`): the kiln-clay body, sun-baked warmth.
- **Supporting — warm ochre band** (`yellow_terracotta`): catches horizontal sunlight, marks each setback.
- **Supporting — oxidized copper** (`oxidized_copper`): pilasters and cornice; the forge-metal, weathered by salt air.
- **Accent (sparing) — ember red** (`red_glazed_terracotta`): only the goddess's flame-finial and door surround.

Warm analogous reds-through-coppers give a coherent, sun-drenched body; the single saturated **red** accent is reserved for the sacred — hierarchy through restraint, not rainbow.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
1. **The tide-chevron** — stacked zigzags reading the wave, banding each setback.
2. **The ember-eye** — a recessed circular niche, repeated flanking the door, the goddess watching.
3. **Cresting flame** — vertical fluting that gathers upward into the finial.

## 5. Features & Proportion
**Base:** battered plinth, 1/4 of total height. **Supports:** four copper pilasters dividing the face into a **3-bay** rhythm (wide center, narrow flanks). **Entablature:** ochre architrave + oxidized-copper cornice. **Crown:** stepped pediment to the ember-red flame-finial. **Entrance:** tall recessed center portal under a chevron lintel. **Openings:** two small high ember-eye niches per flank. **Proportion:** facade width:height ≈ **3:4**; central bay = **½** the width; the golden setback rhythm contracts each tier by ~⅓.
