## Finalized design document
```markdown
# Temple of the Vermilion Meridian — Front Facade

## 1. Lore & setting
A sun-temple of an invented Mughal-descended order, the "Meridian Keepers," who track the solstice through a single axial doorway. Like the reference — a serene, perfectly symmetrical mausoleum-temple — this is a building of stillness, geometry, and devotional precision, raised on a plinth above a reflecting court.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference reads as **bilateral perfection**: one dominant pointed-arch *iwan* punched into a cubic body, flanked by stacked recessed niches, crowned by a bulbous onion dome on a drum, framed by four freestanding minarets. The logic is *subtraction* — deep arched recesses give relief without clutter — and *hierarchy* — one great arch governs many small ones. I keep that grammar: a single commanding iwan, ranked niches, a dome that overhangs its drum.

## 3. Color palette (primary triad, warm-dominant)
The reference is white marble; I reject that mandate and translate its *jewel-inlay* tradition (pietra dura) into bold color:
- **Dominant — Cut Red Sandstone:** the warm vermilion body (proven Mughal red-sandstone craft).
- **Supporting — Lapis Lazuli Block:** deep ultramarine filling the iwan recess and niche backs (Persian cosmic blue).
- **Accent — Gold Block:** the onion dome, finial, and inlay lines — the crowning blaze.
- **Tracery — Smooth Quartz (sparingly):** thin outlines around the pointed arches only.

Red/blue/gold is a true primary triad; warm red holds the mass, blue cools the recesses, gold sings at the crown.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- **Pointed (ogee) arch**, repeated at three scales — one giant, two flanking, many niche-sized.
- **Pishtaq frame:** a rectangular gold-and-quartz border wrapping the central iwan.
- **Spandrel medallions** in lapis with gold centers, echoing inlaid florets.
- **Chattri kiosks** capping the corners.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** raised red plinth, full width, ~1/8 of total height.
- **Body:** near-square red mass; central iwan **2:1** tall-to-wide, rising **⅔** of the body.
- **Crown:** gold onion dome whose belly overhangs the drum, dome height ≈ body height; gold finial above.
- **Framing:** four slender quartz-banded minarets, taller than the body, anchoring the corners.
- **Symmetry:** strict bilateral about the central door.
```

## Scale & RELIEF — use it (this is judged)
- Width up to ~56 (X), height up to ~48 (Y) including the crown. Build big.
- DEEP relief: up to ~24 blocks of depth into −Z, and genuinely use it. Columns/buttresses
  project several blocks PROUD of the wall; the central portal RECESSES several blocks deep;
  cornices, string courses, and the crown step forward and back across MULTIPLE Z-layers. A
  near-flat screen only 4–6 deep is a failure.
- CROWN proportion: the crowning element (pediment / parapet / attic / cresting) must span the
  facade's full width and have real height — a deliberate culmination of the body, never a
  small cap perched on a wide base.
