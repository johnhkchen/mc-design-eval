# Temple of the Tidewright — Front Elevation Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
The **Sundari Coast cult of the Tidewright**, a deity who "draws the sea in and lets it go" — patron of fishers, salt-harvesters, and safe return. The temple sits on a windward headland where monsoon rain is heavy and salt spray constant. This is an *invented brackish-tropical tradition*: part Southeast-Asian glazed-ceramic temple, part stepped coastal lighthouse-shrine. Worship is **tidal** — the great central door opens only at high water, so the facade is built to frame that single luminous threshold.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The order is a **stepped pishtaq-and-flanking-tower silhouette**: a tall recessed central portal between two squat buttress-towers, crowned by a tiered finial. *Why:* heavy monsoon load demands a steep shedding profile and thick masonry piers; salt air rots stone but spares **glazed ceramic tile**, so the whole skin is fired and glazed — practical *and* radiant. The deep portal recess (relief depth ~3 blocks) shelters worshippers and casts a strong shadow that reads as the deity's "drawn breath." Verticality pulls the eye to the crowning finial — heaven over horizon.

## 3. Color palette (complementary, restrained)
- **Dominant — deep teal** (glazed sea): `cyan_glazed_terracotta`. The sea itself; covers field walls and tower faces.
- **Supporting — warm sandstone** (the headland): `smooth_sandstone` for base, piers, framing bands — grounds the cool teal.
- **Supporting — bleached coral white**: `diorite`/`quartz_block` cornice lines for crisp shadow edges.
- **Accent — amber-gold** (sun on water): `gold_block`, used *only* on the finial and door lintel.

Teal↔amber is a **complementary** pair: cool body, warm focal spark. Sandstone/white mediate so it never shouts — hierarchy, not rainbow.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- **Wave-scroll frieze** — running tile band along the entablature and base plinth (the tide's edge).
- **Drawn-thread lattice** — vertical fluting on the towers, evoking nets and rain.
- **The Eye-of-Calm** — a single gold roundel centered above the door (the Tidewright's gaze).

## 5. Features & proportion
Governing ratio **1 : φ (≈1.6)** — total height ≈ 1.6× width. Three-part vertical: **base plinth (1 unit) : portal body (3) : crowning finial (1.5)**. Central portal occupies the middle third of the width; flanking towers each a quarter. Cornice projects 1 block; portal recesses 3. Two slim niche-windows per tower, gold-rimmed. The crowning element is a **stepped three-tier finial** capped in gold.
