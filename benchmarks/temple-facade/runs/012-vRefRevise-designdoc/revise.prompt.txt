# Temple of the Azure Meridian — Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
A dawn-shrine of the **Lapis Meridian**, an invented river-delta tradition that built jewel-faced mausolea to honor the sunrise. Like the reference — a serene Mughal funerary monument — it is contemplative, axial, and eternal: a face meant to be met head-on across still water.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference reads as a **cube crowned by a bulbed onion dome**, framed by four detached minarets and four corner kiosks. Authority comes from perfect bilateral symmetry and a single recessed central arch (the *pishtaq*) that swallows the eye. I adopt this: a calm square body, one deep central iwan, paired flanking niche-arches, and a crowning dome whose belly overhangs its drum so it never collapses into a pyramid.

## 3. Color palette (split-complementary)
The reference is white marble — **information, not mandate.** I commit to a confident jewel scheme:
- **Dominant:** `lapis_block` — ultramarine body, the shrine's signature.
- **Supporting:** `warped_planks` — teal paneling and dome skin, cooling the blue.
- **Accent:** `gold_block` — finials, the dome's equatorial band, arch keystones (warm complement that makes the blue sing).
- **Inlay:** `purpur_block` — violet string-courses, bridging blue toward its complement.

**Harmony:** split-complementary — blue + teal against gold-orange, with purpur as the violet hinge. Bold, never white.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference I take the **pointed (four-centred) arch**, the **recessed spandrel panel**, the **horizontal string-course**, and the **lotus-bud finial**. Adapted: gold-keystoned arches repeat at three scales; purpur inlay bands mark each storey; chattri kiosks echo the great dome in miniature.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** dark plinth (1/6 of height) lifting the body, as the reference floats on its terrace.
- **Body:** lapis cube, double-height flanking niches at z-recess; central pishtaq recessed deepest for shadow.
- **Cornice:** full-width gold-and-purpur string-course capping the cube.
- **Crown:** bulbed onion dome on a banded drum; gold equatorial ring; lotus finial. **Dome height ≈ body height (1:1).**
- **Framing:** four minarets at the corners, rising to the dome's spring line; four chattris on the body corners.
- **Ratios:** pishtaq ≈ central 1/3 of width and 2/3 of height; symmetry absolute about the central axis.
