## Finalized design document
# The Lantern Temple of Saltmere: Front Elevation

## 1. Lore & setting
Saltmere's pilots lost eleven boats in one storm. Their guild raised this temple to the Keeper of Lights. The tall towers hold dusk lamps that guide boats home, and the Lantern at the crown never goes dark. Serene, symmetrical, devotional: like the reference, it keeps the lost in sight by keeping light on.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference rests on strict bilateral symmetry, one dominant dome, slim towers framing the mass, a plinth lifting the whole, and inlay rather than deep carving, so the dome is the single big moment. Blocks cannot show marble's soft shading, so the restraint becomes depth: a projecting frame, a recessed portal, a stepped dome. Form comes from the reference; color comes from the brief.

## 3. Color palette: split-complementary
Orange dominates. Its split complements (teal and ultramarine) carry the cool dome and inlay. Gold, analogous to orange, binds the scheme.
- **Dominant, 60%:** `minecraft:orange_terracotta`: walls, plinth, portal frame
- **Supporting, 20%:** `minecraft:prismarine_bricks`: drum, dome, kiosks, lesser towers, minaret caps, cornice (stairs/slabs)
- **Inlay, 15%:** `minecraft:blue_glazed_terracotta`: scroll band, rosettes, plinth panels, minaret rings
- **Accent, 5%:** `minecraft:gold_block`: finial, drum ring

## 4. Motifs & ornament
- **Ringed shafts:** reference minarets carry bands at about one-quarter and one-half height → blue rings on rows 8 and 13
- **Inlaid panel above the arch:** → blue wave-scroll band on row 10, non-script
- **Inlaid side panels:** → rotated glazed rosettes on the frame
- **Corner domes:** → prismarine kiosks flanking the drum
- **Blind-arch base:** → blue panels on plinth row 1, every third column

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
Ground is row 0; axis x=22; width 45.
- **Base:** plinth rows 0–2, full width, projecting 1 block
- **Supports:** body x 12–32, walls rows 3–12, set back 1 block; minarets 3×3 at x 0–2 and 42–44, rows 3–21; lesser towers 2 wide at x 9–10 and 34–35, rows 3–13
- **Entablature:** row 12 prismarine cornice
- **Crown:** drum 9 wide, rows 13–14, gold ring on row 13; onion dome rows 15–21, narrowing to a 3-wide neck; gold finial (the Lantern) rows 22–24; kiosks 3 wide at x 15–17 and 27–29
- **Entrance:** pishtaq 7×9 at x 19–25, rows 3–11, projecting 1 block (flush with plinth); portal 5×7 with pointed top, recessed 2 blocks behind the frame; niches 3×5 at x 14–16 and 28–30, recessed 1 block
- **Ratios (build ↔ photo):** total height/width 0.56 ↔ 0.55; body/width 0.47 ↔ 0.48; wall/body 0.48 ↔ 0.49; dome diameter/body 0.43 ↔ 0.44; dome height/diameter 0.78 ↔ 0.77; minaret height/width 6.3 ↔ 6.5. Minaret tops sit level with the dome crown, as in the reference.

## Scale & RELIEF — use it (this is judged)
- Width up to ~56 (X), height up to ~48 (Y) including the crown. Build big.
- DEEP relief: up to ~24 blocks of depth into −Z, and genuinely use it. Columns/buttresses
  project several blocks PROUD of the wall; the central portal RECESSES several blocks deep;
  cornices, string courses, and the crown step forward and back across MULTIPLE Z-layers. A
  near-flat screen only 4–6 deep is a failure.
- CROWN proportion: the crowning element (pediment / parapet / attic / cresting) must span the
  facade's full width and have real height — a deliberate culmination of the body, never a
  small cap perched on a wide base.
