# Temple of the Sevenfold Dawn — Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
A pilgrim-chapel of a sun-and-star cult, built by flame-carvers of a late "Flamboyant" age — the era when stone was worked thin as lace and worship meant *light through glass*. The reference (Sainte-Chapelle de Vincennes) reads as a single soaring devotional face: vertical, restless, crowned. I keep that mood — a reliquary turned to architecture.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference's language is **verticality and bristling silhouette**: a steep crowning gable, slender buttresses dividing the face, and a skyline that *erupts* in pinnacles and spirelets. Nothing is flat — every plane is broken by relief. This works because the eye is dragged upward in stages (portal → rose → gable → finial), never resting. I adopt this staged ascent and the flanking-turret frame, translating tracery into stepped voxel relief.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
The reference is pale honey limestone under a slate-blue roof — I take its *structure*, not its pallor. **Harmony: warm split-complementary.**
- **Dominant (~55%) — Red Sandstone** (warm amber body), the sun-baked stone.
- **Supporting (~30%) — Blue Terracotta** (steep roof, turret spires, gable field), the slate echoed but saturated.
- **Accent — Gold Block** (crockets, finials, tracery ribs) and **Blue + Red Stained Glass** blazing in the rose.

Amber against blue, lit by gold and jewel-glass: confident, devotional, unmistakably colorful.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference: the **flamboyant rose window**, **crocketed gable**, **pinnacle spires**, and **deep pointed portal**. Adapted — the rose becomes a stepped concentric glass medallion; crockets become regular gold studs climbing the gable rake; pinnacles become engaged blue-terracotta spirelets bonded to the corner turrets (never detached, no sky between).

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** broad sandstone plinth and stepped portal landing.
- **Supports:** two engaged buttress-turrets framing a central bay; quoined sandstone with gold bands every 8 rows.
- **Entrance:** deep pointed iwan-portal, recessed, gold-edged.
- **Openings:** one great rose (jewel glass) above the portal; paired lancets in each turret.
- **Crown:** steep ~60° gable, gold finial at apex.
- **Ratios:** facade height : width ≈ **1.6 : 1** (golden); rose diameter ≈ **0.45×** central-bay width; gable occupies the top **~40%**.
