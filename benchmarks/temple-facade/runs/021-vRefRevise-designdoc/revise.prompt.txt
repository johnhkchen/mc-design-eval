## Your finalized design document (honor it)
# Temple of the Solar Triumph

## 1. Lore & setting
A votive gate-temple to a sun-victory deity, raised by a desert river-empire to consecrate a returning army. Like the reference — a triumphal arch built not to be entered but to be *passed through* in procession — this is a threshold, not a shrine. Its single great mouth swallows the road; the god is the doorway itself.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
The reference reads as one square monolith pierced by a single tall barrel-vaulted passage — mass first, opening second. Strength comes from the **uninterrupted block silhouette** capped by a heavy attic, never a dome or spire. Authority is conveyed by deep relief on broad flanking piers, not by height. I keep that grammar: a cubic body, one commanding arch, a flat crowning parapet pressing the whole composition down into the earth.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
**Complementary-accented analogous harmony** — warm body, one cold strike.
- **Dominant — Smooth Red Sandstone:** terracotta-orange field, the sun-baked mass.
- **Supporting — Polished Granite:** salmon-pink piers and relief grounds, an analogous warm neighbor that gives the wall two-tone depth.
- **Accent — Gold Block:** gilded cornice, rondel shields, and finial trophies — the solar metal.
- **Cold strike — Lapis Lazuli Block:** the deep arch vault and attic frieze recesses, the single complementary azure that makes the gold blaze.

No white wall: the reference's pale stone is *translated* into warm desert stone.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
From the reference: (a) two colossal high-relief sculptural groups flanking the arch — here, gold-and-granite sun-warriors; (b) winged **Victory figures in the arch spandrels**, gilded; (c) an **attic band of repeated rondel shields** between pilasters; (d) a dentil/modillion cornice. All carried in block relief — stepped granite frames, gold-block bosses, lapis-recessed roundels.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Elevation:** near-square, ~1:1 (reference is ~equal width and height).
- **Arch:** central opening ≈ ½ total width; springs at ~⅖ height, crown at ~⅔ — a deep lapis-vaulted void giving real relief depth.
- **Base:** low stepped plinth in red sandstone.
- **Piers:** broad flanking masses (each ~¼ width) bearing the relief groups.
- **Entablature/attic:** the upper ~¼ is a gold-corniced parapet carrying the rondel band — a firm horizontal crown.

## A facade is ONE connected plane (critical)
Borrow the reference's RHYTHM, not its 3-D standalone parts. A real building's corner towers or
minarets are freestanding in the round; in a flat elevation they must read as ENGAGED — bonded to
the wall and projecting forward as relief, NEVER as detached pillars with sky between them. Do not
pull columns, towers, or framing off the body; every mass stays connected into a single facade.
