# The Verdigris Sanctuary of Qamar-Ashan

## 1. Lore & Setting
The Qamar-Ashan are a riverine dynasty of desert astronomers. They built this sanctuary as a mausoleum-temple to the Moon-Mother. Its sandstone is quarried from the red cliffs of the dynasty's homeland. Its copper dome was left to weather green, which they read as the sky slowly claiming the dead. Pilgrims walk the long water-axis at dusk and see the facade lit coral and gold.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
The reference uses perfect bilateral symmetry and a pyramid of forms. A raised plinth carries a square block with chamfered corners. A giant arched portal (iwan) rises in the center, a bulbous dome crowns it, small domed kiosks (chhatris) flank the dome, and minarets frame the whole. The eye climbs from the wide base to a single gilded point. This works because each tier repeats the arch-and-dome idea at a smaller scale, so the facade reads as one unified whole.

## 3. Color Palette: Complementary (red-orange vs blue-green), plus a metallic accent
- **Dominant:** `cut_red_sandstone`, a warm coral used for the walls, plinth and minarets. Mughal red sandstone replaces the reference's white.
- **Supporting:** `waxed_oxidized_cut_copper`, a verdigris teal used for the main dome, chhatri domes and the panels framing the iwan. It is the direct complement of the coral.
- **Shadow:** `dark_prismarine` fills the deep arch recesses and the tympana, the solid panels inside the arches. It gives depth without black.
- **Accent:** `gold_block`, used sparingly for finials, the dome's neck band, minaret balcony rings and the calligraphy band around the iwan.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
- **Pointed-arch niches** sit in two stacked tiers on each wing, recessed 2 blocks. They are drawn from the reference's double-storey alcoves.
- **A rectangular frame (pishtaq) around the iwan** carries a gold calligraphy border.
- **A blind arcade along the plinth** gives a repeating rhythm of small arches.
- **Slender pinnacles** rise at every corner of the main block, each topped with gold.
- **Lotus-and-spire finials** crown every dome.

## 5. Features & Proportion (in blocks)
- **Plinth:** 69 wide × 5 tall, with a blind arcade every 4 blocks.
- **Main block:** 33 wide × 27 tall, with 3-block chamfered corners. Width to height is about 1.2:1, as in the reference.
- **Iwan:** 11 wide × 31 tall, rising 4 blocks above the parapet. Its pointed arch opening is 7 × 19 and recessed 4 blocks, with an arched doorway 3 × 6 at its base.
- **Wings:** two tiers of arched niches on each side, each 5 × 9.
- **Dome:** 4-block drum with a gold band, then a 19-wide onion dome. The dome's height roughly equals the main block's height, as in the reference. Its apex is at y=60 and the finial reaches y=64. It is modeled as half-relief, 9 blocks deep.
- **Chhatris:** 5 wide, on 4 open copper-roofed posts, one on each side of the dome.
- **Minarets:** 5-wide octagons standing 3 blocks clear of the plinth corners, 52 tall. Three gold-ringed balconies split each shaft into four equal parts, and a chhatri sits on top.
- **Relief depth:** 3 blocks overall, 4 at the iwan and 9 at the dome.
