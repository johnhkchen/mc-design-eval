# Temple of the Amber Meridian — Facade Design Document

## 1. Lore & setting
A sun-temple of an invented desert dynasty, the *Meridian Order*, who worshipped the moment noon light strikes water. The reference reads as a serene, perfectly symmetrical mausoleum-shrine; I keep that funerary calm but recast it as a living temple to the daytime sky — heat below, heaven above.

## 2. Aesthetic & architectural logic
From the Taj I read **rigorous bilateral symmetry**, a **dominant central iwan** (recessed pointed-arch portal) framed by **stacked half-height niches**, a swelling **onion dome on a tall drum**, **four corner chattri kiosks**, and **flanking minaret towers** that fence the composition. This works because one giant arch commands the eye while the smaller niches set a rhythm and scale ladder. I adopt all of it — the strong centerpiece plus repeated subordinate openings — translated to voxel relief.

## 3. Color palette (color theory)
The reference is white marble — information, not mandate. I commit to a **complementary amber↔azure harmony**:
- **Smooth red sandstone** — dominant warm amber mass (plinth, walls, minarets).
- **Smooth sandstone** — supporting honey-pale courses and arch frames.
- **Lapis lazuli block** — azure complement; the dome and iwan spandrel inlay.
- **Gold block** — sparing accent on finial and the calligraphic border band.
- **Quartz** — thin crisp white pinstripe edging only.

Warm body, cool crown: the blue dome reads as captured sky against amber stone.

## 4. Motifs & ornament
The Taj's *pietra dura* inlay, calligraphic arch borders, and herringbone spandrels become: a **gold-and-quartz border band** outlining the central iwan, **lapis quatrefoil inlays** set into each niche tympanum, and a **vertical pinstripe** rhythm articulating the minarets.

## 5. Architectural features & proportion
- **Base:** raised quartz-edged plinth, ~1/6 of total height, lifting the facade off the ground.
- **Body:** square amber block; central iwan rises ~2/3 of body height, niches ~1/2.
- **Entablature:** a sandstone cornice with gold dentil band.
- **Crown:** lapis onion dome whose belly **overhangs the drum** (so it never collapses into a pyramid), capped by a gold finial; four corner chattris echo it.
- **Openings:** one deep recessed iwan, four flanking half-niches per side.
- **Key ratios:** facade width ≈ height (1:1 square mass before dome); iwan height ≈ φ × niche height; dome diameter ≈ drum width.
