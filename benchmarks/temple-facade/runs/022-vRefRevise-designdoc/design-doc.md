# Temple of the Cobalt Ascendant

## 1. Lore & Setting
A memorial-temple to a unifier-sage of an invented Azure Dynasty, raised on a mountain stair so pilgrims *climb* to meet it — echoing the reference's monumental podium and processional steps. It is a hall of remembrance, not worship: solemn, civic, eternal. The character is grave dignity made radiant, exactly the mood of that blue-crowned hall floating above white granite.

## 2. Aesthetic & Architectural Logic
The reference speaks a clear tripartite language: **massive pale stone body, deep bracketed eaves, a dominant glazed roof.** It works because weight is honest — a heavy granite podium grounds a lighter, soaring roof, and the eye is led up. I borrow the **double-eave silhouette**, the **three-arch front**, the **crowning tablet**, and the **dougong bracket band** that visually carries the overhang. Symmetry is absolute; every mass mirrors across the central axis.

## 3. Color Palette (Complementary: Cobalt ↔ Gold)
The reference hands me one true color — that cobalt roof — and I commit to it boldly rather than collapsing to its white walls.
- **Dominant:** `blue_glazed_terracotta` — the glazed tile roof, vivid and patterned like real琉璃 tile.
- **Field:** `calcite` — pale granite body, cool and luminous (neutral, never the star).
- **Accent (complement):** `gold_block` — gilded tablet, characters, finials. Gold opposite blue is the harmony's engine.
- **Counter-accent:** `red_concrete` — vermilion doors, the imperial spark against cool stone.

Blue + gold + a vermilion heartbeat on pale stone reads unmistakably bold and ceremonial.

## 4. Motifs & Ornament
From the reference: the **carved tablet over the central bay**, **gilded inscription characters** across the lintel band, **arched spandrels** with relief scrollwork, **bracket clusters** repeating under each eave, and **upturned roof horns**. I adapt these as a gold-framed central plaque, a row of three gold glyph-panels, calcite voussoir arches with stepped relief, and a rhythmic gold-tipped bracket course.

## 5. Architectural Features & Proportion
- **Base:** broad calcite podium with central stair, ~1/3 of total height.
- **Body:** three engaged bays; **central bay 1.3× the side bays**, pierced by the tallest arch.
- **Entablature:** projecting bracket band, eave overhang ≈ ¼ of wall width.
- **Crown:** double cobalt roof; **roof height ≈ wall height (1:1)**, the defining gesture.
- **Openings:** three vermilion-doored arches, central tallest, flanked by gilded glyph-panels.
