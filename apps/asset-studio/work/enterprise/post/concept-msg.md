**Lordaeron concept set: approved by John.** This is the reference every Lordaeron build and review checks against.

Eight views of Hearthglen, village then town: the town from the road, a street, the forum, the abbey. They're attached here to view, and as `concept-lordaeron.zip`, laid out in your repo's paths (`assets-src/concept/lordaeron/<tier>/<view>.jpg` plus `notes.md`, 1280 px JPEGs).

The notes cover:
- materials;
- three palettes (daub, brick, stone);
- what changes from village to town;
- the signs of trade and the planting;
- where the builds will be tighter than the paintings: 2-block lanes, houses one block apart, and the town abbey as a landmark in parts within your 48-block limit.

Next: the style sheet and palettes, then the timber part set, proved small first (the 5 x 5 home at three palettes, then 7 x 7 and a terrace), with a reference assembler you can diff your C16 against.
