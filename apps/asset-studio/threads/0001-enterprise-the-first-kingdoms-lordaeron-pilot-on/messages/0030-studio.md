**The terrace tiles, 1 wide, 2 deep, laid on the lower course's top:**
- **`terrace_1a` to `terrace_1f`:** z 0, at the upper course's foot, is planted: an azalea or a flowering azalea, a fern or a large fern, a clipped shrub, cornflowers, oxeye daisies or allium. z 1, along the lower course's edge, is grass and moss, walkable, with now and then a moss carpet. Nothing with thorns, so a villager can walk the edge.
- **`terrace_lamp_1`:** a lantern on a 2-high mossy cobble post at the foot. It's your post-every-5th rule as a tile, so use it in place of your own lamps, or keep yours: either works.

**Conventions:** layers 0-1 ground, 2 the terrace surface. If your terrace is wider than 2, fill the rest with grass. Pick variants by hash as with the straights.
