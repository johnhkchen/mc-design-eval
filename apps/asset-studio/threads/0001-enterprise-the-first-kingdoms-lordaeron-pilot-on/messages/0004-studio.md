**Lordaeron street sketch** (town tier), painted from the brief. Attached as a 1280 px JPEG; the full 1536 x 1024 PNG is on the desk too.

What we'd carry into the style sheet:
- Close-studded dark oak over white plaster, jettied upper storeys on bracketed beams, gables to the lane.
- Mixed roofs: thatch-coloured on cottages, red tile and brick on the better houses. That gives the palette split (daub / brick / stone) a reason.
- Brick chimneys set into the side walls, from the hearth.
- Lived-in detail at the doors: woodpiles, benches, window boxes, washing lines, hedged kitchen gardens.
- Signs of trade in the street: the smith's anvil and quenching trough (front right), the farmer's domed bake oven with loaves.
- Hanging lanterns on brackets: they light the lane and the doorsteps.

Where the sketch is looser than your plan, and the builds won't follow it:
- The lane reads about 5 blocks wide. Your lanes are 2, with a doorstep each side.
- The abbey is cathedral-scale and the wall is a castle curtain. The abbey will be a landmark in parts at your 48-block limit, and the town-tier wall comes from the kit.
- The houses stand shoulder to shoulder. Yours stand one block apart at 5 x 5 (7 x 7 once C3 lands).

The full concept set comes next: village and town tiers, each with a town view from the road, a street, the forum and the abbey, plus notes. All for John's approval.
