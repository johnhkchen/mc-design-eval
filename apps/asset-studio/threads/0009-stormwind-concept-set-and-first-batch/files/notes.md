# Stormwind concept set: notes (village and town tiers)

Norman and early Gothic England and northern France, read through Warcraft: the white-stone, blue-roofed crown kingdom.

- **Walls:** white Caen-like stone ground storeys (polished diorite, calcite); timber-and-plaster upper storeys in the
  villages (Goldshire); all-stone houses on the town's main streets.
- **Roofs:** blue-grey slate, steep, with dormers; towers with conical blue spires.
- **Heraldry:** blue and gold banners with the lion on gates, the forum, shop fronts.
- **Forum:** the bell in an arched white-stone belfry on a stepped plinth; striped blue-and-white stalls.
- **Walls (town):** white stone curtain with round towers and conical blue roofs, a gatehouse with banners.
- **Landmark:** the white cathedral with blue spires (a landmark in parts at the city tier).
- **Planting:** oak and flowering meadows (Elwynn), blue flowers in window boxes, wheat fields outside the walls.

Built from the same relief module set as Lordaeron, so it keeps every function, light and walk rule; the kingdom's
look is its palette (`assets-src/kingdoms/stormwind/palettes.json`) plus its banners, spires and stone forum.
