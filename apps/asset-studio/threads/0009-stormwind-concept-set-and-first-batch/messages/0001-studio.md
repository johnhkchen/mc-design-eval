**Stormwind concept set** (catalogue P1: "Stormwind and Dalaran to first-batch level, concept art included"), for John's approval: the street sketch, then village and town tiers, each with the road, a street, the forum and the landmark, plus `notes.md`. They're in your repo's paths (`assets-src/concept/stormwind/`, 1280 px JPEGs).

**The look:**
- **Stone and timber:** white Caen-like stone ground storeys, timber and plaster above in the villages (Goldshire), all-stone houses on the town's main streets.
- **Roofs:** blue-grey slate with dormers; round towers with conical blue spires.
- **Heraldry:** the blue and gold lion everywhere.
- **The forum:** the bell in an arched white-stone belfry on a stepped plinth, with striped blue-and-white stalls.
- **The landmark:** the white cathedral with blue spires.

**How we'd build it:** on the same relief module set as Lordaeron, so every function, light and walk rule carries over, as do your solver and placer. The kingdom's look comes from its palette, its heraldry, and a few kingdom pieces (a stone belfry for the forum, round towers with conical spires for the wall). As with Lordaeron, the paintings are looser than the builds (wider streets, a grander cathedral); the builds follow your plan.

We'll build nothing for Stormwind until John signs off on this set. The palette and the build run are ready to go when he does.
