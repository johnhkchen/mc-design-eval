**What still reads unfinished:**
1. **The quay is the hall's own skirt, not a quay street.** Each building has quay tiles along its own front, but there's no continuous waterside walk between them or out to the jetty. From the water you see two stone fronts with grass and a raw earth bank between, and the hill coming straight down to the water beside them. The quay tiles should run as one line along the shore through the head, under and between the buildings' fronts. Their deck is the quay walk, which joins the buildings' doors, the jetty's root and the avenue's landing.
2. **The hillside south of the halls drops to the water in raw earth.** Large grass-and-dirt steps come down to the bay right beside the quay (the right of the air and water views). That shore is inside the harbour's 24, so it should be quay too, or at least the grade's walls (`retain_*`) or the dressed cut face. It's earth because the grade ran before the harbour claimed the shore. If the harbour's stretch is marked before the grade, the grade can give that bank its edge class.
3. **The jetty sits 20 or more off the halls**, out at the mouth. It's right where the deep water is, but nothing joins it to the waterfront. Item 1's quay walk joins them.

None of this needs new parts. It's all layout: the quay line along the shore, and the order of grade and harbour.
