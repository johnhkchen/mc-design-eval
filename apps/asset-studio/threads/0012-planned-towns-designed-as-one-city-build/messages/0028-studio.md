**r91 rendered.** Each pair is r90 on the left and r91 on the right: the air, the water and above.

**Better:**
- **All three buildings stand.** The customs house is out on reclaimed ground at the head, the warehouse and the fish market along the shore.
- **The quay runs as one line.** From the water it's a continuous stone quay wall along the shore south of the halls, with lamps on red posts. That's where r90 had the earth bank.

**One fault, and it's large: the band's levelling left the hillside's old terrace stones hanging in the air.** I count **326 stone blocks hanging over 2 or more air**, at x -790 to -735, z 150 to 215. That's the jagged grey wreckage over and into the second hall's roof in the air view, and at the top right of the water view.

**The cause, from a slice at x -786, z 195–208:**
- **r90:** the hill there is earth with the grade's terrace courses in it (stone bricks, polished andesite copings at y 66–78).
- **r91:** the earth is gone down to the deck (y 64, a path), and **the course stones are still there, floating**. For example, -786 71 200 is a cracked stone brick with 6 air under it, and -786 75 204 to 207 is a row of course stones with 10 air under them.

So the levelling removes natural ground but treats the grade's own earthworks tiles as built, and keeps them.

**The fix:**
- The band should clear **everything the grade laid** in it (the earthworks tiles, their copings and parapets), not just natural ground.
- It should stop only at streets, plots and buildings. Earthworks belong to the land, and the band reshapes the land.

**Also at that slice, the band's back face is 14 high** (z 208: stone and earth to grass at y 78, over the path at 64), and it's left as earth. Over 4, it wants the same treatment as the grade's steps: courses of up to 4 a tile's depth apart, or a band ending on a terraced stair of retaining walls. A single 14-high retaining wall reads as a dam. The cheapest honest fix is to shorten the band where the land behind rises more than 4 within its 6: let the band stop at the first 4-high step, and give that step the kingdom's wall.

**Small:** one dirt-topped block stands in the quay wall line just south of the fish market (the water view, middle). It's a quay cell not given its tile, likely the old bank's top left where the line passes a corner.

Everything else reads as a harbour now: boat, jetty, quay and halls.
