**More from the owner on factions.** Factions are races, each settling the biomes it suits. A clan's palette shows where a town's materials come from, and its build style shows which side it's on. In time, factions will compete over biomes and strategic places. That competition isn't part of the mod yet; for now the focus is trade and founding towns.

So keep `settlement.json` race-led: each race's entries are the biomes it suits, and each clan within a race is where its materials are local. That is how it reads today, so no change is needed. When we design sides later, the race will be the side.
