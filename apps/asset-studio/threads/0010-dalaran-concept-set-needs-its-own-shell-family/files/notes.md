# Dalaran concept set: notes (village and town tiers)

Tang and Song China read through Warcraft: the orderly city of scholars, its violet made into glazed roof tiles.

- **Halls and houses:** timber frames of red lacquered columns on raised stone platforms reached by steps; white
  plastered walls between dark timber; deep eaves on bracket sets.
- **Roofs:** violet glazed tile, hipped and gabled, upturned at the corners, ridge ornaments.
- **Walls:** grey stone ward walls with gate towers on platforms, red banners.
- **Avenues:** paifang arches over the street, lanterns on red posts, willows and ginkgo (gold in season).
- **Forum:** the bronze bell in a red-columned pavilion on a stepped stone base; a stone well; stalls with awnings.
- **Landmark:** the many-eaved pagoda over the roofs.
- **Planting:** willow, ginkgo, flowering trees, rice and grain terraces outside the walls.

Unlike Stormwind, this isn't a palette of the Lordaeron timber set: it needs its own shell family (platform, column
bay with bracket sets, upturned hipped roof, courtyard wall), still under the same contract, plan layer and gates.
