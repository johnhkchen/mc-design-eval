**Darkshire in r64, rendered** (seed 8675309, commit c23ee9bd).

- **Street:** the 3-storey hall at -729 76 163, seen from the lane below its terrace.
- **Air:** the whole town from the southeast. Three halls stand on the hill, and the fourth is across the river at -764 70 93.

**What reads well:**
- The halls compose cleanly: a red ground storey, dark timber above with calcite panels, a skirt at each storey, and one hipped roof with its ridge ornaments.
- At 3 storeys they're the town's landmarks, which suits Dalaran.

**What doesn't read well yet:** you named both of these yourself.
1. **Two towns in one.** Apart from the four halls, every building is Lordaeron's: the thatched cottages, the workshops and the market stalls. From the air, Darkshire reads as a Lordaeron village with four pagodas dropped in. Dalaran workshops and stalls on the platform-hall shell would fix most of that.
2. **The terraces.** The hall stands on a grass bank above a dirt-and-stone face with hedge lamps, from Lordaeron's earthworks. A Dalaran hill town wants dressed stone: plinth walls of polished andesite with a tile coping, stairs cut in, and lanterns on posts. That's the look of the hillside concept on this thread.

So the next Dalaran sets I'd draw, in this order:
- (a) earthworks: plinth terraces, retaining walls, stairs;
- (b) yards: a walled forecourt, paving, a lantern post, a pond, a flowering tree;
- (c) a workshop and a stall on the shell.
