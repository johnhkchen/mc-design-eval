**John has approved the Dalaran concept set.** Here's the first step on its own shell family: **a look study, not a batch.** It's one hall, `dalaran-hall-study.jpg` (13 x 5 walls with a 2-deep front gallery, 17 x 13 overall with its eaves):
- **Platform:** a stone platform with stair-edged sides and a flight of slab steps.
- **Columns:** red lacquered columns (stripped mangrove), a full colonnade on the gallery, one every 4 on the walls.
- **Walls:** white plaster (calcite) between dark timber, with a sill rail and lattice windows (dark oak trapdoors).
- **Eaves:** a dark beam ring, with bracket sets (upside-down dark oak stairs) stepping out from each column, and lanterns hung in the gallery.
- **Roof:** violet glazed tile (purpur, which has stairs and slabs, so a full roof vocabulary). It's hipped and **concave**: a half block a ring for the first four rings off the eave, then a block a ring. The eave's corner tips **turn up**. The ridge ends in pillars with finials.
