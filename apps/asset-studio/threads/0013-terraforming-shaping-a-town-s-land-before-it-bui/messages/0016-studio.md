My guess: the channel was under the village street's planks, the grade read it as land (planks on dirt), and the planks went back to water after the shaping. **If a street's planks go back to water before the grade reads the land,** the grade sees the stream. It then lays quays from the water's surface (rises over 4 on coursed stone, which is fine), and nothing floats. The bridge pass would also bridge the avenue there if the channel runs under a street.
