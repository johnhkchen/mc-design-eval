**Farms: fields worth looking at.** John asked for more interesting farm plots. In the renders so far, the fields round the towns are plain farmland rectangles with a water line: they work, but they're the flattest thing in the picture. We'd like to give fields the same treatment as yards: **tileable field plots** with character, plus the catalogue's farm buildings (farmstead, field barn, mill).

**What we'd make (Lordaeron first, other kingdoms by palette):**
- **Field tiles, 9 x 9** (a water source in the middle reaches every cell, the game's hydration rule):
  - strip fields: 2 or 3 crops in strips;
  - a pumpkin and melon patch;
  - a market garden: carrots, potatoes, beets and a bean row on fence posts;
  - a sunflower and flax border;
  - a fallow tile with a haystack and a scarecrow;
  - an orchard tile (trees in rows, a beehive);
  - a small water meadow with a pond.
- **Edges:** hedgerows with field maples, dry-stone walls (cobble walls), split-rail fences, gates and a headland path. Edges carry sockets, as the yards do, so a run of fields reads as one landscape.
- **Props:** a field barn, haystacks and stooks, a scarecrow, a cart, a well and trough, a field shrine.
- **Buildings:** the farmstead (barn plus yard) and a mill, from the module set.

**What we need to know from your sim, so the fields keep working:**
1. **How fields are laid and tended:** does the sim place farmland itself and let Farmers work it? Must the crop cells be a contiguous block, or can a tile mix crops, paths and features? And does the census count farmland cells?
2. **Which crops your economy trades:** wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, pumpkin, melon? Anything modded, like cotton or flax?
3. **Field size and placement:** do fields sit on a grid outside the wall, by hex, or on plots like homes? What footprint should a field tile be, if not 9 x 9?
4. **Animals:** pastures are in the catalogue too. Should the fields and pastures share one edge vocabulary?

Answer whatever you know now. We'll start the field tiles on 9 x 9 with vanilla crops and adjust to your answers.
